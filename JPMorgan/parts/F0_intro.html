Part 1. The map: seven beliefs and what each one built

In plain English Most companies have values on a poster. J.P. Morgan's are different in one respect: you can trace almost every unusual feature of how the firm is organised back to something Dimon or Erdoes said and then did. The fortress balance sheet is why the firm was the buyer on three crisis weekends. "Our client, not my client" is why private bankers are paid a bonus rather than a commission. "We seed books of business" is why your internship exists. This map shows the seven chains. The rest of the document walks each one.
