## Question

> Why does Ropes & Gray appeal to you and what do you hope to gain from attending this masterclass? (150–200 words)

## Answer — 176 words

Ropes & Gray appeals to me because it chose depth over breadth. Rather than covering everything, it built around private capital and life sciences, and that focus shows in the work.

What interests me most, coming from an asset management angle, is that the firm sits on both sides of a line most firms only cross once. It is regular counsel to more than 2,000 registered funds or their independent trustees, while running the market's leading secondaries practice, advising ten of the fifteen largest global buyers. Those two worlds are converging through evergreen and interval structures, and Ropes is already positioned where that happens. Advising EQT on its acquisition of Coller Capital is the clearest example: a sponsor buying a secondaries manager because liquidity, not origination, is now the binding constraint in private markets.

From the masterclass I want to test that reading against people who do the work — to hear from trainees how the funds, transactional and regulatory teams genuinely share one sponsor client, and to get direct feedback on my application before I submit.
