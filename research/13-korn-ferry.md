## 13.1 Ownership / corporate / what it is
- **Talent Q founded 2006 by Roger Holdsworth**, the occupational psychologist who co-founded **Saville & Holdsworth Ltd (SHL)** and left SHL in 2002; founding aim = "tests that adapted to the candidate." [INDEPENDENT — 123test/psychometrictests corroborated] https://www.123test.com/assessment-training/talent-q/
- **Hay Group acquired Talent Q, April 2014** (strategic partners since 2010). [INDEPENDENT — BusinessWire primary press release] https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20140415005164/en/
- **Hay Group acquired by Korn Ferry, 2015** → Talent Q now administered by **Korn Ferry** (a listed exec-search/organisational-consulting giant; assessment is one arm of a consulting business, unlike pure-play SHL). [INDEPENDENT — Umbrex profile + consistent]

## 13.3 Why a firm picks Korn Ferry
- **Adaptive + short**: Elements ≈12–15 questions/test vs SHL's 24–30 — better candidate experience, harder to share answers. [PREP-VENDOR consistent]
- **Per-item timing** discourages disproportionate dwelling; claimed fairness/anti-cheat benefits. [PREP-VENDOR/INFERRED]
- **Whole-person + consulting bundle**: KF4D links assessment to Korn Ferry's success-profiles library, leadership consulting, pay data (a Hay legacy) — attractive to firms already buying Korn Ferry search/consulting. [VENDOR/INFERRED]
- **Bespoke builds**: UBS Culture Match = custom values-keyed SJT. [PREP-VENDOR — assessmentday/jobtestprep UBS pages]
- 40+ languages / global norms. [VENDOR-era]

## 13.4 FULL MECHANICS
### Elements (adaptive ability; per-item clocks) [PREP-VENDOR — jobtestprep, graduatesfirst, practiceaptitudetests, assessmentday; consistent]
| Test | Items | Timing | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Elements Verbal** | **15** | **75 s first question of each passage-bundle, 60 s subsequent** | passage → statements (T/F/Cannot-Say-style options) |
| **Elements Numerical** | **12** | **90 s first question per table, 75 s subsequent** | 4 tables/data-sets × 3 questions; multi-step calc; calculator generally permitted [INFERRED — not explicitly stated; flag] |
| **Elements Logical** | **12** | **~75 s/question** (~15–23 min total) | abstract/diagrammatic series, find next |
- **ADAPTIVE**: answer correctly → harder next item. [PREP-VENDOR consistent + founding-thesis INDEPENDENT]
- **Per-item clock, NOT whole-test clock** — the signature difference vs SHL. Unanswered item at clock-expiry = moved on (effectively scored as not-correct); **cannot bank time across items**. [PREP-VENDOR]
- Answer-option format quirk: numerical/verbal often have **many options (e.g. 8–12+)** making blind guessing weak. [PREP-VENDOR — jobtestprep noted large option sets; tag [PREP-VENDOR]]
- Device: browser, unproctored typical; disconnect/resume behaviour [UNKNOWN].

### Aspects (volume/early-careers line) [PREP-VENDOR — graduatesfirst]
- **Aspects Ability**: verbal / numerical / checking; **up to ~12 min** each; question counts not stated [UNKNOWN].
- **Aspects Styles**: behavioural/competency questionnaire, **~8 min**, maps to **16 competencies**.

### Dimensions (personality) [PREP-VENDOR — jobtestprep/graduatesfirst/prepterminal, consistent]
- **160 statements**, presented in **blocks of 4**; **rate each** on 'completely untrue' → 'very true'; **if two+ statements in a block get the same rating you must RANK them** ("rate-then-rank" hybrid — semi-ipsative tiebreak, resists uniform fake-good).
- **Untimed, ~25 min typical.** Three domains: **People & Relationships, Tasks & Projects, Drives & Emotions.** Used later-stage + development.

## 13.7 How to prepare (legit)
- **Per-item clock strategy** (vs SHL whole-test): cannot bank time; use full window on hard items (no reward for finishing an item early); **never dwell past the clock** — when it expires you're moved on. Answer every item within its window; guessing weak with large option sets → **eliminate then commit**.
- **Adaptive**: harder = winning; early items shape the difficulty path; start careful.
- Numerical: 4-table structure means **first question of each table carries the extra reading load** (90 s) — use it to digest the table for the following two questions.
- Verbal: strict passage-only discipline (as §6.4).
- **Dimensions**: authentic + decisive; the rate-then-rank tiebreak punishes uniform fake-good (forced differentiation within blocks); consistent across 160 items; see §6.4 personality rules.
- **UBS Culture Match**: study UBS's published "Keys"/values; most/least keying logic as §6.4 SJT section.

## 13.9 False-positive / honest-candidate risks
- **Per-item clock + unstable connection**: a lag/disconnect during an item's window can eat the whole item (no whole-test buffer to absorb it). Resume behaviour undocumented → screenshot/record if connection shaky. [INFERRED]
- **Adaptive anxiety**: rising difficulty misread as failing → panic. (It means winning.)
- **Neurodivergence + forced ranking** (Dimensions tiebreaks) and time-per-item pressure (processing-speed differences) — adjustments case. [INFERRED]
- Low surveillance overall → small false-flag surface (like Aon). [INFERRED]
