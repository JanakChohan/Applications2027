## 12.2 Why the test exists (hiring problem) + construct & validity

- **Pitch:** cognitive aptitude ("general intelligence / *g*") is among the strongest single predictors of job performance and trainability, cheaper and more scalable than interviews. Criteria sells "science + structure + fairness" against gut-feel hiring, aimed at employers too small for enterprise SHL/Aon consulting. [VENDOR — about-us, InfoBrief]
- **CCAT construct:** general cognitive aptitude via three sub-abilities — **Spatial Reasoning, Verbal Ability, Math & Logic.** Claims to predict how well someone will "acquire, organize, retain and apply information on the job." [VENDOR — Criteria-InfoBrief-CCAT.pdf]
- **Vendor validity marketing (NOTE: two DIFFERENT charts, internally inconsistent — flag this):**
  - **CCAT InfoBrief / UCAT one-pager text:** cognitive aptitude is "**1.6x as predictive as unstructured interviews and 4x as predictive as experience**." [VENDOR]
  - **eBook "Definitive Guide" Figure 1** bar chart: Job Experience **r=0.07**, Unstructured Interviews **r=0.19**, Cognitive Aptitude **r=0.31**. [VENDOR — Criteria-eBook-DefinitiveGuidetoPreEmploymentTesting.pdf]
  - **UCAT Overview one-pager** bar chart uses DIFFERENT numbers: Education **0.13**, Job Experience **0.18**, Interviews **0.26**, Aptitude Tests **0.50**. [VENDOR — Criteria-Overview-UCAT.pdf]
  - `[INFERRED]` The 0.50 figure echoes the classic **Schmidt & Hunter (1998)** operational-validity estimate for GMA; the 0.31 figure in the eBook is closer to the **downward-revised estimates of Sackett, Zhang, Berry & Lievens (2022)**, who corrected GMA operational validity to ~**0.31** (arguing prior meta-analyses over-corrected for range restriction). The cross-cutting psychometrics chapter should lean on Sackett et al. 2022 as the current best independent estimate and treat Criteria's "4x / 0.50" marketing as the optimistic older figure. [INDEPENDENT — general IO-psych literature; not Criteria-specific]
- **CCAT published psychometrics (primary, from vendor technical text) [VENDOR — Criteria-InfoBrief-CCAT.pdf "Psychometric properties" §]:**
  - **Normative sample: 106,740 individuals**, adults 18+, incumbents + applicants, "hundreds of companies," roles = managerial / technical services / customer service / sales. **Median = 24, SD = 7.00.** Administered **>4 million times** since standardisation.
  - **Reliability: Cronbach's α = 0.86** (n=97,929) — "very high."
  - **Construct validity:** CCAT vs **Wonderlic (WPT) r = .78** (n=34); CCAT vs **Minicog MRAB r = .55** (n=355). ("Correlations with competing aptitude tests range .55–.8.")
  - **Criterion validity:** sales reps **r = .33** with training performance (n=745); government social workers **r = .44** with supervisor-rated performance (n=42).
  - ⚠ **DISCREPANCY:** the CCAT product web page (fetched) reports an OLDER/smaller norm: **985 working adults, mean 24.2, SD 8.58** — this is a legacy figure. The current InfoBrief PDF's **106,740 / median 24 / SD 7.00** supersedes it. Flag that vendor materials are inconsistent; use the PDF. [VENDOR — both]
- **EPP reliability:** test-retest reliabilities for scale scores reported ~**.67–.90** ("within acceptable limits"). [VENDOR — search of EPP materials]

## 12.4 FULL MECHANICS by product

### CCAT — Criteria Cognitive Aptitude Test (flagship) [VENDOR — InfoBrief + ScoreReportGuide-CCAT]
- **50 multiple-choice questions, 15-minute hard time limit** (= **~18 seconds/question**). **"Very few people finish all 50 items"** — vendor confirms most candidates do NOT complete (candidate lore "~1 in 3 finish" is directionally right but the precise fraction is `[UNKNOWN]`; vendor only says "very few").
- **Three interleaved categories:** Spatial Reasoning (odd-one-out, pattern/rotation), Verbal Ability (antonyms, sentence completion, analogies, attention to detail), Math & Logic (word problems, number/letter series, syllogisms, tables/graphs). 5 answer options typical.
- **NO calculator, no scratch tool provided by the engine** — candidate must do mental math / use own paper (vendor doesn't supply; unproctored candidates commonly use scratch paper — allowed unless employer proctors). `[VENDOR silent on calculator → INFERRED "no calculator"; corroborated by prep-vendors]`
- **NOT a single fixed form, and NOT adaptive:** the CCAT is a **"dynamic assessment drawing on an item bank of thousands of individually optimised items — no two candidates get the exact same set."** So it is **randomised/linear-on-the-fly, non-adaptive** (item order/difficulty do NOT change in response to your answers). This corrects the common "fixed-form" assumption — it's fixed-*length*, fixed-*time*, randomised-*content*. [VENDOR — InfoBrief §Dynamic item bank]
- **Device-agnostic**, any location, any time. **Disconnect behaviour:** each candidate gets a **Test Event ID**; if they drop, they return to **www.oda1.com**, re-enter the Event ID, and resume "from where they left off" (the timer context is server-tracked; session logs capture connectivity). [VENDOR — ScoreReportGuide]
- **Session logging:** "detailed test logs" from login to finish — time in assessment, internet connectivity, individual issues/actions. [VENDOR]

### UCAT — Universal Cognitive Aptitude Test [VENDOR — Overview-UCAT + ScoreReportGuide-UCAT]
- **40 questions, 20-minute limit** (~30 s/question). **Language-independent** — deliberately **no verbal/vocabulary content**, so it is translation-free and used for **international candidates and non-native English speakers.**
- **Four sub-scores:** Logic & Analysis, Numerical Reasoning, Attention to Detail, Spatial Reasoning. Raw score /40 + percentile vs norm.
- **"Top positions for the UCAT": Analysts, Accounting/Finance, Technical Support, Web Designers, Software Developers** — i.e., Criteria explicitly positions UCAT as the finance/analyst-oriented cognitive test (relevant if a UK/international finance employer picks Criteria). [VENDOR]

### CBST — Criteria Basic Skills Test [VENDOR product page + prep-vendors]
- **40 items, 20 minutes.** Measures basic **math, grammar, spelling, language, attention to detail, overall trainability** — an entry-level literacy/numeracy screen, **not** front-office finance calibre. Verbal raw + Math raw sub-scores. [VENDOR/PREP-VENDOR]

### CAST — Criteria Attention Skills Test (the "attention" product; prompt's "CAB" ≈ this) [PREP-VENDOR + VENDOR]
- Measures sustained/selective attention: **Divided Attention (multitasking), Selective Attention (vigilance & filtering), Perceptual Reaction Time.** Used for roles needing vigilance (dispatch, operations, safety). `[NOTE]` The prompt's label "**CAB**" does not map to a current Criteria product name; Criteria's attention test is **CAST**, and its basic-skills test is **CBST**. Treat "CAB" as a mislabel; both covered here. Exact item count/time for CAST `[UNKNOWN]` — not on the pages fetched.

### EPP — Employee Personality Profile [VENDOR — ScoreReportGuide-EPP + product page]
- **140 prompts. Untimed but ~10 minutes.** Measures **12 traits: Achievement, Assertiveness, Competitiveness, Conscientiousness, Cooperativeness, Extroversion, Managerial, Motivation, Openness, Patience, Self-Confidence, Stress Tolerance.** Built on the **Big Five / Five-Factor Model** ("most widely validated system"). [VENDOR]
- **Response format:** the vendor score-report guide and product page do **NOT** state forced-choice vs Likert. Prep-vendor descriptions and the "140 prompts / ~10 min / traits scored on continuous percentile scales" strongly imply a **single-stimulus agree–disagree Likert-type** format (NOT ipsative forced-choice). `[INFERRED — HIGH confidence but not vendor-confirmed → mark [UNKNOWN] on exact scale points]`
- Each trait → **percentile vs global norm** (not good/bad; "job fit"). **Job-family match:** associate a job family and the report shows an **overall % match** (e.g. "67% matched with Analysis, Planning & Consulting") based on which traits are benchmarked for that family; **12 job families** available; report highlights in green the trait ranges linked to success + generates **customised interview questions**. [VENDOR]

### Emotify — emotional intelligence (Revelian-built) [VENDOR blog/BusinessWire + PREP-VENDOR + GraduatesFirst]
- **Ability-based EI**, ~**20 minutes**, launched North America July 2020 post-Revelian. Three tasks measuring **Emotional Perception, Emotional Understanding, Emotion Management**:
  - **Matching Faces (~30 rounds)** — identify emotions in facial expressions (perception).
  - **Emotional Ties (~10 rounds)** — reason about which emotions follow from situations (understanding).
  - **Emotions in Action / workplace scenarios (~20-min portion)** — choose how to manage emotions in work situations (management).
  [VENDOR + PREP-VENDOR — round counts from prep-vendors, corroborate]

### Revelian Cognify (game-based cognitive) — legacy Revelian, now Criteria [PREP-VENDOR + GraduatesFirst]

Games: **Short Cuts, Grid Lock, Resemble, Numbubbles, Tally Up, Proof It.** Grouped: **Problem Solving (3 games — fluid reasoning: Grid Lock = Tetris-like packing, Resemble = pattern recreation/mirror-rotate, Short Cuts), Numerical Reasoning (2 — Numbubbles = pop bubbles summing to target, Tally Up = highest-value token group), Verbal Knowledge (1 — Proof It, optional/employer-dependent).** Captures **telemetry/behavioural process data** (speed, error patterns, approach) not just outcomes. [PREP-VENDOR + GraduatesFirst]
- **"Theme Park Hero"** — Revelian's other game-based product (game-based cognitive with a theme-park narrative); largely ANZ legacy. Mechanics detail `[UNKNOWN]` beyond name. [INFERRED from acquisition materials]

## 12.5 Is it tailored to the role?

- **Same core instrument ships to everyone** — the CCAT/UCAT/EPP items are NOT bespoke per employer. Tailoring happens at the **interpretation layer**, not the test:
  - **CCAT "suggested score ranges by position"** — a fixed library of **~46 job families** (see §12.6) each with a recommended raw-score band, computed from **>700,000 CCAT assessments**. The employer "associates" a job family with the test event; the report then shows within/above/below that band. [VENDOR — InfoBrief + ScoreReportGuide]
  - **EPP job-match profiles** — 12 job families each with benchmarked "success" trait ranges → overall % match. [VENDOR]
  - Employers **can build custom score ranges** from their own incumbents/applicant pool via Criteria's team. [VENDOR]
- So: **role-tailoring = norm/benchmark overlay on a universal test**, not a role-specific test. [INFERRED — clearly implied by vendor docs]

## 12.7 How to prepare LEGITIMATELY

- **CCAT is a SPEED test first.** ~18 s/question, no calculator, most don't finish. Legit strategy: (a) **triage/skip-and-return** — bank the easy verbal/logic points, don't sink 90 s into one spatial item; (b) drill **mental arithmetic, fractions/percentages, averages, ratios** so no calculator isn't a handicap; (c) learn the recurring item types (antonyms, analogies, number/letter series, odd-one-out, syllogisms, seating/logic puzzles, tables/graphs); (d) practise under a **strict 15-min clock** to build pace tolerance; (e) don't leave blanks at time-up (no evidence of negative marking → guess remaining). [INFERRED from mechanics + PREP-VENDOR consensus]
- **UCAT:** same speed logic, 30 s/question, no verbal — heavier on numerical/spatial/attention; practise data interpretation. [INFERRED/VENDOR]
- **EPP / personality: answer authentically and consistently.** The EPP has **faking/consistency detectors** (§12.8) — trying to "game favourable" triggers the Self-Enhancing scale and can trigger an **invalid result**. Read every prompt (racing → Inconsistent Responding flag). There are "no right answers"; the honest strategy is to answer as your genuine work-self, steadily. [VENDOR + INFERRED]
- **Emotify / Cognify (game-based): don't try to reverse-engineer "correct" personalities** — Emotify is ability-based EI (there ARE better/worse answers, so genuine emotional reasoning helps); Cognify rewards fast accurate fluid reasoning + numeric fluency; a couple of familiarisation runs to remove UI-shock is the main legitimate edge. [INFERRED + PREP-VENDOR]

## 12.8 INTEGRITY MONITORING

- **Proctoring model = single "AI Proctoring" add-on, one-click per assessment (NOT tiered live/record-review menu on the public page):** signals collected — **(1) Image Capture** (periodic candidate photos → detect a substitute taking over), **(2) Image Analysis** (AI compares captures → flags multiple faces / unauthorised devices), **(3) Screen & Audio Sampling** (periodic screen grabs + ambient audio snippets → detect second devices / outside help / unauthorised resources). **No browser lockdown / no invasive install** ("works within the standard assessment experience"). Employer receives a **proctoring report of flagged incidents + supporting evidence**; the employer (not Criteria) decides action. Candidates are **"informed about monitoring before they begin"** (transparency-stated; explicit written-consent flow `[UNKNOWN]`). [VENDOR — criteriacorp.com/ai-proctoring]
- **Baseline security even WITHOUT proctoring:** **dynamic item bank** (no two candidates get identical items — deters answer-sharing) + **detailed session logging** (time, connectivity, actions). [VENDOR — InfoBrief]
- **CCAT "Invalid Result" (prior-exposure detector):** the system flags an **Invalid Result** when it determines a candidate has **seen that CCAT version before**, judged by **speed + accuracy** — moving through "too quickly and without error" ⇒ flagged as already-familiar. Shown as a warning on the score report/graph. [VENDOR — ScoreReportGuide-CCAT §"How to Identify an Invalid Result"]
- **EPP validity/honesty scales (three):** **(1) Self-Enhancing** (exaggerating positives / hiding negatives — i.e. faking-good), **(2) Self-Critical** (faking-bad), **(3) Inconsistent Responding.** Scores may be **adjusted** based on self-enhancing/critical tendency. **Inconsistent Responding ≥ 3 ⇒ the whole EPP is flagged INVALID** (red error symbol in platform; warning on PDF p.4). [VENDOR — ScoreReportGuide-EPP §Validity and Response Style / How to Identify an Invalid Result]
- **How flags are actioned / candidate awareness:** flags surface to the **employer** in the report; there is **no evidence the candidate is told** they were flagged (they see only a generic "results couldn't be validated" at most, if anything). Action is entirely at employer discretion — could mean auto-reject, a proctored re-test, or ignore. [INFERRED — vendor docs describe employer-facing flags only]

## 12.9 FALSE-POSITIVE risks for honest candidates

- **CCAT "Invalid Result" false positive:** a genuinely fast, accurate first-timer (strong candidate) can look statistically like someone who has "seen it before" → risk of being wrongly flagged as having prior exposure. Speed+accuracy heuristic penalises the very top of the distribution. [INFERRED from vendor's own detection description — real risk]
- **EPP Inconsistent Responding ≥3:** rushing, misreading, language barrier, neurodivergence, or genuinely nuanced/ambivalent self-view can trip the consistency check → invalid result on an honest test-taker. [INFERRED]
- **EPP Self-Enhancing adjustment:** a genuinely confident, high-conscientiousness candidate answering truthfully may register as "self-enhancing" and have scores docked. [INFERRED]
- **AI proctoring false positives:** looking away (thinking), a family member/background noise, poor webcam, dual-monitor legitimately unused, low light → "multiple faces / unauthorised device / outside help" flags. No browser lockdown means fewer hard-blocks but the AI image/audio heuristics are probabilistic. [INFERRED — generic to record-and-review proctoring]
- **Disconnect mid-CCAT:** although Event-ID resume exists, a bad connection eats wall-clock/creates log anomalies that could read as suspicious; candidate should screenshot the Event ID. [INFERRED]
