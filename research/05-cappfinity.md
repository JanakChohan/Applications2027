## 5.2 WHY IT EXISTS — STRENGTHS-BASED HIRING RATIONALE

- Core pitch: instead of asking only whether you *can* do something (competency), strengths assessment measures **"what energises you and where you naturally perform at your best."** [PREP-VENDOR — intervyo/practiceaptitudetests, near-verbatim marketing]
- Vendor rationale (skills/strengths product page): measures "**80 behavioural skills, expressed in a shared language**"; every skill "**benchmarked against workplace performance over multiple years**"; aim = "performance-led, skills-based organisation." Product line "Skills Discovery." [VENDOR — cappfinity.com/solutions/.../skills-strengths-assessment/]
- Claimed benefits: predict performance AND **engagement/energy**; reduce coachability of pure competency answers; **DEI / level playing field** ("hiring based on strengths… level the playing field"). [VENDOR/PREP-VENDOR]
- Linklaters vendor case study frames the DEI angle explicitly: "**bias-free algorithmic assessments enabling the firm to look past grades, subjects and institutions and recruit for ability, fit and potential.**" [VENDOR case study, relayed via search + recruiter.co.uk]

### IO-psych evidence on strengths vs competency / SJT validity (INDEPENDENT, general — not Cappfinity-specific)
- SJTs (the technical family the SST sits in): **incremental validity over cognitive + personality is small, ΔR ≈ .03–.08.** [INDEPENDENT — SJT review literature via NCBI/ResearchGate]
- **Fakeability/coaching:** SJT items are "often transparent: candidates can often identify which response the employer wants"; **coaching significantly improves scores, especially on high-transparency items.** Knowledge-based ("what is best?") SJTs MORE fakeable than behavioural-tendency ("what would you do?") SJTs. [INDEPENDENT — SJT literature]
  - Implication for Cappfinity SST: to the extent the SST has a keyed "most effective" answer, it inherits SJT transparency/coachability risk; the strengths "what would you most enjoy/do" framing is claimed to be more faking-resistant but candidates can still infer target strengths from the firm's published values.
- https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11024836/ (evidence-based appraisal of SJTs, revisited)
- https://www.researchgate.net/publication/228362596_Situational_judgment_tests_A_review_of_recent_research
- **[GAP]** No independent peer-reviewed validation of Cappfinity's *specific* strengths instruments located; validity claims are vendor-sourced.

## 5.4 FULL MECHANICS

### Situational Strengths Test (SST) — the flagship
- Item format: a **work scenario (short paragraph) + a set of possible responses.** Two response paradigms appear across sources — likely **firm/version dependent**:
  1. **Rank all options** 1→5 (or 1→4) from most to least likely / most to least effective; "**you cannot assign the same likelihood rating to two responses**" (forced distinct ranks). [PREP-VENDOR — jobtestprep, learnandpass, aptitudetests.org]
  2. **Rate each response on effectiveness** AND (strengths signature) **on enjoyment/energy** — "most and least enjoy." [PREP-VENDOR — intervyo]
- Item counts: **[UNKNOWN as a fixed number]** — SST length varies by employer build. Prep vendors decline to give a fixed count for the SST specifically.
- Keying: prep vendors insist "**there is no right or wrong answer**" (evaluated vs strengths needed for the role) — BUT worked examples reveal a **keyed 'more effective' response** (e.g. graduate example: response viewing challenge as opportunity = strongest; expressing uncertainty = weakest). So functionally **there IS a keyed/preferred answer** (competency/strength-mapped), unlike pure personality. [PREP-VENDOR — practiceaptitudetests "how to pass SST"]
- **Bespoke per employer** — "each SST is bespoke to each employer according to the positions they have to fill." [PREP-VENDOR consensus]

### Aptitude (Capp) tests
- **Numerical reasoning:** ~**12–15 questions**, data from graphs/tables/charts; multiple-choice, free-form ranking, ranked formats; calculator + pen/paper allowed. Some builds "one question based on one diagram," fast reaction. [PREP-VENDOR — graduatesfirst, jobtestprep, learnandpass]
- **Verbal reasoning:** ~**8–15 questions**; matching responses, fill-gaps, ranking statements, true/false; some builds test spelling/grammar + comprehension. [PREP-VENDOR]
- **Critical reasoning:** untimed; short text + conclusions, judge whether conclusion logically follows. [PREP-VENDOR]

### Immersive / blended products
- **Capptivate** — "digital assessment combining behavioural + cognitive." Basis of the Linklaters build. [VENDOR/PREP-VENDOR]
- **Tempo** — mobile-friendly pre-interview assessment (skills + personality). [PREP-VENDOR]
- **Altitude** — high-potential/executive; 8 strength areas: **Agile thinking, Inclusive leadership, Relationship navigation, Courage, Accountability, Strategic vision, Self-insight, Change facilitation.** [PREP-VENDOR — graduatesfirst]
- **Snapshot** — video/async: "recorded video responses, written answers, or both." [PREP-VENDOR — graduatesfirst]

### Timing / device / adaptivity
- Overall immersive runtime **~60–90 min** (HSBC ~1 hr; Linklaters 70–90 min, 6 modules). [PREP-VENDOR + VENDOR]
- **No hard overall time limit** on many components, BUT "**the time taken to answer each question feeds into your score**" (speed-accuracy composite; ranked vs other applicants). Complete in one sitting; cannot pause. [PREP-VENDOR consensus]
- Mobile/laptop supported ("recommended browser and hardware, Laptop or Mobile"). [PREP-VENDOR]
- **Adaptivity: DISPUTED.** practiceaptitudetests claims difficulty adapts to performance ("harder with correct, easier with incorrect"); jobtestprep page "does NOT explicitly describe an adaptive format." → **[UNKNOWN / likely not fully adaptive for SST; possible for some aptitude builds]** — treat adaptivity claims with caution (prep-vendor marketing).

### Example concrete build — HSBC Online Immersive Assessment (Cappfinity-built)
- One prep source: "**38 questions overall — 16 cognitive (numerical + verbal reasoning), 22 situational-judgement + personality-profiling questions based on HSBC Core Values.**" [PREP-VENDOR — practiceaptitudetests / fintest]
- HSBC values assessed: **Dependable; Open to different ideas & cultures; Connected to customers, communities, regulators & each other.** Stage 2 (after application form); pass → Job Simulation stage. [PREP-VENDOR]

### Example — EY (EY "One" assessment, Cappfinity-built)
- Measures strengths, cognitive ability, SJT, values/working-style fit. EY strengths list cited: **Accountable, Agile, Adaptable, Analytical, Curious, In the Know, Number Savvy, Resilient, Strong Communicator, Team Player.** [PREP-VENDOR — graduatesfirst EY page]

## 5.6 SCORING / NORMS / CUT-OFFS

- **Blended/composite score:** immersive assessments produce a profile combining **strengths fit + aptitude performance + consistency checks + role-specific benchmarking.** [PREP-VENDOR — intervyo]
- **Speed-accuracy composite:** both accuracy and per-question time feed the score; applicants **ranked relative to other applicants** ("norm/peer-referenced" implied). [PREP-VENDOR]
- **Keying:** SST is **criterion/strength-keyed** (a preferred/"more effective" answer exists mapped to target strengths) — NOT pure ipsative personality. [PREP-VENDOR "how to pass" worked examples]
- **Norm vs criterion:** appears to blend **peer-norm ranking** (aptitude, timing) with **criterion keying** (SST against firm framework). [INFERRED]
- **Pass mark vs rank / multi-hurdle:** functions as a **sift with a threshold**; candidates can fail (see candidate testimony). Whether a hard cut-off or top-N rank is employer-configurable. [INFERRED — multi-hurdle strongly implied: pass online → job simulation → interview/AC]
- **What recruiter sees:** algorithmic score/profile; candidate sometimes gets a **personalised feedback report** (Linklaters). [VENDOR]
- **Retake policy: [UNKNOWN]** — no source detailed a formal retake rule. Candidate testimony references people "failing the Capp assessment multiple times," implying reapplication across cycles rather than instant retake. [CANDIDATE, indirect]

## 5.7 HOW TO PREPARE LEGITIMATELY

- **SST:** understand the **effectiveness logic** + the **firm's published values/strengths framework**; identify response themes; when ranking, lead with responses showing initiative/opportunity-framing/integrity, bottom-rank uncertainty/avoidance. Watch the **forced-distinct-rank** constraint (can't tie two options). [PREP-VENDOR — practiceaptitudetests, learnandpass]
- **Strengths items:** answer with **authenticity but be decisive** — strengths assess energy/preference; over-second-guessing hurts. Research the strengths the role rewards so genuine strengths are surfaced, not fabricated. [PREP-VENDOR / INFERRED]
- **Aptitude:** practise numerical (graph/table interpretation, timed), verbal, critical reasoning; note **timing is scored** even when "untimed."
- **Provider-specific trap:** the **most/least effective (or most/least enjoy) keying** — reading only one dimension when the item wants both effectiveness AND enjoyment/energy ratings. [PREP-VENDOR — intervyo]
- **Immersive/RJP:** treat like a work simulation — triage, prioritise, communicate clearly under light time pressure.
- Distraction-free environment; recommended device/browser; one sitting.

## 5.9 FALSE-POSITIVE RISKS

- **Coachability/transparency:** SJT literature shows keyed SJT answers are coachable; well-prepped candidates who reverse-engineer firm values can score above their true fit. [INDEPENDENT]
- **Unproctored:** identity/assistance not verified at sift → possible impersonation or collaborative answering. [INFERRED]
- **Strengths inference:** because firm strength frameworks are published (EY, HSBC values), candidates can **fake target strengths** rather than report authentic ones — undermining the "energy/authenticity" premise. [INFERRED from IO-psych + published frameworks]
- **Speed-scoring:** rewards fast test-takers; a genuinely strong-fit but deliberate candidate may under-score. [INFERRED]
- **Small incremental validity** of SJTs generally (ΔR .03–.08) means the composite may add limited signal over aptitude/personality — false positives/negatives from an over-weighted SST. [INDEPENDENT]
