## 5.2 — Why it exists / construct targeted / predictive validity

- **Hiring problem it sells against:** (a) self-report personality questionnaires are fakeable ("impression management"); (b) CVs/credentials carry adverse impact and social bias; (c) since ChatGPT, text-based aptitude tests are cheatable. Arctic Shores' pitch: behaviour can't be faked and AI "can't complete our task." [VENDOR]
  - https://www.arcticshores.com/insights/chatgpt-has-broken-traditional-aptitude-assessments-your-solution-to-future-proofing-recruitment
- **Constructs targeted (IO-psych):** Big-Five-adjacent personality traits + fluid cognitive processing + **Learning Agility** (rule acquisition, feedback use, strategy-switching) + risk/decision style + emotion recognition. Legacy product marketed "**34 cognitive & personality traits**." [VENDOR]/[PREP-VENDOR]
- **Vendor's "world's first task-based assessment for Learning Agility"** — observes in real time how candidates "pick up new rules, use feedback, and switch strategies." [VENDOR] https://www.arcticshores.com/insights/introducing-the-worlds-first-task-based-assessment-for-learning-agility
- **Predictive validity — evidence quality:** Vendor claims "valid, reliable neuroscience," "dozens of studies a year," "**reduce turnover by 40% in 6 months**," but the public /science page gives **NO validity coefficients, NO reliability figures, NO named criterion studies, NO peer-reviewed citations.** [UNKNOWN — validity numbers not public]. https://www.arcticshores.com/science
  - [INFERRED] The underlying constructs (cognitive ability, conscientiousness, learning agility) DO have strong meta-analytic predictive validity in the IO literature (e.g., GMA r≈.5). But that literature validates the *constructs*, NOT Arctic Shores' specific task-to-trait inferences. Game-based measures typically show LOWER and more variable criterion validity than well-built cognitive tests, and construct validity (does the balloon game really measure "risk"?) is the contested question. Treat vendor's turnover/validity claims as unaudited marketing.
  - [INDEPENDENT — proxy] No independent peer-reviewed validation of Arctic Shores specifically was located in this pass. Broader academic literature on game-based assessment (e.g., work by Landers, Auer; and critiques of gamified selection) is the best external proxy. **GAP.**

## 5.4 — FULL MECHANICS (per game/task)

> Legacy "Skyrise City" battery. Different employer "worlds" ship different subsets: **Skyrise City** (full set), **Pinnacle Valley** (Balloon, Face, Arrow, Security Door, Team Selling, Sequence), **Cosmic Cadet**, **Firefly Freedom**. A **numerical module** is bolted on for finance/analytics roles. [PREP-VENDOR: graduatesfirst, jobtestprep, psychometric-success]
> KEY PSYCHOMETRIC POINT — **the games have NO intuitive "score."** Sample report states explicitly: *"Your game score was NOT considered for your psychometric profile."* Raw performance (points, time) is largely a red herring; inferences come from behavioural telemetry patterns. [PRIMARY — sample report PDF, see 5.6]

Per-game detail (mechanic → traits vendor/prep claim → likely raw telemetry [INFERRED]). Sources: jobtestprep.co.uk/arctic-shores-assessment; graduatesfirst.com/arctic-shores; tradinginterview (Maven). All [PREP-VENDOR] unless noted.

1. **Balloon (Balloon Analogue Risk Task / BART)** — inflate balloon for money over ~45 rounds; bank anytime or risk burst losing that round. Traits: risk appetite, reward motivation, drive, pattern recognition. Telemetry [INFERRED]: avg pumps, pumps after a burst (risk recovery), variance, learning of burst probability.
2. **Arrows / Direction (Flanker task)** — 80 rounds; is the CENTRE arrow left/right while flanking arrows distract; speeds up; too-early/too-late = error. Traits: attention, inhibitory control, focus under pressure. Telemetry [INFERRED]: reaction time, congruent-vs-incongruent RT cost, error rate under speed.
3. **Tickets** — ~80 rounds; sort tickets by changing rules from two boxes; rules visible then disappear; strict timing. Traits: executive function, processing speed, attention. Telemetry [INFERRED]: RT, rule-switch cost, error recovery.
4. **Tile Sequence (Corsi-style memory span)** — memorise & repeat lengthening tile patterns; distractor icons added; ends after 2 consecutive errors (adaptive). Traits: short-term/working memory, controlled vs automatic behaviour. Telemetry [INFERRED]: max span, error position.
5. **Energy / Power Generator** — click buttons that unpredictably raise/drain power; infer the hidden rule. Traits: learning ability, impulsivity, risk tolerance, pattern recognition. Telemetry [INFERRED]: exploration vs exploitation, learning rate.
6. **Team Selling / Team Sales** — 12 rounds set price high/low vs an opposing team whose choices affect payoff. Traits: decision-making, risk-taking, fairness, cooperation/teamwork. Telemetry [INFERRED]: cooperation ratio, response to defection.
7. **Expressions / Face** — 50 images, pick emotion from 7 options; some deliberately ambiguous; correct answer may be absent. Traits: emotional intelligence / emotion recognition. **[FALSE-POSITIVE FLAG — see 5.9: contested for autistic candidates.]**
8. **Security Door / Lock** — ~20 rounds stop a rotating ring on highlighted numbers to build a passcode; speed varies; error resets; can exit. Traits: reaction time, focus, adaptability.
9. **Predict / Night & Day (probabilistic/statistical learning)** — ~88 rounds classify icon pairs Day/Night; 3-sec window; only 8 unique pairs repeat; later rounds test retention. Traits: decision confidence, statistical learning, pattern recognition.
10. **Order** — infer the 4th frame from 3 preceding (colour/direction/size/position); ~15 min, ~1 min/item. Traits: logical/inductive reasoning, attention to detail. (Optional.)
11. **Pattern** — ~20 rounds/17 min, next-in-sequence abstract reasoning; rules change per item. Traits: fluid/logical/spatial reasoning, processing speed. (Optional — not in all batteries.)
12. **Analysis (numerical module)** — TRADITIONAL multiple-choice: data tables/charts, ~18 questions/~18 min. Traits: numerical reasoning, information processing. **This is the finance/analytics bolt-on and is NOT game-based.** (Optional.)

- **Item counts/time limits:** per-game as above; total battery varies by employer selection.
- **Device support:** desktop (Win/Mac) AND mobile (iOS/Android) — mobile-first is a genuine differentiator vs legacy psychometrics. Employer specifies a recommended device; wrong hardware can affect response-time capture. [PREP-VENDOR]
- **Pause/disconnect behaviour:** **[UNKNOWN]** — not documented publicly; credentials are time-limited; [INFERRED] adaptive/telemetry design means mid-session disconnects are risky. **GAP.**
- **Current task-based product ("Balance" game etc.):** 2024 candidate threads reference a newer **"Balance" / balancing task** and other unfamiliar tasks — consistent with the post-2023 task-based redesign rather than Skyrise City. Detail sparse. [CANDIDATE, n≈several TSR threads] (TSR titles only; page bodies blocked). **GAP — mechanics of current tasks under-documented.**

## 5.7 — How to prepare LEGITIMATELY

- You **cannot revise content** — it's behavioural. Vendor and prep vendors agree: play authentically; faking behavioural telemetry consistently is hard and can look inconsistent. [VENDOR/PREP-VENDOR]
- Legit prep: (1) understand the target role's competencies so you know which natural behaviours matter; (2) familiarise with the FORMAT so novelty/anxiety doesn't distort RT (practice BART, Flanker, Corsi, emotion-recognition analogues); (3) optimise conditions — rested, alert, quiet, recommended device, stable connection, "cognitive peak" timing; (4) read each game's instructions carefully (rules change between games). [PREP-VENDOR: jobtestprep, graduatesfirst, tradinginterview]
- **[PREP-VENDOR bias note]** JobTestPrep/GraduatesFirst sell paid simulations (GraduatesFirst ~£26.95, was £49.95; JobTestPrep subscription). Their "practice raises scores" framing is commercial; corroborate against vendor's "you can't really prepare" stance.

## 5.9 — FALSE-POSITIVE risks for honest candidates

- **Emotion-recognition (Face) task** is the sharpest concern: identifying emotions from faces is precisely an area where **autistic candidates** may score differently for reasons unrelated to job performance → potential **direct-discrimination / adverse-impact risk**. Raised publicly (National Autistic Society community; neurodiversity commentary). [CANDIDATE/COMMUNITY, n≥1 thread]
  - https://community.autism.org.uk/f/adults-on-the-autistic-spectrum/41075/is-this-legal
- **Speeded Flanker/Tickets/Security-Door tasks** penalise slower motor/processing speed → risk for **ADHD, dyspraxia/DCD, dyslexia, motor or visual impairment, older candidates**, or anyone on a **poor device/slow connection** (response-time capture is device-sensitive). [INFERRED from task design + prep-vendor device warnings]
- **Environment/state effects:** fatigue, anxiety, interruptions distort RT and risk-taking telemetry; the instrument reads "natural default under pressure," so situational stress can misrepresent. Vendor itself concedes learned coping strategies can shift results. [VENDOR caveat + INFERRED]
- **Candidate testimony of mismatch:** several report the personality/feedback PDF "didn't match them," felt labelled (e.g. "impulsive"), got **different feedback from different employers on the same games**, and called feedback "garbage"/"a crock" after rejection with no actionable reason. [CANDIDATE, n≈4–6 across TSR threads + prep-forum summaries — titles/snippets only; TSR & Reddit bodies were HTTP-403 to the fetcher].
  - TSR threads (titles confirm topic; bodies not fetchable): t=6241922 (Skyrise City personality test), t=5765200 ("what score did you get"), t=7642395, t=7628602, t=7558443/7645894 (Balance game), t=6913924 (PwC), t=6147532 (PwC Power Generator).
