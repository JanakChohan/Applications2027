**0.1 — The SHL-vs-pymetrics conflict.** *(see §3 for full evidence)*

- Barclays' **own careers site does NOT name any vendor.** It says only: candidates complete *"two short interactive assessments, which will tell us how you might perform in the workplace, and your typical or preferred ways of working."* [EMPLOYER] — https://search.jobs.barclays/internship-graduate-application (accessed 2026-08-01)
- **[INFERRED]** The word "interactive" and the pairing "how you might perform" + "typical or preferred ways of working" = one **ability/cognitive** measure + one **behavioural/personality** measure. That phrasing is vendor-neutral and is compatible with EITHER an SHL Verify Interactive + SHL behavioural pair OR a game-based pair.
- **Weight of dated third-party evidence for the 2025–26 cycle points to SHL, not pymetrics** — see §3.
- **pymetrics** attribution appears to be **weakly sourced and possibly a generic-list artefact** — see §3.3. pymetrics was acquired by **Harver** (2022), which is itself relevant to whether any legacy contract survives.

**0.2 — Official stage list is SHORTER than prep vendors claim.**

It does **not** list a standalone one-way video interview as a distinct stage for internships/graduates. Prep vendors near-universally insert a **HireVue video interview** as stage 3 of 4. See §2.3 — this is a real, resolvable discrepancy and matters for candidate planning.

**0.3 — Thresholds.** No Barclays-published pass mark, cut score, or percentile exists. Everything numeric in circulation is prep-vendor estimate. See §5 — **do not treat any number there as fact.**

## 2. THE ASSESSMENTS — WHAT THEY ACTUALLY ARE

**Completed 2026-08-01 in main thread after the research agent failed mid-run (API error).**

- **[EMPLOYER]** Barclays itself: **two short interactive assessments** — one measuring "how you might perform in the workplace" (= ability/cognitive), one measuring "your typical or preferred ways of working" (= behavioural/personality/working-style). Mobile/tablet completable. Technical programmes add a **technical skills assessment**.
- **[PREP-VENDOR — GraduatesFirst, "Barclays Recruitment Process 2026/27", fetched full, accessed 2026-08-01]** https://www.graduatesfirst.com/barclays-aptitude-tests :
  - Assessments "typically delivered via **SHL or Cappfinity**"
  - Branded/blended into **"Business Insight Stage 1" and "Business Insight Stage 2"**
  - Components listed: **Numerical Reasoning, Verbal Reasoning, Logical Reasoning, Values and Mindset Assessment, Personality Assessment**, plus a **coding/CS assessment for technical roles** (e.g. Explorer Developer)
  - **Total time: 60–90 minutes** across the stages
  - A **video interview stage exists** (vendor not named on the page — HireVue NOT stated)
  - **Overall timeline 4–8 weeks**; results typically **1–2 weeks** after the assessment centre
- [INFERRED] The two official "interactive assessments" most plausibly map to (a) a **combined/interactive reasoning measure** and (b) a **values-and-mindset/personality measure** — matching both the Barclays wording and the GraduatesFirst component list. The prep-vendor list is likely enumerating *constructs across the whole funnel*, not five separate standalone tests at stage 2.
