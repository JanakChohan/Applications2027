## 0. HEADLINE FINDINGS (the load-bearing claims)

1. **MS UK/EMEA online assessment = Aon / cut-e ("scales" family), NOT SHL** — despite some prep pages hedging "SHL or cut-e". The candidate-reported format (6-min verbal/numerical, T/F/Cannot-Say across ~6 panels, `ix` inductive odd-one-out, `switchChallenge` game, email-style SJT) is **unambiguously the cut-e/Aon design**. One prep vendor dates the MS↔cut-e partnership to **2019**. [PREP-VENDOR + CANDIDATE-via-snippet, consistent] — cross-ref chapter 02 (Aon/cut-e) for the mechanics of each named test.
2. **The OA is programme- and division-gated.** Repeated claim: MS runs online aptitude testing chiefly for **IBD summer** and **Spring Insight**; **many non-IBD divisions are "interviews only"**; **off-cycle typically SKIPS the OA** but **HireVue is mandatory for essentially everyone**; **quant/derivatives tracks add a separate timed OA**. [PREP-VENDOR + CANDIDATE-via-snippet] — this directly confirms the project's "off-cycle compresses the OA" thesis (§6.8).
3. **HireVue is the one near-universal MS stage** (one-way async video, ~3 questions for IBD = 1 behavioural + 2 light-technical). Vendor = **HireVue** (external). [CANDIDATE-via-snippet + PREP-VENDOR]
4. **Volatility flag on HireVue "AI/facial analysis":** prep pages still claim MS HireVue scores "facial expressions / body language." **HireVue publicly DISCONTINUED facial analysis (built out ~March 2020, announced Jan 2021).** Any current claim that MS HireVue scores your face is **outdated**. [INDEPENDENT — SHRM/Fortune, see sources]
5. **Assessment centre / "superday":** post-COVID frequently **virtual (Zoom)**; historically London AC included a **written test mirroring the OA + group case + individual case + 2 interviews (1 technical, 1 fit)**. No evidence of a distinct MS-built "immersive/virtual experience" product (that's HSBC/Cappfinity and JPM/Forage, not MS). [CANDIDATE-via-snippet]

## 3. PER-STAGE MECHANICS (5.4)

### 3a. Online aptitude (cut-e "scales") — EMEA
[PREP-VENDOR: jobtestprep morgan-stanley-summer-internship / spring-week-preparation; graduatesfirst; CANDIDATE-via-snippet: TSR t=5728928, t=7530073]
- **Numerical reasoning:** ~6 min; **~18 statements**; interpret data across **~6 panels** (tables/charts); answer **True / False / Cannot Say**. (Some prep pages quote a longer "20 min / 18–20 Q" variant — likely a different/legacy config; **conflict, flag**.)
- **Verbal reasoning:** ~6 min; **15–18 statements**; T/F/Cannot-Say across 6 panels of text.
- **Logical / inductive (`ix`):** ~5 min, ~20 items; **odd-one-out** / find element breaking the pattern.
- **`switchChallenge` (smartPredict game):** ~6 min, unlimited tasks; deductive logic/attention under escalating difficulty; "unique to Morgan Stanley" per one vendor (i.e. MS opted into this cut-e game module). [PREP-VENDOR]
- **SJT:** ~30 min (largely untimed); **email/inbox format** — reply to emails asking for advice / actions; scored on competency + fit to the **Business Unit** applied to; candidates receive a results report. [CANDIDATE-via-snippet]
- **Typically 3 of 4 aptitude modules assigned** per candidate; 48-hour completion window. [PREP-VENDOR]
- **Measures:** numeric data interpretation under time pressure; verbal critical reasoning; abstract pattern ID; workplace judgement/values alignment.
- **Vendor conflict to resolve:** practiceaptitudetests lists "20 tests / 324 questions, error-checking test, GCSE-level" (looks like a generic cut-e/SHL PrepPack description, NOT MS-confirmed). Treat aggregate counts as [PREP-VENDOR marketing], not MS fact.

### 3b. HireVue video
[CANDIDATE-via-snippet (WSO IBD) + PREP-VENDOR (theinterviewguys, Leland, igotanoffer)]
- One-way / **asynchronous**, no live interviewer; **~30 min total**.
- **IBD reported as 3 questions = 1 behavioural + 2 light-technical**; other prep pages say **3–5 questions**.
- **~30–60 sec prep, 1–2 min answer**; **one retry per question** (some pages say "no retakes" — **conflict**, likely varies by config/year).
- Question types: motivation/"why MS"/"why division", behavioural ("tell me about a time"), light technical (basic accounting/finance, valuation basics) — NOT full Superday-level technicals.
- **Scoring:** prep pages claim AI/NLP on "verbal content, tone, facial expressions, body language → employability score." **CORRECTION [INDEPENDENT]:** HireVue removed facial/visual analysis (~2020, announced Jan 2021; ACLU/FTC + Illinois AI Video Interview Act pressure; nonverbal ≈0.25% predictive contribution). Current HireVue = **language/verbal content only**; whether MS even uses HireVue's algorithmic scoring vs human review of recordings is **[UNKNOWN]** — many banks use HireVue purely as a recording tool reviewed by humans.

### 3c. First-round interview
Phone / Zoom / sometimes in person; behavioural + some technical; 1–2 rounds. [PREP-VENDOR]

### 3d. Assessment Centre / Superday
[CANDIDATE-via-snippet: WSO "Morgan Stanley London Assessment Centre"; Leland/igotanoffer]
- Post-COVID **often virtual (Zoom)**; "Superday" (US) ≈ "Assessment Centre" (UK).
- Historically reported London AC (IBD/GCM): **2 × 30-min interviews (1 technical, 1 fit)** + **individual case** (30-min prep, ~20-min present to 1 interviewer) + **group case** (~5–10 min prep, group of ~6–7, ~4 observers incl. HR + bankers) + **written test similar to the online one**.
- Group exercise archetype: **budget-allocation** task (candidates argue for funding of projects; one made "head of department"). [PREP-VENDOR]
- Superday (interviews-only variant): **3–5 back-to-back** 1:1s across analyst→MD levels. [PREP-VENDOR]

## 6. HOW TO PREPARE (MS-specific) (5.7)
- Prep **cut-e/Aon** specifically (not generic SHL): T/F/Cannot-Say discipline, 6-min pace (~14–20s/item), `ix` odd-one-out, `switchChallenge` logic game, email-inbox SJT keyed to MS values. Use ch.02 drills.
- HireVue: rehearse resume stories; prepare "why MS / why division"; know **recent MS transactions** (MS "Transactions" page + FT/Bloomberg/Reuters); basic technicals for IBD (accounting linkages, valuation, DCF basics). Practise 1–2 min structured answers; use the one retry deliberately.
- AC: prep group-exercise behaviours (contribute + include others, don't dominate), a 20-min case present, and expect a **written test mirroring the OA**.

# SECONDARY — OTHER FIRM-BUILT / VENDOR ASSESSMENTS (seed for short chapter)

**Consulting (firm-commissioned simulations):**
- **McKinsey Solve** (formerly Problem Solving Game / Imbellus digital assessment). Built by **Imbellus (now part of Roblox)**. Gamified, **process-scored** (how you solve, not just answers). Current modules 2025–26: **Redrock** (ecosystem/data), **Sea Wolf** (ocean, math-heavy), **Sustainable Futures Lab**; **Ecosystem game phased out** in most regions late 2025; ~**70–100 min**; now includes a behavioural/judgement module. [PREP-VENDOR: mconsultingprep, myconsultingcoach, hackingthecaseinterview] — **most unique prep required.**
- **BCG Casey** — **chatbot-style case simulation**; closest to a real case interview; most directly preparable. (BCG also historically used **pymetrics** games + Online Case.) [PREP-VENDOR]
- **Bain** — **Bain SOVA test** (traditional aptitude: numerical/verbal/logical; standard test-prep transfers). (Bain has also used an Online Test / gamified elements by region/year.) [PREP-VENDOR]

**Banks — firm-built / distinctive:**
- **Goldman Sachs** — **HireVue pre-recorded interview** as the signature screen (IBD); historically an aptitude test with **negative marking** (+5 correct / −2 wrong) reported; **HackerRank** for tech; then **Superday**. GS also runs AI-related updates to its process (2024–25 press). [PREP-VENDOR: graduatesfirst; INDEPENDENT: Fortune 2024/2025]
- **JPMorgan** — **pymetrics** 12 mini-games (~25–30 min, "91 traits") = the real **selection** OA; **HireVue** video; **Forage "virtual experience"** is **NOT selection** (self-paced marketing/upskill). ⚠️ Don't conflate the two. **pymetrics acquired by Harver (2022)** — cross-ref ch.04. [PREP-VENDOR]
- **HSBC** — **"Online Immersive Assessment"** by **Cappfinity** (cognitive numerical/verbal/inductive + SJT). This IS the "immersive/day-in-the-life" archetype. [PREP-VENDOR]
- **BlackRock** — **HireVue coding assessment** (6 coding assignments + 1 pre-recorded HR question) → superday loop. [PREP-VENDOR]
