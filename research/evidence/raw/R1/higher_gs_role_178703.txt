THE ORGANISATION
Goldman Sachs Asset Management is one of the world’s leading investment managers. We have leadership positions across all major public and private asset classes and key client segments. 
Our Client Solutions Group works to develop meaningful client relationships and execute on thoughtful investment solutions for institutional and third-party wealth clients across the globe. Our team cover a broad spectrum of products across all asset classes, including fixed income, equities, liquid alternatives, private equity, private credit, infrastructure and real estate. 
We help our diverse set of clients navigate today’s dynamic markets and identify the opportunities that shape their portfolios and long-term investment goals. We conduct our business in increasingly complex markets and our people must continually find new ways to manage risk and provide investment opportunities for our clients to enable them to reach their goals. 
We judge ourselves on our ability to help clients anticipate and respond to changing market conditions and to create opportunities that merit the trust they place in us. 
Responsibilities
- Responsible for coverage of South and Southeast Asia Institutional clients and drive commercial outcomes 
- Work closely with internal stakeholders involving multi-disciplined teams including Portfolio Management, Trading, Operations, Performance Measurement and Risk, Legal, Compliance, and Accounting/Billing and across regions for ongoing client servicing and sales support
 Basic Qualifications 
- Strong academic credentials (Bachelor's degree at the minimum) and 5-7 years of work experience in fund management or finance industry. CFA qualification will be a plus 
- Established strong relationship and proven fundraising track record with Institutional clients in South and Southeast Asia
- Strong leadership quality and the ability to work in a team-based environment 
- Strong interpersonal and communication (written and verbal) skills and ability to interact with global stakeholders, senior management and team members effectively 
- Candidate needs to have intellectual curiosity and the drive for commercial success 
- Highly self-motivated team player with a strong analytical mindset and resilient temperament 
- Knowledge of investment products across all major asset classes (equities, fixed income, money markets and alternatives) will be a plus
