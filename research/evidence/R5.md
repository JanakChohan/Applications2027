## 3. How the client bases differ (Layer B unless cited)

- **Gulf sovereign funds and official institutions:** very few, very large, sophisticated buyers who want partnerships, co-investment and local presence. The sale is a senior, multi-year institutional relationship.
- **Nordic pension institutions and insurers:** large professional in-house teams, fee-sensitive, strong sustainability requirements; local-language coverage [R5-S08].
- **Swiss private banks:** intermediaries; a gatekeeper approves a fund, then advisers must actually use it. Alternatives for wealth is a current product push [R5-S11].
- **Dutch pension funds:** often delegate to fiduciary managers; the system change to the Wtp (30 funds, €542bn converted by 31 March 2026; deadline 1 January 2028 [R5-S35]) is rewriting hedging and return portfolios.
- **German institutions:** insurers and professional pension schemes commonly invest through dedicated institutional funds (Spezialfonds). No source was read on this point; treat as background, unsourced.
- **Italy, France, Spain:** distribution runs largely through banks, insurers and private banks [R5-S43] for Italy; France and Spain unsourced.

## 4. Liquidity Solutions

- **Scale and scope:** "Our Liquidity Solutions team oversees $1.2+ trillion in money market and short-term assets" as of 30 Sep 2026; 40+ years; 25+ investment professionals [R5-S01] (Verified; global AUS, includes non-discretionary assets).
- **Clients:** "financial institution, hedge fund, institutional, corporate, and wealth management clients" [R5-S01].
- **Products on the UK page:** GS US$ Liquid Reserves and GS Euro Liquid Reserves (LVNAV); GS US$ Treasury Liquid Reserves (Public Debt CNAV); short-duration accounts and funds; fixed income ETFs; Mosaic portal [R5-S01]. WAM up to 60 days, WAL up to 120 days.
- **Structure:** Global Head, Insurance Asset Management and Liquidity Solutions; Global Head, Liquidity Solutions Client Business [R5-S01]. It is a product-line client business, not a region.
- **EMEA:** London "EMEA Liquidity Solutions Sales (UK Corporates)" VP seat covering UK corporate and insurance clients [R5-S09] (summary).
- **Regulation:** FCA, 8 June 2026: UK MMF Regulation to be repealed by end-2026; guidance of 40% weekly liquid assets for stable-NAV funds, 20% for VNAV; fees/gates link removed [R5-S32]. EU: no major legislative reform; same levels as supervisory benchmarks [R5-S33]. EU-domiciled funds (about 90% of sterling MMF assets) stay on sale under an extended temporary regime [R5-S34].
- **Tokenisation:** BNY/Goldman mirror-token solution, 23 July 2025; GSAM among first managers; US-focused [R5-S40].
- **Fees and yield (Layer B):** the investor receives portfolio yield net of the fund charge; the manager earns that charge on average balances and may waive part when rates are very low. No GS fee or waiver number was read; do not quote one.

## 5. Insurance

- **Scale:** "$651 billion in general account assets on behalf of insurance clients" as of 31 Dec 2025 [R5-S02] (Verified).
- **Offer:** partial to full outsourcing across fixed income, alternatives and equities; advice on asset-liability management, strategic asset allocation, capital-efficient strategies [R5-S02].
- **Survey:** 15th annual, 25 March 2026, 434 CIOs/CFOs; 62% to raise private assets; asset-backed finance net +38%; 55% expect a US recession within three years [R5-S02][R5-S03]. No EMEA breakdown on pages read.
- **Structure:** global vertical; London and Frankfurt "Insurance Client Solutions Group" seats [R5-S21][R5-S22]. A CSG ad lists "institutional, third-party wealth, insurance and consultant relations teams" side by side [R5-S42] (summary).
- **Client constraints:** EU Solvency II amendments apply from 30 January 2027 [R5-S39]. UK: PRA CP7/25 Matching Adjustment Investment Accelerator (5% / £2bn limit, 24 months) [R5-S38]; final policy not read.
