## 4. Future of the work

### Observed (with source)

- **AI in distribution.** 81% of institutional sales and service teams use AI for RFP content; 77% for meeting notes; 32% for prospecting letters; 26% to identify prospects; 26% for sales strategy [R8-S41, fetched]. Analyst view: AI "is unable to replace the credibility that comes from well-crafted and verified human communication" (opinion, not proof).
- **AI in investment and operations.** 55% of managers have AI in at least one investment process; 73% use it for operational efficiency; only 5% give it autonomous authority [R8-S42, fetched].
- **UK fiduciary managers.** 85% use AI, "primarily for efficiency and lower-risk operation, such as drafting reports and responding to emails" [R8-S10].
- **Fees.** Institutional fees down ~3% a year; margins ~30%; revenue +5.1% vs costs +5.4% a year 2010–2025 [R8-S38, fetched]. US fund asset-weighted fee 0.32% in 2025 vs 0.80% in 2006 [R8-S37, not fetched]. UK fiduciary fees −27% over five years, but rising below £50m [R8-S10].
- **Market dependence.** ">80% of gross revenue growth in 2025 was driven by market appreciation" [R8-S38].
- **Passive and active ETFs.** Europe: active equity ~€4.1tn vs passive ~€3.4tn (end Q1 2026); passive ETFs ~57% of Q3 2026 flows; active ETFs €117.1bn, ~3.4% of ETF assets [R8-S39, R8-S40, not fetched].
- **Private markets in wealth.** Blackstone private wealth $330bn; elevated BCRED redemption requests [R8-S34]; BoE notes funds limiting redemptions [R8-S52].
- **Consolidation.** ~$53.8bn sector M&A to late Aug 2026 [R8-S54, not fetched]; Nuveen–Schroders complete [R8-S08]; Mercer–Cardano [R8-S10].
- **UK pension consolidation (fiduciary slice only).** Mandates down 4% in the year to June 2025, first fall since 2008; 26% of fully delegated schemes expect buyout within three years [R8-S10]. Wider pension consolidation is R3's scope.
- **Tokenisation.** BNY–Goldman MMF mirror tokens (July 2025) [R8-S55]; GDF/ISDA collateral sandbox report (July 2026) [R8-S56, contents not read].
- **Headcount.** Reported cuts: BlackRock ~1% incl. sales teams (Jan 2026); BNP Paribas AM ~1,200 after AXA IM; Morgan Stanley ~3% (Mar 2026); Goldman phased reductions from April 2026 incl. asset and wealth management [R8-S46, R8-S47, R8-S48, none fetched].

### Task-level inputs

See `automation_tasks.R8.json`. Pattern in the evidence: adoption is highest for drafting and summarising (RFP text, notes, routine reports), lowest for deciding whom to pursue and what to recommend. Sources name hallucination and accountability as the constraint, which implies checking work shifts to humans. That is an inference from surveys, and the effect on junior learning is unmeasured.

## 6. Conflicts and cautions

- **Schroders net outflow H1 2026:** £8.3bn (Portfolio Adviser, Professional Pensions) vs £4.2bn including joint ventures (Financial Planning Today). Status: Conflicting evidence on the flow figure.
- **Goldman OCIO AUS:** $480bn at 31 Mar 2026 [R8-S23] vs $540bn+ at 30 Jun 2026 [R8-S22]. Different dates, plausibly reflecting the July mandates; not reconciled by Goldman.
- **Goldman alternatives:** $491bn alternatives AUS (earnings table) vs ">$706bn total alternative assets" (boilerplate). Different definitions. Do not quote one as the other.
- **"Goldman Sachs Asset Management to cut up to 5%":** a search summary suggested this; on checking it refers to the firm's historical annual review cull, not an asset-management-specific 2026 cut. Not used.
- **JPMorgan $5.2tn:** segment AUM for Asset *and* Wealth Management, not J.P. Morgan Asset Management alone.
- **Fidelity:** Fidelity International (non-US) is separate from Fidelity Investments (US, $7.1tn).
- **Vendor surveys** (DiligenceVault, Clearwater, Acuity) have commercial interests and small samples.
- **BoE FSR:** only the first ~100,000 characters of the page were read; named defaults came from search summaries.
