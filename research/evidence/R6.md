## Sales versus relationship-management balance in the observed sample

Method: each of the 42 directory entries has one `function_interpretation`, with the basis recorded. Counts come from `data/fragments/people.R6.json`.

| Function (my interpretation) | All 42 entries | The 24 marked Yes or Likely current |
|---|---|---|
| Leadership (heads of teams, regions, groups) | 27 | 17 |
| Sales, distribution or capital formation | 6 | 2 |
| Relationship management (explicit) | 0 | 0 |
| Both or blended | 1 | 0 |
| Product or investment specialist | 4 | 4 |
| Unclear | 4 | 1 |

- Numerator and denominator: 6 explicit sales roles and 0 explicit relationship-management roles out of 42 public profiles. 31 of the 42 cannot be assigned to either side.
- **No ratio is offered.** The sample is too small and too skewed. Trade press announces heads of sales and new hires. It almost never announces a relationship manager. The zero says more about what gets reported than about staffing.
- The intern's claim that sales has fewer seats than relationship management is **Unverified account**. This stream found nothing for or against it.
