# R1: Official programme, role definitions and recruitment process

Research date and cutoff: 14 November 2026. Source IDs refer to `data/sources/R1.json`; claim IDs to `data/claims/R1.json`. Raw captures of the public pages read are in `research/evidence/raw/R1/`.

## How this stream was researched

- Goldman's careers site (`higher.gs.com`) renders listings in the browser, so each page was loaded in a normal headless browser and the site's own public search was used to list roles. No login was used and nothing behind a login was read.
- Closed listings return "role not found" on the official site. Where a job-board mirror was still live it was read and is labelled as a mirror. Where the mirror had also expired, only the title or snippet shown in a search result is recorded, with `fetched: false` and a status no higher than "Supported inference".
- **Limit reached:** the session's web-search allowance ran out part-way through. Items marked "not yet investigated" below were not searched for that reason; items marked "searched but not established" were.
- **Hosts that could not be read:** `unitedkingdom.careers.cfainstitute.org` is outside the session's allowed network domains. Glassdoor, The Muse, jobs247 and WhatJobs refused the request (HTTP 403). Several Jobijoba, Y-Axis, ASU+GSV and refresh.cv mirrors had expired (HTTP 404/410).

## 2. Terminology: what the names mean in Goldman's own words

### Client Solutions Group (CSG)

- **Official one-paragraph definition:** "Our Client Solutions Group works to develop meaningful client relationships and execute on thoughtful investment solutions for institutional and third-party wealth clients across the globe. Our team cover a broad spectrum of products across all asset classes, including fixed income, equities, liquid alternatives, private equity, private credit, infrastructure and real estate." (Singapore ad, live Nov 2026, R1-S10.) Verified.
- **CSG is the sales organisation.** Campus titles read "Client Solutions Group / Sales" (Frankfurt) and "Client Solutions Group (Sales)" (Tokyo). A CSG strategy ad says its team leads "key strategic initiatives within the sales organization" (R1-S14). Another calls the unit the "Global Client Solutions Group" (R1-S13). Verified.
- **But CSG is not new-business-only.** The London-based BeNeLux ad says: "The BeNeLux Client Solutions Group is responsible for new business development and managing existing institutional client relationships." (R1-S01.) Verified.
- **Labels are interchangeable for at least one team.** The mirror of that same ad, dated 12 Oct 2026, reads "The BeNeLux Institutional Sales Team is responsible for new business development" and its URL keeps the earlier title "Institutional Sales Support (Dutch Speaking)" (R1-S19). The live official page now says "Client Solutions Group". Verified.
- **CSG sub-tracks named in titles:** Institutional Sales (Frankfurt), Third Party Wealth (Paris, Dubai), Alternatives Capital Formation alongside CSG (Doha), Southeast Asia Institutional Sales (Singapore), Regional Consultant (Chicago, US wealth channel). Verified from titles.
- **CSG runs on tracked data.** CSG management roles mention the "annual sales budgeting process", "sales and fundraising projections", "weekly business summaries, KPI progress updates", "governance around Salesforce data", "client focus lists" and "sales alpha tracking" (R1-S13, R1-S14). This is direct evidence of the kind of pipeline and client-data work that exists around the sales teams.

### Client Coverage Group (CCG)

- **The name is real.** "The Client Coverage Group (CCG) within Asset Management is dedicated to retaining, expanding and supporting existing client relationships by ensuring exceptional client service, operational support, and risk management. This involves partnering closely with the sales team, keeping them abreast of recent interactions with clients, and collaborating with business and operations teams to address client queries." (Hong Kong ad for the Korea market, live Nov 2026, R1-S11.) Verified.
- **Task list (official):** main point of contact across "Portfolio Management, Trading, Operations, Legal, and Compliance"; "management of client inquiries, ad hoc issue resolution"; "customized and standardized report delivery"; "life-cycle events of client accounts, including implementation of new business, account restructures/terminations"; "information questionnaires, audit requests". The ad's own name for the job is "Client Experience Manager". It lists "Strategic Client Services" and "Client Implementation" as separate teams.
- **London use of the label:** "CCG Client Relationship Manager - Vice President - London" and "Client Coverage Group Analyst - London" appear as ad titles in search results, with the snippet "responsible for the management and retention of our existing client relationships" (R1-S32). Mirrors expired; Supported inference only.

### Test of the former intern's claim: "CSG = sales, CCG = relationship management"

| Part of the claim | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|
| CSG is an umbrella equivalent to "sales" | Goldman's own titles and ads say so | Verified (R1-C09) |
| "CCG" stands for Client Coverage Group | Yes | Verified (R1-C13) |
| CCG is the existing-client / relationship side | Official definition matches, and adds "expanding" | Verified for the global definition; London wording seen in snippets only |
| CSG means only winning new clients | Contradicted by the BeNeLux CSG ad, which gives CSG existing-relationship duties | Conflicting with the simple split (R1-C10) |
| Within each London team, "CSG" labels the sales side and "CCG" the relationship side | No public source read shows this | Not publicly established (R1-C15) |
| CCG sits inside the CSG umbrella | No public source read shows this; CCG ad says "within Asset Management" | Not publicly established |

Working conclusion (Layer B): "CSG" is used at two levels, as the name of the whole sales organisation and loosely as a synonym for sales; "CCG" is a distinct, named group focused on existing clients that partners with sales. How the two are drawn in each London team should be treated as team-dependent until someone inside confirms it.

### Third Party Wealth (TPW)

- "The UK Third Party Wealth (TPW) Sales Team is responsible for new business development" with "private banks, wealth managers, financial advisors and multi-managers", serving "the UK client base" (closed London ad on a mirror, R1-S17). Verified.
- A London-based sales-support analyst was hired for the "TPW Switzerland team", serving "the Swiss client base" (R1-S18). Verified. So at least some Swiss third-party-wealth support sits in London.
- Snippet-only: the EMEA TPW client channel "includes discretionary and advisory wealth managers, private banks, and fund platforms" (R1-S37); an alternatives-for-wealth role "resides in the Third Party Wealth ('TPW') Client Business" (R1-S38). Supported inference.

### Consultant Relations

- "GSAM Consultant Relations is responsible for working with the investment consultants that advise institutional investors." The team should "work closely with the regional institutional sales teams" and creates "a pipeline of business for the sales teams". It also project-manages "operational due diligence requests across all GSAM products". (Undated older London ad on a mirror, R1-S21.) Verified as historical wording.
- Snippet-only: the team covers "over 100 investment consultant firms worldwide"; a "Global Consultant Relations" VP role in London helps "drive both public and private markets fundraising efforts" (R1-S34). Supported inference.

### OCIO, insurance, marketing and other adjacent functions

- **OCIO delivery sits in investing, not sales.** "The OCIO Insurance team is a global team that provides investment solutions and OCIO services for insurance companies", within Multi-Asset Solutions, working with "sales and client relationship professionals" and "across global teams in The Hague, London, and New York" (R1-S15). Verified. The ad's tasks include responses to "RFIs, RFPs and site-visits", so RFP work is shared with investment teams.
- **Client Relationship Manager (OCIO), VP, London**, reported as posted April 2026 and asking for 7-10+ years' experience (R1-S33). Snippet only. It suggests OCIO has its own relationship managers, at senior level.
- **Insurance Client Solutions Group** and **CSG Retirement Solutions** exist as ad titles (R1-S35, R1-S36). Snippet only; the second refers to "Institutional, Third Party Wealth, Insurance, and Consultant Relations internal stakeholders", which reads like a list of CSG channels. Supported inference.
- **Institutional marketing (EMEA)** is a separate function supporting "pension funds, endowments, foundations, sovereign wealth funds, and insurers" (R1-S20). Verified.
- **Product specialists** are named as partners of the sales-support role ("Partner with product specialists on a wide range of Goldman Sachs Asset Management products", R1-S01). "Client Portfolio Manager" roles exist in the US and India (titles in the site search). No London client-portfolio-manager or RFP-team ad was live.
- **US wealth channel, for contrast only:** a CSG "Regional Consultant (internal wholesaler)" sells mutual funds, ETFs, private alternatives and separately managed accounts to financial advisers by phone and video, partnered with "Regional Directors (external wholesalers)", and is paid base plus quarterly commission (R1-S12). This is the US intermediary model and should not be read across to London.

## 4. Recruitment process

**Official statements (R1-S23, read 14 Nov 2026)**

1. Application, then acknowledgement, a mid-season status update and an end-of-season update: "We evaluate applications throughout the recruiting season, which means you may hear back regarding different roles at different times."
2. "First Interview: If your application is selected, you will be asked to participate in a video interview, which will take approximately 30 minutes to complete." The next step calls it "your HireVue interview".
3. "Superday: If selected following your HireVue interview, you'll be invited to a series of final round interviews. Typically, we conduct between two and five interviews for campus hires, depending on the division. You can expect to meet a cross-section of people whom you could possibly work with."

**What recruiters say they look for (R1-S24, PDF dated 12 Jun 2026):** "Interview questions are situational or behavioral"; "Attitude and aptitude: these are two aspects that Goldman looks at. We are not as worried about your technical and functional expertise because we know we have the resources to help you to build that"; "They are really trying to follow your thought process"; "Give us examples of teamwork, give us examples of problem solving and critical thinking". This is a general careers video with a disclaimer. It is not a scoring rubric.

**Audit of the former intern's recruitment claims**

| Claim | Evidence found | Status |

No keyword tactics should be built on the "drafting" claim. The defensible preparation point is narrower: the final round is with people from a specific team, so being able to explain why a given client segment interests you is useful whichever team it turns out to be.

**AI.** The only official statement found is the CEO's advice to 2026 interns to try "experimenting with our AI solutions to enhance your teams' workflows" (R1-S28). It concerns work, not interviews. Goldman's rules on candidates using AI in interviews were not found on the pages read.

## 5. Titles

- **Executive Director = Vice President outside the US.** Listings titled "Executive Director" in London and Singapore carry the corporate title "Vice President" (R1-S10, R1-S29), and the firm announces a single "Vice President and Executive Director Class" (R1-S26, 20 Nov 2025). They are one level, not consecutive rungs. Verified.
- **Ladder seen in official material:** Summer Analyst, Analyst (with "senior analysts" mentioned), Associate, Vice President / Executive Director, Managing Director, Partner (R1-S26, R1-S27, R1-S29). One live listing uses the corporate title "Extended Managing Director".
- **Partner is a selected class.** "a group of 109 individuals who have been invited to become partners of Goldman Sachs as of January 1, 2027" (R1-S27, 5 Nov 2026). The release lists name, division and office only.
- **Lead for the people stream.** The Partner Class of 2026 lists five Asset & Wealth Management people based in London (Adam Dawson, Amy Jupe, Banu Kisakurek, Gabriel Mollerberg, Patrick Ordynans). The release does not say which AWM business each works in, so none can be placed in the Client Solutions Group from this source.
- Several ads are titled "Analyst/Associate" or "Associate/Executive Director", so a role can be filled at more than one level.

## 6. Compensation

- **London analyst base of £52k (sales) and £48k (relationship): not publicly established.** No Goldman source publishes EMEA pay; the careers site's salary fields are empty for the London, Frankfurt, Paris and Dubai listings read. Levels.fyi returned no readable data and Glassdoor refused the request; both are self-reported and neither splits Asset Management sales from relationship management. Status: Unverified account (R1-C42).
- **US reference points only (official, pay-transparency disclosures):** New York CSG Management & Strategy analyst, base $85,000-$110,000 (R1-S13); New York CSG Business Strategy & Finance VP, base $125,000-$250,000 (R1-S30). These are US, non-client-facing roles.
- **Pay structure varies by channel.** A US CSG regional consultant is "a commission based role in addition to the base salary" (R1-S12). Nothing found shows how London institutional or TPW roles are paid.
- No conclusion about prestige or role quality should be drawn from the reported £4k gap.

## 7. What a summer analyst does, per official wording

- Official and programme-wide: orientation, training, "real responsibilities alongside fellow interns and our people" (R1-S22). Nothing CSG-specific is published (R1-C25).
- Nearest official proxy, to be labelled as inference: the London BeNeLux CSG Analyst/Associate ad (R1-S01) and the UK TPW Sales Support Analyst ad (R1-S17). Their junior tasks are: prepare and follow up client and prospect meetings; answer day-to-day client queries on products and services; work with product specialists and with marketing, compliance and operations; support the Country Head with "presentations focusing on market intelligence, business development, financial analysis and planning". The BeNeLux ad calls the job a "sales support position".
- For the relationship side, the proxy is the CCG task list in section 2 (queries, reporting, account life-cycle coordination, questionnaires).
- These are full-time roles at a higher level than an intern. A summer analyst would plausibly help with parts of these tasks under supervision; that is a Layer B inference.
