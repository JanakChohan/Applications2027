## 1. SCORING — family-wide (core mission)
**Strongest evidence — official Aon-authored candidate guide** (Singapore Management University "Candidate Guide on Aon Assessments for SMU Admissions Test", PDF, shows real mapTQ screens incl. smartplayer.maptq.com; content is Aon's own test instructions):
- **scales numerical** (mobile consumer compact, 18 items/6 min): *"each correct response is worth 1 point, and each incorrect response is worth **-0.5 points**. No points will be awarded for empty responses."* [VENDOR-VIA-CLIENT: https://masters.smu.edu.sg/sites/masters.smu.edu.sg/files/2023-08/SMUAT%20Guide.pdf p.16, acc. 2026-08-01]
- **scales verbal** (mobile consumer compact, 18/6 min): identical wording — **+1 / −0.5 / 0 blank**. [same source, p.26]
- **scales cls** (12 items/12 min): *"Negative marking is applicable in this test for incorrect responses only. No points will be deducted for empty responses."* — magnitude NOT stated for cls. [same source, p.39]
- Official in-test instruction (cls): *"If you are unsure what the right answer is, choose the answer that seems to be right to you. **Avoid guessing**."* [same source, p.40]

**Resolution of the magnitude conflict flagged in 02-aon-cute.md:**
- 02 file recorded: PracticeAptitudeTests "points deducted for wrong"; JTP-family "minus ~1/3 or ~1/4"; Fintest "one mark lost per error". [PREP-VENDOR, acc. 2026-08-01]
- New: JobTestPrep hub (verbatim): "During most AON cut-e tests, points are given for right answers and deducted for incorrect ones – this means that guessing is not as useful as it is for other tests." [PREP-VENDOR: jobtestprep.co.uk/cut-e-tests]
- New: TestSolve: "formula scoring: correct answers minus a fraction (usually one-third or one-quarter) of incorrect answers… scores reported as percentiles relative to a country and role-type benchmark group." [PREP-VENDOR: testsolve.ai/tests/aon/ via search summary]
- New: Fintest publisher hub: "Cut-e scores their tests using negative markings… any questions answered incorrectly will lead to a deduction in points"; scales verbal "incorrect answers have an active negative impact on your score". [PREP-VENDOR: https://www.fintest.io/publishers/cut-e/ acc. 2026-08-01]
- **Best-evidenced figure: −0.5 per wrong (i.e., wrong answer costs half a correct answer) on numerical/verbal** — from the Aon-authored guide. Whether the same −0.5 applies to cls/lst/ix/eql/e3+/special-skills is **[UNKNOWN]**; the guide confirms negative marking exists on cls but not the size. Prep-vendor "⅓/¼" figures are unsourced and now second-ranked. Show both in the report; formula may also be client-/version-configurable [INFERRED].
- **Blanks: officially 0 points** (two verbatim statements above) — upgrades the 02 file's [INFERRED] to [VENDOR-VIA-CLIENT].
- **Percentile/norm conversion:** raw → percentile vs norm group (country + role-type benchmark; demographic/education data collected at login "to evaluate this instrument"). [PREP-VENDOR + mapTQ screens in SMU guide]
- Speed feeds the score only via attempt count (more items answered correctly in the fixed time); no evidence of a separate speed metric on classic scales tests (unlike smartPredict games). [INFERRED; UNKNOWN whether Aon reports speed/accuracy sub-metrics to employers for scales]

**Winning-technique consequence (family-wide):** never blind-guess (−0.5 makes random T/F/CS guessing EV-negative at 1/3 hit rate: EV = ⅓(1) + ⅔(−0.5) = 0); answer only when confidence >⅓–½ depending on eliminations; leave true unknowns blank; maximise attempt rate on items you can solve fast; skip-and-return is allowed (item selection panel exists in numerical/verbal/cls). ## 2. scales numerical
- **Mechanic:** statements evaluated one at a time against 6 "data sheets" (tabs: tables, pie/line charts, text) for a fictional company (e.g., Evergreen Ltd.); answer **True / False / Cannot say**; each statement relates to exactly ONE data sheet which you must find yourself. Only one correct answer; answers can be changed; free navigation via statement-selection panel; test auto-submits at time-out; timer starts when first statement appears; cannot pause. [VENDOR-VIA-CLIENT: SMU guide pp.16–20]
- **Versions/timing:** full **37 tasks / 12 min**; **compact/mobile 18 tasks / 6 min** ("Numerical Reasoning (mobile consumer compact)" is the literal mapTQ task name). Whole-test timing, no per-item clock. [VENDOR-VIA-CLIENT: SMU guide; PREP-VENDOR: jobtestprep, practiceaptitudetests — acc. 2026-08-01]
- **Industry variants CONFIRMED:** vendor catalogue has separate entries for **(consumer)** and **(industry)**; AssessmentDay lists **admin / consumer / finance / industry** versions; JTP-side sources say five areas incl. **instruct**; difficulty identical — "the difference… is not in the level of difficulty or mathematical ability but subject matter only." [VENDOR page titles; PREP-VENDOR: assessmentday.com/aon.htm, jobtestprep.co.uk/cut-e-numerical-practice — acc. 2026-08-01]
- **Adaptive:** No — items drawn per candidate from a large pool (anti-cheat), fixed during the test. [PREP-VENDOR consistent; see 02 file]
- **Scoring:** +1 / −0.5 / 0 (see §1). Norm → percentile. Prep-vendor claim: "submitting an answer with the wrong tab open results in lost points, even if the answer is correct" — [PREP-VENDOR, single-source via search summary (jobtestprep/practiceaptitudetests family), acc. 2026-08-01; plausibility uncertain — the official instructions only require referring to the right sheet; treat as unverified].
- **Calculator IS allowed** (official "things to prepare": calculator, rough paper — scientific not required). [VENDOR-VIA-CLIENT: SMU guide p.3]
- **Technique:** read statement FIRST, then open the one relevant tab (tab icons are themed: revenue, market share, costs…); "Cannot say" is a first-class answer (2 of 4 official practice answers were Cannot Say / require distinguishing data-absence from falsity); expect not to finish ("Not everyone will be able to complete all 18"); skip long-calculation items and return. [VENDOR-VIA-CLIENT practice set + INFERRED]

## 3. scales verbal
- **Mechanic:** same True/False/Cannot-say engine as numerical but data sheets are prose (company structure, strategy, governance principles, products, locations, board bios). Each statement maps to one sheet. **49 tasks / 12 min** full; **18 / 6 min** compact-mobile. ~14–15 s/item effective. [VENDOR-VIA-CLIENT: SMU guide pp.26–38; PREP-VENDOR: graduatesfirst, jobtestprep]
- **Variants:** **admin / consumer / finance / industry** confirmed (AssessmentDay); plus **scales verbal (instruct)** which is a distinct simpler test (§11). [PREP-VENDOR: assessmentday.com/aon.htm; VENDOR: aon-assessment-solutions.com scales-verbal-industry page title — acc. 2026-08-01]
- **Scoring:** +1 / −0.5 / 0 [VENDOR-VIA-CLIENT]; Fintest: "incorrect answers have an active negative impact"; "one mark lost per error" [PREP-VENDOR — magnitude conflict noted §1].
- **Technique:** strict "based only on the brief" epistemics — official rationales hinge on "the data does not specifically point to X" → Cannot Say, and universal claims broken by one counter-instance → False; don't import outside knowledge; hard items combine two sheets. Rushing degrades accuracy under negative marking (Fintest: "Rushing is not advised as it will impact accuracy"). [VENDOR-VIA-CLIENT answer keys + PREP-VENDOR]

## 4. scales cls / clx / ix (inductive)
**cls** (table-classification):
- Official description: six tables of numbers+letters, colour-marked into two categories (marking above vs below; blue vs gold); discover the rule; assign 4 unassigned tables. **12 items / 12 min**; "Most people are unable to complete all 12 items." Skip/reorder allowed via control panel (recommendation: do them in order). Exactly one solution per item. Cannot pause. [VENDOR-VIA-CLIENT: SMU guide pp.39–41]
- Scoring: negative marking confirmed, magnitude [UNKNOWN]; blanks 0. Official advice: "Avoid guessing." [VENDOR-VIA-CLIENT]
- Target roles (vendor): analysis of information/data sets — administrative controllers, processing staff, R&D, commercial apprentices/trainees, junior-middle managers. [VENDOR: aon-assessment-solutions.com scales-cls page snippet, acc. 2026-08-01]
- Technique: common rules are letter/number counts, repeats, positions (e.g., "contains the same letter 3×"); test one candidate rule against ALL 6 example tables before assigning; a 12-min/12-item budget (1 min/item) rewards methodical rule-hunting over speed. [PREP-VENDOR consensus + INFERRED]
**clx** (grid-pair): 6 min; example pair of grids share a rule; pick the matching 2 of 4 candidate grids; **no back-navigation** (differs from cls). [PREP-VENDOR: graduatesfirst — 02 file; fintest confirms 6 min + 5 min practice]
**ix** (odd-one-out): 5–6 min, up to 20 tasks; 9 figures, find the one breaking the common rule. [PREP-VENDOR — 02 file]
- clx/ix scoring specifics: [UNKNOWN — assume family negative marking per §1, PREP-VENDOR-supported but no per-test magnitude]

## 5. scales lst (deductive grid)
- 6 min test (+8 min instructions/practice per Fintest); sudoku-like: every symbol appears once per row/column; identify the symbol for the marked cell; grids grow 4×4 → 5×5 (→ 6×6 per some sources) — **adaptive difficulty**. [PREP-VENDOR: fintest.io/publishers/cut-e/, assessmentday.com/aon.htm, 02 file]
- Technique (Fintest, verbatim-ish): "You don't need to complete the entire grid, only enough squares to settle upon the correct answer" — scan the target cell's row+column first. [PREP-VENDOR]
- Scoring: [UNKNOWN magnitude; family negative marking assumed per §1. How adaptivity interacts with scoring (ability-estimate vs raw points) is not documented — 02 file says adaptive tests give "stable percentile estimates" [PREP-VENDOR]]

## 8. scales mt — MULTITASKING (user priority; note: NOT "tmt")
- Vendor description: candidate juggles **simultaneous sub-tasks**: "responding to a signal under time pressure, focused calculation and focused checking"; **immediate feedback is given regarding their answer**; **graduated difficulty levels**. [VENDOR: cut-e.com scales-mt page snippets via Google, acc. 2026-08-01]
- Variants: **scales mt** (general), **scales mt (avi)** — "assess potential for roles within the aviation sector which require multi-tasking capabilities" (used in pilot/cadet selection; pilot-prep sites describe added audio layers and measurement of "control stability, correction discipline" in aviation batteries), **scales mt (drv)** — driving/rail. [VENDOR page titles; PREP-VENDOR: pilotaptitudetest.com, cadetprep.academy — search-level, acc. 2026-08-01]
- Duration/item counts/scoring formula: **[UNKNOWN — both vendor domains (cut-e.com, aon-assessment-solutions.com) are DNS-dead from this environment and Wayback was blocked; flag for gap audit]**. Given immediate feedback + graduated levels, scoring is plausibly level/accuracy-under-load based rather than simple right-minus-wrong [INFERRED].
- Technique (from mechanic): prioritise the reaction-signal sub-task (missed signals are unrecoverable), let calculation/checking absorb the slack; expect deliberate overload — errors under load are normed, not disqualifying [INFERRED].

## 12. Platform, proctoring, device, retakes, practice
- **Platform:** mapTQ — login at {client}.maptq.com with unique Login ID; player at **smartplayer.maptq.com**; per-test language selector; dashboard shows per-test estimated minutes (e.g., ~9 min for an 18-item 6-min test incl. instructions) and states Pending / In progress / Completed / Error. Do not close/refresh/back — you can be blocked; **a test cannot be paused or resumed once started** ("if you exit the assessment before completion, you will not be able to continue from where you left off"). [VENDOR-VIA-CLIENT: SMU guide pp.4–14]
- **Device:** "Computer/Tablet/Mobile Phone with stable internet" officially acceptable; dedicated "mobile … compact" test versions exist. Calculator + rough paper explicitly allowed for numerical. [VENDOR-VIA-CLIENT: SMU guide p.3]
- **Proctoring (updates 02 file's "webcam not default"):** Aon's own **virtual proctoring** module (consent-based): regular webcam photos → face detection, mobile-phone object detection, face recognition vs a one-shot reference photo; microphone monitored only for presence/duration/volume of audio (content not stored); logs "instances of switching out of the assessment" (**tab-switch telemetry confirmed** — closes a 02-file gap); refusing consent may or may not block progress depending on the employer; withdraw via gdpr.assessment@aon.com. [VENDOR-VIA-CLIENT: SMU guide consent-screen text, pp.6, 11–13]
- **Practice/retakes:** every scales test has built-in untimed instructions + practice examples before the clock starts (repeatable before starting the real test); Aon hosts **public demo/trial versions** of each test on its product pages (the SMU guide links candidates to aon-assessment-solutions.com demo pages for numerical/verbal/cls) — the official free-practice route [VENDOR-VIA-CLIENT]; whether a candidate may retake a completed real test is employer-controlled, no vendor retake right documented [UNKNOWN/INFERRED].
- **Norm data capture:** login flow collects year of birth, country, education, work/management experience, occupational area "to evaluate this instrument" → feeds norm groups. [VENDOR-VIA-CLIENT: SMU guide p.9]
