## 0. Naming correction (IMPORTANT)
- **"scales tmt" is NOT the multitasking test.** Legacy cut-e vendor catalogue: **scales tmt = "Applied Numeracy" / "Calculating Capacity"** — for roles with intense use of numerical information. [VENDOR: cut-e.com/nc/details/scales-tmt-applied-numeracy/ via search snippet; site itself now unreachable — DNS dead, see §12]
