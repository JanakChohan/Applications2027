## 5.2 — Why it exists (rationale)

- **Outsourced early-careers campaign model:** employers running high-volume grad/apprentice/school-leaver intakes outsource the whole funnel (attraction → sift → assessment → AC → offer/onboarding) to Amberjack rather than build it in-house. Assessment is the sift engine inside that. [VENDOR: /solutions/rpo-solutions — "Attraction & Sourcing, Assessment, Candidate Management, Operational Support"]
- **SJT + strengths/behaviours + realistic-job-preview rationale:** blended design pitched as (a) predicting future potential over experience (fairer to grads with thin CVs), (b) **"immersive tests that reflect real-life scenarios"** = realistic job preview / employer-values alignment, (c) one engaging candidate experience that collapses multiple legacy stages into one. [VENDOR: talent-assessments "one overall score mapped directly to our Future Skills Model"; /assessment-and-selection "immersive tests that reflect real-life"]
- **Volume economics:** "assessing a cohort of ten or ten thousand"; blended single-score sift to cut assessor time. [VENDOR: talent-assessments]
- DEI framing: reduce bias, support diversity/neurodiversity, widen talent pool. [VENDOR: FAQs; rpo-solutions]

## 5.4 — FULL MECHANICS

### The blended online assessment (early sift)
- **~25 total items** = **"25 behavioral, situational judgment and cognitive items"**, typically broken as **22 multiple-choice/ranking items + 3 interview questions (written/video)**. [PREP-VENDOR: aptitude-test-prep.com main guide]
- **Employer-specific variants confirm the ~25 shape but vary the split:**
  - **Atkins Immersive Assessment:** 25 Qs = **12 SJT + 10 cognitive (numerical+verbal) + 3 interview**. [PREP-VENDOR: aptitude-test-prep.com/employers/.../atkins-immersive-assessment/]
  - **Morrisons Interactive Evaluation:** **18 SJT + 15 numerical/data + 4 video interview**. [PREP-VENDOR: aptitudeprep.com/morrisons-interactive-evaluation/]
  - **BDO Interactive Assessment:** simulates a workday / email inbox, **~22 items** mixing numerical, reading comprehension, SJT. [PREP-VENDOR: search snippet jobtestprep/graduatesfirst; TSR BDO thread]
- **SJT format:** brief job-related scenario + **4 responses**; candidate picks **Most** and **Least** recommended (most/least-effective ranking keyed to pillars). **A single SJT item often assesses 2 pillars simultaneously** (Atkins) → harder. [PREP-VENDOR: aptitude-test-prep Atkins]
- **Cognitive items:** **framed as emails from a colleague** with embedded data; numerical = "information overload" graphs/tables; verbal = short passages needing inference; clustered in groups of 3–4 sharing a data source; 3–4 options each. [PREP-VENDOR: aptitude-test-prep, aptitudeprep]
- **Video / async interview:** typically **2 minutes to record**, **one shot, no pause/restart**; unlimited prep time before recording. Some variants 2 short motivational Qs; **an Amberjack assessor (not the client) reviews video against the 4 pillars.** [PREP-VENDOR: aptitude-test-prep Atkins/main]
- **Timing:** overall **"untimed, but time affects your score, particularly cognitive"**; est. **45–60 min**. [PREP-VENDOR]
- **Device:** own device; **webcam + microphone required** for video; **calculator + scratch paper** recommended for numerical; mobile-responsive (iOS/Android) platform per G-Cloud. [PREP-VENDOR; VENDOR G-Cloud]

## 5.6 — Scoring / norms / cut-offs

- **Blended single score:** all components combine into **ONE overall score mapped to the Future Skills Model / model of potential** (4 pillars). [VENDOR: talent-assessments "one overall score"]

## 5.7 — How to prepare legitimately

- **Learn the 4 pillars + 11 sub-traits and ask on every SJT item "which pillar(s) is this assessing?"** — prep guides frame this as the core legitimate strategy. [PREP-VENDOR: aptitude-test-prep]
- Research the **specific employer's values/competencies** (they overlay the SJT) — e.g. Morrisons values. [PREP-VENDOR]
- Practise **email-format numerical/verbal under mild time pressure** (info-overload data extraction). [PREP-VENDOR]
- Prep **2-minute one-shot video answers** to motivational/behavioural Qs against pillars; use unlimited prep time. [PREP-VENDOR]
- **VAC behaviours:** immersive business-scenario exercises → demonstrate collaboration in group tasks, structured analysis in case, values alignment; behaviours reflect "real-life role". [VENDOR framing]
- Prep-vendors sell packs (e.g. aptitude-test-prep ~$30 / 42 Qs / 30-day; JobTestPrep BDO pack; GraduatesFirst). [PREP-VENDOR — commercial, treat as marketing]

## 5.9 — False-positive / false-negative risks

- **Speed penalty on an "untimed" test** → candidates who work slowly (or over-prepare each item) may score lower on cognitive despite ability; ambiguous "untimed but timed" instruction risks mis-pacing. [INFERRED from PREP-VENDOR wording]
- **One-shot 2-min video, no retry** → tech glitches / nerves → unrepresentative sample; false negatives for anxious-but-able candidates. [INFERRED; CANDIDATE anxiety common in analog SJT/video literature]
- **Dual-pillar SJT items** → construct-contamination; a single mis-keyed judgement can cost 2 pillars. [INFERRED from Atkins design]
- **Coaching effect:** heavily marketed prep packs + memorisable pillar framework → SJT/values items susceptible to preparation → potential false positives for coached candidates. [INFERRED; PREP-VENDOR exists]
- **Values-alignment scoring** risks screening for cultural conformity / social desirability over ability. [INFERRED]
