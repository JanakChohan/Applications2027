Treat Willo as a lightweight, human-reviewed screening tool that an early-careers candidate is **more likely to meet at an SME, scale-up, apprenticeship, or non-IB graduate scheme** than at a bulge-bracket / elite-boutique IB. `[INDEPENDENT]` / `[CANDIDATE, n≈several]` — see §5.11.

**KEY NUANCE / STANCE UPDATE:** The old "Willo = no AI at all" framing is now **partly out of date**. As of 2026 Willo markets **"Willo Intelligence"** — AI **transcription + summarisation + skill-surfacing**, and a **"Benchmark"** auto-reject on binary multiple-choice only. Willo still states it does **NOT** do automated scoring/ranking of qualitative answers and does **NOT** appear to do facial/emotion analysis. So the honest positioning is: **AI-assisted, human-decided** — not "no AI." Confirm live before publishing. `[VENDOR]`

## 5.1 Ownership / corporate / what it is / stage & duration

- **What it is:** Asynchronous ("one-way") video interview / candidate-screening platform. Employer sets questions; candidate records video/audio/text/multiple-choice/file responses via a browser link on their own time; employer reviews later. Browser-based, no app/account/plugin for candidate. `[VENDOR]` https://www.willo.video/ ; https://www.willo.video/features
- **HQ / origin:** **Glasgow, Scotland** (UK-founded). Some press also calls it Scotland/Edinburgh-based; primary/most-cited = Glasgow. `[INDEPENDENT]` https://futurescot.com/glasgow-video-interviewing-platform-secures-investment-for-us-expansion/ ; https://www.scotsman.com/business/interview-euan-cameron-co-founder-and-ceo-of-glasgow-based-recruitment-focused-tech-firm-willo-4457645
- **Founders:** **Euan Cameron (CEO)** and **Andrew Wood**, co-founded **2018**; product **launched January 2020**. `[INDEPENDENT]` (Scotsman, Insider) https://www.insider.co.uk/news/video-interview-firm-raises-25-28710960

Private, VC/angel-backed scale-up. Investors named incl. ex-Mimecast GM Alan Kenny, Freeserve's Mark Danby. `[INDEPENDENT]` https://www.willo.video/blog/investment-in-pioneering-video-interview-firm-willo-tops-2-5-million ; https://www.scottishfinancialnews.com/articles/willo-secures-20k-funding-package-from-scottish-enterprise ; Crunchbase https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/willo-video

## 5.2 Why it exists — lightweight async screening; contrast with AI-scored HireVue

- **Purpose:** Replace/augment CV screening and reduce scheduling load at the **top of funnel**; "hire 40% faster," cut time-to-hire ~50%. Positioned for **high-volume + distributed/remote** hiring. `[VENDOR]` features page; https://www.willo.video/use-cases/graduate-hiring-software
- **Deliberate contrast with HireVue-style AI scoring:** Willo markets **human-first** hiring and states **"Willo does not offer automated scoring and decision-making for your hiring team"**; "the decision to move anyone forward stays with you"; "every meaningful judgment" stays with humans. `[VENDOR]` https://www.willo.video/blog/ai-candidate-scoring-tools
- **No facial/emotion analysis found** in any Willo material (contrast: HireVue dropped facial analysis 2020). Willo content contains **no mention** of facial-expression scoring. `[VENDOR]`/`[INFERRED — absence of evidence, not explicit denial]`
- **BUT current AI reality (must confirm live):** Willo now sells **"Willo Intelligence"** = AI that "transcribes and summarises every interview, surfaces skills and identifies gaps." And **"Benchmark"** = optional auto-reject **only on binary multiple-choice** questions (not qualitative). So: assistive AI yes; automated qualitative scoring no. `[VENDOR]` features page + ai-candidate-scoring-tools blog.
- Independent reviewer frames Willo as **lacking** "AI-powered evaluation, sentiment analysis, or bias detection" — i.e., analytics-light vs enterprise tools. This somewhat contradicts Willo's own richer AI marketing → treat as **positioning tension**; the heavy "AI proctoring" claims may be add-ons/aspirational. `[INDEPENDENT]` https://recruitcompare.io/tool/willo/

## 5.4 FULL MECHANICS (async video) — CORE SECTION

Sourced primarily from **Willo Knowledge Base (support.willo.video)** + candidate testimony.

- **Question types:** **video, audio, text**, **multiple-choice / "select from pre-defined answers"** (build tests/assessments inside Willo), and **file upload** (documents, **up to 25MB/file**). `[VENDOR]` https://www.willo.video/features ; support.willo.video/article/26-create-an-interview
- **Thinking time** (employer-configurable, PER QUESTION): set in **hours + minutes**. When shown, candidate sees the allocated time. **Timer counts UP.** Crucially: **"the time recorded includes the time taken to answer the question and any retakes."** Guard-rail model — if candidate exceeds it they are **not blocked** and can still submit; reviewer sees actual time + colour indicator (under/over). `[VENDOR]` https://support.willo.video/article/118-thinking-time
- **Answer / recording time ("Time to answer"):** employer sets max, **1 to 5 minutes** per question. Reviewer sees exactly how long candidate took + colour indicator over/under. `[VENDOR]` https://support.willo.video/article/118-time-to-answer
- **Retakes / re-records:** employer-configurable number, only for **supported types (video/audio)**. Can be changed any time, even after responses arrive. **Zero retakes = candidate's first take auto-saved/uploaded.** Willo **recommends ≥1 retake** for inclusivity. **Candidate can see how many retakes remain at any time.** `[VENDOR]` https://support.willo.video/article/14-change-the-number-of-retakes
- **Does reviewer see prior takes?** KB does **NOT** state that reviewers see discarded takes — implication is reviewer sees the **final submitted** response only, plus timing metadata. **Not explicitly confirmed → `[UNKNOWN]`.**
- **Candidate testimony on takes (Amazon apprenticeship, Willo):** "one of the easiest video interviews"; **infinite/unlimited time to think** on simple questions; **"4 retries"**; **responses are timed but preparation time isn't.** `[CANDIDATE, n≈several]` https://www.thestudentroom.co.uk/showthread.php?t=7425540 (Amazon Apprenticeships 2024 thread)
- **Deadline model:** async — candidate records "whenever they're ready"; employer sets an interview deadline/expiry per invite. Anti-cheat: if invited by **email/SMS/ATS**, timer keeps running even if candidate restarts/closes/refreshes browser (can't game thinking time). `[VENDOR]` thinking-time article.
- **Practice:** **unlimited practice attempts** before the real recording; test camera/mic. `[VENDOR]` features; how-does-willo-work guide.
- **Human vs AI review:** **Human reviewers make decisions.** AI ("Willo Intelligence") **assists** via transcription/summary/skill-surfacing; **no automated qualitative scoring**; optional binary-MCQ auto-reject ("Benchmark"). `[VENDOR]` ai-candidate-scoring-tools blog + features.
- **Device:** any device with a camera; browser-based; mobile or desktop; low-bandwidth tolerant; no install. `[VENDOR]`

## 5.5 Tailoring — questions set per employer; no fixed competency engine

- Questions are **fully employer-authored** (typed or recorded by the employer), or drawn from Willo's **question library / templates**. `[VENDOR]` create-an-interview KB; https://www.willo.video/blog/top-video-interview-questions-for-recruiters-and-hiring-managers
- **No proprietary fixed competency framework / trait model** (unlike psychometric-style vendors). Willo provides the container + optional library; **the competencies assessed are whatever the employer chooses.** `[INFERRED from VENDOR docs]`
- Scoring criteria are therefore **the employer's own** review workflow, not a Willo-normed engine (see §5.6). `[VENDOR]`

## 5.6 SCORING — reviewer workflow

- **Reviewer workflow:** watch responses (can **watch at 2x speed**), **rate candidates using Willo's scorecard system**, share with team for collaborative decision. `[VENDOR]` features; how-does-willo-work.
- **Scorecards are employer-configured ratings**, not norm-referenced. **No percentile / no norm group / no benchmark cohort scoring** of qualitative answers. `[VENDOR]`/`[INFERRED]`
- **What recruiter sees:** the response media, **transcript + AI summary** (if Willo Intelligence enabled), **time-taken metadata + over/under colour flag**, retakes-remaining state, and their team's scorecard ratings. `[VENDOR]`
- **Benchmark auto-filter:** can auto-reject **only on binary multiple-choice** (e.g., knockout eligibility Qs) — never on video content. `[VENDOR]` ai-candidate-scoring-tools.
- **Retake at scoring stage:** employer can send back / re-invite, but no built-in norm re-score; decisions are human. `[INFERRED]`

## 5.7 How to prepare (candidate)

- **Same core async-video technique as HireVue-style one-ways:** structured answers (**STAR / CARL**), good lighting, camera at eye level, look at the lens not the screen, controlled pace, quiet background, test camera/mic first (Willo gives **unlimited practice**). `[PREP-VENDOR]`/`[INFERRED — generic async-video best practice]`
- **Willo-specific advantages to exploit:** **thinking time** (often generous / sometimes unlimited per candidate reports) — use it to structure; **retakes** (often ≥1, Amazon report says 4) — but **note the timer keeps running through retakes and thinking time**, so don't burn all your time re-recording; the reviewer sees your total time + over/under flag. `[VENDOR]` thinking-time/retakes KB; `[CANDIDATE]` Student Room.
- **Answer length:** cap is 1–5 min per Q — rehearse to land inside the allocated window (over/under is visibly flagged to reviewer). `[VENDOR]`
- Candidate sentiment: many find async **less stressful** (own schedule) but some find it **impersonal**. `[INDEPENDENT G2]` https://www.g2.com/products/willo-video-interviewing/reviews

## 5.8 INTEGRITY / PROCTORING

- **By design, lightweight / minimal-friction** ("without creating a Big Brother feel"). Core anti-gaming is the **timer that keeps running** across browser restart/close/refresh when invited via email/SMS/ATS. `[VENDOR]` thinking-time KB.
- **Willo's own comparison blog claims** it can monitor **tab-switching, copy-paste blocking, multiple faces/voices, eye-gaze anomalies, background noise, and AI-generated/plagiarised responses.** `[VENDOR]` https://www.willo.video/blog/ai-tools-for-candidate-screening — **CAUTION:** this is marketing in a "compare the tools" blog and may describe premium/add-on capability or aspiration; an **independent reviewer says Willo lacks bias/sentiment/AI evaluation.** Tension → verify what's actually in-product before publishing as fact. `[INDEPENDENT]` recruitcompare.
- **Optional add-ons:** **Digital identity / right-to-work / criminal background checks** with a certified report; Willo is **UKDIATF-certified**. Can be placed at any stage or be the only stage. `[VENDOR]` https://support.willo.video/article/129-digital-identity-check ; https://www.willo.video/integrations/identity-verification-right-to-work-checks
- **Flags actioning:** no evidence of automated rejection on proctoring flags for video; humans review. `[INFERRED]`

## 5.9 FALSE-POSITIVE risks (fewer, because human-reviewed)

- Because qualitative scoring is **human**, the algorithmic false-positive risks of AI/facial scoring largely **don't apply**. `[INFERRED]`
- Residual risks: (a) **time-pressure artefact** — over/under-time colour flag may bias a reviewer against a slow-but-good candidate; (b) **retake exhaustion** — timer runs through retakes/thinking-time, so a nervous candidate can "run out" and submit a weak take; (c) **impersonal format** penalises candidates who present better live; (d) any **AI transcription errors** feeding the reviewer's summary; (e) if **Benchmark** binary auto-reject is misconfigured, a wrong knockout answer = hard reject with no human look. `[INFERRED]`/`[VENDOR]`

## 5.10 Adjustments / accessibility / GDPR

- **Accessibility:** strives to meet **WCAG 2.1** (features page says WCAG 2.0); keyboard-navigable, screen-reader compatible, high-contrast/colour-blind considerations, browser font-size control; **text-based question option** for candidates who can't watch/listen to video. `[VENDOR]` https://support.willo.video/article/92-accessibility-statement ; features page.
- **Adjustments:** async format itself is framed as an accessibility benefit (own time/place, no travel/childcare clash, good for neurodiverse/disabled). **No documented formal "extra time / deadline extension" adjustment workflow found** — thinking time & deadlines are employer-set, so any adjustment is **the employer's to grant**, not a Willo feature. `[UNKNOWN — no explicit adjustment process found]`
- **GDPR / data:** Willo actively markets **GDPR compliance + ISO 27001 + encryption + Trust Center**; UKDIATF certified. Data-residency specifics **not stated on pages fetched** → `[UNKNOWN — verify residency/region]`. `[VENDOR]`
