# Applications 2027

Interview preparation research pack for the 2027 Corporate Access Services Intern role at Millennium (London). Research ran 4 to 17 September 2026. Round 1 (7 September) was passed. Round 2 is confirmed by invite as a single thirty-minute Webex interview on Tuesday 29 September 2026 at 10:30 BST with Joanna Chinappen, "more technical" in the recruiter's words; the earlier documents' assumption of two one-hour interviews came from Round 1 and is superseded by `Round_Two_Technical_Brief.pdf`.

**Start here:** `Millennium_Corporate_Access_Complete_Interview_Pack.pdf` (211 pages, 73 figures, 426 sources) is the single complete report produced by running `Master_Prompt_Template.md` for this firm and role. It consolidates every document below into the template's sixteen parts (how to use it and the 10-minute version; the role and the team; the firm from the inside; why it is built this way; what stands out; the hedge fund industry from zero; corporate access and its regulation; what is happening now; forming your own view; the interview process, Round 1 record, Round 2 plan and question bank; interviewer briefing; the scenario playbook; "why Millennium" in three lengths; cheat sheet; a 111-term glossary; the source table; gaps). Sections and figures are renumbered to run through the pack and the SVG figures are inline in the `.html`. The separate documents remain for anyone who wants one topic on its own.
