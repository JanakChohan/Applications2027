# Applications 2027

Interview preparation research pack for the 2027 Corporate Access Services Intern role at Millennium (London). Research completed 4 September 2026 for Interview Round 1 on 7 September 2026.

- `Millennium_Corporate_Access_Interview_Guide.pdf` (74 pages) and the source `.html` it was rendered from (Chromium via Playwright; re-render with `page.pdf({format:'A4', printBackground:true})`).
- `Cheat_Sheet.pdf` (2 pages) and its `.html`.
- `Hedge_Funds_From_Zero.pdf` (25 pages) and its `.html`: what a hedge fund is versus other buy-side firms, and the nine forces acting on the industry as of September 2026. Shares the source numbering of the main guide.
- `Inside_Millennium.pdf` (25 pages) and its `.html`: how Millennium actually works and why it is different. The three contracts (PM franchise, investor deal, balance sheet), what the centre does all day, why the maths favours structure over talent, Millennium versus Citadel, the four investment decisions the centre makes, and a mechanism-based answer to "why Millennium". Built from Millennium's own job postings and leadership pages, SEC records, a former head of enterprise risk's public remarks, analyst newsletters and allocator research. Shares the source numbering of the main guide (new sources S300 to S333).
- `Question_Bank.md`: 52 questions with scaffolds and 10 questions to ask.
- `Reading_List.md`: every source found, grouped by theme, with a one-line reason to read it.

Every non-obvious claim in the guide carries a source marker [Sxx] resolved in Part 11. Claims are tagged Confirmed, Reported or Inferred. Millennium is private; figures about it are press estimates unless stated.
