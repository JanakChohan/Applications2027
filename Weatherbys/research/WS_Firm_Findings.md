# Weatherbys Bank / Weatherbys Banking Group: firm research

Compiled 2026-10-07. Main primary source: **Weatherbys Banking Group Annual Report and Financial Accounts 2025** [S4]. I downloaded the full PDF and read it as text. It covers the year to 31 Dec 2025 and was signed 21 April 2026. Source IDs are in brackets. Status codes: FETCHED means I read the full page or PDF. SNIPPET means I saw only search-engine text. Anything marked "summariser" was read through a WebFetch summary, not verbatim.

## 1. Findings

### 1.1 Legal entities, regulation, ownership

- **Weatherbys Bank Limited**
  - Company no. **02943300**, registered in England. Incorporated **28 June 1994**. Previous name **Weatherbys & Co. Ltd.** (28 Jun 1994 to 5 Jan 1999). SIC 64191 (Banks). Active [S2].
  - Registered office: **52–60 Sanders Road, Finedon Road Industrial Estate, Wellingborough, Northamptonshire NN8 4BX** [S2][S4].
  - Last accounts made up to 31 Dec 2025. Confirmation statement dated 6 Jul 2026 [S2].
- **Regulation:** "Authorised by the Prudential Regulation Authority and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority and the Prudential Regulation Authority". **Financial Services Register number: 204571** [S3][S4].
- **Trading names of Weatherbys Bank Limited** [S4]:
  - **Weatherbys Private Bank**
  - **Weatherbys Racing Bank**
  - **Weatherbys Business Bank**, launched Q4 2025
- **"Weatherbys Banking Group"** [S4] is made up of:
  - Weatherbys Bank Limited
  - **Arkle Finance Limited**: no. 03398034, FCA FRN 630582, asset finance, established 1997 per the history page [S5]
  - Weatherbys Bank (Nominees) Limited
- **Ownership chain:**
  - Weatherbys Bank Limited is "a wholly owned subsidiary of **Weatherbys Bank Holdings Limited (WBHL)**" [S4].
  - Companies House lists WBHL (no. 05659237, incorporated 20 Dec 2005) as the PSC with 75%+ of shares and voting rights, notified 6 Apr 2016 [S6].
  - WBHL's own PSCs are trustee-type controllers, all notified 1 May 2024, each with "Right to appoint or remove directors with control over the trustees of a trust": David Charles Bellamy, Pollyanna Mary Carr, Harry Alexander Lawson-Johnston [S7].
  - Former PSCs that ceased include Roger Nicholas Weatherby and Johnny Roger Weatherby (both ceased 1 May 2024) [S7].
  - The annual report says: "No director had any interest in the share capital of the Bank or of any other Group company" [S4].
  - Taken together, this points to family ownership held through trusts. The firm calls itself "a seventh generation, family-owned bank" and refers to "the family shareholders" [S4]. Wikipedia says Weatherbys is "solely owned by the Weatherby family" [S1, SNIPPET].
- **Weatherbys Ltd** (racing administration: General Stud Book, racing services, scientific testing) is separate from the Bank. The annual report calls them "companies under common control". Weatherbys Limited, Weatherbys Ireland GSB Limited, Weatherbys GSB Limited and (until 1 Aug 2025) Weatherbys Hamilton LLP were all under common control with the Bank [S4, note 30].
  - The history page says the business "became two separate entities: Weatherbys Ltd and Weatherbys Banking Group". The year is **2005**, inferred from the order of the timeline [S5].
  - CEO of Weatherbys Limited: **Sharon O'Regan from July 2025**, succeeding Russell Ferris, who joined Racecourse Media Group on 18 Aug 2025. She was previously Deputy CEO of Weatherbys Limited and General Manager of Weatherbys Ireland [S28][S29].
- **Weatherbys Hamilton** (insurance broker):
  - Founded 2012 as a partnership between Weatherbys Bank and Charles Hamilton [S5].
  - On **1 Aug 2025** the Bank sold its investment in Weatherbys General Services Ltd, the holding company for its interest in Weatherbys Hamilton, to **Brown & Brown (Europe) Ltd** for **£10.5m cash**, a **£10.2m gain** [S4]. Deal announced around 24 Jul 2025 [S10][S11].
  - Rationale given: "simplifying its operating model and concentrating on its core strengths in banking, investment and wealth advice and asset finance" [S4].
- **Ireland:** I found **no evidence of a "Weatherbys Private Bank" in Ireland**. Weatherbys' Irish presence is through the Weatherbys Ltd side: Weatherbys Ireland GSB Ltd and Weatherbys Ireland (stud book, lab and DNA services) [S1][S4]. The Bank's offices are UK only: London, Wellingborough, Edinburgh and Manchester [S3][S13]. Treat any claim of an Irish private bank as unverified.

### 1.2 History and leadership

- **History timeline.** The firm's history page [S5] lists dates and events in order; I matched them by sequence.
  - **1770:** James Weatherby, a Northumbrian solicitor, appointed by the Jockey Club as Secretary, Keeper of the Matchbook and Stakeholder.
  - **1773:** first Racing Calendar.
  - **1791:** Introduction to the General Stud Book.
  - **1970:** Wellingborough HQ opened by the Queen Mother.
  - **1979:** seventh generation joins. The annual report says Sir Johnny joined in 1982.
  - **1994:** banking licence issued by the Bank of England.
  - **1997:** Arkle Finance established.
  - **2005:** split into Weatherbys Ltd and Weatherbys Banking Group.
  - **2006:** Weatherbys Private Bank and Weatherbys Racing Bank formed, and a central London office opened.
  - **2012:** Weatherbys Hamilton.
  - **2013:** launched in Scotland.
  - **2015:** Edinburgh office.
  - **2020:** 250th anniversary.
  - **2022:** Manchester office.
- **Sir Johnny Weatherby (1959–2025)** [S4 tribute]
  - Chairman of Weatherbys Bank from **1994 to 2017**; "instrumental in obtaining our banking licence in 1994".
  - Chairman of Ascot Racecourse 2008–2018.
  - Died December 2025.
  - His son Max joined the business in early 2026 as "the first member of the eighth generation" [S4].
- **David Bellamy** (ex-St James's Place CEO) was Banking Group chairman for about six years until stepping down in March 2024 [S12, SNIPPET].
- **Chairman: Roger Weatherby**, from **March 2024** [S4]
  - Joined the group board as a NED in 1992, became executive in 1997, Group FD in 1998.
  - **CEO of Weatherbys Bank 2000 to September 2023.**
  - Background: Eton; Masters in Finance from LBS; 15th/19th King's Royal Hussars; Cazenove & Co 1985–1996.
  - Senior Steward of The Jockey Club 2014–2019.
  - Started "Creating The Future" in 2018.
- **Group CEO: Quentin Marshall**, from **September 2023** [S4][S8]
  - Joined in June 2015 from Coutts, where he was Head of Advisory in the Investments Team and Deputy Chairman of the Investment Strategy Committee, "overseeing c. £30bn of assets".

### 1.3 Business model

- **Client base:** "predominantly high net worth individuals". Alongside private banking and wealth advisory services, the Group provides banking and VAT services to the horseracing industry, banking to UK SMEs, and asset finance [S4].
- **Private Bank clients:** "entrepreneurs, land owners, from entertainment, sports or those enjoying retirement" [S16].
- **Specialist segments** listed on the website [S13][S16]:
  - Arts, Media & Creative
  - Entrepreneurs
  - Farms, estates & landowners
  - Finance professionals
  - Law firm partners
  - Non-residents
  - Racing professionals
  - Sports professionals
  - US citizens
  - Women and Finance
  - Intermediary services
- **Service model:** "Named private banker and assistant from day one" [S16].
  - Bankers are holistic across lending, investment (through the in-house **Investment & Wealth Advice, "IWA"**, team) and daily banking [S9].
  - Each banker covers "a portfolio of 100+ private banking clients", per the Senior Private Banker advert [S24, SNIPPET].
- **Products:**
  - Current accounts in GBP, EUR and USD
  - Fixed-term, notice and instant-access deposits
  - FX
  - Charge Card (launched 2025)
  - USD and EUR debit cards (2025)
  - Execution-only investing via **Saxo Markets** (2025)
  - Financial planning, investments, tax planning
  - Lending and mortgages: residential mortgages, bridging, secured loans, Lombard [S4][S16][S17]
- **Lending approach** [S17]:
  - "flexible underwriting rather than fixed criteria and automated credit scoring"
  - "Every decision is made by a person who understands complex HNW income structures"
  - No upper age limit
  - Land and investment portfolios accepted as security
- **Lombard lending** [S18][S27]:
  - Up to 50% LTV
  - "1.48% over Base Rate", 1% arrangement fee (SNIPPET from the firm's Lombard page)
  - From Aug 2026, extended to portfolios managed elsewhere
- **Loan book** [S4]:
  - Private banking lending is **68%** of the loan book, "secured principally on residential properties at an average loan to value ratio of approximately 34%".
  - Asset finance (Arkle) is **32%**.
  - Elsewhere the report says "approximately 70%" of lending is to private clients.
- **Racing Bank** [S21]:
  - Classic Racing, Multi Owner and Racing Gold accounts
  - Racehorse VAT Return Service
  - Racing Payment Service
  - Racehorse Sponsorship Scheme
  - In 2025 it changed onboarding for multi-owner (syndicate) accounts after industry feedback [S4].
- **Business Bank** (launched Q4 2025) [S4]:
  - Business current accounts, term and notice deposits, client monies accounts
  - Target clients: owner-managed businesses and corporates "in specialist or complex sectors", including finance, insurance, charities and high-growth companies
- **Bloodstock and agricultural lending:** I found **no specific "bloodstock lending" product page**. Farms and estates are served through the general private bank lending and Lombard products, with a named landed-estates team [S19]. Treat "bloodstock lending" as unconfirmed.
- **Client numbers:**
  - "a total of 22,000 clients, according to its 2024 annual report" [S15].
  - The CEO says the "upper limit for being able to be so flexible is 10,000 to 15,000 private banking relationships" [S15].
- **Minimum criteria:** not published on the pages I read [S16].
- **Net Promoter Score:** 84 in 2025 (81 in 2024) [S4]. A banker testimonial calls it "the highest published of any UK bank" [S9]. That is the firm's own claim.
- **2030 strategy:**
  - Plans to "double in size over the next five years" [S4][S20].
  - Arkle aims to double to "£600 million of lending by 2030" [S4].
  - Strategy is "100 per cent human, 100 per cent digital" [S15].
  - A new online and mobile bank and a new client interface are due in 2026 [S4].

## 6. Interview and application angles

1. **Family ownership and long-termism, with numbers.** Seven generations; Max Weatherby joined in 2026 as the first of the eighth. The firm reports a 53% loan-to-deposit ratio, an LCR of 1,080% and a 34% average LTV on private client lending. Prudence is visible in the balance sheet, not only in the brand.
2. **Rate sensitivity.** NIM fell from 4.53% to 4.05% as Base Rate fell, so PBT before exceptionals halved to £12.8m. Growth in fees and investment AuA is part of the answer: AuA rose 30% and the Saxo platform launched. A candidate could discuss balancing NII dependence with advice and investment income.
3. **"100 per cent human, 100 per cent digital."** The new online and mobile bank is due in 2026, and the CEO talks about leaving "judgement and discretion with the front line". The racing syndicate account story [S15] is a good illustration of human underwriting.
4. **Growth plan.** "Double in size over the next five years". Recent hires include Duncalf (Manchester, 2025), Manduca (London), and Wotton plus Page (Oct 2026). The Manchester move to Sunlight House and wider Scotland coverage show the regional build-out; a candidate can ask where APBs are placed.
5. **Holistic banker model.** Bankers cover lending, IWA investments and daily banking, with no product pushing. That links to Tapner's "Pushing products won't work for the type of clients Weatherbys seeks to attract." Mention CeMAP, IAD or PCIAM study plans.
6. **Segments.** Farms and estates, sports and entertainment (the new head is ex-Coutts), law firm partners, racing, and US citizens. A candidate can pick one and say how they'd add value; Aidan Faik's Withers interview on landed estates is useful prep.
7. **Business Bank (Q4 2025).** It extends the service model into SMEs (charities, schools, VC funds, startups). It shows ambition beyond the traditional HNW core.
8. **Culture signals.**
   - Weatherbys Way values: Forward-looking, Individual, Responsive, Straightforward, Trusted.
   - NPS 84; engagement 72.6.
   - Independent culture review by Lagercrantz & Lombardi.
   - Creating The Future conference with over 400 attendees.
   - Staff volunteering (City Harvest; Daylight Centre in Wellingborough).
   - British Racing School tutoring partnership (Sep 2026).
9. **Hiring process awareness.** For private banker roles there is a case-based lending and client-scenario stage and a business-plan stage. APBs use lending and investment role-plays. Prepare a simple secured-lending case: LTV, affordability, complex HNW income.
10. **Recent milestones to cite.** Citywealth Private Bank of the Year (May 2026). Hamilton sale (£10.2m gain) as strategic focus. Lombard extended to third-party portfolios (Aug 2026).
11. **Gender pay gap honesty.** Median 31.4%. HR Director Tom O'Reilly writes "we must do more". This can be discussed tactfully if diversity comes up.
