Say this in the interview
"A private bank like Weatherbys is mostly a lending and deposit business wrapped in a relationship. About 87% of its income is net interest. Investment advice is growing, and that matters because it is less sensitive to Bank Rate." [S4]

5.2 The structural forces reshaping UK private banking

  • Consolidation. In H1 2026, UK financial services deal value rose from £4.2bn to £33.7bn, and wealth and asset management deals from £200m to £22.7bn (EY data) [S159]. Legacy names have gone: Child & Co's last branch closed in June 2022, and Adam & Co's banking moved into Coutts in 2022 [S123][S120].
  • Deposit competition. Hoare's grew deposits 5% "despite the headwind of quantitative tightening and competition from tax-advantaged gilts" [S105]. Hampden grew deposits 36% in a year [S128].
  • Wealth leaving the UK. HMRC data shows non-dom taxpayers fell from 83,100 to 81,900 in the last year of the old regime [S149]. Henley projected a net loss of 16,500 millionaires in 2025, but its projections are disputed [S161].

5.3 Three competing views of the next three to five years

View A: scale wins

Bank-owned groups combine banking, investment and advice. They spread technology and regulatory costs across more clients and squeeze smaller independents. Evidence: NatWest plus Evelyn at about £131bn of client assets; the M&A surge [S103][S159].

View B: boutiques win

When big brands raise minimums and standardise, clients who want a named banker and flexible lending move to independents. Evidence: Weatherbys NPS 84, Hampden +36% deposits, Investec adding 5,000 UK clients; the lighter SDDT capital regime from 2027 [S4][S128][S113][S143].

View C: tax and policy decide

Tax changes shift value from holding deposits to advice, succession and lending around tax events. Rates stay volatile. Evidence: APR/BPR cap, pensions in IHT, and the September hike votes [S152][S154][S102].

The three views are the research team's synthesis from the sources tagged, not one publication's forecast Inferred.

Get these dates exactly right. Most applicants will be vague, and a precise date is an easy way to sound credible.

RuleWhat it is, in plain EnglishWhy Weatherbys cares
Consumer Duty
31 Jul 2023 / 31 Jul 2024		FCA Principle 12: firms must "act to deliver good outcomes for retail customers". It covers products, price and value, understanding and support [S140]. An FCA review in July 2026 found firms "describing processes" without showing that products had actually been adapted [S141].Wealthy individuals are still retail customers. Covers fair value of fees and interest, and care for vulnerable clients.
FSCS deposit limit
1 Dec 2025		If a UK bank fails, the Financial Services Compensation Scheme repays depositors up to £120,000 (was £85,000). Temporary high balances, such as house-sale money, are covered up to £1.4m for six months [S145].Private clients often hold far more, so the bank's own strength is part of the product.
APR / BPR cap
6 Apr 2026		Agricultural and Business Property Relief: farms and family businesses used to pass on free of inheritance tax. Now 100% relief applies only to the first £2.5m per person. Above that, the effective rate is 20%. Couples can pass on up to £5m [S152]. The cap was raised from a proposed £1m on 23 December 2025.The most important one for you. Farms and estates are a core Weatherbys segment. Families need succession planning, lifetime gifting, and liquidity to pay tax without selling land.
Pensions in IHT
6 Apr 2027		Unused pension pots will count towards the estate for inheritance tax [S154].More estates pay inheritance tax, so demand for planning rises.
Non-dom abolition
6 Apr 2025		The old "remittance basis" for UK residents domiciled abroad was replaced by a four-year residence-based regime [S148].Affects internationally mobile clients. Weatherbys has a non-resident segment, but most of its clients are UK-rooted Inferred.
Strong and Simple (SDDT)
capital regime 1 Jan 2027		Simpler prudential rules from the PRA for small domestic deposit-takers with up to £20bn of assets [S143][S144].Could cut the regulatory cost burden for a bank of Weatherbys' size. Whether it has opted in is not verified, so ask rather than assert.
Basel 3.1
1 Jan 2027		The final set of international capital rules, phased in fully by 2030 [S146].Changes how much capital a bank holds against mortgages and loans.
Bank Rate
3.75%		Held on 17 September 2026 by 6 votes to 3, with three members wanting 4%. CPI inflation was 3.1% in August. Next decision 5 November 2026 [S102].Directly drives Weatherbys' interest margin (Fig 2.4).
The single highest-value technical point
"Since 6 April 2026, farms and family businesses only get full inheritance tax relief on the first £2.5 million per person, with about 20% tax above that. For Weatherbys' farming and landed clients, that turns succession from a lawyer's question into a banking question too: how to fund a tax bill without selling land. The banker's job is to coordinate with the family's lawyer and accountant, not to give the tax advice." [S152][S19]
