How to use this pack

Two deadlines matter. The form closes on Friday 23 October 2026 (16 days from today). The assessment centre follows on 30 November or 7 December.

The 10-minute version

  1. What Weatherbys is. A family-owned private bank founded on a 1770 racing business. It got its banking licence in 1994 and opened in London in 2006. In 2025 it had £1.9bn of deposits, £1.0bn of loans and 483 staff, and is aiming to "double in size over the next five years" [S4][S5] Confirmed.
  2. How it makes money. It takes deposits from wealthy clients and lends out about half of them (a loan-to-deposit ratio of 53%). It earns the interest margin, plus a smaller amount in fees. When Bank Rate fell, its net interest margin fell from 4.53% to 4.05%, and underlying profit roughly halved, from £26.7m to £12.8m [S4] Confirmed.
  3. What makes it different. Each client has a named banker who covers lending, investments and day-to-day banking. Lending decisions are made by people, not credit scores. It has specialist teams for farms and estates, racing, sport and entertainment, and law-firm partners. Its client satisfaction score (Net Promoter Score) is 84, and the CEO says he leaves "as much judgement and discretion with the front line as I possibly can" [S4][S15][S17].
  4. What is happening. It is hiring bankers from Coutts. It won Citywealth "Private Bank of the Year" in May 2026, launched a Business Bank in late 2025, sold its insurance stake in August 2025, and extended Lombard lending to portfolios held at other firms in August 2026 [S4][S26][S27][S31].
  5. What is happening to its clients. Since 6 April 2026, farms and family businesses get full inheritance tax relief only on the first £2.5m. Pensions come into inheritance tax from 6 April 2027. Bank Rate is 3.75%, and three of the nine Bank of England rate-setters voted to raise it in September [S152][S154][S102].
  6. The application. The form has three questions. Q1 tests motivation and research, Q2 tests relationship-building (the core skill of the job) and Q3 tests what you are like outside your degree. Weatherbys publishes no weighting. Plan on roughly 30 / 40 / 30 effort, for the reasons in Part A.
  7. The one line to remember. "Weatherbys is built so that one person who knows the client makes the decision. I want to learn to be that person."
