One question to ask

"Since the APR and BPR cap came in, how have conversations with farming clients changed?"

Answer protocol

Steady (restate) → Sort (client, rules, time) → Act (steps + who you'd involve) → Close (client safe, nothing at risk). 45 to 75 seconds.

Priority order

1 Rules and the bank's name → 2 Client's real interest → 3 Process and record → 4 Speed.
"Quickly, but not in a way that puts the client or the bank at risk."

Counters

  • Interrupted: answer, then "coming back to..."
  • Contradicted: "Which part?" Hold if reasoning holds.
  • Silence: stop talking; offer more detail.
  • False choice: look for the third option.
  • Don't know: "My understanding is X; I'd check with Y."
  • Badmouth bait: never criticise rivals.

Never

  • Give advice or tax advice as an intern
  • Skip a control to please a senior person
  • Talk about clients outside work
  • Claim Weatherbys is the oldest or biggest bank
  • Fake a love of racing
