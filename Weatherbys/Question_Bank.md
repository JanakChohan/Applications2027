# Weatherbys Private Banking Internship 2027: Question Bank

44 practice questions. Scaffolds, not scripts. Source IDs [Sxx] resolve to Part 15 of the complete pack.

## Motivation

### 1. Why private banking?
- **Really testing:** Do you know what the job is, and is your interest earned?
- **Structure:** Moment -> insight about the job -> evidence you acted on it
- **Facts to weave in:** One banker across lending, investments and banking [S9]; 87% of income is interest [S4]
- **Trap:** Describing investment banking or stock-picking

### 2. Why Weatherbys rather than Coutts or Hoare's?
- **Really testing:** Research depth; can you name a mechanism?
- **Structure:** Two mechanisms + one live fact + one personal link
- **Facts to weave in:** Human credit decisions [S17]; NPS 84 [S4]; Coutts hires moving to Weatherbys [S31]
- **Trap:** Running down a rival

### 3. What do you know about our history?
- **Really testing:** Did you go beyond the homepage?
- **Structure:** 1770 stakeholder -> 1994 licence -> 2006 London -> today
- **Facts to weave in:** James Weatherby, Jockey Club, 1770; licence 1994 [S5][S4]
- **Trap:** Calling it the oldest bank in the UK

### 4. Where do you see yourself in five years?
- **Really testing:** Fit with the APB route; realism
- **Structure:** APB -> qualified -> building own client book
- **Facts to weave in:** APB is two years to Private Banker [S9]; CeMAP, IAD, PCIAM [S9]
- **Trap:** Saying you want to move to investment banking

### 5. What would you want to get out of the internship?
- **Really testing:** Self-awareness; will you add value?
- **Structure:** Learn X, contribute Y, test Z about yourself
- **Facts to weave in:** Live projects; exposure across the bank [advert]
- **Trap:** Only talking about what you get

### 6. Which of our client segments interests you most and why?
- **Really testing:** Genuine curiosity about clients
- **Structure:** Pick one; name their need; name the bank's answer

## Commercial

### 7. How does a private bank make money?

- **Structure:** Deposits -> lending and liquidity -> margin + fees -> costs -> profit
- **Facts to weave in:** NII £79.0m of £91.1m income; NIM 4.05% [S4]
- **Trap:** Saying 'fees from investments' only

### 8. Why did our profit fall in 2025?

- **Facts to weave in:** PBT £12.8m vs £26.7m pre one-offs; costs +19% [S4]
