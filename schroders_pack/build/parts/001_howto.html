Part 0

How to use this pack, and the 10-minute version

Your next step is probably Stage 3 (the Cappfinity work simulation and video) and then an assessment centre. This page tells you what to read, in what order, depending on how much time you have.

0.1 The 10-minute emergency version

If you read nothing else
  1. The ownership changed this week. Nuveen, the asset manager owned by the US pension provider TIAA, buys Schroders for 590p cash plus up to 22p of dividends a share, about £9.9bn. The court sanctioned the deal on 29 September 2026; it takes effect on 1 October and the shares delist on 2 October [S75]. The brand stays, London becomes the combined group's non-US headquarters, and Schroders runs as a standalone business for at least 12 months [S76]. Never call Schroders "FTSE 100" or "family-controlled" in the present tense.
  2. What Schroders is. An active asset and wealth manager founded in 1804, with record assets of £867.8bn at 30 June 2026 [S70]. Three businesses: Public Markets, Schroders Capital (private markets) and Wealth Management (Cazenove Capital) [S71].
  3. What Client Group is. The central function that "integrates expertise in product, marketing, and sales", in three areas: Value Proposition, Sales Activation and Delivery [S263]. Led since August 2025 by Matt Oomen, Global Head of Client Group, who sits on the executive committee [S306].
  4. Why it was rebuilt. In 2025 Schroders "designed a new sales and marketing model where central support is focused on fewer core propositions" [S72]. More than 16% of the public markets fund range is being merged or closed [S71].
  5. The number that shows commercial sense. Fee margins range from 6bp in core solutions to 57bp in private markets [S71]. So what Client Group sells matters as much as how much.
  6. Latest results (H1 2026). Profit up 46% to £459.8m; cost:income 68%; net outflows of £8.3bn, including one £6.6bn low-margin redemption; intermediary gross inflows up more than 40% [S70].
  7. Your market view. Energy-driven inflation is back. The Bank of England held at 3.75% on 17 September 2026 with three votes to raise; the next decision is 5 November [S232]. Schroders' economists see global growth of about 2.5% and name a spike in long-term yields as the main risk [S242].
  8. Your rule for every scenario. "I won't trade the rules or the client's interest for speed. Inside those, I'll be as fast and helpful as I can, and I'll tell people early." (Part 11)
  9. Your video answer shape. Energy (what you enjoy), evidence (one real example), learning, link to Client Group. About two minutes. One take, no pause [S262].
  10. Your question to ask. "Since Client Group moved to fewer core propositions, how do regional sales feed what they hear from clients back into Value Proposition?"

0.3 How to read the markers

Every non-obvious fact has a source marker such as [S71] that resolves to Part 15, with outlet, date, URL and whether the page was read in full. Claims are tagged [Confirmed] (primary source or two credible outlets), [Reported] (one credible source, or a search summary only) or [Inferred] (the author's reasoning). Estimates are labelled as estimates, including on charts.
