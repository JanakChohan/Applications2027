PART 13

Cheat sheet: Schroders Client Group Sales Intern 2027

The protocol (45 to 75 s)

  • Steady: "So the situation is..."
  • Sort: "Two things matter: the rule and the client."
  • Act: "First I'd..., then..., and I'd tell [owner] by [time]."
  • Close: "That way it's fast and right." Stop.

Priority order

1 Rules and Schroders' name. 2 The client's real interest. 3 Fair service, process and the record. 4 Speed.
Frame: "I won't trade the rules or the client's interest for speed. Inside those, I'll be fast and helpful, and I'll tell people early."

Never

  • Send unapproved or changed client material (COBS 4.2.1R).
  • Guess performance, forecast returns, promise recovery.
  • Repeat, forward or trade on possible inside information (UK MAR). Tell Compliance.
  • Take client data outside approved systems (UK GDPR).
  • Call Schroders "FTSE 100", "listed" or "family-controlled" in the present tense.
  • Use AI to write your answers (Schroders prohibits it).

Eight frame-breakers, eight counters

  • Interrupt: answer the new point, then finish yours.
  • "That's wrong": "Tell me more." Update or hold with a reason.
  • Silence: wait 3 s; offer to go deeper.
  • Escalation: same order, escalate sooner.
  • "The MD says": rank doesn't change the rule; chase approval.
  • False choice: find the third door.
  • Knowledge trap: "I don't know; here's how I'd find out."
  • Badmouth invite: generous and specific.

Video answer shape (about 2 min, one take)

Energy (what you enjoy) → Evidence (one real example, a number) → Learning → Link (Client Group Sales). If time is short, say the link early.

Why Schroders (30 s)

Structure: one Client Group owns sales, product, marketing; refocused in 2025 on "fewer core propositions". Economics: margins from 6bp (core solutions) to 57bp (private markets), so what you sell matters. Moment: Nuveen from 1 Oct 2026, ~$2.5trn, London the non-US HQ, brand kept. Close: the Mercer LTAF: how a proposition becomes a mandate.
