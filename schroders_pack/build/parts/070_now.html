Part 7

What is happening right now

The twelve stories from April to September 2026 that a Client Group interviewer expects you to know, each with why it matters and a question you could raise. Refresh the market numbers in the week of your interview.

7.1 Your 60-second market view (as at 29 September 2026)

Say this, in your own words

"The big driver this year has been an energy shock. Since the Iran conflict started in late February, disruption around the Strait of Hormuz has kept oil around $100, and inflation is rising again. UK CPI was 3.1% in August, and the Bank of England expects it just above 4% early next year. So the debate has flipped from rate cuts to possible rises. The Bank held at 3.75% in September, but three members voted to raise, so the 5 November meeting is live. That has hit long bonds hardest: 30-year gilt yields reached their highest since the late 1990s.

Equities have held up, but the US market is very concentrated: the seven biggest tech names are roughly a third of the S&P 500. Schroders' own economists describe a stagflationary tilt without a recession, with global growth around 2.5%, and name a spike in long-term yields as the main risk.

For clients, I'd focus on three things: diversifying away from index concentration, the higher yields now available in good-quality credit, and active management where dispersion is high. UK savers are defensive right now, holding cash and bonds. That money is there to win when rates turn."

Sources: [S232] Bank of England; [S242] Schroders Q3 viewpoint; [S241] concentration; [S237] gilt yields [Reported], approximate; [S240] conflict background [Reported]; [S248, S250] UK flows.

Don't say this

"Rates are coming down." Both the Bank of England and the Fed had a tightening bias in September 2026 [S232, S234]. "Rachel Reeves' Budget." The Chancellor is John Healey, and the Autumn Budget is on 28 October 2026 [S247, S246]. "Keith Wade, Schroders' chief economist." The Q3 2026 viewpoint is by David Rees, Head of Global Economics, and colleagues [S242].

7.2 The story cards

1. Nuveen's £9.9bn takeover of Schroders completes on 1 October 2026Must know
Source
Court sanction RNS, 29 Sep 2026 [S75]; Disruption Banking, 25 Sep 2026 [S223]; Global Banking & Finance, 22 Sep 2026 [S228]. Bloomberg's report is PAYWALLED-NOT-READ [S226].
What
A court-approved takeover ("scheme of arrangement") at up to 612p a share. The combined group runs about $2.5trn. Schroders keeps its brand and London HQ, runs standalone for at least a year and is led by Richard Oldfield, who reports to Nuveen CEO William Huffman. Matthew Westerman becomes chair.
For Schroders
Scale, US distribution and a much bigger private markets platform. The H1 statement says institutional fundraising in "longer-dated capabilities" was temporarily lower while the deal was pending [S70].
For the role
Clients will ask what changes for them. Sales needs one calm, consistent message: brand retained, standalone for at least a year, London HQ, broader shelf.
Ask
"After the first standalone year, how do you expect Client Group to bring Nuveen's capabilities, such as private credit or real assets, to UK and European clients?"
2. H1 2026: record assets, profit up 46%, net outflows £8.3bnMust know
Source
Schroders half-year results, 30 Jul 2026 [S70]; Professional Pensions [S221].
What
AUM £867.8bn; adjusted operating profit £459.8m; cost:income 68%; gross inflows £69.3bn; net outflows £8.3bn ex JVs, including one £6.6bn low-margin institutional redemption; intermediary gross inflows up more than 40%.
For Schroders
The cost plan worked. Organic growth is still the weak point, especially institutional equities.
For the role
The gap between gross and net flows is a sales story: winning new money is not enough if existing money leaves.
Ask
"Equities saw £8.7bn of net outflows in H1. Is that performance, a shift to passive, or particular client types?"
3. Benchmark sold to Söderberg & Partners: wealth refocused on CazenoveShould know
Source
Money Marketing, 6 Jul 2026 [S231]; H1 results [S70].
What
Schroders sold its UK advice business to a Swedish group for about £215m and becomes a long-term asset management partner to it. Wealth now centres on Cazenove Capital in the UK and Schroders Wealth Management internationally. Oliver Gregson: "It further sharpens our focus".
For Schroders
A move up-market to high-net-worth families, charities and family offices.
For the role
An example of partnership distribution: one relationship bringing sticky flows.
Ask
"With Benchmark sold, how will Schroders reach UK mass-affluent savers: partners, platforms or active ETFs?"
4. Active ETFs: two fixed income launches as European active ETF inflows break a recordShould know
Source
Schroders release via EuropaWire, 29 Sep 2026 [S230]; QuotedData, 18 Sep 2026 [S251].
What
Schroders listed a CoCo financial credit active ETF and a USD investment grade corporate bond active ETF in London. Its UCITS active ETF range was $4.3bn at 21 September. European active ETFs took $33.9bn in the eight months to August, beating 2025's full-year record.
For Schroders
A cheaper, tradeable wrapper for its active skills that competes for money that would otherwise go to passive.
For the role
Salespeople must explain when a client should use the ETF rather than the fund version of the same strategy.
Ask
"Do active ETFs bring in new clients, or mostly move existing investors into a lower-fee wrapper?"
5. Bank of England holds at 3.75%, three votes to raiseMust know
Source
Bank of England, 17 Sep 2026 [S232].
What
A 6-3 vote to hold. CPI 3.1% in August, expected slightly above 4% in Q1 2027. Brent about $106 at the meeting. GDP +0.4% in Q2; unemployment 4.9%. Next decision 5 November.
For Schroders
Higher-for-longer rates favour cash and bond products and hurt long-duration assets.
For the role
Clients ask whether to lock in yields now. You explain the house view; you don't give advice.
Ask
"If the Bank raises in November, how do conversations differ between a DB pension client and a wealth client?"
6. The Fed raises rates to 3.75 to 4.00%Good to know
Source
Federal Reserve statement, 16 Sep 2026 [S234]; CNBC [S235]. Both search summaries only [Reported].
What
A 25bp rise, reported as the first in more than three years, driven by oil-led inflation. Schroders' economists had forecast the Fed would start raising [S242].
For the role
Higher US rates weigh on expensive growth stocks and support the dollar.
Ask
"With the Fed raising and the Bank of England holding, is there a case for sterling bonds for global clients?"
7. Global bond sell-off: 30-year gilt yields at their highest since 1998Should know
Source
Bloomberg, 1 Sep 2026, PAYWALLED-NOT-READ [S236]; Trading Economics summary [S237] [Reported], levels approximate.
What
Long-dated government bonds sold off on budget and debt worries; UK 30-year yields reached about 5.7 to 5.9%. A new Chancellor, John Healey, delivers the Autumn Budget on 28 October [S247, S246].
For Schroders
Gilt yields drive DB pension funding levels and LDI collateral. Schroders runs large UK solutions and LDI books.
For the role
Better-funded DB schemes move towards buyout, which changes what institutional clients need.
Ask
"With long gilt yields near 6%, do more DB schemes lock in and buy out, and what does that mean for Schroders Solutions?"
8. UK savers go defensive: cash and bonds in, equities outShould know
Source
Investment Association, 6 Aug 2026 [S248]; Calastone, 8 Sep 2026 [S249, S250].
What
UK retail funds took £12.3bn in H1 2026: fixed income +£4.5bn, money market +£3.8bn, mixed asset +£7.4bn, equities -£7.0bn. Trackers took £9.7bn and active funds £2.6bn. Calastone reports equity outflows in 14 of the last 15 months.
For Schroders
Its own H1 flows match: multi-asset +£2.6bn, fixed income +£1.9bn, equities -£8.7bn [S70].
For the role
This is the demand picture intermediary sales works within.
Ask
"Money market funds keep gathering cash. Can Schroders win it into short-dated credit or multi-asset income as rates peak?"
9. The oil shock and AI concentration: the two big market risksShould know
Source
Yahoo Finance, 24 Sep 2026 [S238]; Motley Fool, 27 Sep 2026 [S241]; conflict background from reference sources [S240] [Reported].
What
A US-Israel conflict with Iran from late February 2026 disrupted the Strait of Hormuz; Brent traded around $99 to $104 in late September. The seven largest US tech stocks are about 31% of S&P 500 value but about 26% of expected earnings. Sources differ on the exact share, so say "roughly a third".
For the role
Schroders' 2026 institutional survey reports concentration risk as a leading reason investors add active management [S244] [Reported].
Ask
"Is concentration risk the strongest argument for active management in a decade?"
10. Schroders' house view: inflation is the bigger worryMust know
Source
Schroders Economic and Strategy Viewpoint Q3 2026, 16 Sep 2026 [S242].
What
Authors David Rees, George Brown, Tina Fong and Luke James. Global GDP about 2.5% (2026) and 2.6% (2027); inflation 3.3% and 2.7%. "The biggest threat to risk assets is that question marks over central bank credibility cause long term yields to surge higher."
For the role
Quoting the house view accurately, with its date, is the easiest way to sound like you already work there.
Ask
"How does the economics team's view reach salespeople before client meetings?"
11. Pension Schemes Act 2026: megafunds, value for money, six LGPS poolsShould know
Source
LCP, May 2026 [S183]; The Actuary, 7 May 2026 [S254] [Reported].
What
Royal Assent on 29 April 2026. DC default "megafunds" of at least £25bn by 2030; a value for money framework; small pots consolidation; all LGPS money invested through six pools; a reserve power to mandate private market allocations, capped at 10% (5% UK).
For the role
Fewer, bigger, more expert buyers. Mandates get larger and fees get tighter.
Ask
"Does Schroders sell to megafunds as a specialist manager or as a partner building the whole portfolio?"
12. Retail rules: targeted support live, CCI coming, cash ISA cut in 2027Should know
Source
FCA PS25/22 [S170] and PS25/20 [S168]; Professional Adviser [S253]; GOV.UK [S187].
What
Targeted support went live on 6 April 2026 with a "slow start". CCI product summaries became optional on 6 April 2026 and are mandatory from 8 June 2027. The cash ISA limit falls to £12,000 for under-65s from 6 April 2027.
For the role
New routes for savers into funds, new partners for asset managers, and a rewrite of every retail fund document.
Ask
"Should an asset manager help platform partners with ready-made solutions for targeted support, or stay out of it?"
