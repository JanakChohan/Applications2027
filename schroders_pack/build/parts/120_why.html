Part 12

"Why Schroders" in three lengths, and the counter-case

An answer built from how the firm works, not adjectives. It could not be moved to a rival without changing the facts. Rebuild it in your own words.

12.1 Thirty seconds

Say this, in your words

"Three reasons. First, the structure: Schroders puts sales, product and marketing into one Client Group, and last year refocused it on fewer core propositions, so one team decides what to sell and takes it to clients fast. That's the part of asset management I want to learn. Second, the economics: margins run from about 6 basis points in core solutions to 57 in private markets, so what Client Group sells matters as much as how much. Third, the moment: with Nuveen completing this week, the same team will be selling a much bigger public-to-private shelf from London."

12.2 Two minutes

  1. Structure (30 seconds). Schroders has three businesses, Public Markets, Schroders Capital and Wealth, and one Client Group that owns sales, client service, product and marketing across regions [S71, S306]. In 2025 it "designed a new sales and marketing model where central support is focused on fewer core propositions" [S72].
  2. Mechanism (30 seconds). That maps onto the three areas in the JD:
    • Value Proposition chooses and shapes propositions with the investment teams.
    • Sales Activation takes them to clients locally.
    • Delivery keeps the data and CRM running [S263].
    Centralising means a pension scheme sees one Schroders, not three. Two examples: core solutions took £8.1bn of net inflows from fiduciary and outsourced CIO work, and the Mercer LTAF brought private assets into a DC pension default [S71].
  3. Economics (20 seconds). Private markets earn about 57bp and core solutions about 6bp [S71]. H1 2026 showed why that matters. The headline net outflow was mostly one low-margin mandate, while intermediary gross sales rose more than 40% [S70].
  4. Honesty (20 seconds). Active management is under pressure. Most European active equity funds lag passive over ten years [S319]. That's why the firm is concentrating on credit, private markets, solutions and wealth, where active has an edge.
  5. Moment (20 seconds). From 1 October Schroders is part of a roughly $2.5trn group. London becomes the non-US headquarters, the brand stays, and it runs standalone for at least a year [S76]. Client Group's job gets bigger.
  6. Close (20 seconds). The specific thing below.

12.3 The close: one specific thing you want to work on

Choose one and read up on it before the day:

  • The Mercer LTAF mandate (December 2025). A Value Proposition plus Sales Activation case study: a product co-designed with a client that takes private markets into UK DC pensions [S71]. "What I'd most like to learn is how a proposition like that goes from an idea with the investment team to a signed mandate, and what the sales team hears that shapes the next one."
  • European active ETFs. $4.3bn by September 2026, with two fixed income launches on 29 September [S230]. "I'd like to understand how the intermediary team decides whether a client should use the ETF or the fund."
  • UK Institutional's partnership model. The team is being repositioned "around long-term strategic partnerships" for fewer, larger pension pots [S23]. "I'd like to see how selling changes when the buyer is a £25bn megafund."
