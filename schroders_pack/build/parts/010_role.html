Part 1

The role, decoded line by line, and the team mapped

What a Sales intern in Schroders' Client Group actually does, who they work with, how they are judged, which rules bind them, and a reconstruction of the team they sit in.

1.1 The seat in one paragraph

You would join the London office at 1 London Wall Place for ten weeks, from Monday 14 June to Friday 20 August 2027, on £37,000 pro rata [S263]. Your formal department is "Sales (within Client Group)" [S2]. Client Group is the part of Schroders that sits between the investment teams and the clients: it decides what to offer, takes it to clients and keeps the data and processes running [S263]. You get introductory training, a live project, shadowing, a graduate buddy, "Coffee Hours" with senior leaders and the chance of a place on the 2028 graduate programme "subject to performance feedback" [S263]. Schroders calls the internship "the key feeder to our graduate programme" but says a place "isn't guaranteed" [S260].

1.4 How work moves through the function

The hub logic explains a lot about the seat. Ten parties who all talk to each other need up to 45 separate links. Put one team in the middle and they need ten. That is why a Madrid intern routes requests "to the London HQ" [S8], and why interns in London spend so much time routing, checking and recording rather than pitching.

1.6 What the team is judged on

  • Net new business. The headline measure. +£11.2bn in 2025; -£8.3bn in H1 2026 excluding joint ventures [S71, S70].
  • Gross sales. £69.3bn in H1 2026; intermediary gross inflows up more than 40% year on year [S70].
  • Mix and margin. The firm itself contrasts "higher-margin intermediary" inflows with a "low-margin institutional" redemption [S70].
  • Retention. Average client duration rose from 4.3 to 4.6 years in 2025 [S72].
  • Sales effectiveness. "Sales effectiveness was a central topic of the Client Group transformation", and the remuneration committee discussed linking performance ratings to pay [S72].
  • Engagement. "client engagements increasing by more than 30% year on year" in 2025 [S71].
  • Client experience. Oomen is "Responsible for client experience" [S306]. No specific satisfaction metric was found.

1.7 The compliance dimension

Every piece of paper that leaves Client Group is regulated. Part 6.4 gives the full table. The bright lines for an intern are these:

Bright lineRule behind it
Never send new or changed client material before it is approved.Communications must be "fair, clear and not misleading" (COBS 4.2.1R) [S401]. UK intermediary material carries the label "for professional clients or advisers only" [S27].
Never guess performance, forecast returns or promise recovery.COBS 4; Consumer Duty's consumer understanding outcome [S401, S161].
Know whether the audience is professional or retail.Pension funds and central banks are "per se professional" clients under COBS 3.5.2R; the end clients of a wealth adviser are usually retail [S39].
Keep hospitality modest and recorded.Acceptable minor benefits include "hospitality of a reasonable de minimis value, such as food and drink during a business meeting" (COBS 2.3A.19R) [S40].
Never repeat, forward or trade on price-sensitive information.UK MAR: insider dealing and unlawful disclosure [S402]. Schroders plc was a listed issuer until 2 October 2026 [S75].
Keep client personal data accurate and inside approved systems.UK GDPR accuracy and security principles [S403].
Behave to the conduct rules.SM&CR conduct rules; the FCA's PS26/6 reforms took effect mostly from 24 April 2026 [S43].
Say this unprompted, once

"In a sales seat I'd expect anything I send a client to be fair, clear and not misleading, approved, and right for the audience, whether professional or retail. If I'm unsure, I'd ask before I send, not after."

1.8 A realistic career path

The graduate programme lasts two years with one global intake a year, usually in mid-September; graduates then take "a permanent role in the team that best suits your skills and interests" [S15]. Graduate hiring covers "Client Group (including Sales, Marketing, Product)" [S16]. All graduates sit a Level 4 qualification (IMC), and "the majority of our graduates in front office roles (such as ... Client Group) will go on to be supported to study for and sit the CFA qualification (Level 1)" [S15]. Past Client Group graduate adverts described rotations across Sales, Client Service, Product and Marketing [S52] [Reported].

Titles in live postings include Sales Support Specialist, Relationship Manager, Client Manager and Client Director [S7, S12]. A typical ladder of analyst, associate, then director is common in the industry but is not published by Schroders [Inferred].

1.9 The team, mapped

Schroders publishes no Client Group org chart. The chart below is reconstructed from the executive committee page, appointment releases and live job postings. The set of open roles is an org chart in disguise.

Headcount: an estimate, with the method shown

Schroders discloses headcount by segment only: 6,110 average employees in 2025, of whom 4,801 in Asset Management, 1,288 in Wealth and 21 at group level [S73]. Client Group is inside Asset Management. Two methods [Inferred]:

  • Ratio. Distribution, product and marketing are commonly 20 to 30% of an active manager's asset management staff (an industry rule of thumb, not sourced). Applied to 4,300 to 4,800 people gives about 900 to 1,400.
  • Bottom-up. About 36 locations, most with 5 to 20 client-facing staff (300 to 500), plus hubs in London, New York, Zurich, Singapore, Hong Kong and Luxembourg with 100 to 300 each (600 to 900). Total about 900 to 1,400.
Estimate

Client Group is roughly 1,000 to 1,200 people worldwide (range 900 to 1,400), of whom perhaps 300 to 450 in London. Order of magnitude only [Inferred].

How the work splits

  • By channel. Intermediary AUM £157.5bn, net inflows +£3.3bn in H1 2026; Institutional AUM £478.6bn, net outflows £14.1bn [S70].
  • By region. 2025 management fees by client location: UK £974.9m, Continental Europe and Middle East £870.2m, Asia Pacific £557.4m, Americas £343.3m [S72].
  • UK sub-channels. Intermediary splits into Advisory; Discretionary and Wealth Managers; Investment Trusts; and a Business Development Desk [S27]. UK Institutional is being repositioned "around long-term strategic partnerships" for "fewer, but far larger, pots of pension capital" [S23].
  • Overlays. Schroders Capital's private markets sales team [S71]; strategic partnerships and Global Financial Clients under Karine Szenberg [S306]; client service hubs [S72].

Where the team sits, and who owns it

Client Group reports to the Group CEO through Matt Oomen, who joined from BNY Investments, where he was global head of distribution [S91, S306]. Karine Szenberg, who led Client Group before him, is now Executive Vice Chair [S306]. From 1 October 2026 the chain continues up to Nuveen: Richard Oldfield "will report to William Huffman, CEO, Nuveen" [S76].

Are your interviewers on this team?

Schroders says assessors are trained and use "an assessment skills framework" [S262]. Candidate reports describe an interview with someone from early careers plus a manager from the business area; one says "two members of the team you have applied for" [S49, S288] [Reported]. Expect at least one practitioner from UK Client Group at the assessment centre. Part 10 covers the people.
