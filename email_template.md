# Cold-email template — speculative internship

Brief (4 sentences), states intent plainly, and asks directly about an internship for a set timeframe. The `[bracketed]` bits map to columns in `targets.csv` so you can copy-paste per firm.

## Placeholders → CSV columns
- `[Contact]` → `contact_name` (use first name)
- `[Firm]` → `company_name`
- `[what they do]` → `personalisation_hook` (written to drop straight in after the dash; lowercase its first word if you like)
- `[timeframe]` → you fill in, e.g. "4–6 weeks this summer" or "September–December 2026"
- send to → `contact_email` (🟢 published = safest; ⚪ generic inbox = open with "FAO [Contact]")

---

## Template

**Subject:** Speculative internship enquiry — [Your name]

Dear [Contact],

I've recently finished at Pictet Asset Management and am keen to understand more about what you do at [Firm] — [what they do]. I'm writing to ask directly whether you'd consider taking on an intern for [timeframe]. I'd bring hands-on asset-management exposure and strong analytical and Excel skills, and would gladly work around your team's schedule. If that's something you're open to, I'll send my CV straight over.

Best regards,
[Your name]
[phone] · [email]

## Worked example (Barratt & Cooke, Norwich)

**Subject:** Speculative internship enquiry — Janak Chohan

Dear Sam,

I've recently finished at Pictet Asset Management and am keen to understand more about what you do at Barratt & Cooke — one of the UK's oldest independent stockbrokers and private-client investment managers, with c.£1.5bn under management in Norwich. I'm writing to ask directly whether you'd consider taking on an intern for 4–6 weeks this summer. I'd bring hands-on asset-management exposure and strong analytical and Excel skills, and would gladly work around your team's schedule. If that's something you're open to, I'll send my CV straight over.

Best regards,
Janak Chohan
[phone] · janak.chohan26@gmail.com

---

### Tips
- Keep it to these 4 sentences — the direct ask is the point; don't bury it.
- Swap the "I'd bring…" sentence for whatever's true for you (languages, modelling, a relevant module/dissertation).
- For firms with only a generic inbox, open "Dear Sir/Madam (FAO [Contact])" so it still reaches the right person.
- Double-check the person is still at the firm before sending (regional teams change often).
