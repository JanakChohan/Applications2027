The eight frame-breakers, countered

Interruption stop, answer, then reclaim. Contradiction "It may be — here's my source, what am I missing?" Silence stop talking. Escalation principle doesn't move, urgency does. Authority separate person from request. False choice name a third option. Knowledge trap say you don't know. Badmouth bait generous about people, specific about problems.
