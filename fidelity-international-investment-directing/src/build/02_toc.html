How to read the markers

[S14] points to a numbered row in the source table in Part 15, with outlet, title, date, URL and access date. Confidence is tagged inline: Confirmed means a primary source or two credible outlets agree; Reported means one credible source said it; Inferred means it is reasoning by the author of this pack, not a fact anyone published. Only the first of those is safe to state flatly in an interview.
