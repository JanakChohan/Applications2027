One closing thought

The strongest thing in your Question 1 material is not the story. It is the fact that the redemption wave ran far ahead of the actual credit deterioration. That observation is available to anyone who looks at both the fund filings and the default data, and almost nobody does, because it requires checking whether the stated reason for an event is supported by evidence.

That habit — taking a claim, including a claim made by a company about itself, and testing it against the data before repeating it — is the thing this industry actually pays for. If your answer demonstrates it, the topic almost does not matter.
