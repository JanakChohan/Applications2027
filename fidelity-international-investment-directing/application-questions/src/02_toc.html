Why there is no drafted answer in this document

You asked for the research and not the prose, twice, and you were right to. Three reasons it matters here. The process is strengths-based, which is designed to catch rehearsal. Your written answers are likely to be read back to you at the assessment centre, where you will have to defend every claim in them. And an answer assembled from someone else's sentences is detectable, increasingly screened for, and would not survive the first follow-up question.

What this pack gives you instead: the facts, the causal chain, the counter-arguments, the numbers with their sources, and several different shapes an answer could take. The sentences are yours.
