Two things, mainly.

First, how to explain a private credit return accurately. Reading Ares' fund disclosures, SDL III's levered sleeve shows a 24.6% gross and 17.3% net IRR, but the net MoIC is only 1.1x, so most of that is leverage and deployment timing. Sterling-committed investors see different figures again. I want to get to the point where I can explain that to someone without oversimplifying it or hiding behind it. Working on DDQs would be the fastest way, because they force you to source every claim.

Second, how investors actually decide. With roughly 79% of institutional fundraising coming from existing investors, retention isn't a service function at Ares, it's the growth engine. I want to understand what makes someone commit to a second product.

I'd also rather learn this in a hard year. Explaining a pro-rated tender to a worried client is harder than explaining a good quarter, and I suspect it teaches you more.
