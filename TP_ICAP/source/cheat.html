Mental maths cheat sheet

Do not do the sum you were given. Turn it into easier sums.

The five tricks

1
Round one number, then fix it. For multiplying. 17 × 18: do 17 × 20 = 340. Take off 17 × 2 = 34. 306 14 × 16: do 14 × 10 = 140. Add 14 × 6 = 84. 224
2
Build percentages from 10% and 1%. Move the decimal point, then add the pieces. 15% of 480: 10% is 48. 5% is half of that, 24. 72 16% of 16: 10% is 1.6. 5% is 0.8. 1% is 0.16. 2.56
3
Flip the percentage if that is easier. 16% of 25 is the same as 25% of 16. 25% of 16 is a quarter of 16. 4
4
Halve one number and double the other. Good with 5, 25 or 50. 16 × 25 becomes 8 × 50, then 4 × 100. 400 23 × 5: do 23 × 10 = 230, then halve it. 115
5
Count up instead of subtracting. 4.62 minus 4.15: count from 4.15 up to 4.62. 0.47 Middle of 4.20 and 4.26: the gap is 6, half is 3. 4.23
Two facts to memorise
  • One basis point, or one pip, on one million is 100. So on 30 million it is 3,000.
  • Fractions as percentages: a half is 50%. A quarter is 25%. A fifth is 20%. A tenth is 10%. A twentieth is 5%.
How to answer out loud
  1. Repeat the question. It buys two seconds.
  2. Say your steps. They are judging calm, not just the answer.
  3. Biggest part first. Tens before units.
  4. If you slip, correct it calmly. "Sorry, 306, not 316."
