Why this helps your application: all three of your workstreams share a thesis. As AI makes information and output abundant, the human value shifts to understanding clients, judging what's true and earning trust. You can express that idea differently in each answer, using each industry's specific evidence, and it will read as a coherent, informed point of view.

Worked example (IM): Trend: UK pension consolidation. View: "Megafunds will make institutional distribution more consultative and more concentrated." Evidence: L&G and Fidelity defaults passed £25bn; DC scheme numbers fell 15% while assets rose 22%. Counter: scale could commoditise managers into low-fee passive suppliers. Change my mind: if megafunds bring most management in-house. So what: client-facing staff need deep product and private-markets knowledge, not just relationships.

The "one sentence for your answer" boxes at the end of each industry (B13, C10, D10) are ready-made starting points. Make them your own, and never stack several trends in one answer.
A note on accuracy: these are fast-moving stories. Before quoting a figure in an interview, check the latest version: deals complete or collapse, and forecasts change. Items marked "secondary" or "unverified" should be checked against the original before you quote them as fact. If you're unsure, say "around" or "reportedly"; that's how professionals hedge too.
