# Harrison Street Asset Management (HSAM): firm research for the interview explainer
Compiled 2026-10-08. Confidence tags: [Confirmed] means a primary source (company release, SEC/Colliers filing, Companies House, or the firm's own site) was fetched and read. [Reported] means quality press or a third party, or a search snippet only. [Inferred] means my own deduction.

## 1. FINDINGS

### 1.1 Founding, founder, insight
- **Founded 2005, Chicago.** [Confirmed] Sources: F2 (Colliers 6-K, May 2018: "Founded 2005"), F6 ("Since inception in 2005..."). First fund-series capital raised from 2006: F6 says "Since inception in 2006, Harrison Street has raised an aggregate of $30 billion in equity". Wikipedia reports the first fund, Harrison Real Estate Partners I, raised $75m in 2006 [Reported, F30 snippet].
- **Founder: Christopher Merrill**, Co-Founder. His current titles are "Co-Founder, Chairman and Global CEO" (F14 Abu Dhabi release, June 2025) and "Co-Founder and Global CEO" (F1, F20). Form ADV lists him as "GLOBAL CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER" from 12/2011 and as elected manager of Harrison Street Real Estate Capital, LLC from 09/2005 [Confirmed, F11].
- **Co-founders and seed capital:** Christopher Galvin (former Motorola CEO) and his brother Michael Galvin. Merrill: "We started as a 50-50 partnership. They owned half the business, I owned the other half" [Confirmed via firm repost of the Commercial Observer interview, F4/F5]. Wikipedia and Crain's (2016) also name Galvin [Reported, F30/F31 snippets].
- **Prior career:** Heitman (Chicago), which he joined as a summer intern before working in acquisitions. He launched Heitman's first foreign investment fund, became a partner and managing director, and "moved to London in 1997 with the idea of building a business around Central European real estate" [Confirmed F5 repost; Reported F30]. MBA from City University London's Cass Business School, now Bayes [Reported, F30/F31 snippets].
- **Founding insight:** "Let's not necessarily be a macro-investor, but a demand investor." He wanted to be "the first firm that was a pure-play investor around alternative real estate" (F4/F5). Wikipedia paraphrases this as "noncyclical events made the best investment catalysts" [Reported F30]. The firm's boilerplate describes "differentiated investment solutions focused on demographic-driven, needs-based assets" (F6, F7). In 2018 Colliers described it as "one of the largest real estate investment firms dedicated to the education, healthcare and storage sectors" (F2). Sectors now covered: senior housing, student housing, build-to-rent, healthcare delivery (medical office), life sciences, storage, data centres, and social, utility and digital infrastructure (F6).

### 1.3 AUM history, headcount, offices
See the key numbers table below.
- **AUM:** about $14.6bn (May 2018, F2). About $55bn (July 2023, F7; Oct 2024, F6). About $56bn (June 2025, F14). Over $100bn after the rebrand on July 23, 2025, which combined Colliers' investment management businesses (F1, F15). **$108.2bn at Dec 31, 2025**, of which fee-paying AUM was $54.2bn and 86% sat in perpetual or long-dated vehicles (Colliers FY2025 40-F, F3). $109bn (May to Aug 2026 releases, F12/F17/F10). **$110bn (Sept 9, 2026 release, F8)**.
  - **Important:** The jump from about $56bn to over $100bn in July 2025 came from consolidating Colliers' investment management arms under the HS brand, not from organic growth. Merrill: "It wasn't really a jump as much as it was a coming together" (F4/F5) [Confirmed].
  - The current London ISG intern posting uses "over $108 billion" and "more than 520 professionals", which matches the JD (F21) [Confirmed]. These figures appear to be year-end 2025 numbers. The firm's newest boilerplate says $110bn and over 600 employees (F8) [Confirmed].
- **Headcount:** over 250 (2023, F7). Over 280 (Oct 2024, F6). Over 290 (June 2025, F14). 620 in Colliers' Investment Management segment at FY2025, made up of 380 professional staff plus 240 executive, management and admin (F3). "More than 520 professionals" (F21). "Over 600" (2026 releases, F8/F12/F17). The Form ADV for the main US adviser shows **321 employees, 225 in advisory roles** (F11) [Confirmed].
- **Offices:** HQ in **Chicago, Toronto and London** (F8). Earlier releases said "Chicago and Toronto" (F1) or "Chicago and London" (F6, F14). Other offices are across North America, Europe, Asia and the Middle East. The ADV lists Chicago (principal) plus 10 other offices, including Toronto, Washington DC, San Francisco, New York, Madrid, Seoul, London (20 St James's Street, SW1A 1ES) and Luxembourg (F11) [Confirmed]. Commercial Observer also mentions Tokyo, Singapore and Abu Dhabi (F4). HSAM Europe has offices in London, Berlin, Geneva, Jersey, Luxembourg, Madrid and Munich (F12).
- **Client counts:** "over 1,100 institutional investors and over 300 Registered Investment Advisors" (F8, F10, F12). The May 2026 release said "over 1,200 institutional investors" (F17), an inconsistency. In July 2025 the RoundShield release said "approximately 900 institutional investors and over 10,000 private wealth investors" (F16).

### 1.4 Corporate history with Colliers (verified)
- **14 May 2018:** Colliers announced it would acquire **75% of Harrison Street from its founders for $450m**, plus "an additional $100 million payable in 2022" tied to accelerated performance targets. Management kept 25%. AUM was about $14.6bn. Hennick: "The investment in Harrison Street is transformational and the most significant in our history" (F2) [Confirmed]. Some press gives "$550m", which includes the earn-out (F4, Bisnow headline). Expected close was Q3 2018. The ADV shows Colliers Investment Management Holdings as indirect owner from 07/2018, so the deal closed in July 2018 (F11) [Confirmed].
- **Colliers' other investment management deals** (F3 Colliers FY2025 40-F) [Confirmed]:
  - ICADE, 2016 (EUR 2bn in Europe)
  - Antirion, April 2022, later renamed Colliers Global Investors
  - **Basalt Infrastructure Partners**, June 2022 (75% per CoStar [Reported])
  - **Rockwood Capital**, July 2022 (65% [Reported])
  - **Versus Capital**, Oct 2022 (75% [Reported])
  - **RoundShield Partners**, July 2025
- **23 July 2025, rebrand:** "Colliers... announced the rebranding of its Investment Management division to Harrison Street Asset Management ('Harrison Street' or 'HSAM')." HSAM draws on Basalt, Versus Capital, Rockwood Capital and Colliers Global Investors. Merrill was named Global CEO, Michaud Global CFO and Gordon Global COO. AUM was over $100bn (F1, F15) [Confirmed].
- **30 July 2025:** HSAM acquired a **60% equity interest in RoundShield Partners**, a European credit manager with $5.4bn AUM. RoundShield was founded in 2013 and is based in Jersey, with offices in Geneva, London, Madrid and Munich. Terms were not disclosed (F16) [Confirmed].
- **Oct 2025:** The ADV shows "Harrison Street Asset Management ULC" became an indirect owner between Colliers International Group Inc. and Colliers Investment Management Holdings (F11) [Confirmed]. This is a new holding entity.
- **Ownership now:** Colliers' economic interest in HSAM is **82%**. The subsidiary table lists Harrison Street Real Estate Capital, LLC at 77% (F3) [Confirmed; the two figures measure different things].
- **Late July 2025:** The Versus Capital interval funds and adviser were renamed. Versus Capital Real Assets Fund LLC became **Harrison Street Real Assets Fund LLC** (effective 28 July 2025). Versus Capital Real Estate Fund LLC became **Harrison Street Real Estate Fund LLC** (28 July 2025). Versus Capital Infrastructure Income Fund became **Harrison Street Infrastructure Income Fund**. Versus Capital Advisors LLC became **Harrison Street Private Wealth LLC**, located in Denver (F22 SEC filings snippet [Reported]; IAPD API lists HARRISON STREET PRIVATE WEALTH LLC with former names VERSUS CAPITAL ADVISORS [Confirmed F11b]).
- **20 July 2026:** Harrison Street Europe was integrated into RoundShield, and the combined platform was rebranded **HSAM Europe**. It has over $10bn AUM and more than 90 staff (F12) [Confirmed].
- **Colliers segment (FY2025):** Investment Management revenue $532.3m (10% of Colliers). Adjusted EBITDA $214.8m, a 40.4% margin (F3) [Confirmed].

### 1.5 Strategies and fund list
- **Open-end core (flagship): Harrison Street Core Property Fund, L.P.**, plus parallel vehicles A to E. It launched in Nov 2011 and was described as the industry's first open-end core fund focused on student housing, senior housing, MOB and storage [Reported, F34 IREI snippet]. **Gross asset value $15.12bn per the ADV filed 31 Mar 2026** (F11) [Confirmed]. Commercial Observer: the flagship core fund is "about $15 billion" and is 100% alternatives (F4).
- **Other ADV-listed private funds** (F11) [Confirmed]:
  - **Harrison Street Infrastructure Fund, L.P.** (f/k/a Harrison Street Social Infrastructure Fund), GAV $3.22bn
  - **Harrison Street Canada Alternative Real Estate Fund, L.P.** (plus a trust parallel), GAV $1.16bn
  - The RAUM of Harrison Street Advisors, LLC is $20.18bn across 12 accounts. The closed-end series are evidently not reported under this registrant, which is a gap.
- **Closed-end US opportunistic: Harrison Street Real Estate Partners series.**
  - Fund VIII closed in 2021 at its $2.0bn hard cap, or $2.5bn including co-invest [Reported, F35 BusinessWire headline].
  - **Fund IX** final close Oct 7, 2024 at **about $2.5bn including co-invest vehicles**, with over $7bn buying power. Existing investors supplied about 60%. Around 70% was allocated across 70 assets, with the largest exposures in student housing, senior housing and data centres. PM is Rob Cook (F6) [Confirmed]. PERE had reported a $3bn target and $4bn hard cap [Reported, snippet].
  - **Fund X (HSREP X):** launched April 2025 per SEC filings. **$1bn first close after about one month on the road, against a $3bn hard cap** (PERE podcast, May 30, 2025, F23) [Reported]. **No final close found** (gap).
- **Europe: European Property Partners series.**
  - EPP II: "EXCLUSIVE: Harrison Street raises EUR700m for second European fund" (headline only) [Reported, snippet].
  - **EPP III:** final close Feb 2022 at **over EUR800m from more than 25 institutional investors**, the firm's largest European fund at the time. CalSTRS committed EUR150m [Reported, F26/F27 snippets of BusinessWire 2022-02-10 and IREI]. A firm fund table reportedly lists EUR655m [Reported, unverified].
  - **EPP IV:** launched Aug 2023 with a **EUR1.5bn target** (PERE, 23 Aug 2023: "With a target of EUR1.5bn, the vehicle is the third-largest Europe-focused fundraise announced this year", F28) [Confirmed headline/teaser; body paywalled]. It targets a 15%+ net IRR [Reported]. **No final close found** (gap).
- **Infrastructure (about $30bn platform AUM per Aug 2026 release, F10)** [Confirmed]:
  - **Core Infrastructure Strategy**, launched 2018 ($6.3bn portfolio, as of June 30, 2026; F29). This is the former Social Infrastructure Fund, now Harrison Street Infrastructure Fund.
  - **Infrastructure Closed-End Fund Series (Basalt)**, launched 2011: mid-market transport, power, utilities and digital in North America and Europe, "over $9 billion in gross investments across five main funds" (F29).
  - **HS Digital Fund:** first dedicated digital infrastructure fund, about $600m final close in Dec 2024, announced Jan 2025 [Reported, F36 snippet].
  - Infrastructure debt and real assets strategy for private wealth (F10).
- **Credit:** RoundShield (European asset-backed credit and private capital solutions). **HSAM European Private Debt Fund**: evergreen senior secured real-estate-backed lending across Western Europe; first loan was for a UK hotel (F12) [Confirmed].
- **Wealth / RIA channel (Harrison Street Private Wealth, ex-Versus, Denver):**
  - Interval funds: Harrison Street Real Assets Fund LLC (ex-VCRRX), Harrison Street Real Estate Fund LLC and Harrison Street Infrastructure Income Fund (F22) [Reported via SEC filing snippets].
  - **Harrison Street Infrastructure Active ETF (NFRX)**, the firm's first ETF, started trading on Nasdaq on 2 Feb 2026 and invests in listed utilities, midstream, digital and transport. Sources conflict on the adviser: one says Tidal, with sub-advice from Harrison Street Real Assets Managers [Reported, F37 snippets].
  - I could not verify a vehicle named "Harrison Street Real Estate Income Fund". The former "Versus Capital Multi-Manager Real Estate Income Fund" is now Harrison Street Real Estate Fund LLC (F22).
- **Other HSAM platforms:** Rockwood Capital (US real estate, New York) and Colliers Global Investors (ex-Antirion) (F1, F3).

### 1.8 Investor Solutions Group (ISG)
- "Investor Solutions Group" is HSAM's name for its IR and capital-raising function. Its stated focus is "global capital formation and client service, deepening relationships with institutional investors." It has **50 professionals worldwide** (F8, Sept 2026) [Confirmed]. Until about mid-2025 it was called "Investor Relations": the Sept 2022 release was headed "Harrison Street Continues Expansion of Investor Relations Group With Three New Senior Hires", when the team "exceeded 20 professionals" (F33 snippet). In June 2025 Sheehan's title was "Head of Investor Relations – North America" [Reported snippet]. **I found no rename announcement**; the date of the rename is [Inferred] as mid-to-late 2025.
- **Sub-teams:** Investor Services (an "Analyst – Investor Services" posting says Investor Services is "part of the Investor Solutions Group" and reports to the Global Head of Investor Services [Reported snippet]), Marketing, consultant relations and investor analytics.
- **Role titles seen publicly** (professional only): Global Co-Head; Co-Head US ISG; Co-Head Asia ISG; Head of ISG – Europe; Head of IR – Middle East; Managing Director (infrastructure specialist); Associate/Senior Associate – Investor Solutions; Analyst – Investor Services; ISG Intern.
