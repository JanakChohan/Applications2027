What this does and does not do

  • It listens, it does not film. Your speech is turned into text and timed. If you want to watch yourself back, film on your phone at the same time.
  • It does not judge your face. HireVue's own scoring reads only your words, and a human marker is checking what you said against the question.
  • It checks the thing that fails most often: whether each part of the question got an answer, and whether you gave the kind of answer it asked for.
  • Your answers are saved on this page so you can review them, and so they can be looked at with you in a coaching session.
  • The marks are a practice aid. No firm publishes its marking sheet, so they cannot predict a result.
