# WS1B: The KCS team in London / EMEA, and how the six internship teams link to it

Workstream WS1B. Source IDs S140 to S199. Research date: 11 October 2026.

How to read this file. Each claim has a source tag like [S150] and a confidence tag:
- [Confirmed] = primary source (KKR's own website, KKR filings, Companies House accounts) or two credible outlets.
- [Reported] = one credible secondary source.
- [Inferred] = my own reasoning from the sources. Treat these as estimates.

Method note (important). KKR does not publish an org chart. But its public "Our People" directory on kkr.com runs on a searchable data feed. I queried that feed directly for every EMEA city and for the Global Client Solutions team, and pulled 238 EMEA biographies and 232 global KCS biographies [S149]. The directory lists only Principal, Director, Managing Director and Partner level people. Analysts and Associates are not listed. I then read each EMEA KCS biography [S150 to S157]. This is the backbone of the org chart below. I also pulled KKR's live job postings (KKR uses Greenhouse, not Workday; the Workday address in the brief returns errors) [S140, S141], KKR's UK statutory accounts on Companies House [S171, S172], and KKR's latest SEC filings [S174, S175].

## 1. Findings

### 1.1 What KCS is, in KKR's own current words

- KKR's live job posting for a 2027 KCS off-cycle intern (Frankfurt, posted 8 Oct 2026) describes KCS like this: KCS is "KKR's dedicated investor solutions team", "first formed in 2009", and "consists of over 300 executives located in over 20 [offices] across four continents" [S141] [Confirmed].
- The same posting says KCS is "primarily organized around two specialized focus areas: Sales/Relationship Management and Product/Strategy Management" [S141] [Confirmed]. This matches the brief's description (relationship managers, product specialists, junior generalists).
- KCS "seeks to 'connect the dots' across all businesses at KKR, from our Private Equity investing teams to Infrastructure, Real Estate, Credit, Capital Markets and Insurance-Funded Strategies" [S141] [Confirmed]. This is the single best sentence for explaining how the six internship teams link to KCS.
- The long-term goal is "to diversify KKR's client base to include new investors, across all regions and institution types, and to raise capital for the Firm's expanding product platform" [S141] [Confirmed].
- KCS has three client channels, visible in the bios and on kkr.com: (1) Institutional investors (pensions, sovereign wealth funds, insurers, endowments, consultants); (2) Family Capital (single-family offices, founders, entrepreneurs; business set up in 2014 [S178]); (3) Global Wealth Solutions, "GWS" (private banks and wealth platforms that sell to wealthy individuals) [S144, S150, S157, S195] [Confirmed].
- Global KCS leaders (all New York unless stated), from the KKR directory [S149] [Confirmed]:
  - Eric Mogelof, Partner, Head of Global Client Solutions.
  - Amy Lesch, Partner, Head of Global Product Strategy.
  - Jim Burns, Partner, Head of Institutional Client Solutions, Americas.
  - Doug Brody, Partner, Head of Americas and EMEA Family Capital.
  - Darryl Herrick, MD, Head of Global Insurance Client Solutions.
  - Markus Egloff (London), MD, Head of Global Wealth Solutions, International.
- KKR's firm scale (useful context): $796 billion AUM at 30 June 2026, of which $220 billion is Global Atlantic insurance [S175] [Confirmed]. About 4,200 employees at end-2025 (about 2,700 in asset management and about 1,500 in insurance), plus about 800 in subsidiaries; about 980 "asset management professionals including those in investments, capital markets, and KKR Capstone operating roles" [S174] [Confirmed]. Investors come from 65 countries [S174] [Confirmed]. K-Series (KKR's evergreen funds for wealthy individuals) AUM was $42 billion at 30 June 2026, up from $34 billion at end-2025 [S174, S175] [Confirmed].

Step 2. Scale up for junior staff. Globally the directory lists 232 KCS people [S149], while KKR's own posting says KCS has "over 300 executives" [S141]. So the directory covers about 75% or less of KCS. EMEA is 46 of 232, about 20%, of senior KCS bios [Inferred].

Step 3. Estimate. 20% of 300+ gives about 60. Adding Analysts and Associates in the same proportion as globally gives roughly 60 to 65. If EMEA has a higher junior ratio (it is actively hiring London analysts [S140] and Frankfurt interns [S141]), or if some Dublin CAPS (RFP/DDQ) staff count toward KCS, the total could be higher.

**Estimate: about 55 to 90 KCS people in EMEA, central estimate about 65, of whom about 20 to 30 are in London** [Inferred, medium-low confidence]. London alone has 20 listed senior people, so London is probably 25 to 35 including juniors [Inferred].

Mix of the 46 listed EMEA KCS people by title [S149] [Confirmed]: 13 Managing Directors (including Markus Egloff), 15 Directors, 18 Principals (my count from the directory feed; Mark Danzey adds one Partner).

### 1.4 How the KCS work splits in EMEA

From the bios and postings [S140, S141, S146, S150 to S157] [Confirmed facts; the framing is Inferred]:

1. **By region (relationship managers).** Each senior RM owns a geography: UK, DACH, France/Benelux/Southern Europe, Nordics, Middle East, CEE.
2. **By channel.** Institutional (pensions, sovereigns, insurers, consultants), Family Capital (single-family offices), Global Wealth Solutions (private banks and wealth platforms). Insurance clients have their own small team (Gerber, Paris).
3. **By product (product specialists).** Private equity (including evergreen K-PRIME / K-PEC), Infrastructure (including Climate Transition), Real Estate, Credit (run largely from Dublin, a legacy of KKR's 2014 purchase of Avoca Capital [S152]), plus product development and product origination.
4. **Support.** CAPS answers RFPs, RFIs and due diligence questionnaires (DDQs) from Dublin and New York [S146]. The new London analyst role covers "investor reporting, fundraising support, KYC and investment onboarding" [S140, S148].

What the junior London KCS role actually involves, from the live posting (EMEA Institutional Client Relations Analyst, posted 7 Oct 2026) [S140] [Confirmed]:
- "a member of the fundraising support and investor relations team for KKR's EMEA based institutional & family capital clients".
- Covers "private equity, infrastructure, real estate, private and liquid credit strategies".
- Tasks: "Managing a variety of reporting requirements for investors"; "Supporting Relationship Managers in fundraising activities"; "Partnering with clients to help with KYC & investment onboarding"; "Collaborating with various internal divisions globally to answer client requests (Product teams, Investment teams, Operations, Finance, Legal, Compliance, Risk)"; "Ongoing servicing of existing clients including delivering investment updates in meetings"; "Regular involvement in event coordination, team budgeting and business planning".
- Skills: "Strong working knowledge of AI-enabled productivity tools"; "Technical and aesthetic mastery of PowerPoint and Excel"; "Additional language fluencies, particularly German or French, a plus".

What the Frankfurt KCS intern does (very close to what a London KCS summer intern would see) [S141] [Confirmed]:
- Support "DACH relationship managers and Associates" on ad-hoc client requests.
- "Coordinate responses to client due diligence requests/RFPs, including underlying portfolio statistics, marketing materials and presentation decks".
- "Support in the organization of external and KKR investor events".
- Work with "strategies teams, client services, the RFP team".
- "Maintain information on the DACH budgets and pipeline".

### 1.8 The six internship teams in Europe and how each links to KCS

General rule: KCS raises money for every fund the investing teams run. Investing teams give KCS the material it sells: track record, deal stories and access to senior investors at client meetings and annual meetings. KCS then brings back what clients want, which shapes new products [S141, S174] [Confirmed facts, Inferred framing].

**Plain-English glossary for a beginner:**
- LP (limited partner): an investor in a KKR fund, for example a pension fund.
- Drawdown fund: a fund where investors promise money that KKR calls over time (European Fund VI, GII V).
- Evergreen / K-Series: funds without a fixed end date that individuals can join more easily (K-PRIME, K-PEC).
- RFP / DDQ: long questionnaires that investors send before committing money; KCS and CAPS answer them.
- Family Capital: KKR's team for very wealthy families and their family offices.
- GWS: Global Wealth Solutions, KKR's team that works with private banks to reach wealthy individuals.
- Co-investment: an investor puts extra money directly into one deal alongside a KKR fund.
