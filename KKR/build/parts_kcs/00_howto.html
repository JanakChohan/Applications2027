The ten-minute emergency version

If you read nothing else, read these ten points. Each is expanded, with sources, later in the pack.

  1. What KKR is. A listed alternative asset manager: it invests clients' money in private equity, private credit, infrastructure and real estate, and owns an insurer, Global Atlantic. $796.5bn of assets under management at 30 June 2026 [S1] [Confirmed].
  2. Who pays KKR. Its clients (limited partners): pensions, sovereign wealth funds, insurers, endowments, family offices and, increasingly, wealthy individuals. They pay management fees and a share of profits (carried interest).
  3. What KCS is. The team that raises and looks after that money: formed in 2009, 300+ people, split into relationship management and product/strategy management [S103, S141] [Confirmed]. KKR's words: it exists to "connect the dots" across all of KKR's businesses [S141].
  4. The three client channels. Institutions, Family Capital and Global Wealth. Private wealth is now 18% of KKR's AUM, ahead of pensions at 14% [S71] [Confirmed]. The wealth products are the evergreen K-Series, which grew from $6bn (2023) to $42bn (June 2026) [S72, S73].
  5. Why KCS works with Capital Markets. KKR can underwrite a deal and syndicate co-investment to clients through KCM; KCS brings the clients [S70]. In Europe one Partner, Mark Danzey, heads both KCM and Institutional Client Solutions [S156] [Confirmed].
  6. Why KKR is built this way. One firm, one P&L, one compensation pool [S5]; employees own about 30% of the stock [S3]. So the client team can sell what fits the client, not what one team needs to fill [Inferred].
  7. The year so far. Record fundraising pace: $305bn raised since the start of 2024 against a $300bn target [S76]; Global Infrastructure Investors V closed at $19.2bn in August 2026, KKR's largest infrastructure fund [S176].
  8. The awkward facts. A proposed $250m DOJ settlement over merger filings (Aug 2026) [S205]; K-FIT paid about 80% of redemption requests in Q1 2026, then 100% in Q2 [S338]; founders' control ends by 31 Dec 2026 [S2]. Know them; state them neutrally.
  9. Where KKR does not lead. Blackstone and Apollo are larger, Blackstone leads private wealth by a wide margin, and in-house capital markets is not unique [S213, S215, S220]. Never overclaim.
  10. Your answer. "KCS is the team that has to understand all of KKR. KKR is built for that seat. I want to learn the whole firm by explaining it honestly to the people who fund it." Then one personal example of explaining something or keeping a commitment.

The rule that matters most

Only say a number in an interview if you can name where it came from. "According to KKR's second-quarter release..." is more impressive than a precise figure without a source, and it protects you if the interviewer has a newer number.
