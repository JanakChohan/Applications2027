KKR KCS cheat sheet: one page, read it in the lift

THE ONE SENTENCE

"KCS is the team that has to understand all of KKR. KKR is built for that seat: one P&L and one compensation pool, Capital Markets beside it, and a client base that now runs from pensions to private wealth. I want to learn the whole firm by explaining it honestly to the people who fund it."

ANSWER PROTOCOL (45 TO 75 SECONDS)

  1. Steady (3s): pause, breathe. "Let me make sure I understand the situation."
  2. Sort (10s): name what is at stake. "There are two things here: X and Y."
  3. Act (30s): what you would do, in order, and who you would tell.
  4. Close (10s): the principle. "Because accuracy and the client's trust come before speed."

PRIORITY ORDER WHEN GOOD THINGS COLLIDE

  1. The rules and KKR's name (accuracy, marketing rules, information walls)
  2. The client's real interest; every client treated fairly
  3. The process and the record (CRM, compliance sign-off)
  4. Speed

Holding line: "I'd rather be a day late than wrong in front of a client."

NEVER SAY

  • "KKR is the biggest" (Blackstone $1.35tn, Apollo $1.05tn; KKR $796bn)
  • "KKR leads private wealth" (Blackstone about $324bn vs K-Series $42bn)
  • "Only KKR has in-house capital markets" (BXCM, Apollo Capital Solutions)
  • "Industrialist vision" as KKR's words (not found in KKR material)
  • Anything bad about a rival, a past employer or a client
  • A number you cannot source
