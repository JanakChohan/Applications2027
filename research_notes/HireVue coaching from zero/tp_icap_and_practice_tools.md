## 1. What TP ICAP is: business model, divisions, figures, strategy, competitors

### Takeaway
TP ICAP is the world's largest interdealer broker by revenue (£2,353m in 2025; £1,912m for the first nine months of 2026, up 9% at constant currency). It earns commission for matching institutional buyers and sellers and does no proprietary trading; about 62% of 2025 revenue came from "name passing", where it is never a counterparty. Global Broking (the ICAP and Tullett Prebon desks at 135 Bishopsgate) is 58% of revenue and the fastest-growing division in 2026.

## 2. What a broker does day to day; qualities desks want; how it differs from sales, trading and banking

### Takeaway
A trainee supports a desk (inputting trades, updating price boards, giving live quotes over chat, resolving trade queries) while learning to talk to traders at banks and funds, find the other side of a trade and agree a price. TP ICAP's own postings stress sociability, rapport, composure under pressure and a view on markets; pay is formula-linked to desk revenue and conduct. Unlike a trader the broker holds no position and takes no market risk.

### Inferences
- Broker versus trader: the trader at a bank or fund owns the position and the profit or loss; the broker finds the counterparty, keeps the client anonymous until terms are agreed and earns commission. Sourced basis: "No proprietary trading" and the name-passing definition above.
- Broker versus bank salesperson: a salesperson distributes their own bank's prices to that bank's clients; an interdealer broker sits between many banks and funds and shows the best price across them. This is my framing from the business-model wording, not a firm quote.
- Broker versus investment banking: IB is project work (deals, models, pitch books); broking is live, flow-based and measured daily in commission. Inference only.
- The qualities list maps cleanly onto likely competency prompts: rapport and relationships, composure under pressure, initiative, teamwork, attention to detail in trade input, and a view on markets.

## 6. Regulation and conduct context for scenario questions

### Takeaway
TP ICAP's UK businesses are FCA-regulated and both founding firms carry historical FCA fines for broker misconduct: ICAP Europe (£14m, 2013, yen LIBOR) and Tullett Prebon Europe (£15.4m, 2019, wash trades and failing to be open with the regulator). No FCA fine on a TP ICAP entity after 2019 was found. The live issues are German cum-ex proceedings inherited from ICAP and the FCA's non-financial misconduct rules that began applying to non-bank firms on 1 September 2026.

### Cited Findings
- [firm-official, FY25] "The FCA is the lead regulator of the Group's UK businesses". — [FY25](https://tpicap.com/tpicap/sites/g/files/escbpb106/files/2026-03/TP%20ICAP%20-%20FY%202025%20RNS%20-%20FINAL.pdf)
- [regulator, 25 Sept 2013, HISTORICAL] FCA fined ICAP Europe Limited £14m (£20m before a 30% settlement discount) for misconduct relating to LIBOR between October 2006 and November 2010. Brokers colluded with UBS traders to manipulate Japanese yen LIBOR, sent skewed "run-throughs" to panel banks, and one broker received corrupt bonus payments; UBS made at least 330 written requests; the desk at the centre was not audited for the whole period. Tracey McDermott: "IEL's significant failings in culture and controls allowed that misconduct to flourish". ICAP Europe was the first interdealer broker fined over LIBOR. — [FCA press release](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/icap-europe-limited-fined)
- [regulator, 25 Sept 2013, HISTORICAL] The US CFTC imposed a $65m penalty on ICAP Europe for the same yen LIBOR conduct. — [CFTC release 6708-13](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/6708-13)
- [regulator, 11 Oct 2019, HISTORICAL] FCA fined Tullett Prebon (Europe) Limited £15.4m (£22m before discount). Between 2008 and 2010 its Rates Division "had ineffective controls around broker conduct"; "lavish entertainment" and weak controls allowed improper "wash" trades with "no change in beneficial ownership" and "no legitimate underlying commercial purpose", generating unwarranted brokerage. The firm failed to produce broker audio requested in August 2011 until 2014 and gave an incorrect account of how it was found. Mark Steward: "The market performs important public functions and is not a private game of self-enrichment." Senior management and compliance were "cocooned from seeing the misconduct". — [FCA press release](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/fca-fines-tullett-prebon-154-million); [Final Notice](https://www.fca.org.uk/publication/final-notices/tullett-prebon-europe-limited-2019.pdf)
- [search excerpt, law firm summary, 2019, HISTORICAL] The Tullett Prebon breaches were of FCA Principles 2 (skill, care and diligence), 3 (management and control) and 11 (relations with regulators). — [Clifford Chance](https://cliffordchance.com/insights/resources/blogs/regulatory-investigations-financial-crime-insights/2019/10/key-points-from-the-fcas-disciplinary-action-against-tullet-prebon.html)
- [regulator, 2018, HISTORICAL] CFTC ordered ICAP Capital Markets LLC to pay $50m for aiding and abetting attempted manipulation of the USD ISDAFIX benchmark. — [CFTC release 7793-18](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/7793-18)
- [press, 2019, HISTORICAL] CFTC fined Tullett Prebon Americas a total of $13m for failing to supervise a swaps desk and for misleading CFTC staff. — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/regulation/tp-americas-fined-13-million-by-cftc/)
- [regulator, Dec 2020, HISTORICAL] A US court consent order required TFS-ICAP to pay a $7m penalty over "flying" prices and "printing" trades (showing bids, offers or trades that were not real). — [CFTC release 8335-20](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8335-20)
- [press, date not shown, conduct period Sept 2020 to March 2025] FINRA fined TP ICAP Global Markets Americas $80,000 for supervisory failures. — [FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/institutional/finra-fines-tp-icap-global-markets-americas/)
- [firm-official, FY25] "In April 2025 the Commodities and Futures Trading Commission ('CFTC') closed its investigation into certain Group entities with no resultant action being taken against the Group." The investigation concerned pricing of bond issuances using TP ICAP's indicative broker pricing screens and "recordkeeping matters including in relation to employee use of personal devices for business communications". — [FY25](https://tpicap.com/tpicap/sites/g/files/escbpb106/files/2026-03/TP%20ICAP%20-%20FY%202025%20RNS%20-%20FINAL.pdf)
- [firm-official, FY25] Cum-ex: "The Group is subject to a number of ongoing criminal and civil investigations and proceedings relating to alleged historical involvement of ICAP in certain German dividend tax arbitrage ('cum-ex') transactions, arising from activities that took place by ICAP prior to the creation of TP ICAP in 2016". Prosecutors in Frankfurt and Cologne are involved; "No Group company, employee or director, or former employee or director, has been charged or indicted." Civil claims include MM Warburg in Hamburg (partial judgment against TP ICAP Markets Limited in March 2025, under appeal) and a Portigon AG complaint served in New York in February 2026. — [FY25](https://tpicap.com/tpicap/sites/g/files/escbpb106/files/2026-03/TP%20ICAP%20-%20FY%202025%20RNS%20-%20FINAL.pdf)
- [firm-official, FY25] LIBOR litigation still open: a Dutch "Stichting" class action (claims reinstated on appeal in 2024; TP ICAP "benefits from an indemnity from NEX Group Limited"). ICAP Europe's dismissal from a US Euribor class action was affirmed in August 2025. — [FY25](https://tpicap.com/tpicap/sites/g/files/escbpb106/files/2026-03/TP%20ICAP%20-%20FY%202025%20RNS%20-%20FINAL.pdf)
- [firm-official, AR25] Current standing: "Non-financial misconduct remediation has also been aligned with FCA initiatives"; "the positive tone of the FCA's latest Firm Evaluation letter and encouraging feedback from the FCA on our conduct controls, resilience and governance." The Board approved an "enhanced Code of Conduct responding to the increased focus on financial and non-financial conduct from regulators". — [AR25](https://tpicap.com/tpicap/sites/g/files/escbpb106/files/2026-04/ARA_Full%20report.pdf)
- [regulator and law-firm summaries, 2025-2026] FCA rule COCON 1.1.7FR extends the conduct rules at non-bank firms to "bullying, harassment or violence against colleagues" from 1 September 2026, covering about 37,000 firms; final guidance came in Policy Statement PS25/23 (December 2025); the rule is not retrospective. — [FCA non-financial misconduct page](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/culture-governance/non-financial-misconduct); [White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/fca-extends-non-financial-misconduct-rules-non-banks-what-you-need-know-1-september)

### Inferences
- The two FCA cases map directly onto scenario prompts a broker could face: a client or colleague asks you to help move a benchmark or pass on another client's activity (ICAP 2013); a client suggests a trade with no commercial purpose that earns the desk commission, or hospitality is offered in return for business (Tullett Prebon 2019). In both, the regulator's criticism was of culture and controls that let revenue override conduct, and in 2019 also of not being open with the regulator.
- A sound integrity answer therefore has three sourced elements: refuse or pause the request; keep communications on recorded, firm-approved channels; escalate to the desk head or Compliance and cooperate fully with any investigation. All three appear in the Code of Conduct.
- A candidate should not volunteer the fines unprompted, but should be able to say accurately, if asked, that these were legacy cases from 2006-2010 at the predecessor firms and that pay now explicitly depends on conduct.

### Gaps
- No FCA final notice against a TP ICAP group entity dated after October 2019 was found; I did not search the FCA register entity by entity, so "none found" is not proof of none.
- The European Commission's yen LIBOR cartel case against ICAP and any French or other European regulator fines were not researched.
- The search summary that described TP ICAP as "the renamed ICAP plc" is wrong and has been excluded: per FY25, TP ICAP was created in 2016 when ICAP's voice broking businesses were acquired, and the indemnifying party for legacy matters is NEX Group Limited.
- The exact date of the FINRA settlement is not confirmed.

### Inferences
- The gap the custom tool is meant to fill is real: across the documented tools, none advertises decomposing a prompt into its parts and marking each part as answered or missed. A general-purpose chatbot given the question and a transcript can do this, but offers no timer, camera or one-take pressure.
- A sensible stack for this candidate: the custom tool for reworded and combined prompts and part-by-part marking; Shortlist.Me (if their university licenses it) or HireVue's own practice interview for camera, countdown and one-take conditions; ignore emotion-reading feedback.
- Given the one reported TP ICAP limit of one minute per answer, practice timers should be set to 60 seconds as well as the more usual 90-120 seconds until the invitation confirms the real limit.
