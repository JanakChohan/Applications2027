### 1.4 Fund Solutions (fund finance): lending to private equity and VC funds
**What it is:** A private-equity fund has promises of cash from its investors ("commitments") that it calls in over time. A **subscription line / capital-call facility** is a short-term loan secured on those promises, so the fund can buy a company quickly and call the investors' cash later. A **NAV facility** is a loan secured on the companies the fund already owns, used later in the fund's life. **GP financing** lends to the fund manager itself.

### 1.9 Treasury Risk Solutions (TRS): hedging
**What it is:** Many companies buy or sell abroad, or have floating-rate loans. TRS sells contracts that fix an exchange rate or interest rate in advance (forwards, swaps, caps), so a sudden market move cannot wipe out the company's profit. **Why:** A UK importer paying in dollars loses money if the pound falls. A company with a floating-rate loan pays more if rates rise. Hedging turns those unknowns into a fixed cost.
- The UK/Ireland TRS team has served mid-market clients for 20+ years across FX, rates, inflation, commodities and money markets. It has a **specialist FX desk for funds and private-capital firms**. It expanded into Europe in **Aug-2024** (Reported, Treasury Today / investec.com) — https://treasurytoday.com/insight-and-analysis/press-release-investec-expands-successful-treasury-risk-solutions-business-into-europe
- Named client: **none published**. Hedging clients are almost never named. The **N Brown** deal (above) used "FX & Treasury solutions" as part of the deal team (Reported, snippet).

## 2. How a company relationship turns into a private-bank relationship (and the reverse)

- **Best named example: N Brown (2024-25).** Investec says its PLC Advisory and M&A teams worked "extensively with other teams from the bank, including **Investec Private Capital, FX & Treasury solutions, and Private Banking**" on Joshua Alliance's £191m take-private. A founding-family individual was the client. He got M&A advice, deal financing, FX and private banking from one bank (Reported, investec.com deal-page snippet) — https://www.investec.com/advisory/deals/takeover-offer-for-n-brown-group-plc/
- **Fund managers become private clients.** Investec publishes private-bank guides aimed at **private-equity professionals, hedge fund managers and investment bankers**, for example "What tailored foreign exchange support is available to private equity professionals". These are the same people whose funds Fund Solutions lends to (Reported, page titles/snippets) — https://www.investec.com/en_gb/focus/my-money/what-tailored-foreign-exchange-support-is-available-to-private-equity-professionals.html ; https://www.investec.com/en_gb/focus/my-money/what-private-banking-support-can-investec-provide-fund-managers.html
- **Investec Private Capital** helps entrepreneurs "release liquidity from existing businesses". This sits between business lending and personal wealth (Reported, snippet) — https://www.investec.com/en_za/business-finance/owner-managed-businesses/private-capital.html
- **Typical path (Inferred):** (1) A founder's company borrows from Growth & Acquisition Finance or hedges with TRS. (2) Investec later advises on the sale. (3) The founder now has cash and becomes a private-bank client (deposits, mortgage, FX, advice through Rathbones). It works the other way too: a private client who runs a business gets introduced to the corporate bankers. Investec's FY26 wording is "the power of One Investec". **No UK figures on referral numbers are published.**

## 3. How long relationships last
- **Corporate broking is open-ended.** Mandates such as Victrex's (Jun-2025) and Cohort's have no end date. The broker earns retainer fees each year plus fees on deals. Cohort shows how one broking relationship produces ECM fees (£40m placing) and M&A advisory fees in a single transaction (Confirmed, Cohort RNS).
- **Treatt shows how a long relationship pays off.** One broker or adviser relationship ran through two rival bids over about eight months (Sep-2025 to Apr-2026) (Reported).
- **Repeat lending.** Pulse Clean Energy got an upsized facility. AELF came back for a second aircraft deal. Real estate reports £1bn+ of syndicated loans to support larger repeat deals. Fund Solutions says it has made £25bn+ of fund-finance loans since 2008 with "zero defaults" (Reported, snippets).
- **Fund finance follows the fund cycle (Inferred).** A subscription line usually lasts 1-3 years for each fund. NAV loans run 1-5 years (per Investec's NAV page, cited in relationship_model.md). A manager like Goldenpeak that starts with Fund I could return for Fund II, III and so on.
- No published data on average client tenure or retention.
