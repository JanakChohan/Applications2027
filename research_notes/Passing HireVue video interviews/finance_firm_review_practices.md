# How financial-services employers actually review and score HireVue / one-way video interviews (UK early careers, 2025-26 and 2026-27 cycles)

Research date: 26 November 2026.

**Evidence labels used on every claim**

- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` the employer's own careers page, job ad, blog or spokesperson
- `[VENDOR-OFFICIAL]` HireVue staff or HireVue documentation
- `[REGULATOR]` UK Information Commissioner's Office
- `[REVIEWER]` first-hand account from someone who says they have reviewed videos (all anonymous forum posts; unverifiable)
- `[CANDIDATE]` candidate report (forum, Glassdoor)
- `[JOURNALIST]` trade press assertion without a named source
- `[PREP-SITE]` commercial prep / coaching vendor (treat as weak unless it quotes a primary source)
- `[SPECULATION]` author opinion with no stated basis

**Retrieval caveat (important for the report writer).** Wall Street Oasis, The Student Room, Glassdoor/Fishbowl, Reddit, HireVue's help centre, PwC's and Goldman Sachs's own careers pages all returned HTTP 403 to direct fetching. These items are marked `(snippet only)`. Items marked `(fetched)` were read in full.

## 1. Who reviews the videos: recruiters, outsourced reviewers, junior bankers, or an algorithm?

### Takeaway
The best-supported picture is a human review by campus recruiters and by front-office staff drafted in as raters (anonymous reviewer accounts mention juniors, a "semi-senior analyst", hiring managers, and at one bank a group of MDs), with the bank choosing whether HireVue's optional language-only AI score is switched on to rank the pile. No bank on the target list publicly confirms using AI scoring; Goldman Sachs and PwC UK explicitly say humans assess, and HireVue itself says under 30% of its interviews carry any AI analysis. Review is uneven: some reviewers say they decide on the first answer, others say they watch everything at 1.5-2x speed.

### Inferences
- The folklore that a facial-analysis robot auto-fails candidates is not supported by anything dated after 2021. Where AI is on, it is a transcript-based ranking aid; where it is off (the majority of HireVue interviews per the vendor), the mechanism is a human rater with a rating form.
- Reviewer accounts consistently describe batch assignment ("assigned a group of videos") to people with other jobs. That implies thin attention per candidate and makes the first answer disproportionately important; two independent WSO reviewers describe first-answer triage as common.
- Several accounts separate the person who watches from the person who decides ("the ultimate decision isn't necessarily made by those who view the HVs"; HR recommends on the resume then a hiring manager watches). The video rating is therefore one input into a ranking, not a standalone pass/fail.
- The trade-press line that "AI will transcribe and assess your answer" (eFC, Sept 2026) and the firms' own human-review statements can both be partly true if a bank uses transcript scoring to order the queue and humans to decide. No bank has confirmed that arrangement.

### Gaps
- No evidence found of outsourced (RPO) reviewers at any named bank or Big Four firm. Not confirmed absent; simply nothing surfaced.
- No reviewer stated a minutes-per-candidate figure. The only speed evidence is "first question then decide" and "1.5-2x speed".
- No UK-specific first-hand reviewer account was found; all reviewer posts appear US-centric or unlocated.
- No current (2025-26) firm-official statement on AI scoring from JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley, BofA, Citi, Barclays, UBS, Deutsche, HSBC, BlackRock, Deloitte, EY or KPMG.
- Reddit could not be searched effectively; no recruiter AMA was retrieved.
- The Goldman blog post's date and full text could not be verified (403).

## 2. What scoring forms are used?

### Takeaway
No bank's marking sheet has leaked into any source found. What is documented is the tool the raters use: HireVue's evaluator interface offers a configurable 3-, 4- or 5-point scale applied per question or per candidate, star ratings averaged into an overall score, optional competency ratings on behaviourally anchored scales, and a recommendation. Reviewer anecdotes match this ("feedback per Q + a general score"). The UK Big Four publish their frameworks openly (KPMG's 12 strengths; Deloitte and Barclays strengths/values), which is the closest thing to a published rubric.

### Cited Findings
- `[VENDOR-OFFICIAL]` HireVue help centre: admins choose a 3-point, 4-point or 5-point scoring scale for On Demand interviews, set "per candidate or per question"; evaluators select a star rating per question and the platform "averages the ratings for all questions to produce an overall average rating for the candidate"; evaluator recommendations and ratings appear in a summary table on the candidate report; appearance varies by account configuration (snippet only; help centre 403) — [HireVue: Evaluating an Interview](https://hvclassichelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/43070695967380-OnDemand-Evaluating-an-Interview-On-a-Computer); [HireVue: Evaluation Settings](https://hvclassichelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/43071269985300-My-Company-Settings-Evaluation-Settings); [HireVue: Rating and review](https://hirevuehelpcenter.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/45490682567572)
- `[VENDOR-OFFICIAL]` HireVue's structured-interview builder uses behaviourally anchored rating scales with "a 5-level proficiency scale, ranging from Novice to Expert"; some "Interview Level" competencies are not tied to one question and are rated on every competency-based question (snippet only) — [HireVue: Competencies in use](https://hvclassichelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/43070380217748-Creating-Structured-Interviews-using-HireVue-Builder-Competencies-Currently-in-Use)
- `[VENDOR-OFFICIAL]` Zuloaga (HireVue, 2021): "We have a standardized rubric for what a medium or low answer sounds like"; "A bad answer might be simply, 'I like to work as a team.'"; strong answers give more detail than weak ones; competencies screened include negotiation, persuasion, service-orientation, relationship-building, dependability, adaptability and pursuit of results, with the mix set per role by HireVue's psychology team (fetched) — [eFinancialCareers, 3 Mar 2021](https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news/2021/03/hirevue-interview-bank)
- `[VENDOR-OFFICIAL]` Cornell (HireVue, 2022): answers should be matched to the role's competencies, e.g. "One role might have problem-solving and relationship building"; question types are motivational/fit and competency ("tell us about a time you did x"); HireVue advises clients against hypothetical questions (fetched) — [eFinancialCareers, 8 Nov 2022](https://www.efinancialcareers.co.uk/news/2022/10/hirevue-interview-banking)
- `[VENDOR-OFFICIAL]` HireVue candidate FAQ: an AI assessment "considers the whole context of a response, not just certain words here or there" and evaluates competencies such as communication, problem-solving and initiative (fetched) — [HireVue candidate FAQ](https://www.hirevue.com/candidates/faq)
- `[REVIEWER]` WSO: "HR tends to require feedback per Q + a general score"; "the weight given to each question depends on your reviewer" (snippet only) — [WSO: How are Hirevue videos looked at/rated?](https://www.wallstreetoasis.com/forum/investment-banking/how-are-hirevue-videos-looked-atrated)
- `[REVIEWER/CANDIDATE]` WSO: AI "pre-rated them on a scale of 5 stars before teams review them" at one unnamed firm (snippet only) — [WSO: Are Hirevues really reviewed by HR?](https://www.wallstreetoasis.com/forum/investment-banking/are-hirevues-really-reviewed-by-hr)
- `[CANDIDATE/REVIEWER]` WSO: weight falls "mostly on personality and basic knowledge of the firm" (snippet only) — [WSO: Do HireVues even matter?](https://www.wallstreetoasis.com/forum/investment-banking/do-hirevues-even-matter)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL, 2018]` Goldman recruiters: "Be specific and be sure to answer all parts of the question"; "Don't be a robot"; prepare personal examples; most questions behavioural, one division-specific and more technical (fetched) — [eFinancialCareers, 20 Jul 2018](https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news/2018/07/video-interview-goldman-sachs)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` KPMG UK assesses 12 named strengths: Career Motivation, Learning & Curiosity, Resilience, Relationship Building, Effective Communication, Initiative, Planning & Organisation, Results Focused, Critical Thinking, Inclusive Collaboration, Integrity, Applying Expertise & Technology. The video interview "aims to test your skills, strengths, and motivation for your chosen programme" (fetched) — [KPMG UK: Applying to KPMG](https://www.kpmgcareers.co.uk/apprentice/applying-to-kpmg/)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` Barclays: "We'll be looking to see if you share the same key attributes and Values as Barclays colleagues"; assessment centre assesses "relevant strengths, behaviours, or technical skills" (fetched) — [Barclays application process](https://search.jobs.barclays/internship-graduate-application)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` Deloitte UK returns a "strengths-based feedback report" after the Immersive Online Assessment and Job Simulation, and states this report "is not the assessment outcome"; no benchmark or pass mark is published (fetched) — [Deloitte UK early careers assessment](https://www.deloitte.com/uk/en/careers/early-careers/early-careers-assessment.html)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` Schroders' Stage 2 is a motivation and behavioural questionnaire about how strengths align with the role and values; Stage 3 pairs scenario questions with "a short recorded video answer" and says "It is not about being polished" (fetched) — [Schroders graduate FAQs](https://www.schroders.com/en-gb/uk/individual/about-us/careers/graduates/faqs/)
- `[JOURNALIST]` JPMorgan's reported 2026 HireVue prompts cluster on motivation, persistence toward a goal, an innovative solution, doing the right thing when it was hard, breaking down information, showing humility, solving a business problem (fetched) — [eFinancialCareers, 9 Sep 2026](https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news/hirevue-questions-banking)

### Inferences
- A per-question 1-5 (or 1-3/1-4) rating that is arithmetically averaged means one blank or rambling answer drags the mean in a way a holistic interviewer would forgive; it also means there is no "killer answer" bonus beyond the top of the scale. Consistency across questions should matter more than one standout.
- Goldman's "answer all parts of the question" plus HireVue's rubric logic (more specific detail = higher band; generic assertion = low band) implies raters check coverage of each question component and evidence of a concrete example, i.e. something close to a "hit the points" check even if no checklist is published.
- Because JPMorgan's reported prompts map one-to-one onto behavioural themes (persistence, integrity, humility, innovation), each question is probably tied to one named competency on the rating form. This is an inference; JPMorgan publishes no rubric.
- Since reviewer weighting is discretionary ("depends on your reviewer") and attention front-loaded, the effective rubric in practice is likely: first answer forms the rating, later answers confirm or adjust it.

### Gaps
- No leaked or published bank marking criteria for the HireVue stage were found for any of the 26 firms.
- No evidence on whether banks require double-marking or calibrate raters.
- University careers-service guides (LSE, Warwick, UCL, Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial) and finance-society prep documents were not reached in this research.
- HireVue help-centre pages could not be opened, so scale details rest on snippets.

## 3. Firm by firm

### Takeaway
Format settings converge across US banks (about 30 seconds' prep, 90-120 seconds per answer, 3-5 questions, one take or one re-record), while BlackRock and Blackstone invert the timings. UK retail-rooted banks and the Big Four put the video after, or inside, a scored online assessment, so many candidates there are cut before any human sees a video. Reliable current data is thin for Nomura, Rothschild, Lazard, Evercore, BNP Paribas, Fidelity, Vanguard, Man Group, Citadel and Jane Street.

### Inferences
- Two structural models exist. (a) "Video as first-round interview" (Goldman, JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley, BofA, BlackRock): the video is the main early evidence beyond the CV and is human-rated. (b) "Video inside or after a scored assessment" (HSBC, Schroders, Deloitte, KPMG, PwC, Barclays, Deutsche, UBS, Citi in the UK): an auto-scored test gates the video, so a rejection can precede any viewing.
- For BofA and JPMorgan the video is effectively part of the application (BofA: must be completed by the deadline; JPMorgan: application "will not be considered for further review" without it). Receiving it instantly therefore says nothing about having passed a screen; the CV screen logically happens alongside or after the video.
- JPMorgan's 6-month reuse of a submitted video across "similar positions" means one weak recording can be read against several applications in the same season.
- HSBC returning online-assessment outcomes "within a couple of days" for a stage that includes video responses, and Schroders issuing a report within 24 hours of a stage with a recorded answer, suggest those embedded video answers are either machine-scored or not what determines the initial outcome. Neither firm says which.

## 4. Pass rates and application volumes

### Takeaway
No bank publishes a HireVue-stage pass rate. The only attributable figure is HireVue's own consultant saying 60-75% of candidates are typically approved to the next stage across all clients, which cannot hold for banks whose end-to-end acceptance is under 1%. Published end-to-end numbers: Goldman 360,000+ applicants and 0.7% in 2025; JPMorgan 630,000 applicants for 4,100 intern seats (0.7%) in 2025; Morgan Stanley 0.5% in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- `[VENDOR-OFFICIAL]` Tom Cornell (HireVue, Nov 2022) estimates 60% to 75% of candidates are approved for the next stage, depending on role and stage; this is across HireVue clients generally, not banks — [eFC, 8 Nov 2022](https://www.efinancialcareers.co.uk/news/2022/10/hirevue-interview-banking)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL via Fortune]` Goldman Sachs internships: 2026 under 1% for the third straight year, applicant count undisclosed, about 2,500 interns; 2025 more than 360,000 applicants, 0.7%, 2,600 accepted; 2024 315,126 applications, 0.9%; roughly 5% a decade ago; class drawn from 500+ universities — [Fortune, 8 Jun 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/06/08/exclusive-goldman-sachs-intern-acceptance-rate-falls-below-1-for-third-straight-year/)

### Inferences
- Arithmetic check: at JPMorgan 630,000 applicants for 4,100 seats is 1 in about 154. Even if superdays convert at 1 in 3 to 1 in 5, only roughly 12,000-20,000 candidates (2-3% of applicants) can reach a final round. If every applicant submits a HireVue, the combined CV-plus-video cut must remove about 97% of them. A vendor-style 60-75% pass rate is impossible at these firms; either the video pass rate is in low single digits, or most of the cut is made on CV/eligibility with the video as a secondary input. Reviewer accounts (HR recommending on resume first) point to the latter.
- These figures are global and across all divisions. London front-office ratios are not published and may be worse or better.
- For a candidate, failing "many HireVues" at firms with about 97% pre-superday attrition is statistically the expected outcome, not evidence of an unusually bad video.

### Gaps
- No HireVue-stage pass rate from any bank, asset manager or Big Four firm.
- No UK/EMEA-only application or acceptance numbers.
- No acceptance data for Citi, BofA, Barclays, UBS, Deutsche, HSBC, BNP, Nomura, the boutiques, asset managers or hedge funds was retrieved (an eFC piece on a European bank with more exclusive internships than Goldman and JPMorgan exists but was not read).
- Number of superday/assessment-centre invitations per programme is unknown everywhere.

## 5. Does a fast rejection indicate automated or non-video screening? Are many "HireVue failures" decided on other factors?

### Takeaway
Yes, with good evidence for the mechanism though not for individual firms. Same-day and next-day rejections are consistent with auto-scored online tests or eligibility rules, not with human video review; candidates report exactly that at Deutsche Bank and EY. HireVue's own consultant says candidates do not "fail" the video: they are ranked, and those lower in the pile simply wait, sometimes for months. Reviewer accounts say the resume recommendation and the video rating are made by different people.

### Cited Findings

- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` Goldman draws its class from 500+ universities and highlights 30+ elite athletes, nearly 50 musicians and close to 20 nonprofit founders, i.e. it states that profile markers beyond grades feed selection — [Fortune, 8 Jun 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/06/08/exclusive-goldman-sachs-intern-acceptance-rate-falls-below-1-for-third-straight-year/)
- `[REGULATOR]` ICO: many employers using automated recruitment "are likely relying on solely automated decisions"; human involvement often "not meaningful" — [ICO Recruitment Rewired](https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/what-we-do/recruitment-rewired/)
- `[JOURNALIST]` An asset management firm rejected a qualified applicant 7 minutes after submission (09:22 to 09:29) with an "After careful consideration" email. The role was an executive assistant post, not early careers; cause inferred, not confirmed — [eFC, 12 Jun 2026](https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news/my-friend-applied-for-a-banking-job-she-was-perfect-for-and-was-rejected-7-minutes-later)
- `[CANDIDATE, UK]` A Canary Wharfian poster notes diversity questions collected at application may also affect outcomes and are overseen by diversity teams (speculative; 2023) — [Canary Wharfian](https://forum.canarywharfian.co.uk/goto/post?id=2174)

### Inferences
- A decision inside roughly 48 hours of submitting a video is very unlikely to reflect a human watching it, given batch assignment of videos to bankers and recruiters and Goldman's own "about two weeks" guide. The likely causes are an auto-scored test falling below a cut-off, an eligibility rule (graduation year, grades, work authorisation, duplicate application), or the programme already being full.
- A slow rejection (weeks to months, often at season end) fits Cornell's "bottom of the pile" description: the candidate was ranked but never reached, then closed out when places filled. That is a ranking outcome driven by the whole file and by capacity, not a verdict on the video alone.
- Where the video is bundled with the application (BofA, JPMorgan), "rejected after HireVue" is indistinguishable from "rejected at CV screen". Candidates attribute to the video what may have been decided on the CV.
- Diversity or school targeting as a hidden cause is plausible but unevidenced beyond forum speculation and Goldman's own remarks about profile breadth.

### Gaps
- No firm confirms the order in which CV, test score and video are consulted, or their weights.
- No direct evidence on quotas, school lists or diversity targets affecting the video stage.
- The 2025-26 and 2026-27 Student Room, WSO and Trackr timing data (submission-to-outcome by bank) could not be read; a manual pass through those threads would be the best way to build a per-firm timing table.
- No bank-specific evidence of an automated cut-off on HireVue AI scores.

## 6. How much does timing of application or submission matter?

### Takeaway
Firms say outright that review is rolling and programmes can close early (Goldman, JPMorgan, HSBC, Schroders, Deloitte, BofA EMEA), so timing matters. One counterweight: HireVue's consultant says its scoring lets banks start with the best-ranked rather than the first to apply. No quantitative data on how fast places fill was found.

### Cited Findings
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` Goldman: "We review applications on a rolling basis and encourage you to apply as soon as you are ready" (quoted from the EMEA Summer Analyst page by a third party) — [GameAssessmentPrep quoting GS](https://www.gameassessmentprep.com/employers/goldman-sachs)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` JPMorgan: "we review applications to our programs on a rolling basis" — [JPMorganChase FAQs](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/careers/how-we-hire/faqs)

- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` Schroders: recruitment is rolling, applying early advised; assessment centres run October to December — [Schroders](https://www.schroders.com/en-gb/uk/individual/about-us/careers/graduates/faqs/)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` Deloitte: applications may close early if volume is very high; apply as early as possible — [Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/uk/en/careers/early-careers/early-careers-assessment.html)

### Inferences
- Two timing clocks matter: when the application goes in, and how quickly the video is returned after the invite. Under rolling review with batch-assigned reviewers, a video submitted late in the window joins a queue when most interview slots may already be allocated.
- Rolling review plus ranking produces the "silent months then rejection" pattern: late or mid-ranked candidates are held as reserves until the firm is sure it has filled the programme.
- For the 2027 cycle the Goldman and JPMorgan London windows have already closed (31 Oct and 1 Nov 2026).

### Gaps
- No data on what share of interview slots is allocated by a given date at any firm.
- No evidence on whether completing the video on day 1 versus day 5 of the invite window changes outcomes.

## 7. What firms themselves publish as advice (non-generic parts only)

### Takeaway
The revealing official statements are few: Goldman says humans, not an algorithm, assess the video and tells candidates to answer every part of the question; JPMorgan treats the video as the first-round interview, allows one re-record, and keeps it on file for six months; PwC says no AI assesses early-careers candidates but compares video transcripts across candidates; several firms now publish explicit bans on candidate AI use, with Barclays stating that use ends the interview and withdraws the application.

### Cited Findings
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` Goldman Sachs: video interviews "assessed by trained human recruiters and colleagues across our firm" (snippet only) — [Goldman Sachs careers blog](https://www.goldmansachs.com/careers/blog/posts/goldman-sachs-video-interviewing-facts.html)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL, 2018]` Goldman recruiters: "Prepare as if you were going into a traditional interview"; "Be specific and be sure to answer all parts of the question"; "Don't be a robot"; one video covers all divisions applied to; one division-specific, more technical question — [eFC, 20 Jul 2018](https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news/2018/07/video-interview-goldman-sachs)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` Goldman EMEA campus email (2025): "HireVue best practices" are to review the bank's financial results, business principles and core values; external sources including ChatGPT prohibited — [eFC, 10 Jun 2025](https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news/goldman-hirevue)
- `[JOURNALIST]` Fortune notes HireVue's safeguards can track when applicants switch browser tabs or take too long to respond (Fortune's description of the platform in the Goldman story; search summary only) — [Fortune, 11 Jun 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/06/11/goldman-sachs-students-ai-chatgpt-interviews-amazon-anthropic/)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` JPMorgan: one re-record per response; unlimited practice; video valid for 6 months and reusable for similar positions; Digital Handbook treats HireVue as the first-round interview and forbids notes, pre-written documents and answer-generating software — [JPMorgan careers](https://careers.jpmorgan.com/us/en/how-we-hire/prepare-for-an-interview); [EP Advisory JPM guide](https://ep-advisory.com/blog/jp-morgan-investment-banking-analyst-application/)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` BlackRock: candidates "may use AI tools as part of their preparation" but not during any video-recorded assessment; banned behaviours include "Reading or paraphrasing AI-generated responses in real time" and "Using another open device or browser for suggestions"; use "may impact your candidacy" — [BlackRock AI interview guidance](https://careers.blackrock.com/en/ai-interview-guidance)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` Barclays: "third-party AI tools are not permitted during your Barclays interview"; "Any use will result in your interview being stopped and your application withdrawn" — [Barclays](https://search.jobs.barclays/internship-graduate-application)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` PwC UK: no AI assessment of Early Careers candidates; transcript comparison across candidates for identical wording — [PwC UK](https://www.pwc.co.uk/careers/early-careers/apply/selection-process.html)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` Deloitte UK: candidates record an integrity statement confirming Deloitte's Integrity and AI use guidelines; using answers from third-party websites breaches them — [Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/uk/en/careers/early-careers/early-careers-assessment.html)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` Schroders on its recorded video answer: "It is not about being polished" — [Schroders](https://www.schroders.com/en-gb/uk/individual/about-us/careers/graduates/faqs/)
- `[FIRM-OFFICIAL]` KPMG publishes the 12 strengths it assesses and says the video tests "skills, strengths, and motivation for your chosen programme" — [KPMG](https://www.kpmgcareers.co.uk/apprentice/applying-to-kpmg/)
- `[VENDOR-OFFICIAL]` HireVue (Clark, 2016): "You are not being assessed for stage presence" — [eFC, 19 Aug 2016](https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news/finance/hirevue-interview-banking)

### Inferences
- PwC's transcript-matching and the banks' AI bans mean transcripts are being generated and compared even where scoring is human. Answers that share wording with circulated model answers or with other candidates are a detectable integrity risk, separate from quality.
- Goldman's "answer all parts" and "be specific" plus JPMorgan's ban on notes indicate raters are primed to mark down partial coverage and read-aloud delivery, which are the two failure modes a rater can spot in the first 20-30 seconds.
- Firms that publish frameworks (KPMG's 12 strengths, Barclays Values) are effectively publishing the headings of the rating form.

### Gaps
- Morgan Stanley's and BlackRock's substantive HireVue guidance for students could not be retrieved beyond the items above.
- Goldman's blog post, Prepare page and JPMorgan's Digital Handbook were not read in full; quotes are via third parties or snippets.
- No firm publishes what its assessors rate on the video beyond the general statements listed.
