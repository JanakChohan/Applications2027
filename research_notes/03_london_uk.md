### The ring-fencing question — genuinely live, and it matters for Chase UK
UK ring-fencing (from the Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013, in force 2019) forces
a bank with retail deposits above a threshold to put its retail bank inside a legally separate,
separately capitalised "ring-fenced body" that cannot do most investment-banking activity.
- Threshold was **raised from £25bn to £35bn of core deposits in February 2025** (SECONDARY).
- JPMEL disclosure (SECONDARY, via search of the entity accounts): management "has been
  considering the strategy and long-term implications of the regulatory requirements for
  ring-fencing the Chase business if its total core deposits exceed the ring-fencing deposits
  threshold of £35 billion... as Chase's deposit balances increase."
- **18 May 2026**: HM Treasury published *"Safeguarding Stability, Enabling Growth: The
  Ring-Fencing Review"*, confirming it will relax the regime. Key elements: a "New Growth
  Allowance" letting ring-fenced bodies do some currently-prohibited activity; the £35bn
  threshold to be reviewed every three years. Delivery via the Financial Services and Markets
  Bill 2026-27, secondary legislation and PRA rule changes. PRA to report on ring-fencing
  reporting requirements in 2028. (SECONDARY: Linklaters, Freshfields, Norton Rose, Bank of
  England — multiple independent law-firm summaries agree, which raises confidence.)
- Banks currently in scope named in coverage: Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds, NatWest, Santander UK.
  JPMorgan/Chase UK is NOT currently in scope — it is approaching the threshold, not over it.
=> WHY A CANDIDATE SHOULD CARE: this is the single best "current affairs" talking point for a
Chase UK / UK-focused interview. Chase UK's growth strategy and the UK's deregulatory
direction are on a collision course that is being actively legislated right now.

## BREXIT — AND THE PARTIAL REVERSAL
- Post-Brexit, JPM built out J.P. Morgan SE (Germany) as its EU entity, and grew Paris,
  Dublin, Luxembourg, Milan, Madrid, Frankfurt.
- **April 2026 reversal**: JPM moved a number of roles from Paris back to London, with
  executives reportedly believing they had **overestimated how many EU-based staff post-Brexit
  rules required**. Paris headcount had grown to >1,000 since Brexit; UK ~23,000.
  (SECONDARY: Bloomberg 27 Apr 2026 "JPMorgan Moves Some Paris Traders to London in Brexit
  Rethink"; corroborated by City AM, Business Matters.)
- Corroborating PRIMARY signal in the JPMS plc accounts: **the Paris branch of JPMS plc was
  deregistered on 13 November 2025.** This is an entity-structure fact from audited accounts
  that is directionally consistent with the press reporting. Note it is a branch of the UK
  entity being closed — J.P. Morgan SE's own Paris presence is separate.

  office, rates technology, credit quant trading, securitised products underwriting,
  EMEA sponsors lending & fund finance, MENAT private banking, cross-currency FX payments
  product, UK Innovation Economy payments sales. (PRIMARY — job postings harvested 2026-08-31.)
INFERENCE (clearly marked): firmwide functions — group finance, the CIO/Treasury investment
portfolio, firmwide risk appetite, and ultimate segment P&L ownership — are run from New York.
Segment CEOs (Petno, Rohrbaugh, Erdoes) are US-based. London runs the EMEA franchise and
is a global booking/trading hub, but is not where firm-level capital allocation is decided.
