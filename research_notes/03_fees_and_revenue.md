### Key qualitative statements (verbatim)
- "Generally, PIMCO's fees for providing discretionary investment management services are based upon a **percentage of the market value of assets under management**."
- "Our fees are generally **billed quarterly or monthly in arrears**" (some clients pay in advance).
- Fees negotiated based on: "a Separate Account's investment strategy, the level of discretion given to PIMCO, the extent of the Separate Account's servicing requirements including reporting, the Client's total assets under management aggregated across the Client's relationship with PIMCO... the source of the assets, relationship to PIMCO (i.e., whether the Client is an affiliate or employee), and the type and nature of the Separate Account."
- **MFN clauses**: "certain Separate Account Clients have negotiated... most favored nation ('MFN') clauses... generally require PIMCO to notify the MFN Client if PIMCO has entered or subsequently enters into a more favorable fee arrangement with a similarly situated or comparable Client and, where applicable, offer the MFN Client the same fee arrangement." → structural downward pressure on fees.
- **Fee rebate on internal fund use**: if a separate account buys PIMCO Funds, "we will generally rebate a portion of the Separate Account fees back to the Client in an amount that approximates the advisory fee of the Fund" — i.e. PIMCO does not double-charge.
- Account minimums: CRS says direct account minimums "range from **$75,000 to $250 million**, depending on the investment strategy."
- Appendix B caveat (verbatim): "Our basic Separate Account fee schedules are listed below... The fee schedules, **which are subject to change, may be negotiated** under certain circumstances... **Clients may pay higher or lower fees than outlined below.**" → these are RACK RATES / list prices, not realised rates. Large clients pay less. IMPORTANT caveat for the report.

### PERFORMANCE FEE STRUCTURES (verbatim) — where they apply in the LIQUID business
Standard template: **Base Fee + X% of outperformance over the applicable index PLUS the base fee; subject to quarterly draw on the base fee; NO CAP; rolling 12-month measurement period.**
Note the "over the index plus the Base Fee" construction — PIMCO must earn back its own base fee before any performance fee accrues. Sophisticated, client-friendly framing.

INFERENCE (mark as such): the performance-fee option roughly *halves* the base fee (e.g. Total Return 0.300% → 0.150%) in exchange for 15% of alpha. For a client, this is break-even if PIMCO delivers ~100bps of outperformance. It converts PIMCO revenue from stable to volatile, which is why the fixed-fee schedule is the default and performance fees are opt-in with a higher minimum account size.

### The fee gradient — the single most important economic fact
Rack rates run from **11.25 bps (money market)** to **70 bps (Capital Securities)** in the liquid separate-account business — a ~6x spread. Private/alternative vehicles sit far above this (see note 04 on alternatives, typically 1.00–1.75% management + 10–20% carried interest over a hurdle). The gap between a money-market mandate and a private credit fund is roughly **10–15x on the management fee alone**, before carry.
