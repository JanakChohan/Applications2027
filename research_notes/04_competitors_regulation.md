## Private credit (SECONDARY)
- Asset class ~$3tn globally entering 2025; forecast ~$5tn by 2029. US share ~$1.4tn — comparable to leveraged loans and high yield.
- Ares ~$116bn raised (largest direct lending platform); HPS ~$101bn.
- Why sponsors like it: speed, certainty of execution, fewer regulatory requirements, single-lender unitranche.
- **JPMorgan's response: committed $50bn to its own direct lending platform in 2025, plus $15bn from co-lenders.** (secondary — CNBC/industry reporting)
- CNBC Mar-2026: "Private credit's cracks open door for Wall Street banks' comeback" — banks re-entering leveraged lending.
- Corroborating PRIMARY signal: JPM 2026 guidance names "healthy acquisition finance activity" and "subscription finance" as loan growth drivers.

## UK ring-fencing (SECONDARY but multi-source agreement = high confidence)
- Regime from Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013, in force 2019.
- Threshold raised **£25bn → £35bn core deposits, February 2025**.
- In scope now: Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds, NatWest, Santander UK. NOT JPMorgan.
- **18 May 2026: HM Treasury policy paper "Safeguarding Stability, Enabling Growth: The Ring-Fencing Review"** — confirms relaxation.
  - "New Growth Allowance" permitting RFBs to undertake some prohibited activities
  - £35bn threshold reviewed every 3 years
  - Delivered via Financial Services and Markets Bill 2026-27 + secondary legislation + PRA rules
  - PRA to review ring-fencing reporting requirements, reporting 2028
- **PRA CP10/26** (July 2026): "Ring-fenced bodies: Changes to the continuity of provision of services rules"
- PRA announcement May 2026 on ring-fence change to reduce costs
- Sources: Linklaters, Freshfields, Norton Rose (Regulation Tomorrow), Bank of England, Global Financial Regulatory Blog — all consistent.

## MiFID II research (SECONDARY)
- Unbundling in force 3 Jan 2018: buy-side must pay for research separately from execution.
- UK Investment Research Review concluded unbundling had adverse impacts on UK research provision.
- **FCA PS24/9**: payment optionality for MiFID investment firms — bundled payment permitted again subject to guardrails: written joint-payment policy; annual assessment of value/quality/use; research charges clearly separate and identified.
- **FCA PS25/4**: extends payment optionality to fund managers.
=> Relevance: this is the "why now" question for a Global Research interview.
