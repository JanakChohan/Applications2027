## 1. The derating and the AI-disruption thesis

### Takeaway
The derating was driven less by earnings, which kept rising, than by a valuation-multiple reset on the fear that general-purpose AI agents will commoditise terminals and aggregated data. As of October 2026 the market still treats LSEG as a "show me" story: shares around 8,200p, about a third below the February 2025 peak. LSEG's rebuttal (proprietary data, licensing to AI firms, the MCP server) has stopped the fall, but it has not yet produced visible AI revenue.

### Cited Findings
- **[Fact] The trigger on 3 Feb 2026.** Anthropic released a legal-workflow plug-in for its Cowork agentic desktop app. LSEG was down 7.3% by midday London time, and Relx fell close to 11%. On 4 Feb LSEG fell a further c.2% to an intraday low of 6,774p. The size of the one-day fall differs by source: Morningstar says 9.7%, Reuters "nearly 13%" and J.P. Morgan "approximately 15%", probably because they mix intraday and closing prices. Interview tip: say "around 10-13%", not a precise figure. — [City AM, 3 Feb 2026](https://www.cityam.com/lseg-sage-rightmove-shares-fall-as-anthropic-ai-rattles-markets/); [City AM, European data stocks](https://www.cityam.com/anthropics-new-ai-legal-tool-wipes-billions-off-european-data-stocks/); [Yahoo/Investing.com, Feb 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/why-analysts-see-opportunity-lse-115827517.html)
- **[Reported] UBS, Feb 2026:** the shares had fallen 16% year-to-date and 34% since end-2024 "without any material change to consensus EPS". This is the core of the "multiple, not earnings" argument. — [Yahoo/Investing.com](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/why-analysts-see-opportunity-lse-115827517.html)

Q3 trading update (revenue only) is due 22 Oct 2026. — [Proactive Investors headline, 8 Oct 2026 (page returned 403; detail via search snippet)](https://proactiveinvestors.com/companies/news/1099814/ubs-says-ai-overhang-will-persist-for-lseg-despite-valuation-discount-1099814.html); [LSEG IR](https://www.lseg.com/en/investor-relations)

- **LSEG response / best counter:**
  - MCP server: more than 90 customers connected and 60 more in the pipeline at Q1 2026. Q1 2026 income was +9.8%, the strongest quarter in more than five years. — [Resultsense/Reuters](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-06-12-lseg-sheds-ai-risk-tag/)
  - BofA's Hubert Lam: LSEG is "part of the AI ecosystem rather than competing against it". About 90% of covering analysts rate it a Buy. — same source

### Inferences
- The honest framing: the bull and bear cases agree on the *split*. Real-time data, indices and clearing are protected; terminals and aggregated reference data are exposed. They disagree on how fast the exposed part shrinks and whether AI licensing replaces it.
- Because EPS kept rising through the fall, the bear case is a claim about *terminal value*, which no quarter of results can disprove quickly. That is why UBS calls it "show me".
- The appointment of an enterprise-software and AI operator to the Board can be read two ways: as capability, or as an implicit admission that LSEG's product and tech bench needed strengthening. The second reading is mine; no source says it.

### Gaps
- I found no Q3 2026 data; the trading update is 22 Oct 2026, after this note's date.
- I found no disclosed revenue figure for AI licensing (Anthropic, OpenAI or others). That absence is itself the critics' point.
- I could not read the full UBS Oct 2026 note (403 error).

## 2. Elliott Management: what it asked for, and what has happened since

### Takeaway
Elliott wanted three things: a bigger buyback (£5bn), deeper cost cuts and a "root-and-branch" portfolio review, with the 51% Tradeweb stake seen as a cash source. LSEG gave it a £3bn buyback, a raised margin target and better disclosure, and refused asset sales. I found no public escalation between March and October 2026, and no sale of Tradeweb, FTSE Russell or LCH.

### Cited Findings
- **[Reported] Asks, about 18-25 Feb 2026:** accelerate buybacks to £5bn in 2026; squeeze the cost base; conduct a full portfolio review. Reuters reported that Elliott made no specific disposal requests but sees the Tradeweb stake (c.$13bn) as a potential cash source. — [Proactive via Yahoo, 25 Feb 2026](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/elliott-managements-lseg-push-much-160006597.html); [Global Banking & Finance (Reuters)](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/elliott-presses-lseg-portfolio-review-5-billion-pound/); [Bloomberg headline, 18 Feb 2026: "Elliott Pushes for Divestments, £5 Billion Buyback at LSE Group" (paywalled, not read)](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-18/elliott-pushes-for-divestments-5-billion-buyback-at-lse-group)
- **[Reported] UBS feasibility analysis, Feb 2026:**
  - £5bn of buybacks is "comfortably achievable": about £2.65bn of 2026 equity free cash flow, with net debt/EBITDA going to roughly 2.2x against a 1.5-2.5x target. Theoretical capacity is up to £7.5bn.
  - UBS listed four separable assets: FTSE Russell, Risk Intelligence, the 51% of Tradeweb and LCH. Their combined estimated value of £35.8bn is slightly above LSEG's then £35.0bn market cap. This is the sum-of-the-parts critique in one line.
  - Source: [Proactive via Yahoo](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/elliott-managements-lseg-push-much-160006597.html)
- **[Fact] Elliott statement, 27 Feb 2026:** LSEG's steps were "a positive first step" but "there is still an opportunity for further value-enhancing actions" to "close the valuation gap to industry peers". The statement makes no specific public demand. — [Elliott via PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/elliott-management-statement-on-london-stock-exchange-group-plc-302699173.html)
- **LSEG response:**
  - Executives said the buyback was not a response to Elliott and that there were no plans for asset sales. — [Global Banking & Finance](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/elliott-supports-lseg-buyback-sees-value-potential/)
  - Management's likely arguments, per Proactive: diversification smooths the cycle; cross-business synergies support enterprise contracts; and selling the most richly valued assets would not cure the AI discount. — [Proactive via Yahoo](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/elliott-managements-lseg-push-much-160006597.html)
  - At H1 2026 (30 Jul) Schwimmer cited "good, constructive dialogue with Elliott" (already in WS5). LSEG has since moved the *other* way on LCH, buying minority stakes to take ownership above 95% (WS5).

### Inferences
- The net position is that Elliott got capital return and a margin target but not structural change. If the AI discount persists into 2027, the portfolio-review ask is the obvious next lever (inference).
- No source I found calls for separating "Markets" as a whole. The live break-up candidates are Tradeweb (which is listed, so it can be sold down easily) and possibly FTSE Russell.

### Gaps
- I found no Elliott news dated Jul to Oct 2026 (searches returned only Feb 2026 coverage). I also found no disclosure of the stake size.

## 3. Data business competition: Workspace vs Bloomberg, and the Eikon retirement

### Takeaway
LSEG is a clear but distant number two to Bloomberg in desktops. Credible public user numbers do not exist. The Eikon retirement (30 Jun 2025) completed without a reported customer exodus, but independent reviews still describe feature gaps against Bloomberg. The quantified weakness remains slow Workflows growth (in WS2), not lost share.

### Cited Findings
- **[Fact] Market ranking (FCA, Feb 2024):** among market data vendors, Bloomberg is historically the largest, followed by LSEG (ex-Refinitiv), then S&P Global Market Intelligence, ICE and FactSet. — [Regulation Tomorrow summary of FCA final report, Mar 2024](https://www.regulationtomorrow.com/2024/03/fca-publishes-final-report-on-wholesale-data-market-study/)
- **[Reported] Burton-Taylor (2024):** Bloomberg has the largest share of global market data spend, ahead of LSEG D&A and S&P Global Market Intelligence. These are the only three with double-digit share. Total spend was a record $42bn. — [Markets Media](https://www.marketsmedia.com/financial-market-data-spending-reaches-record-42bn/)
- **[Fact] Eikon withdrawal:** Eikon was withdrawn on 30 Jun 2025 and replaced by Workspace. Migration had originally been promised largely by end-2024, so it slipped about six months. — [LSEG Eikon withdrawal page](https://www.lseg.com/en/data-analytics/products/eikon-trading-software); [A-Team Insight](https://a-teaminsight.com/briefs/lseg-commits-to-transition-clients-from-eikon-to-workspace-by-end-of-2024/)
- **[Reported, low-quality sources]** Users report inconsistencies between legacy Eikon features and Workspace, and "some functionality gaps remain compared to Bloomberg's mature offering". Bloomberg's messaging (IB chat) network is cited as a decisive lock-in. Market-share figures on these review sites (e.g. "Workspace 19.6%") are unsourced; do not quote them. — [TradingToolsHub](https://tradingtoolshub.com/review/reuters-eikon/); [NowNews blog](https://nownews.dev/blog/bloomberg-terminal-alternatives-2026)
- **LSEG response / best counter:**
  - LSEG's Microsoft partnership (Workspace inside Teams and Excel) is its answer to the Bloomberg chat moat. This was covered in earlier workstreams.
  - Subscription growth (ASV) is guided at 6.5% for 2026, and UBS expects 6.6% in Q3, i.e. stable rather than collapsing. — [Proactive headline/snippet, 8 Oct 2026](https://proactiveinvestors.com/companies/news/1099814/ubs-says-ai-overhang-will-persist-for-lseg-despite-valuation-discount-1099814.html)

### Inferences
- Do not claim LSEG is "winning" against Bloomberg on desktops. The defensible claim is that LSEG is stronger in data feeds, indices and post-trade. Bloomberg's private ownership and terminal lock-in leave LSEG competing on breadth and openness, not on the desktop itself.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2025-26 user numbers for either Workspace or the Bloomberg Terminal. Bloomberg is private and LSEG does not disclose seats.
- No evidence measuring customers who left for Bloomberg during the Eikon migration.
- The 92.8% retention figure in the brief was not re-verified in this session.

## 4. Market data pricing criticism and regulatory risk

### Takeaway
The FCA found market power and high margins in wholesale data, but proposed no remedies (Feb 2024). The regulatory risk is therefore latent rather than active. Buy-side groups (AFME) agree with the concerns, and the UK consolidated tape adds a second pressure point.

### Cited Findings
- **[Fact] FCA wholesale data market study, final report 29 Feb 2024:**
  - Covered benchmarks, credit ratings data and market data vendors.
  - Found market power in all three, with "users may pay more than they would under effective competition". Typically no more than three key providers per market. Operating margins of at least 30% in 2017-22, and above 60% in some cases.
  - Proposed no rule changes. It did not refer the market to the CMA, but preserved Competition Act powers.
  - Sources: [Macfarlanes](https://www.macfarlanes.com/insights/102j24q/fca-concludes-its-wholesale-data-market-study); [Cottrill Research](https://cottrillresearch.com/fcas-wholesale-data-market-study-welcome-detail-for-data-buyers-no-investigation-recommended/)
- **[Reported] AFME (buy and sell-side trade body):** welcomed the report and said the FCA's findings on features restricting competition "match its members' concerns". — [AFME](https://www.afme.eu/news-insights/press-releases/afme-welcomes-fca-s-final-report-on-wholesale-data-market-study/)
- **[Reported]** The UK equities consolidated tape dispute is a lingering threat to exchange data revenue. — [Resultsense/Reuters, Jun 2026](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-06-12-lseg-sheds-ai-risk-tag/). The UK bond tape went live 22 Jun 2026 (WS5).
- **LSEG response / best counter:** the FCA found no evidence that firms cannot get the data they need, and chose no intervention, which is evidence the market works. LSEG's high margins reflect investment in data quality. [Inference on LSEG's position; the first half is a fact from the FCA report.]

### Inferences
- In an interview, concede the point ("the regulator found market power") and pair it with the fact that no remedy was imposed. Then note that AI-driven price deflation (Redburn) may do what regulation did not.

### Gaps
- No 2025-26 FCA follow-up specific to LSEG found.
- I did not retrieve the Market Structure Partners report this session, so I make no claims about its content.

## 5. London listings decline and the LSE's role

### Takeaway
London's shrinkage continued in 2026. Its biggest names are now completing delistings (CRH in April, Smurfit Westrock in June, Flutter in August), not just moving their primary listing. More than 30 companies are leaving this year, mostly via takeovers, and H1 IPOs were tiny next to the US. LSE's rebuttal (Julia Hoggett, Sep 2026) is that US-listed UK firms often fare worse and the IPO pipeline is the biggest since 2005. Note for balance: primary-markets fees are a small slice of group income, so this hurts LSEG's brand more than its profit [Inference].

## 6. Clearing competition: Eurex, EMIR 3, CME

### Takeaway
LCH still clears the overwhelming majority of euro and dollar swaps. However, Eurex's share in Q1 2026 was its highest ever on Clarus data, CME is creeping up in USD, and the EU has a political mechanism (the active account requirement plus equivalence reviews) to push further. The risk is real but slow so far.

### Cited Findings
- **[Fact] Clarus, Q1 2026 (published 22 Apr 2026):**
  - Eurex share of EUR swaps (Euribor IRS plus €STR OIS, by notional) was 6.9%, its highest ever. It was 3.6% a year earlier and 5.8% in Q4 2025. By product: 9.3% of Euribor IRS and 5.5% of €STR OIS.
  - Clarus says the record was "likely achieved with the help of the ESMA Active Account Requirement".
  - CME's USD OIS share was 4.3% (3.5% a year earlier), so LCH holds about 95.7%.
  - Source: [Clarus FT](https://www.clarusft.com/q1-2026-cleared-rates-swap-volumes-and-ccp-share/)
- **[Reported] Risk.net, 24 Jun 2026:** more than 80% of euro swaps clearing accounts opened by buy-side firms at Eurex had seen no activity ahead of the first reporting deadline. Detail via search snippet only; page not readable. — [Risk.net headline: "Many Eurex accounts remain inactive as reporting deadline looms"](https://www.risk.net/markets/7963712/many-eurex-accounts-remain-inactive-as-reporting-deadline-looms)
- **[Reported, Eurex marketing]** Eurex claims about a 23% EUR share for IRS plus OIS (as of 31 Aug 2026) and about 2,570 onboarded clients and members. This is likely notional *outstanding*, a different metric from Clarus's traded-volume measure, and figures were hard to match to labels in the PDF. Treat with caution. — [Eurex Clearing Partnership summary](https://www.eurex.com/resource/blob/143582/a00ae77c9a0341346ce5626aee9c026f/data/Eurex-Clearing-Partnership-Programme-summary-otc-ird.pdf)
- **[Fact] Metric matters:** an earlier Clarus analysis (2024) showed LCH with about 99% of €STR by DV01 (risk) even when notional share looked lower. — [Clarus, €STR June 2024](https://www.clarusft.com/estr-volumes-and-market-share-june-2024/)
- **LSEG response / best counter:** SwapClear notional was a record $1,165trn in H1 2026 (+29.4%), so LCH is growing in absolute terms even as Eurex gains share (WS5). The "global netting pool" argument holds that splitting euro swaps raises margin costs for EU firms.

### Inferences
- The fair statement: LCH is losing a few points of share at the margin under regulatory compulsion, and its absolute volume is still growing. The real tail risk is political, at the equivalence review before 30 Jun 2028, rather than commercial.

### Gaps
- No Clarus Q2 or Q3 2026 update found. No published aggregate of the first EMIR 3 active-account reports (due 31 Jul 2026).

## 8. Growth versus peers in 2025

### Takeaway
LSEG's 7.1% organic growth in 2025 was solid but mid-pack. Volatility-geared exchanges (HKEX +30%, Cboe +17%) grew far faster, and ICE was similar at +7%. This is the core of Elliott's "valuation gap" point: LSEG has a lower-growth, data-heavy mix but does not get a data-company multiple either. The comparison is not like-for-like (LSEG reports organic constant currency; the others report headline figures).

### Cited Findings
- **[Fact] LSEG FY2025:** total income excluding recoveries £9.0bn, +7.1% organic constant currency (7.7% organic in 2024); adjusted EBITDA +11.8%. — [LSEG FY2025 transcript, 26 Feb 2026](https://www.lseg.com/content/dam/lseg/en_us/documents/investor-relations/financial-results/preliminary-results/transcripts/lseg-2025-preliminary-results-transcript-26feb2026.pdf)
- **[Fact] ICE FY2025:** net revenue $9.9bn, +7% (its 20th consecutive record year); operating income $4.9bn, +14%. — [ICE IR](https://ir.theice.com/press/news-details/2026/Intercontinental-Exchange-Reports-Strong-Full-Year-2025-Results/default.aspx)
- **[Fact] Cboe FY2025:** net revenue $2.4bn, +17%; diluted EPS $10.42, +45%. — [Cboe via PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cboe-global-markets-reports-results-for-fourth-quarter-2025-and-full-year-302680925.html)
- **[Fact] HKEX FY2025:** revenue and other income HK$29.2bn, +30%; profit +36%; second consecutive record year. — [Xinhua, 26 Feb 2026](https://english.news.cn/20260226/80fc15ae7b0e4ae3888db65805608270/c.html)
- **LSEG response / best counter:**
  - LSEG's growth is mostly recurring subscription revenue (about 70%+), while HKEX and Cboe ride trading volumes that can reverse.

### Inferences
