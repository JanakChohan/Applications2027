## 1. Official wording: purpose, mission, brand promise, values, philosophies, culture statements

### Takeaway
The current (2025–2026) official purpose is short: **"Our purpose is to create enduring worth."** "Living in society, not off it" now sits inside the values list as a separate value statement. In 2019–2021 it was part of the purpose line itself ("to create enduring worth, living in, not off, society").

The commonly quoted **four named values** (Client focus, Cast-iron integrity, Distinctive performance, Dedicated partnership) are the older framework, documented in 2019. 2021 used **five** named values, adding "Entrepreneurial spirit". The 2025 and 2026 reports drop the named headings and use a list of **ten "we..." value statements**. A beginner guide should present the four names as the classic, long-standing set and quote the current statements as the latest wording.

### Inferences
- The values have been reworded over time but the substance is stable: integrity, open-and-honest dialogue, a test-decisions/consensus process, entrepreneurial freedom within risk consciousness, unselfish collaboration, and "living in society, not off it". The 2025–26 list folds the four headings into statements and adds diversity, care and environment.
- For an interview it is safe to say "Investec talks about four core values: client focus, cast-iron integrity, distinctive performance and dedicated partnership". Strictly accurate, though, is: "the current annual report expresses these as ten value statements."

### Gaps
- **"We do not do business at any cost":** I found no source for this wording. The closest confirmed phrase is "We do not seek to be all things to all people." Do not use the requested wording as a quote.
- The current investec.com "about us" page wording is unverified, because the page returns 403. Annual report wording is used instead.
- I did not text-check the 2009 "philosophies" list against the PDF; it is a search snippet only.

## 2. Founding story, founder ethos and quotes; zebra logo

### Takeaway
The firm was founded in Johannesburg in 1974 as a small finance and leasing business. The best-sourced culture origin story is Ian Kantor's account that every founder had a veto, so decisions needed consensus or unanimity. In his words, that became "the basis of Investec's culture". The zebra icon stands for the group's South African roots, and Investec links it to a "restless spirit".

### Cited Findings
- **Reported (BusinessTech, 5 Feb 2025).**
  - Ian Kantor: "Every one of the founders at the time had veto power and could use it on anything."
  - Ian Kantor: "This thing of 'you can do anything you like as long as there is consensus' became the basis of Investec's culture."
  - Ian Kantor: "You always want the best for the business, and we didn't always agree."
  - Bernard Kantor: "Ian started a finance company from a leasing business because he'd come from Lease Plan International in those days. He met Larry, and then they met Errol, and they got together, and they formed Investors, Technical and Executors (Investec), and that was in 1974/1975."
  - Bernard Kantor: "If there's anything to pioneer and build, I'm game for that."
  - Source: [BusinessTech](https://businesstech.co.za/news/business/810414/the-south-african-brothers-who-built-a-r100-billion-banking-empire/)
- **Reported (BizNews founders interview with Alec Hogg, 9 Aug 2020, via search snippet; paywalled).**
  - Ian Kantor: "The rule became that anybody could veto anything. And that meant that you had to find a way of understanding why someone was objecting and that became a very valuable process."
  - Ian Kantor: "Anything you like as long as it's unanimous, which means you really get to understand each other pretty well and the substance of what you're dealing with. And that, in a way, became the binding factor between us."
  - Source: [BizNews](https://www.biznews.com/premium/2020/08/09/investec-founders)
  - Note: the wording ("consensus" vs "unanimous") differs slightly between BusinessTech and BizNews. BusinessTech appears to be re-reporting the BizNews interview.
- **Reported. Founders.** BusinessTech names five: Ian Kantor, Bernard Kantor, Larry Nestadt, the late Errol Grolman and Stephen Koseff. Other sources date Bernard (1978) and Koseff (1980) as later joiners. The 2017 Jewish News piece says "five young founders" set it up in Johannesburg in 1974. — [BusinessTech](https://businesstech.co.za/news/business/810414/the-south-african-brothers-who-built-a-r100-billion-banking-empire/); [Jewish News, 30 Mar 2017](https://www.jewishnews.co.uk/the-unique-bank-with-a-dazzling-jewish-history/). WS2 has the detail.
- **Confirmed (2021). Founding-era self-description.** The 2021 "Entrepreneurial spirit" value says: "We are pioneers at heart. Shaped by our non-traditional origin and evolution…" — [P3](https://www.investec.com/content/dam/investor-relations/financial-information/group-financial-results/2021/Investec-Corporate-Profile-2021-Online.pdf)
- **Reported (Jewish News, Jenni Frazer, 30 Mar 2017). Zebra and history.**
  - The London HQ has zebras on every floor.
  - The collective noun for zebras is "a dazzle".
  - Investec uses the zebra to represent its South African origins and a "restless spirit". It defines restless spirits as people "dissatisfied with the status quo" with "a clear sense of how things could be better, and a strong determination and courage in their convictions to see that through".
  - The article also reports the guiding line "We want to live in society, and not off it".
  - It claims the group refused to work with the apartheid regime and that Mandela later acknowledged this. This is a single secondary source; treat with caution.
  - Source: [Jewish News](https://www.jewishnews.co.uk/the-unique-bank-with-a-dazzling-jewish-history/)
- **Reported (search snippet of investec.com).** The zebra "symbolises not just the company's African roots" but each client's uniqueness: "each zebra has its own unique pattern of stripes", matching Investec's "distinctive, personal approach". — [investec.com our-history (403; snippet only)](https://www.investec.com/en_gb/welcome-to-investec/about-us/our-history.html)
- **Reported (SA Jewish Report, 18 Feb 2014).** Koseff: "seven of the 10 big UK banks had to get a government bailout. We survived that crises without any help from anyone." — [SAJR](https://www.sajr.co.za/stephen-koseff-conquers-investec-s-sub-prime-woes/) (via WS2)

### Inferences
- The veto-consensus story is the origin of the "open and honest dialogue to test decisions, seek consensus and accept responsibility" value. Both sides use the same vocabulary, so this inference is strong.
- "Material employee ownership" plus "freedom to operate" amounts to an owner-manager ideal. Fani Titi's "act like an owner" line (Section 3) is the modern expression of it.

## 3. How the culture works in practice; current leaders on culture

### Takeaway
Investec's own documents describe the following:
- a flat, meritocratic structure that favours self-starters;
- direct feedback;
- "freedom to operate" balanced by risk consciousness;
- decisions tested through open dialogue;
- material employee ownership (staff held about 5% of shares in 2019).

CEO Fani Titi frames the culture as entrepreneurial and owner-like.

### Cited Findings
- **Confirmed (2019). Practices.** Flat structure and meritocratic approach; self-starters "drive their careers"; "immediate and direct performance-related feedback"; "We reward people meaningfully for performance and contribution within their teams". — [P4](https://www.investec.com/content/dam/investor-relations/financial-information/group-financial-results/2019/investec-dlc-vol-1-annual-report-2019.pdf)
- **Confirmed (2019). Staff ownership.** "5% of Investec shares continue to be held by staff (excluding directors' holdings)". L&D spend was 1.5% of staff costs; staff turnover was 9.6% in SA and 11.5% in the UK. — [P4](https://www.investec.com/content/dam/investor-relations/financial-information/group-financial-results/2019/investec-dlc-vol-1-annual-report-2019.pdf). Current share figure not found.
- **Confirmed (2026). Freedom to operate.** Value statement: "We trust our people to exercise their judgement, promoting entrepreneurial flair and freedom to operate with risk consciousness…" — [P1](https://apis.bse.co.bw/storage/disclosures/06/2026/6950.pdf)
- **Confirmed (2025). People programmes.**
  - Belonging, Inclusion and Diversity (BID) networks: Pride, Young Minds, Employment Equity forum.
  - Aim of making Investec a place where it is "easy to be me".
  - "Lessons from History" programme (SA).
  - Reverse mentorship.
  - 10 under-35s sponsored to attend One Young World.
  - Source: [P2](https://www.investec.com/content/dam/investor-relations/financial-information/year-end-results/2025/Investec-year-end-results-book-2025-Printable-March-2025.pdf)

Also a Young Leaders Council and reverse mentorship. — [P3](https://www.investec.com/content/dam/investor-relations/financial-information/group-financial-results/2021/Investec-Corporate-Profile-2021-Online.pdf)
- **Reported (Jersey Evening Post, 8 Mar 2023). Fani Titi.**
  - On ownership: "there's a high degree of ownership and a high degree of commitment because, as an owner, if you act like an owner and you walk into your home and there is something amiss you act immediately".
  - On clients: "It is when you have dealt with a client and they feel 'wow', and it's a good experience for them that really matters."
  - On the firm's role: "our role is not just to achieve the highest return, but to be impactful to our clients, impactful for our colleagues and make sure we don't destroy the environment".
  - On the job: "do right by our clients and do that long term, as well as doing right by your colleagues, live in society appropriately, reduce harm and do good if we can".
  - The article also cites ubuntu ("I am because you are") and "creating enduring worth – living in society, not off it".
  - Source: [JEP](https://jerseyeveningpost.com/business/2023/03/08/the-firm-that-places-worth-above-wealth/)
- **Reported (search snippets of investec.com Focus pages, undated; not fetched, 403). Fani Titi.**
  - "early on in the building of Investec, the founding generation realised that our culture and values formed an essential signature and a competitive advantage"
  - "that DNA is what makes us successful and different"
  - "They run their days like they own the business themselves, so we trust them"
  - Source: [Fani Titi Focus page](https://www.investec.com/en_za/focus/focus-talks/fani-titi.html); [fani-titi-possibility](https://www.investec.com/en_za/inside-out-of-the-ordinary/fani-titi-possibility.html). Use with a "reported" label.

  - The public profile stresses belonging, diversity and inclusion, and mentoring young people.

  - Source: [Cambridge Judge profile](https://www.jbs.cam.ac.uk/about/governance/ruth-leas/)
- **Henrietta Baldock** (Chair since 6 Aug 2026): no culture quotes found in this pass. See WS2.

### Inferences
- In practice, "freedom to operate with accountability" means people are trusted with judgement inside a risk framework. For a graduate in the UK bank, the culture pitch to echo is: initiative, challenge in open dialogue, ownership.

### Gaps

- No verified Leas or Baldock culture quotes.
- The internal-mobility claim rests on careers boilerplate only.

## 4. Sustainability and social ethos

### Takeaway
"Living in society, not off it" is the stated basis for Investec's social programmes. These centre on education and entrepreneurship: Promaths in South Africa and Beyond Business in London, the latter run with the Bromley by Bow Centre since 2011. Alongside these sit climate positioning (carbon neutral in own operations, very low coal exposure) and SDG 10 and 13 as impact goals.

### Cited Findings
- **Confirmed (2019). "Living in society, not off it" section.** "We believe that the group can make the greatest socio-economic and environmental contribution by partnering with our clients and stakeholders to have a tangible impact on reducing inequality." — [P4](https://www.investec.com/content/dam/investor-relations/financial-information/group-financial-results/2019/investec-dlc-vol-1-annual-report-2019.pdf)
- **Confirmed (2026).**
  - Carbon neutral in own operations "for the past seven years".
  - Coal exposure 0.09% of loans and advances.
  - Sustainability is "an integral part of our business rather than a peripheral consideration".
  - Source: [P1](https://apis.bse.co.bw/storage/disclosures/06/2026/6950.pdf)
- **Confirmed (2025).**
  - Impact SDGs are "10: Reduced inequalities, and 13: Climate action".
  - Target of £18 billion of sustainable and transition finance by 2030, of which the SA Specialist Bank aims to contribute R74bn.
  - Source: [P2](https://www.investec.com/content/dam/investor-relations/financial-information/year-end-results/2025/Investec-year-end-results-book-2025-Printable-March-2025.pdf)
- **Confirmed (2021). Promaths and CSI.** Promaths was taken online, "enabling the class of 2020 to contribute 5% of the country's national distinctions in maths and 6% in science". Of CSI spend, "65% was on education, entrepreneurship and job creation". The group won "Most Responsible Business in Africa 2020" (SERAS). — [P3](https://www.investec.com/content/dam/investor-relations/financial-information/group-financial-results/2021/Investec-Corporate-Profile-2021-Online.pdf)
- **Reported (investec.com press and Focus pages via search; 403 on fetch). Beyond Business.**
  - Social enterprise incubator run with the Bromley by Bow Centre since 2011.
  - Serves east London (Hackney, Newham, Tower Hamlets, and others).
  - Offers 20k+ in funding plus mentoring from Investec volunteers.
  - Has "launched 64 social impact enterprises, creating in excess of 375 jobs" (date of figure unclear).
  - Source: [Investec press](https://www.investec.com/en_gb/welcome-to-investec/press/investec-beyond-business-annual-awards-programme-grants-over-100k-to-social-enterprise-startups.html); [Focus spotlight](https://www.investec.com/en_gb/focus/beyond-wealth/the-power-of-shared-purpose-a-spotlight-on-investec-beyond-business.html)
- **Reported (Jewish News, 2017). Douglas Krikler (Investec).** "We go beyond simply writing out a cheque: It's about sitting with people and discussing how we can help them achieve what it is they are trying to do." — [Jewish News](https://www.jewishnews.co.uk/the-unique-bank-with-a-dazzling-jewish-history/)

### Inferences
- The social ethos follows the same entrepreneurial logic as the business: backing founders and education rather than pure philanthropy.

### Gaps
- No current (2026) Promaths or Beyond Business figures were verified. The investec.com pages are blocked.
- No detail found on the Entrepreneurship Development Trust (EDT, listed in the 2021 glossary only).

## 5. Third-party perspective

### Takeaway
