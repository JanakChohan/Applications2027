# Investec's relationship-based model: how it shows up in the UK business (research notes, compiled 2026-10-07)

Labels: **Confirmed** = primary source (Investec RNS / Investec's own vacancy page / Extel). **Reported** = press or third-party summary. **Inferred** = my reasoning. "Snippet" = seen only in search-result text and not fetched (investec.com pages mostly return 403). All URLs were accessed 2026-10-07 unless marked otherwise. These notes avoid repeating WS2/WS3/WS5 except where a fact is needed for the argument; prior source IDs (S-numbers) are given where useful.

## Q1. UK Private Bank: who the clients are, and how the relationship-banker model works

### Takeaway
The UK Private Bank is a small, high-touch business: about 8,200 client households, typically earning £300k+ or with £3m+ net worth. A named private banker assesses each lending case manually, working with credit managers rather than a scorecard. The May 2026 strategy is about turning this from a specialist mortgage lender into the client's main bank (current account, credit card, rewards, advice with Rathbones) with the banker at the centre. Its aim is depth with existing clients, not volume.

### Cited Findings
**Client base and thresholds**
- The UK private bank has **8,200 clients**. The plan is to add **about 5,000**, giving about £25m of extra operating profit (Reported, WealthBriefing, 26-May-2026) — [WealthBriefing print article](https://www.wealthbriefing.com/html/printarticle.php?id=207821)
- City AM gives different figures. It says Investec identified **95,000 potential households**, wants to **double clients to 16,000**, and targets about 13% market share, a £9bn loan book and £30m of investment in customer-facing hires (Reported, 21-May-2026) — [City AM](https://www.cityam.com/investec-eyes-city-hiring-spree-in-major-move-into-uk-private-banking/). **Conflict:** "add ~5,000" (to about 13,200) versus "double to 16,000". The WealthBriefing figure comes from Investec's release, so I would treat it as the base case. The City AM figure may be a longer-run or briefing figure.
- In 2018 the client segments were: "corporate executives and entrepreneurs; internationals and professionals; and financial services professionals". The typical client then earned **£300,000+** and had **NAV of £3m+**. The addressable UK population was put at 90,000–100,000 (Reported, WealthBriefing, 16-Oct-2018, old) — [WealthBriefing 2018](https://www.wealthbriefing.com/html/article.php/Targeting-Restless-Entrepreneurs:-Investec's-UK-Private-Bank). The same article focuses on "providing clients with capital, rather than ... managing clients' capital". (Its line that the bank served "slightly more than 300,000 UK persons" looks garbled. Do not use it.)
- A 2025 third-party guide repeats the thresholds: "typically ... annual income of at least £300,000 and a net worth of £3 million+", with mortgages "usually start from £1 million" (Reported, Money Helpdesk, updated 18-Dec-2025) — [Money Helpdesk](https://www.moneyhelpdesk.com/mortgages/mortgage-lenders/investec/)
- Ryan Tholet, 2018: "We are not always especially useful to high net worth individuals who are simply looking to preserve their wealth" (Reported, WS3 S123, same WealthBriefing 2018 article). The bank targets **income-led** clients (people earning and building wealth), not clients who simply hold it.

**How lending decisions are made: human, not algorithmic**
- Per Money Helpdesk: "They do not use standard credit scorecards"; "every application is assessed by a dedicated Private Banker who looks at your global wealth and future earning potential"; they "accept bonuses, profit shares, foreign currency income, and family trust distributions" and can "often use 100% of a proven bonus track record". Also: "Because decisions are made by humans (often locally), they can move very fast" (Reported, third-party broker guide, 18-Dec-2025) — [Money Helpdesk](https://www.moneyhelpdesk.com/mortgages/mortgage-lenders/investec/)
- Investec's own content, seen only in snippets, says the private banker "has direct interaction with a team of credit managers who review every mortgage application". It also says it can take "bonuses, carried interest, or income from profit shares" into account, and that the "relationship-led approach and in-house specialists" have given lending decisions "within days" (Snippet, investec.com, 403 on fetch) — [Investec mortgages page](https://www.investec.com/en_gb/borrow/mortgages.html); [Mortgage options](https://www.investec.com/en_gb/focus/property/mortgage-options.html)
- Investec publishes case studies titled "a £7.65m mortgage in 12 business days" and "£4m mortgage for managing director with complex financial planning needs" (Snippet, title only; fetch 403) — [Case study 12 business days](https://www.investec.com/en_gb/focus/prime-property/case-study-a-7-65m-mortgage-in-12-business-days.html); [Case study £4m MD](https://www.investec.com/en_gb/focus/prime-property/case-study-4m-mortgage-for-managing-director-with-complex-financial-planning-needs.html)
- Investec also publishes pages on specific professional segments, for example "advantage of private banking for management consultants" and a "private equity professional brochure". It has also issued a press release on a new relationship-management hire to build "city professionals coverage" (Snippet titles) — [Management consultants](https://www.investec.com/en_gb/focus/my-money/advantage-of-private-banking-for-management-consultants.html); [PE professional brochure](https://www.investec.com/content/dam/united-kingdom/downloads-and-documents/private-banking/private-equity-professional-brochure-investec.pdf); [City professionals hire](https://www.investec.com/en_gb/welcome-to-investec/press/investec-further-builds-city-professionals-coverage-with-new-relationship-management-hire.html)

**From mortgage banker to relationship manager (May 2026 strategy)**
- Ryan Tholet: "Our shift from mortgage banker to relationship manager enables a greater offering at the source of any lending, increasing our relevance" (Reported, City AM, 21-May-2026; WS2 S89) — [City AM](https://www.cityam.com/investec-eyes-city-hiring-spree-in-major-move-into-uk-private-banking/)
- Tholet also said: "By broadening our proposition, deepening client relationships and leveraging the strength of the wider Investec ecosystem, we are well positioned to deliver a more integrated banking and wealth proposition" (Reported, WealthBriefing, 26-May-2026) — [WealthBriefing](https://www.wealthbriefing.com/html/printarticle.php?id=207821)
- The strategy is "a high-touch, advice-led proposition, enabled by digital capabilities and a globally integrated One Investec ecosystem". In the UK, Investec is moving "from a specialist lender to a full-service primary bank", with current accounts, credit cards, rewards and benefits (Snippet of Investec press release, consistent with WealthBriefing) — [Investec press release](https://www.investec.com/en_gb/welcome-to-investec/press/investec-updates-its-private-client-growth-strategy.html). WealthBriefing lists **24/7 support**, digital access and an omni-channel service (Reported) — [WealthBriefing](https://www.wealthbriefing.com/html/printarticle.php?id=207821)
- From the FY26 RNS: "Our Private Client offering supports clients across their lifecycle with bespoke solutions, combining deep relationships, digital enablement and the power of One Investec." On Rathbones: "evolving our strategic partnership from a referral-based model to an integrated assets-under-advice model. Investec will now lead the client relationship and advice, with Rathbones providing underlying investment capabilities" (Confirmed, FY26 results, 21-May-2026) — [Investegate RNS](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/investec--invp/final-results-31-03-2026/9578809)
- Investec's guidance repeatedly links revenue to "client acquisition and entrenchment strategies". The UK growth plan will "drive client acquisition, deepen client relationships" (Confirmed, FY26 RNS) — [Investegate RNS](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/investec--invp/final-results-31-03-2026/9578809)

**Service standards and culture**
- Fani Titi: "Our standard is that if a client calls in, the phone should not ring for more than three rings" (Reported, City AM, 21-May-2026) — [City AM](https://www.cityam.com/investec-eyes-city-hiring-spree-in-major-move-into-uk-private-banking/)
- Fani Titi, 2023: "a mortgage is a mortgage is a mortgage, but we have the aim of serving clients better, and more flexibly, and that's why we talk about being out of the ordinary". Also: "It is when you have dealt with a client and they feel 'wow' ... that really matters". And: "the kind of mindset we want is to do right by our clients and do that long term ... If you do all that, the returns will be the consequence or perhaps the reward" (Reported, Jersey Evening Post, 8-Mar-2023) — [Jersey Evening Post](https://jerseyeveningpost.com/business/2023/03/08/the-firm-that-places-worth-above-wealth/)
- A search summary of the same article mentions an "ubuntu" ethos and staff who went out to rescue clients stuck in a car park after losing their cards. **I did not find this in the fetched text**, so treat it as unverified (Snippet) — same URL.

**What a private client RM is measured on (vacancy evidence)**
- The Private Client Relationship Manager (req. 13796, London) role is "managing and growing a portfolio of high-net-worth clients". Duties include building "trusted, long-term relationships", using "client engagement plans", generating "new opportunities through referrals and client introductions", identifying "opportunities to increase wallet share across lending, FX, investments, wealth management", building an "opportunity pipeline", and working in line with "Consumer Duty principles". It is measured on "growth, engagement and commercial performance objectives", and no numerical product targets are published (Confirmed, Investec careers page) — [careers.investec.co.uk vacancy 13814](https://careers.investec.co.uk/jobs/vacancy/private-client-relationship-manager-13796/13814/description/). The careers boilerplate describes "a culture of curiosity, entrepreneurial spirit and human connection" (Snippet, eFinancialCareers listing) — [eFinancialCareers](https://www.efinancialcareers.com/jobs-UK-London-Private_Client_Relationship_Manager_13796.id24570676)
- In April 2026 Investec hired a relationship manager (Oades) on the intermediary/mortgage side (Snippet, headline only) — [Mortgage Solutions](https://www.mortgagesolutions.co.uk/news/2026/04/07/exclusive-investec-hires-oades-as-relationship-manager)

### Inferences
- Simple arithmetic: 8,200 clients against a 95,000-household target pool means Investec serves fewer than 10% of its own addressable market. It wins by being chosen, not by mass marketing. [Inferred]
- The model is relationship-led, but it is still commercial. RMs have "wallet share", pipeline and revenue goals. A fair way to put it: Investec builds the relationship first (bespoke credit, one named banker, fast answers) and earns money by deepening it (current account, card, FX, advice through Rathbones), rather than pushing standalone products. [Inferred from the vacancy and the RNS]
- The new current account and card are relationship tools. They make Investec the client's main bank and bring in cheap, sticky deposits, which a mortgage alone does not. [Inferred]

### Gaps
- I found no published **client-book size per banker**, no **NPS / client-satisfaction score**, and no **retention rate** for the UK Private Bank.
- I could not verify UK private-banking awards for 2025–26.
- I did not find specific 2026 sources on **Investec Private Client events and lifestyle** (e.g. rewards partners, hospitality). The rewards programme is announced, but its details are not public.
- The underwriting quotes come from a third-party broker guide and from investec.com snippets. Neither is an Investec statement I could fetch in full.

## Q2. UK mid-market Corporate Bank: "corporate banking that feels like private banking"

### Takeaway
The mid-market build copies the private bank's relationship model for companies. The plan is a dedicated RM team (40+), transactional banking and digital platforms, with a goal of **1,000 mid-market client relationships by FY2030**. Relationship Directors are explicitly asked to partner with Private Client teams when an owner's business and personal needs overlap.

### Cited Findings
- In the UK, Investec Bank plc "will make a significant investment in transactional banking, a dedicated team of relationship managers and intuitive digital platforms. By FY2030, the bank aims to provide relationship banking to 1,000 mid-market businesses". The RM team is expected to grow to "more than 40 relationship managers over time". The mid-market push should add ≥200bps to group ROE by FY2030 (Snippet of Investec press release "Investec accelerates mid-market strategy", 20-Nov-2025; investec.com 403) — [Investec press release](https://www.investec.com/en_gb/welcome-to-investec/press/investec-accelerates-mid-market-strategy.html). **This resolves the "1,000 clients by 2030" query in WS3/WS5:** it refers to 1,000 mid-market corporate relationships, not to private clients using two products.
- Terry Koizou (ex-Citi Commercial Bank) was hired as head of client relationship management, UK corporate banking. The release promises "corporate banking that feels like private banking", "a more personal and specialist approach", and "relationship-led service that is highly personalised and genuinely useful to clients" for "ambitious ... entrepreneurial mid-market businesses". Simon Jacobs (COO) and Dean Sullivan (head of credit and risk) were also appointed (Reported, Bdaily, 12-Jul-2026) — [Bdaily](https://bdaily.co.uk/articles/2026/07/12/investec-strengthens-corporate-banking-team)
- Andy Hart: "We see a clear strategic growth opportunity to extend our offering and bring a private client banking experience to UK mid-market corporates" (Reported, City AM, c.20-Nov-2025; WS2 S90) — [City AM](https://www.cityam.com/investec-looks-to-mid-market-businesses-in-growth-push/)
- The Corporate Banking Relationship Director (req. 13005) role is to be a "proactive, trusted advisor" who builds "deep, long-term relationships with mid-market companies". It must "connect clients to the full breadth of Investec's expertise across lending, treasury, cash management, transactional banking, equity and advisory teams" and partner "with Private Client teams when personal and business needs intersect". It promises "a banking experience that feels personal, entrepreneurial and firmly on the client's side". Goals cover "new-to-bank growth, revenue, product penetration and portfolio quality" (Confirmed, Investec careers page) — [careers.investec.co.uk vacancy 13023](https://careers.investec.co.uk/jobs/vacancy/corporate-banking-relationship-director-13005/13023/description/)
- The Associate Relationship Manager (req. 13087) supports "1-2 Relationship Directors" in a mid-market portfolio with turnover of roughly **£10m–£250m**. Investec Corporate Banking calls itself "the bank behind extraordinary businesses", offering "entrepreneurial, human-focused solutions" (Snippet; Investec page shows vacancy closed) — [job mirror](https://careers.phikappaphi.org/job/associate-relationship-manager-13087/84418200/)
- From the FY26 RNS: the UK corporate lending book grew 14.4% "in line with our strategic investment into the Corporate mid-market segment", with "continued client acquisition". The RNS also mentions "accelerating our transactional banking capabilities" (Confirmed) — [Investegate RNS](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/investec--invp/final-results-31-03-2026/9578809)
- Ruth Leas, 2022: SMEs want "a highly relationship-orientated and service-focused offering, which they cannot get from the High Street banks" (Reported, BusinessDay, 4-Nov-2022; WS3 S125) — [BusinessDay](https://www.businessday.co.za/bd/companies/financial-services/2022-11-04-qa-ruth-leas-ceo-of-investec-bank-talks-uk-banking/)

### Inferences
- An RM-to-client ratio of about 40 RMs to 1,000 clients works out at roughly 25 relationships per RM. That is very small compared with high-street commercial banking (HSBC UK CMB serves 690,000+ clients, WS3 S149). [Inferred arithmetic; the per-RM ratio is not stated by Investec]
- Transactional banking (payments, cash management) is what turns a lender into a primary bank. As on the private side, it deepens the relationship and brings in operating deposits. [Inferred]

### Gaps
- No published UK figures for corporate-to-private referrals or shared clients. The RNS reports "rising shared clients" only for Wealth & Investment International (SA/Switzerland), not the UK.
- No current count of UK corporate banking clients was found.

## Q3. Specialist lending niches: sector specialists, repeat clients, "partner" language

### Takeaway
Investec's own descriptions of its niches (Fund Solutions especially) stress flexibility, "one size does not fit all" and long-term relationships. However, I found no published repeat-client data, so the evidence here is mainly marketing language.

### Cited Findings
- Fund Solutions offers capital call facilities, secondary fund leverage, NAV financing and GP financing. Investec says its approach recognises that "one size does not fit all", and that it is committed to "building long-term relationships built on trust". It credits its UK and European growth to its "solutions-driven approach and depth of client relationships". NAV deals range from $10m to $200m over 1–5 years. It has a partnership with Ares to increase lending capacity (Snippet, investec.com pages, 403 on fetch) — [Fund Solutions](https://www.investec.com/en_gb/corporate-finance/specialist-lending/fund-solutions.html); [NAV financing](https://www.investec.com/en_us/fund-solutions/nav-financing.html); [NA expansion](https://www.investec.com/en_gb/welcome-to-investec/press/investec-announces-further-expansion-of-fund-solutions-team-in-north-america.html)
- The UK loan-book niches are Fund Solutions, Direct Lending, Real Estate, Aviation, Energy & Infrastructure and Fund Finance. The FY26 RNS names growth in "infrastructure finance, and Fund Finance portfolios" (Confirmed, FY26 RNS; also WS3 S120) — [Investegate RNS](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/investec--invp/final-results-31-03-2026/9578809)

### Inferences
- Niche lending relies on teams of sector specialists who see the same sponsors, funds and borrowers again and again. In practice that is relationship banking, even if Investec doesn't report repeat rates. [Inferred]

### Gaps
- No repeat-client percentages for Aviation, Real Estate, Energy & Infrastructure or Growth & Acquisition Finance. I did not fetch individual niche pages (investec.com returns 403).

## Q4. Corporate broking & research: long-term listed-client relationships

### Takeaway
Investec has been #1 in Extel's UK small and mid-cap survey four years running (2026). It acts as corporate broker to 100+ listed companies. A retained broker relationship is ongoing advice, investor access and research coverage, so it is relationship banking in its purest form.

### Cited Findings
- Extel 2026: Investec was **#1 UK SMID broker for the 4th consecutive year**, ahead of Peel Hunt (#2), Deutsche (#3), Panmure Liberum (#4) and Berenberg (#5). Julian Yates was #1 analyst for the 3rd year. The survey covered 248 PMs and analysts at 186 institutions (Confirmed, Extel, 16-Jun-2026) — [Extel Insights](https://www.extelinsights.com/results/uk-small-mid-cap-brokers/uk-small-mid-cap/2026/investec-retains-the-uk-smid-crown)
- Investec says it was also #1 for UK SMID research and #1 for UK SMID sales, and first in four sectors (Construction & Building Materials, Consumer & Retail, Support Services, Technology). It represents "over 100 public companies" on the Main Market and AIM, including "50+" FTSE 350 constituents, with market caps from about £50m to £2bn+ (Snippet, investec.com press and broking pages) — [Investec press](https://www.investec.com/en_gb/welcome-to-investec/press/--Investec-ranked--1-overall-in-the-UK-small-and-mid-cap-broker-survey-for-the-fourth-year-running.html); [Corporate broking](https://www.investec.com/en_gb/business/public-companies/investment-banking-and-equities/corporate-broking.html)

### Inferences
- Extel votes come from institutional investors, not from the corporates. The ranking therefore shows the quality of Investec's relationships with the **buy side**: research, sales and corporate access. Corporates hire a broker partly for that investor reach, so the two sets of relationships reinforce each other. [Inferred]

### Gaps
- No published retention or tenure data for corporate broking clients. "~110 clients" is not confirmed; sources say "over 100".

## Q5. Investec Save: the transactional exception

### Takeaway
Retail savings are sold directly and through platforms (e.g. Raisin), with no relationship manager. They compete mainly on rate, so this is the clearest "product" business inside the UK bank.

### Cited Findings
- The products are easy access, notice accounts (30/32/90-day) and fixed-term deposits, sold directly and through Raisin (£1,000 minimum, £120,000 maximum on Raisin) (Reported, WS2 S88 snippet) — [Investec Save help](https://savings.investec.com/help)

### Inferences
- Savings fund the loan book. The May 2026 move to current accounts can be read as an attempt to replace some rate-sensitive Save deposits with relationship deposits. [Inferred]

### Gaps
- I did not fetch Moneyfacts or best-buy tables this session, and I found no UK retail-deposit split.

## Q6. Sales culture, saying no, client selection, and numbers

### Takeaway
I found **no public statement** that Investec bans product sales targets. Vacancies show RMs are judged on growth, revenue and "product penetration"/"wallet share", alongside relationship quality and Consumer Duty. The selectivity is structural: tight thresholds, a niche focus, and a small number of clients per RM. No Investec quote of the "we don't do business at any cost" type was found.

### Cited Findings
- The Private Client RM is measured on "growth, engagement and commercial performance objectives" and must ensure "compliance with Consumer Duty principles". The Corporate RD is measured on "new-to-bank growth, revenue, product penetration and portfolio quality" (Confirmed, Investec careers pages) — [PC RM 13814](https://careers.investec.co.uk/jobs/vacancy/private-client-relationship-manager-13796/13814/description/); [Corporate RD 13023](https://careers.investec.co.uk/jobs/vacancy/corporate-banking-relationship-director-13005/13023/description/)
- Titi: returns are "the consequence or perhaps the reward" of doing "right by our clients ... long term" (Reported, 8-Mar-2023) — [Jersey Evening Post](https://jerseyeveningpost.com/business/2023/03/08/the-firm-that-places-worth-above-wealth/)
- Tholet (2018) said the bank is "not always especially useful" to clients who are only preserving wealth, an open statement of client selection (Reported) — [WealthBriefing 2018](https://www.wealthbriefing.com/html/article.php/Targeting-Restless-Entrepreneurs:-Investec's-UK-Private-Bank)
- Numbers available: 8,200 UK private clients (+~5,000 target); 1,000 mid-market relationships by FY2030; 40+ corporate RMs; 100+ broking clients; South Africa 128,000 private clients (sources above).

### Inferences
- The honest answer for a beginner: Investec does sell and has commercial targets. But it sells *through* a relationship. It selects clients first (income-led, complex, entrepreneurial), gives them one person and fast bespoke credit, then grows its share of their wallet. [Inferred]

### Gaps
- No published UK **cross-sell / multi-product %**, **retention rate**, **referral volumes**, or **NPS**. The "1,000 clients using at least two Investec solutions by 2030" claim (WS3 S315) remains unverified and is probably a conflation with the 1,000 mid-market relationships target.
- No Investec Consumer Duty board report or client-outcome metrics were found publicly.
- No "say no to business" quote found. Do not attribute one.
