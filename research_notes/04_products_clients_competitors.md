### Fixed income specialists
- **Western Asset Management (Franklin Templeton) — THE CAUTIONARY TALE.** SEC charged **Ken Leech**, former co-CIO, with fraud (SEC press release 2024-187): a scheme "from at least January 2021 through October 2023" in which he "placed trades with brokers and then routinely waited until later in the trading day to allocate the trades among clients... disproportionally allocated trades at a first-day gain to favored portfolios and trades at a first-day loss to disfavored portfolios — a practice known as **cherry-picking**." Western Asset agreed to pay a **$100 million civil penalty**. **At least $120 billion left the firm** after the investigation was announced; ~$68bn of long-term net outflows in Q4 2024 alone, over half in December. Important nuance from the sources: outflows had **already started before** the investigation became public, as Core and Core Plus funds struggled 2022–2024.
  => Directly relevant to PIMCO: it shows (a) how fast an active fixed income franchise can be destroyed by a compliance failure, (b) why PIMCO's "no star manager / team-based Investment Committee" framing is a genuine commercial asset, and (c) that PIMCO was a major beneficiary of the assets that left Western.
- Wellington, Loomis Sayles, TCW, Brandywine, Insight Investment — **specific 2026 AUM NOT FOUND** in this research. Insight Investment is notable for LDI (and the 2022 UK gilt/LDI crisis).
