Dear Sir/Madam,

My journey to Optiver was unconventional; prior to joining in May, I undertook 5 formative internships across investment banking, private equity, and accounting. With each successive role, I developed deeper appreciation for intellectual exactitude and the corporate finance principles that underpin Equity Analyst roles.

Bayesian probability has always been more than just a university module to me; it has shaped my worldview and decision-making framework for the past decade – assigning probabilities to outcomes and identifying maximum-EV decisions in all facets of life. I once believed this perspective to be uniquely my own, until I walked through Optiver's revolving doors and quickly learned that it's precisely the mutual cognitive foundation that the company is defined by.

My internship at Optiver was nothing short of revelatory. As my first exposure to public markets, I appreciated the inherently objective, Bayesian nature of the work that remains absent in private equity. Whilst trading was enjoyable and intellectually demanding, the skillset of an equity analyst aligns much closer to my skills and experience – offering the perfect blend between the competitive, logical public markets and the analytical, business-focused field of corporate finance.

I spoke with Conor Cashman (Equity Analyst, Optiver), who reinforced my initial impressions of the role. On one hand, equity analysts devote significant time to the intricacies of company financials and fundamental analysis, while on the other, they apply probabilistic frameworks to assess the likelihood of events and the expectation of vol impact. My full-time role at BlackRock is largely identical on the corporate finance side, and the Equity Analyst position presents an opportunity to combine this with my mathematical background.

Optiver was by far my favourite internship, and I would treasure the opportunity to return, particularly in a role that aligns even more closely with my skills and experience.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration, and I look forward to hearing from you soon.
