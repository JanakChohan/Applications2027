# J.P. Morgan Global Private Bank: Advisor Summer Internship 2027 (London)

Research pack for the application (job 210773470, closes 1 November 2026, rolling).

## Deliverables
- `Guide 1 - The Advisor, the Private Bank and JPMorgan Chase.pdf` (31 pages). The role from zero, the team model and client journey (with flow diagrams), how the Advisor works with internal teams, the nine-week internship, what JPMorgan Chase is and why it is structured the way it is, the "one firm" referral engine, leadership, culture and Business Principles stripped of marketing, competitor analysis by division, acquisitions and strategic moves 2021 to 2026, the Global Private Bank's work in depth, the London market, and how to turn it into your own reasons. Glossary and sources.
- `Guide 2 - Strategy from the Filings 2021-2026 and the AWM drill-down.pdf` (15 pages). What the Form 10-Ks, shareholder letters and Investor Days say year by year from 2021 to 2026, the ten strategic through-lines with quotes, where the firm says it is going, Mary Erdoes' AWM letters 2021 to 2025 with a keyword tracker, AWM targets and P&L, the business model past the marketing, the wealth continuum and hierarchy, advisor economics, the AI thread, and the International Private Bank / EMEA / UK drill-down.

## Folder layout
- `research/` six research streams with the underlying notes and source references (firm structure and finances; 10-K strategy 2021 to 2026; Private Bank deep-dive; competitors and M&A; the role and intern intelligence; Erdoes letters and the AWM model).
- `build/` the HTML sections, SVG diagram and chart generators (`svg.py`, `diagrams.py`, `charts.py`), stylesheet and build scripts. Rebuild with `python3 build_g1.py && python3 build_g2.py` and render with headless Chromium (Playwright) to A4.

## Caveats
Figures are as reported by JPMorgan Chase unless stated. Items the research could not confirm against a primary source are marked "unverified", "indicative" or "reported" in the text: exact Private Bank asset thresholds, intern and analyst pay, the current-cycle assessment format, some acquisition prices, and several peer valuation multiples. Prepared 8 September 2026.
