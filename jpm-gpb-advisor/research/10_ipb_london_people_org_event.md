## 1. Organisation of the International Private Bank (IPB), as run from London

**Top of the house (verified Oct 2026).** JPM's EMEA leadership page lists: Mary Callahan Erdoes, CEO Asset & Wealth Management; David Frame, CEO Global Private Bank; Andrew L. Cohen, Executive Chairman of the Global Private Bank; Adam Tejpaul, CEO International Private Bank; Harshika Patel, CEO Asia Private Bank; Pablo Garnica, CEO EMEA Private Bank; Martin G. Marron, CEO Wealth Management Solutions; Edinardo Figueiredo, CEO Latin America & GFG Private Bank New York; Sarah Catania and Grace Peters also appear [S701]. Frame became Global CEO on 3 Jul 2025 and reports to Erdoes; Marron and Cohen (also head of 23 Wall) continue to report to Erdoes [S702]. Jennifer Piepszak: firm COO since Jan 2025 (technology, operations, strategy); no 2026 change found [S703].

**Tejpaul (London, 28 years at JPM).** IPB targets >$20m liquid; addressable market ~$15tn; share highest in LatAm, strong in Asia, lowest in Europe, so Europe is the growth priority; booking centres London, Luxembourg, Switzerland; every banker globally starts with the same morning meeting; AI cuts call prep from an hour to "two or three minutes"; hiring prizes "client engagement skills alongside quantitative ability" [S704]. He wants "three very senior, almost CEO-like" region heads (UK; Continental Europe; Switzerland/Middle East) [S705].

**EMEA under Garnica: the three region heads.**
- UK, Channel Islands & Ireland: Maricé Brown, MD, Region Head (announced Jun 2025, succeeding Oliver Gregson who left for Schroders; ~20 years at JPM, previously Market Manager Mexico); runs London, Manchester, Edinburgh, Glasgow and "regional investment in Northern English and Scottish operations" [S706][S707].
- Continental Europe: Sarah Catania, MD, Region Head (France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Benelux, Nordics; joined JPM CIB Milan 2004; Market Manager Italy 2021, Greece 2024; Region Head from Sep 2025) [S708].
- Switzerland, Middle East & Emerging Markets: Karim Rekik, MD, Region Head, based Dubai; teams in the UK, Switzerland, Israel, UAE, Luxembourg and US; sits on the EMEA Private Bank Operating Committee and the Executive Committee of J.P. Morgan (Suisse); co-chairs the EMEA PB Technology Council [S709]. So the London-based Israel and Middle East desks sit under Rekik, not Brown.

## 2. Headcount and scale

- IPB: >1,000 relationship managers, ambition ~1,900 "over time"; EMEA 12 months to Jun 2025: revenue +~14%, AUM +~25%, client-adviser headcount +9%; new or expanded offices Munich, Hamburg, Berlin, Edinburgh, Glasgow; low single-digit market shares in several European countries that Tejpaul says can "scale materially" [S705].
- Erdoes 2025 letter (Apr 2026): GPB advisors 2,200 (2010) → 2,500 (2020) → 4,100 (2025), ~11% a year since 2020, "over 60%" growth; revenue per average banker +15% 2020–25; "the largest U.S. bank in the high-net-worth-plus segment internationally", growing double-digit where it leads and with share "below 1%" in some markets; training hours tripled since 2019; Connect Coach with 25 AI agents and 1m insights; growth to slow "as advisor hiring normalizes later this decade" [S733].
- Q3 2026 (13–14 Oct 2026): AWM net revenue $6,909m (+14%), expense $4,358m (+14%), net income $1,843m (+11%); AUM $5.2tn (+13%); client assets $7.9tn (+15%); firm headcount 322,964 [S734][S735]. The advisor count was not in the retrieved pages.
- Careers page: >4,000 advisors globally (2025); record GPB revenue $11.4bn in 2024; >$2.9tn client assets [S736].
- Middle East: Erdoes (Qatar Economic Forum, May 2025) said regional headcount rises from ~370 to ~500; offices Abu Dhabi, Doha, Dubai, Riyadh; 700 top employees to hold the 2026 kick-off in Doha [S737]. Switzerland: $55.6bn AUM, client assets +~20% in 2025, Zurich/Geneva staff +30% in 2025, goal to double by 2030 [S730].
- UK footprint and new HQ: JPM employs ~23,000 in the UK (13,000 London, 5,300 Bournemouth, 4,000 Glasgow/Edinburgh); Nov 2025 plan for a 3m sq ft Foster + Partners tower on the Riverside plot at Canary Wharf for 12,000 staff, six years from planning consent; 60 Victoria Embankment houses AWM and London staff will consolidate there and in the new tower; 25 Bank Street's future to be reviewed [S738]. Height agreed at 265m with London City Airport in Feb 2026; "£3bn investment" contingent on "a continuing positive business environment in the UK"; formal planning application still to be filed as of Apr 2026 [S739].
- 60 Victoria Embankment history (former City of London School, restored 1991): see file 03.

## 4. The seats

- Banker / Client Advisor (Analyst → Associate → Senior Associate → VP → ED → MD). FIG VP posting (6 Oct 2026): "generate business and acquire new assets", advise on "full balance sheet: asset allocation, investment management, wealth planning, credit and banking", build "holistic, goals-based investment plans", "coordinate firm resources", draw in "internal specialists"; CFA a plus [S745]. Senior Associate on the UK Entrepreneurs & Executives team is already expected to win assets "across the London & South client market" [S746]. UK teams visible in postings: UK Domestic, UK Entrepreneurs & Executives, UK FIG, UK North, Institutional Wealth Group, Scotland [S701][S745][S746].
- Investor / Investment Specialist: owns the investment conversation on the team's book; London has advertised a "Team Lead, Investment Specialist, UK Team" [S718]; sits under Robinson's Investments & Advice [S701][S710].
- Capital Advisor / Lending Advisor: UK Lending Advisors led by a Head of UK Lending within Oyeleye's EMEA Lending Solutions [S717][S718]; Aug 2026 hire of a VP lending specialist shows the seat exists at VP level in London [S731].
- Wealth Advisor: estate, income-tax, philanthropic and succession planning, working with clients' lawyers and accountants; US postings require a JD and 5+ years' estate planning, UK equivalents are ex-private-client solicitors (Chilvers) [S720]; the Philanthropy Centre and Advice Lab practices sit alongside [S725].
- Client Service Associate: day-to-day contact for clients and the "integrated team" on account administration, payments, loans, asset transfers, KYC updates, digital adoption, and helping bankers execute credit facilities (HK posting; same model in London) [S747].
- Analyst programmes: US campus page gives the 9-week summer shape (week 1 welcome in New York with the AWM cohort; weeks 2–5 training plus an assessment workstream; weeks 6–9 senior speaker series and capstone presentation) and the roadmap Summer Analyst → Incoming Analyst (licensing, desk-readiness) → Analyst years 1–2 (portfolio reviews, business development, client presentations) → Associate (business plan, mentoring) → Senior Associate (independent portfolio) [S741]. The London full-time Advisor Analyst programme opens with a "five-week immersive global training program" with incoming analysts from around the world [S742]; the Investment Solutions programme is "our main source of homegrown talent and the development pipeline to subject matter experts" [S743]. Recruiting leads named on the EMEA careers page: Carly Pierpoint, Global Head of AWM Recruitment; Harry Monro, Head of IPB Talent Acquisition [S736].
