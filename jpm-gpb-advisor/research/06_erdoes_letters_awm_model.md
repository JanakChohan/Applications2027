## 2024 letter (pub. Apr 2025)
Opening: "J.P. Morgan has a proud fiduciary history spanning nearly two centuries of managing client assets..."

"We don't believe any fiduciary manager should dictate choice or preferences for a client's investment portfolio"; "Our fiduciary commitment to our clients serves as our north star"; clients "vote daily on our success with their consistent contribution of new assets"; "fortress mindset".

## 2025 letter (pub. Apr 2026)
Opening: "Over the 10-year period ending in 2025, the S&P 500 outperformed the MSCI All Country World Index by more than 6,000 basis points... At J.P. Morgan Asset & Wealth Management (AWM), we never lost our global perspective."
Headings: A leading global asset manager; A truly global private bank; Investing in our people, platforms and AI; A leading industry position.
Record revenue $24bn (9th consecutive record); pretax $9bn; net income $7bn; client assets $7trn; flows $553bn (22nd consecutive positive year; >$1trn over two years "would rank among the top 30 asset managers"); ROE 40%; 83% of 10-yr active fund AUM above peer median; >5,000 companies, >15,000 meetings, >$500m research spend; #1 active ETF AUM ($250bn) and flows ($65bn); JPHY largest active ETF launch; #2 SMA provider $434bn; alternatives AUS $560bn (from $284bn 2020); Workplace AUA $372bn, 1.8m participants; GPB advisors ~2,200 (2010) -> 2,500 (2020) -> 4,100 (2025): "1% CAGR 2010-2020 accelerated to 11% 2020-2025"; revenue per average banker +15% 2020-25; training hours 3x since 2019; cost per advisor trained -27%; >29,000 AWM professionals across 48 countries; "over 95% of our top 50 AWM clients also work with our other lines of business".
"We believe we operate the only private bank in the industry with a uniform business model globally... We are the largest U.S. bank in the high-net-worth-plus segment internationally — growing at double-digit rates where we already lead and have substantial runway in markets where our current share is below 1%."
AI: SpectrumIQ (automated ~75% of equity trading, ~$4bn client trading-cost savings; ~85% FX trading); Connect Coach (12,000 users, 25 AI agents, 1m personalised insights, "every advisor operate like our very best and every client feel like our only one"); AI proxy voting from 1 Apr 2026.
Closing: "Our North Star has never changed: to be the best in the industry for our clients, not the biggest — and our fiduciary commitment to deliver investment performance remains the way we prove it every day. We will keep investing in our people, research and platforms; extend leadership in active management and active ETFs; expand our leading global private bank; broaden access to alternatives and personalization; and embed AI across the franchise to elevate advice and outcomes."
Signal: Workplace "poised to be a growing flows engine as advisor hiring normalizes later this decade".

## Investor Days
Standing targets (set 2020): revenue growth 5%, pretax margin 25%+, ROE 25%+, LT AUM flows 4% of base. Actuals: 2020 5/28/28/5; 2021 8/37/33/19; 2022 2/33/25/5; 2023 7/35/31/12; 2024 9/34/34/9; 2025 12/36/40. "I don't want anyone on the management team to have anything else in their head except for growth... I don't want them to be constrained by raising higher targets."
- ID 2022: AUS $4trn split evenly; revenue $17bn; PTI $6.3bn; margin 37%; ROE 33%; "NII is about 25% of our business". Five drivers from 2020: more advisors, technology, scale in AM, alternatives, M&A. "This isn't the brokerage business model... This is really an apprenticeship model business. This is where we don't buy books of business. We seed books of business... nearly half of our new advisors are homegrown... their productivity doubles after they've been here for a year... our breakevens are still in year three or earlier and our paybacks are in year five... a steady course for being able to double our advisors over the next five years." PB "#1 originator of jumbo mortgages $3 million and up". "Both the bank and the client should want a balance sheet relationship where no one is reliant on just one way out."
