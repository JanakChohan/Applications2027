# Research stream 12: How the Private Bank works, the client scenario, and 2025-26 strategy
Compiled 15 Oct 2026 for the "Inside Private Banking - London" event (19 Oct 2026). Event listing: penultimate-year students meet "Bankers, Investors and Senior Leaders from the London office", learn about 2027 Private Banking opportunities, and do "an interactive client scenario designed to give you insight into the work of the Private Bank"; eligibility is graduating Dec 2027-Jun 2028 for the 2027 Summer Internship [S800].

## 1. J.P. Morgan's own frameworks and vocabulary

**The four service pillars (EMEA site).** The EMEA services page is organised as **Wealth Planning / Investing / Borrowing / Banking**: Wealth Planning = "The advice and the right strategy based on your goals" (goals-based planning "with regular check-ins", Wealth Advisors who "structure and transfer wealth tax-efficiently"); Investing = "Access to a wide range of investment strategies and insights" (plans tied to goals, "ongoing monitoring and rebalancing to the strategic asset allocation"); Borrowing = "A dedicated partner who brings a strategic approach" (financing "tailored to financial situation, cash flow, and balance sheet"); Banking = "Tailored solutions to manage your personal financial needs" (dedicated Client Service professionals) [S801]. Mission: "to build, preserve & transform our client's wealth"; stats "75+ offices", "3.5K advisors", "$2.7 trillion in client assets" (as of 30 Jun 2024); segments: Individuals & Families, Family Offices, Institutional Families, Business Owners, Athletes & Entertainers [S802].

**Investment approach vocabulary.** EMEA uses three labels rather than discretionary/advisory/execution-only: **Entrust** ("A fully-managed investment approach"), **Engage** ("A collaborative investment portfolio" with ongoing dialogue), **Execute** ("A self-directed investment approach"); clients can combine them across sleeves; "no 'one size fits all'"; strategies from J.P. Morgan and third-party managers pass through "manager research teams" and "portfolio construction teams"; clients may exclude "J.P. Morgan managed strategies" (which "carry higher fees for the firm") except cash/liquidity products [S803].

**Wealth Planning & Advice.** The framing question on the global planning page is literally "What's the purpose of your wealth?"; section headings "A Center of Excellence", "Shaped Around You", "Global Perspective"; service cards: Tax Strategy, Retirement and Asset Location, Wealth Strategy, Estate Planning, Philanthropy, Family and Family Office Advisory, Cyber Advisory, Executive Advisory, Private Business Advisory, Behavioral Science; "Our team includes former attorneys in Trusts & Estates, M&A, and taxes" [S804]. **Advice Lab** (internal name, visible in a London Philanthropy Advisor posting): "The Advice Lab is an integral part of wealth management at JPMC... dedicated to providing clients and prospects across the wealth spectrum with differentiated advice"; practices = The Philanthropy Centre, Family Governance Advisory, Executive Compensation Advisory, Tax Advisory, Private Business Advisory, Family Office & Estate Disposition modeling; "The Philanthropy Centre (TPC)... enhances philanthropic impact" [S805].

**Lending.** Three buckets: **Securities-Based Lending** ("Free up money for short-term needs while maintaining your long-term investments"), **Real Estate Lending**, **Specialty Lending** ("purchase big-ticket assets or access their liquidity", aircraft named); approach: "a holistic understanding of your borrowing needs in the context of your entire financial picture"; standard caveat "Not a commitment to lend" [S806]. The London page describes lending as "structured credit for acquisitions, real estate expansion, or accessing capital without selling investments" [S807].

**Banker vocabulary from UK job postings.** A UK Entrepreneurs & Executives team posting: "To generate business results and acquire new assets across London/the South"; advise clients "on their overall balance sheet" covering asset allocation, investment management, wealth planning and "credit and banking needs"; "leverage internal specialists"; "goals-based planning" across short/medium/long term; skills in "profiling, negotiation and pro-active referral generation"; client types "entrepreneurs, corporate executives, single family offices or endowments/foundations/charities"; "CFA is a plus" [S808]. UK leadership (Oct 2026 site): Maricé Brown (Region Head UK, Channel Islands & Ireland), Diana Robinson (Head of Investments & Advice), Maya Prabhu (UK Domestic and Crown Dependencies), Zeynep Ozturk Unlu (Team Head, Institutional Wealth Group), Khayyam Jumani (UK North), Gillian Murray (Scotland); offices London (60 Victoria Embankment), Manchester, Edinburgh; "London ranked 4th in the world as a destination for centi-millionaires" and 7th for billionaires in 2025 [S807].

**Concentrated stock playbook (EMEA page).** Four verbs: **Borrow** ("creative financing strategies" against concentrated/illiquid assets), **Hedge** ("structures to buffer the value of your investment against price volatility"), **Monetize** ("block trade" to "realize your wealth"; combined hedge-and-borrow where sale unwanted), **Give** (gifting/charitable) [S809].

**Business-owner playbook.** Private Business Advisory: "A successful future for your business—whether it is through retention or monetization—requires a plan"; pre-transaction planning = identify transition path, align stakeholders, "detailing post-sale goals", "reviewing future cash flow needs", "preparing to live off the investment portfolio"; the named deal team includes accountant, M&A advisor, valuation expert, corporate attorney, trusts & estates attorney, "personal banker and investment manager" for planning "before, during and after the transaction"; insider sales via leveraged recap/ESOP with seller financing [S810]. Owner-facing questions the bank poses: "Beyond revenue and earnings, what else truly drives the value of your business?", "Do you want to reinvest in new ventures?", "Who are you beyond your business...?" under headings "Liquidity: More Than Just a Windfall" and "The Long Game: Beyond the exit?" [S811].

**Thresholds and segmentation.** No published UK minimum. Fortune (2025): clients "must keep a minimum balance of $10 million" [S812]; a UK wiki says £10m+ (low confidence) [S813]; Swiss onshore focus CHF 10m+ [S814]. Erdoes: "largest U.S. bank in the high-net-worth-plus segment internationally" [S815]. 23 Wall: "about 700 families worth more than $4.5 trillion", a 2020 JV between IB and PB run by Andy Cohen with ~three dozen people across US/Asia/Europe, UBS veteran Henry Knapman hired in UK [S816]; "more than 700 families globally, with dedicated international leadership" (2026) [S817]. Family Office Report 2026: 333 single family offices, average net worth $1.6bn, private markets 30.8% of allocation, AI the top theme for 65% [S818]. UK "Emerging Family Leaders" conference drew ~100 next-gen clients [S819].

## 2. How a client scenario works

**Format precedents.** JPM's APAC Private Bank Challenge: teams of four work "a hypothetical case study", submit decks and recorded presentations, exercising "asset allocation, client management, and scenario analysis", with JPM mentors and judge feedback [S820]. Peers do the same: Morgan Stanley interns built "a full investment plan" for "a hypothetical client scenario" [S821]; Goldman PWM runs "mock client simulations" [S822]. Assessment-centre lens: "contributing but not dominating", reasoning valued over the answer, informal time observed [S823].

**Expected structure (synthesised from JPM's own pages).** (1) Discovery first: "What's the purpose of your wealth?" [S804]; (2) map both sides of the balance sheet (assets, concentrated positions, liabilities, cash-flow needs) because bankers advise "on their overall balance sheet" [S808]; (3) name the specialists to bring in: Investors, Wealth Advisors (trusts/estates, tax), Capital Advisors (lending), Philanthropy Centre, Private Business Advisory [S805][S801]; (4) propose a plan with "regular check-ins" and only then products; (5) define next steps. Pitfall: jumping to products; the bank's own pages stress planning before the deal closes because pre-close gifting cannot be done after.

**Discovery craft (UHNW Institute).** Open broad ("Tell me about your family. What is something you are most proud of?", "If something could be different two years from now, what would that be?", what "keeps them up at night"), let the client talk, "Tell me more", paraphrase back, engage both spouses, explain how you work, close with "What have we not asked you yet?"; do not drill into the first complaint, rush to solutions, "sell off-the-shelf products", or advise outside competence; aim is a second meeting [S824].

**Likely scenario ingredients and the UK facts to deploy.** Liquidity event (sale/IPO): deal team, pre-sale planning, living off the portfolio [S810]. Concentrated stock: borrow/hedge/monetize/give [S809]. Sports/trophy assets: PB lends "against personal assets, such as an art collection" to meet league rules; "Ten of the last 15 major sports transactions... financed by J.P. Morgan"; >$10bn financed since 2021 [S812]. Cross-border/UK tax: from 6 Apr 2025 residence replaced domicile; 4-year FIG regime only for those non-resident 10 prior years; IHT follows residence with a tail of up to 10 years after leaving [S825]; ~25,000 wealthy left London in 2024, UK IHT 40% [S826].

## 3. Day in the life and the economics

Analyst programme: two-year Program Analyst supporting Client Advisors "with the routine of daily business", promotion to Associate via assessment centre [S827]. Banker tasks: acquisition and retention, "profiling", referral generation, investment plans "including portfolio construction and tactical asset allocation shifts", coordinating specialists [S808].
Tooling: Connect Coach (12,000 users) "proactively pushes 1 million personalized AI-driven insights" and is used "to accelerate meeting preparation, support portfolio analysis and generate call summaries" [S815]; "Prospect Portal" for earlier prospect engagement; statement scanning and aggregation to model external assets [S828]; "well over three-quarters of eligible EMEA trades are now executed online"; real-time KYC projected to cut onboarding rejections by about a third [S829].

Economics: AWM "73% of our revenues come from recurring revenues", 34% pretax margin and 34% ROE (2024), $5.9tn assets with "a path to $10 trillion", ~$1tn of assets from new clients over two years, investment spend $2.7bn in 2025 [S830]. Q2 2026 AWM revenue $6.9bn (+19%); expenses +13% from "continued growth in private banking advisor teams" [S831]. Banker book: advisors 2,200 (2010) to 4,100 (2025), "revenue per average banker increased 15% from 2020 to 2025" [S815]. Alts: ~half of portfolio assets for the 200 largest clients, >20% across the UHNW base [S832]. Primary-bank logic: "over 95% of our top 50 AWM clients also work with our other lines of business" [S815].

## 4. Strategy and initiatives 2025-26

- **Growth and advisors.** Erdoes (May 2025): "We have a path to $10 trillion if we continue to invest in the way that we have been" [S830]. Advisor CAGR accelerated from 1% (2010-20) to 11% (2020-25); "over 60% growth since 2020 outpaced major peers"; "substantial runway in markets where our current share is below 1%" [S815]. Training ("grow our own" in effect): tripled GPB training hours since 2019, AI-driven role plays up 150% 2022-25, cost per advisor trained down 27% [S815]. Europe: Swiss onshore business to double again by 2030 after doubling 2020-24, ~$55.6bn assets end-2024, growth ~20% with half from net new money, staff +30% [S814]. The "1,900 international RMs" figure was not found in any source and should not be used.
- **Alternatives.** >$200bn (Jul 2025) from ~$40bn in 2010; goal "to double the business in five years"; 18-person structuring team; evergreen shelf across PE, credit, real assets; "Managed evergreen alternatives" discretionary portfolios launched 2025; PIPPA pacing/liquidity tool; a growth fund raised ~$1bn in just over a week; "If they can't figure out how it fits in the framework of a portfolio, you've lost the game" (Rowland) [S832]. AWM alternatives AUS $560bn [S815]. The "$2bn a month" figure was not confirmed by any source read today.
- **AI.** Connect Coach ~17,000 users by end-2025; "taking things that take days and weeks and turning them into seconds" [S830]; advisers find information "up to 95% faster"; gross sales +20% 2023-24 attributed partly to GenAI; app expected to let advisers grow rosters 50% in 3-5 years; Erdoes: "We are trying to democratize AI" [S833]. Boundary: the bank "deliberately avoids automated portfolio construction or personalised investment advice" client-side; Catterall: "We are a digitally enabled, not digital only, organisation" [S829]. Frame (Nov 2025): "We constantly strive to make every adviser operate like our best and to make every client feel like our only one"; AI to "cut out complexity, reduce errors, and free up our advisers" [S834].
- **UK affluent link.** Nutmeg rebranded J.P. Morgan Personal Investing (3 Nov 2025), inside the Chase UK app; dedicated relationship manager above £250,000; "Wealth Planner" feature; DIY platform planned 2026; assets £3.5bn to >£8.5bn, customers 140k to 265k [S835]. This is the UK analogue of "Bank with Chase, Invest with J.P. Morgan"; the Private Bank sits above it.
- **First Republic lesson.** Advisor and client retention was the risk; some teams left for UBS and smaller firms; JPM declined to disclose how many remained [S836].
- **Sports, philanthropy, sustainability.** Sports group consolidated 2024 under Menell/Sammartano, working with private bankers [S812]. Philanthropy runs through TPC [S805]. Sustainability: no 2026 stance found.

## 5. Competitor snapshot (London UHNW, 2026)

- **UBS**: GWM invested assets $4.942tn at end-Jun 2026, NNA $35.5bn in Q2 [S837]; UK presence since 1999, ">120 advisors... over 15,000 clients", "liquidity, longevity and legacy" framework [S838]; new "Private Client & High Net Worth UK" segment (Apr 2026) under Michael Frey, private client $2-5m, HNW $5m+ [S839].
- **Goldman Sachs PWM**: Euromoney World's Best for UHNW 2026; ~$1.7tn client assets, >17,000 UHNW clients, average account $75m, >1,000 advisers in 39 offices [S840]; AWM revenue $4.60bn in Q2 2026 (+20%) [S837].
- **Morgan Stanley**: record NNA $148.4bn in Q2 2026 and hit its $10tn total client assets target; lending-led growth [S837].
- **Citi Private Bank**: client assets $2.691tn (+15%) at Q2 2026, inflows $13.7bn [S837]; lines are Citigold / Citi Private / Wealth at Work.
- **HSBC GPB**: Private Bank invested assets $458bn within $960bn Wealth invested assets (2026 snapshot) [S841]; "one bank" pitch; new global head of family offices (Sep 2026).
- **Barclays Private Bank & WM**: invested assets £124.4bn (Q1 2025) [S842]; positioning on discretionary and selective private markets.
- **Coutts/NatWest**: PBWM AUM up £9.6bn in 2025 to £58.5bn total assets with £4.6bn net inflows; £2.7bn Evelyn Partners deal takes the combined business to ~£128bn [S843].
- **Schroders/Cazenove**: Wealth Management AUM £120.7bn (Q1 2026); Nuveen's $13.5bn acquisition approved Apr 2026; Benchmark advice arm being sold, focus on Cazenove in the UK [S844].
- **Rothschild & Co WM**: ">15,000 wealthy and successful families, entrepreneurs, foundations and charities"; no AUM in excerpt [S845].
- **Swiss houses in London**: Lombard Odier AUM record CHF239bn, total client assets CHF367bn (H1 2026) [S846]; Pictet ~CHF810bn AUM/custody and Julius Baer ~CHF547bn per a ranking site (low confidence) [S847].
- **MFO/independent model**: pitch is fiduciary independence and "air traffic controller" coordination for $25m-$250m families, against banks whose guidance "trickled down from above" [S848].
