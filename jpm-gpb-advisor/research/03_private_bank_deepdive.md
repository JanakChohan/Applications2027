# Research stream 3: J.P. Morgan Global Private Bank (International / EMEA / UK) and the Advisor track
Compiled 8 Sep 2026. See the assembled guides for how this was used.

## Scale and thresholds
- No official minimum published. Third-party guides: ~$10m investable (US), ~£10m+ (UK), family office coverage from ~$100m+. JPM family office page: family offices generally have "at least a few hundred million in assets".
- Erdoes (2025 letter, pub. 2026): "the largest U.S. bank in the high-net-worth-plus segment internationally, growing at double-digit rates".
- GPB client assets $2.9tn (31 Mar 2025). US Private Bank $1.16tn. Private Banking client assets $3.824tn at Q2 2026 vs $3.191tn Q2 2025 (+20%) per Q2 2026 10-Q excerpt (unverified against primary).
- AWM FY2025: record revenue $24bn (9th consecutive record); record net new client asset flows $553bn (22nd consecutive positive year); client assets $7.1tn end-2025. Q2 2026: revenue $6.9bn (+19%), net income $2.0bn (+33%), AUM $5.1tn (+18%), client assets $7.7tn (+19%); expenses up on "continued growth in private banking advisor teams".
- Advisors: GPB advisors ~2,200 (2010) -> 2,500 (2020) -> 4,100 (2025); "over 60% growth since 2020 outpaced major peers"; revenue per average banker +15% 2020-2025 (Erdoes letter). PB advisors 4,101 at end-2025 (+9%). Investor Day 2025: 9,600 advisors across all wealth channels; Connect Coach rolled out to 4,600 PB advisors. US PB: 3,775 advisors, 98,000+ clients, 6,197 new clients (Global Finance 2026).
- Footprint: 75+ offices globally; uniform model across 48 countries. EMEA: 17+ offices, 11 countries. EMEA locations: Athens; Belgium; Denmark; Paris; Frankfurt, Hamburg, Munich (Berlin new); Milan; Luxembourg; Netherlands; Madrid; Sweden; Geneva, Zurich; Edinburgh, London, Manchester, Glasgow; Dubai; Riyadh; Qatar. UK sites: London, Manchester (Jun 2023), Edinburgh (Feb 2024), Glasgow, Bournemouth.
- Sports: "60% of the major sports team owners are private banking clients"; "10 of the last 15 major sports transactions financed by J.P. Morgan" (Investor Day 2025).

## Team model
- Two internal tracks: Advisors (client-facing, bankers) and Investment Solutions (investors/product). London runs "Advisor" and "Investment Solutions" summer internships separately.
- Banker / Client Advisor: UK ED posting: "Generate business results and acquire new assets", lead new client acquisition and retention, advise on "overall balance sheet, including asset allocation, investment management, wealth planning", "leverage internal specialists": Investors, Capital Advisors, Trust and Estate Advisors, Wealth Advisors. CFA preferred. Leaders "both play and manage".
- Investor: owns the investment conversation across a banker's book (asset allocation, manager selection, market views, portfolio reviews). London: Diana Robinson, Head of Investments & Advice UK.
- Capital Advisor: securities-based lending, real estate lending, specialty lending (aircraft named). Sports-finance = "largest lending business in private banking".
- Wealth Advisor: former attorneys in trusts, M&A, tax; Trusts & Estates, Philanthropy, Family Governance, Rising Generation next-gen programmes, cyber advisory, Goals-Based Planning. UK: James Chilvers, Head of UK Wealth Advisory; Chris Ottenritter, EMEA Head of Wealth Advisory.
- Client Advisor Support Associate: presentations, onboarding, coordination with specialists.
- Alternatives: Kristin Kallergis Rowland, Global Head; alts AUM ~$40bn (2010) -> >$200bn (Jul 2025); ~50% of assets of 200 largest clients and >20% across UHNW base in alts; 18-person structuring team; evergreen funds; PB puts "$2 billion to work every single month" in alternatives.
- 23 Wall: institutional wealth management practice (built 2023) for ~700 families with ~$4.5tn assets; Andrew L. Cohen Exec Chairman GPB / Head of 23 Wall / Global Chair of IB. London Institutional Wealth Group: Zeynep Ozturk Unlu.
- Family Office Practice (US): 150+ professionals incl. 40 former partner-level attorneys; William Sinclair; Global Family Office Report (190 offices, avg net worth $1.4bn).
- Segment groups: Athletes & Entertainers; Business Owners/Founders (Private Business Advisory pre-sale team); London FIG team (Paul Ferry ex-Citi, Nov 2025).
- Erdoes: "We do not have side accounting groups that think about where those revenues get counted for. We are trying to win for the client" (Investor Day 2025).

## Internship and Advisor Analyst programme
- 9 weeks June-Aug; week 1 induction; projects; mentorship; top performers get FT offers. Work with "market strategy, equity, and alternative investment teams" on research, proposals. Eligibility penultimate year. Application: CV + questions -> HireVue video interview -> hiring manager review. 2027 London ad closes 1 Nov 2026 (rolling).
- HireVue: ~3 questions, 30s prep, 1.5-2 min answers. 2026 GPB opener: "What attracts you to J.P. Morgan, and specifically, an opportunity in Global Private Bank?" Another: "Tell us about a recent news article you read about Financial Markets."
- FT Advisor Analyst programme: five-week global training; exposure to investments, lending, banking, trust & estate; promotion to Associate. Forum accounts: 2y Analyst -> 2y Jr Associate -> 2y Sr Associate; Banker vs Investor split at Associate; London hours ~8:00-18:30; first-year base ~£60k + £5k relocation (unverified, undated); Glassdoor £62k for 0-1 yr. Summer pay not published (estimate ~£1,100-1,200/wk).
- Erdoes: "half of our new advisors come from growing them from within"; "these 25-year-olds coming online are just as productive as our 30- to 35-year-old mid-career hires"; productivity "up 3.4 times"; training hours tripled since 2019; "AI-driven role plays".
- UK advisers need FCA RDR Level 4 qualification (typically CISI); US Series 7 and 66.
