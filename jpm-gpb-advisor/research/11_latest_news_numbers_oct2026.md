## 1. JPMorgan Chase 3Q26 results (released Tue 13 Oct 2026 – not 14 Oct)

**Firmwide.** Reported net revenue $50.2bn, managed $50.9bn, both +8% YoY; net income $15.3bn (+6%), EPS $5.57 (+10%); ROE 17%, ROTCE 20%; CET1 13.9% standardised; dividend raised to $1.65/quarter [S750]. Excluding a $658m pretax net investment-securities loss in Corporate, net income was $15.8bn, EPS $5.75, ROTCE 21% [S750][S751]. Pre-results consensus EPS was ~$5.86, so a modest headline miss [S752].

**Dimon quote (release):** AWM revenue grew 14% and "flows were very strong with $263 billion of client asset net inflows"; the US economy is supported by business investment and consumer spending but "fiscal deficits, sticky inflation, elevated asset prices, rising rates and geopolitical conditions" bear watching [S750].

**Asset & Wealth Management (3Q26).** Revenue $6,909m (+14% YoY, +1% QoQ): Asset Management $3,248m, **Global Private Bank $3,661m** (vs $3,531m in 2Q26 and $3,150m in 3Q25, i.e. +16% YoY) [S751]. Expense $4,358m (+14%) on revenue-related comp and "continued growth in private banking advisor teams"; credit costs $84m ($68m reserve build) [S750]. Net income $1,843m (+11% YoY, -6% QoQ); pretax margin 36% (2Q26 38%); ROE 45% [S751]. AUM $5.21tn (+13%); client assets $7.90tn (+15%); long-term net inflows $51bn (fixed income-led), liquidity net inflows $42bn [S751]. Average loans $301.6bn (+20% YoY, +6% QoQ); average deposits $253.5bn (+5% YoY, -3% QoQ) [S751]. No advisor headcount disclosed [S751].

**Call colour (13 Oct, Barnum/Dimon).** Barnum: long-term inflows "largely driven by continued strength in fixed income"; CCB client investment assets "crossed $1.4 trillion, up 15%"; Markets equities revenue +49% YoY; 4Q26 NII ex-Markets ~$25.5bn, 2027 central case ~$107bn; 4Q expense ~$27.5bn, 2027 consensus ~$114bn; "the consumer is currently very healthy" [S752]. On AI exposure: "the AI theme is so broadly diffused into the marketplace… our exposures are still quite modest"; internal strategy is "minimizing lock-in and maximizing optionality" [S752]. Dimon: "some of the credit terms have gotten a little bit loose"; internally "we use agents to do good things here"; IPO pipeline "quite healthy"; a downturn "could happen because of what happens overseas"; "We serve clients through thick or thin" [S752]. Private credit/Blue Owl and the UK were not discussed on the call [S752].

**Outlook slide.** FY26 NII ex-Markets implied ~$97bn; adjusted expense ~$108.6bn; Card NCO ~3.2% [S751].

**2Q26 (14 Jul 2026) for comparison.** Firm net income $21.2bn ($16.9bn ex significant items), ROTCE 29% (23% adj.) [S753]. AWM revenue $6.9bn (+19%), net income $2.0bn (+33%), AUM $5.1tn (+18%), client assets $7.7tn (+19%), long-term inflows $50bn, ROE 48%, margin ~38% [S753].

**FY2025 record (13 Jan 2026).** Firm FY25 net income $57.0bn, EPS $20.02 [S754]. 4Q25 AWM revenue a record $6,516m (+13%), net income $1,808m (+19%); AUM $4.8tn (+18%), client assets $7.1tn (+20%); Dimon: "client asset net inflows totaled $553 billion for the year, helping drive client assets to over $7 trillion"; FY25 long-term inflows $209bn, liquidity $183bn [S754].
