## Why the pieces sit together (management's words)
- CB into CIB (announced 25 Jan 2024, effective 2Q24). Petno ID 2025: "Our clients are growing, scaling and expanding globally at an accelerated pace... a single wholesale operating entity, four business units tightly knit with a very clear strategy to be our clients' most important financial partner... seamlessly serve our clients throughout their lifecycle." Rohrbaugh: "fully integrated yet intentionally segmented to serve our clients at every stage of their lifecycle... client-for-life relationships." Letter: "combustion effect"; "making the whole greater than the sum of its parts".
- Payments in CIB: Neukirchen: "we hold $760 billion dollars of deposits, which is almost one-third of the Firm's deposit funding. Most of them are operating deposits... collaboration with Markets around seamless FX has added hundreds of millions... we enable [clients] to sweep funds into AWM, at the moment about $175 billion".
- PB with AM: Erdoes ID 2025: "two different businesses. On the left is the Asset Management business run by George Gatch, and on the right is the Global Private Banking business... asset management can give you a 4 to 6 times multiple lift... recurring revenue business that's very capital light"; "73% of our revenues come from recurring revenues"; Wealth Management Solutions (Marron) builds the advice/product platform for both PB and Chase: "no matter where you come into this institution, you get the best advice."
- Chase wealth in CCB: Lake ID 2025: "The secret sauce is in leveraging the branches and delivering a deep integration between Banking and Wealth. 90% of new advised clients were referred by bankers... 5.5 million loyal, affluent banking households. Last year, nearly 19% of them invested with us"; only 21% of ~5m affluent households invest with the firm; goal $2tn client investment assets. 4Q20 realignment of certain wealth clients AWM -> CCB. "Bank with Chase and Invest with J.P. Morgan".
- Firm: Dimon: "we strive to build enduring businesses, which rely on and benefit from one another... we are not a conglomerate. This structure helps generate our superior returns." Barnum Feb 2026: "Our completeness, global presence, diversification and scale are not just attributes. They're our competitive advantages... The composition and connectivity of our business lines creates durability... support our clients through their entire lifecycle and through multiple generations."

## Linkages
- "over 95% of our top 50 AWM clients also work with our other lines of business" (Erdoes letter).
- Erdoes ID 2025: "We are every day investing in the companies that are taken public by the investment bank. We're helping the CFOs of those companies to invest their liquidity in [our] money market fund business. We're helping the CEOs on their personal balance sheets, taking their 10b5-1 programs, sending them through Troy's trading desks... or we're taking all the great things in the commercial bank and the leads that they have... There are 2,000 private banking clients that walk into those [Chase] branches every day."

- Dimon 2025 letter: the firm as "its own strong 'neural network' — powerful and healthy connections between our people."

## Culture
- "first-class business in a first-class way": J.P. "Jack" Morgan Jr., prepared statement to Senate Banking and Currency Committee (Pecora hearings), 23 May 1933: "the idea of doing only first class business, and that in a first class way, has been before our minds".
- How We Do Business: 20 Business Principles under four headings. Mission: "building and maintaining the best and most respected financial services company in the world"; "A bank can't be a fair weather friend."
  Exceptional Client Service: 1 focus on the customer; 2 field and client driven, operate at the local level; 3 build world-class franchises, investing for the long term.
  Operational Excellence: 4 highest standards of performance; 5 financial rigor and risk discipline; always maintain a fortress balance sheet; 6 best internal governance and controls; 7 act and think like owners and partners; 8 best, most efficient systems and operations; 9 disciplined in everything we do; 10 execute with both skill and urgency.
  Integrity, Fairness and Responsibility: 11 will not compromise our integrity; 12 face facts; 13 fortitude; 14 respect, inclusiveness, humanity and humility; 15 strengthen communities.
  Great Team and Winning Culture: 16 hire, train and retain great, diverse employees; 17 teamwork, loyalty and morale; 18 open, entrepreneurial meritocracy; 19 communicate honestly, clearly and consistently; 20 be good leaders.
  Sub-bullets: "Treat the customer the way you want to be treated"; "Look at the facts in a cold-blooded way; admit and learn from mistakes"; "Treat all people properly and with respect — from clerks to CEOs"; "information is power; share it as quickly as possible". Origin: "small blue book" after 2004 JPMorgan-Bank One merger.
- Five values (2023 "Faces of Our Values"): Heart, Courage, Curiosity, Excellence, Service. Not on current about pages.
- Fortress balance sheet: Principle 5; "Fortress Principles" box each quarter (4Q25: CET1 $288bn, 14.5%; SLR 5.8%; TLAC $564bn; $1.5tn cash and marketable securities). TCE $49bn (2005) -> $260bn (2024) -> $280bn (2025).
- Dimon canon: steadfast principles (2025 letter): "our work has very real human impact"; "we consider our stock price a measure of our progress over time"; "enduring businesses... we are not a conglomerate"; "a source of strength, particularly in tough times, for our clients"; "the foundation of our success rests with our people". Bureaucracy: "Why complacency, arrogance, bureaucracy and BS kill companies" (2024); "You must kill bureaucracy all the time and relentlessly"; NBIM Apr 2026: "Bureaucracy, complacency, and arrogance will take down a company"; "Bureaucracy is like the petri dish of politics"; "They like the process, not the outcome. Whereas I like the outcome"; "Get the people in the room and work it out"; "get rid of the jerks". 2025 letter: "organize in small teams for super speed... act like Navy SEALs"; "Building a lasting, deeply rooted and common culture is critical"; "neural network". 2024: "What the hell is culture?"; "You have to get the numbers right... You better have great controls." Travels ~145 days/yr; 60+ town halls; "I have to earn my trust and respect every day, too." Barnum "living within our means": "not a cap on expense growth or a crude hiring freeze... a cultural tone to discourage automatically hiring people as the default response". Organic: "Organic growth is hard, but it's your way, your culture, your people, your technology". "complete the mission"/"the whole firm" as verbatim: unverified.

- Tech spend ~$17bn (2024) -> $18bn (2025) -> ~$19.8bn (2026, +10%). "past the point of peak modernization". Dimon: "we're not gonna try to meet some expense target... how did JPMorgan get left behind?" ~60,000+ technologists.
