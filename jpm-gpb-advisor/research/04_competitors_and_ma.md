## UK wealth context
- ~578,400 USD millionaires in UK (5th globally); London leading European wealth city; resident millionaires -9% 2014-25 vs W10 +40%. HMRC: ~3,400 people with £100m+.
- Non-dom abolished 6 Apr 2025: 4-year FIG regime; residence-based IHT with 10-year tail; TRF. HMRC 83,000 non-doms 2023/24; OBR expected ~1,200 to leave; Bloomberg: at least 10% left by mid-2025; Henley forecast 16,500 millionaires ($91.8bn) out in 2025 (disputed); Henley 2026 scores UK 68.3 "competitive jurisdiction under pressure". Destinations UAE (+9,800 in 2025), Italy, Switzerland, US, Singapore, Greece.
- Landscape: Coutts (buying Evelyn), Barclays PB, HSBC relaunch, Lloyds/Schroders Personal Wealth; UBS, Julius Baer, Pictet, Lombard Odier; JPM, Citi, GS, MS; Rothschild, Rathbones, SJP, Brown Shipley, Arbuthnot, Weatherbys, EFG. Consolidation active.
- Why London endures: English law and courts; trust/family-office infrastructure; time zone; deepest capital markets outside NY; gateway for Gulf, African, Indian, European families; JPM IPB is London-led.
- Consumer Duty since Jul 2023; 2025-26 priorities fair value, ongoing advice, vulnerability; CP25/36 professional-client opt-up (~£10m investable, H2 2026) would take much of UHNW book outside the Duty; targeted support from Apr 2026.
