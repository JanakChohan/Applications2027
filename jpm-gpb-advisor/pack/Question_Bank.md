# Question bank: J.P. Morgan Global Private Bank Advisor Summer Internship 2027 (London)

Scaffolds, not scripts. Each entry: the question, what it tests, a structure, facts to weave in, and the trap. Sources and confidence tags are in the full pack (Part 9).

## Motivation and fit

### 1. What attracts you to J.P. Morgan, and specifically an opportunity in Global Private Bank?
- **Tests:** Whether you understand the mechanism or only the brand
- **Structure:** One mechanism, one number, one thing about you, one thing you want to work on
- **Facts to weave in:** Referral engine; seeded books; balance sheet; Europe as the share opportunity; Part 12
- **Trap:** "Global leader", "prestige", "learning"

### 2. Why the Private Bank and not the investment bank or asset management?
- **Tests:** Self-knowledge; whether you want a client-facing sales role
- **Structure:** What the seat is; why that suits you; evidence from your life
- **Facts to weave in:** "Relationship building and sales capacity" in the posting; the five-seat team
- **Trap:** Describing an investing job

### 3. Why the Advisor track rather than Investment Solutions?
- **Tests:** Whether you have read both postings
- **Structure:** Banker versus Investor; the trade-off you accept
- **Facts to weave in:** Investment Solutions is "the development pipeline to subject matter experts"
- **Trap:** Not knowing the other exists

### 4. What is the difference between AWM and the Private Bank?
- **Tests:** Structural literacy
- **Structure:** Two halves of one segment; the numbers
- **Facts to weave in:** AM $11.7bn, GPB $12.4bn; 73% recurring revenue
- **Trap:** Treating them as the same

### 5. Why J.P. Morgan rather than Goldman, Morgan Stanley or a family office?
- **Tests:** Competitive awareness without overclaiming
- **Structure:** Two mechanisms only J.P. Morgan has; one thing a rival does better
- **Facts to weave in:** Part 4.3 and 4.4
- **Trap:** "Best at everything"

### 6. Where do you see yourself in five years?
- **Tests:** Realism about the path
- **Structure:** Analyst, Associate, choosing Banker; the qualifications; what you want to be known for
- **Facts to weave in:** The five-week global training; "many analysts are promoted to Associates"
- **Trap:** "Running my own fund"

### 7. Walk me through your CV.
- **Tests:** Narrative discipline
- **Structure:** Two minutes; a thread; end on why this seat
- **Facts to weave in:** Part 11's CV grill
- **Trap:** Reciting the CV

## Behavioural and competency

### 8. A time you worked in a team to accomplish an important goal
- **Tests:** Collaboration; your specific role
- **Structure:** Situation, your action, result, what you'd do differently
- **Facts to weave in:** "No side accounting groups"; team goal language in postings
- **Trap:** Using "we" for everything

### 9. A time you made a mistake and what you learned
- **Tests:** Business Principle 12: face facts
- **Structure:** A real mistake with a consequence; the fix; the habit you built
- **Facts to weave in:** The firm's own record of admitting errors (Part 2.9)
- **Trap:** A humble-brag mistake

### 10. A time you had to be persistent to reach a goal
- **Tests:** Sales resilience
- **Structure:** The number of attempts; what changed; the outcome
- **Facts to weave in:** Prospecting is most of a junior banker's week
- **Trap:** Persistence without adaptation

### 11. A difficult client, customer or stakeholder
- **Tests:** Composure and empathy
- **Structure:** Steady, sort, act, close (Part 11)
- **Facts to weave in:** "Banking is not a commodity"
- **Trap:** Blaming the client

### 12. A time you built trust with someone new
- **Tests:** The core skill of the seat
- **Structure:** What they needed; what you did that cost you something; the proof
- **Facts to weave in:** "The more you know about me... how could I ever not be with you"
- **Trap:** Trust as a feeling rather than an act

### 13. A time you handled sensitive information with discretion
- **Tests:** The posting's discretion criterion
- **Structure:** What you knew; who wanted it; what you did
- **Facts to weave in:** Part 1.6
- **Trap:** Revealing the confidential thing in the answer

### 14. A time you disagreed with a team and both positions were reasonable
- **Tests:** Judgment; conceding without folding
- **Structure:** The two positions; the test you applied; the result
- **Facts to weave in:** Erdoes on advisors who disagree with clients
- **Trap:** Pretending you were simply right

### 15. A time you evaluated an AI answer you did not trust
- **Tests:** A 2027-cycle theme; critical thinking
- **Structure:** What the tool said; how you checked; what you changed
- **Facts to weave in:** Connect Coach; "really smart AI and really smart advisors"
- **Trap:** Either trusting or dismissing AI wholesale

### 16. A time you convinced someone with data
- **Tests:** Analytical communication
- **Structure:** The claim; the two numbers; the outcome
- **Facts to weave in:** Any figure from this pack you can defend
- **Trap:** Too many numbers

### 17. A time you went above and beyond for someone
- **Tests:** Service instinct
- **Structure:** The need nobody asked you to meet; what it cost; the result
- **Facts to weave in:** "Every client feel like our only one"
- **Trap:** Describing your job description

### 18. How do you handle stress and multiple deadlines?
- **Tests:** The "multitask" criterion
- **Structure:** A system, not a mood
- **Facts to weave in:** The worked day (Part 1.2)
- **Trap:** "I thrive on stress"

### 19. A time your suggestion was rejected
- **Tests:** Coachability
- **Structure:** The idea; the reason it lost; what you did next
- **Facts to weave in:** "Player-coach" culture
- **Trap:** Resentment

### 20. A time you helped someone make a financial decision
- **Tests:** Advice instinct
- **Structure:** Their goal; the options; what you recommended and why; the outcome
- **Facts to weave in:** The four lenses
- **Trap:** Giving regulated advice you were not qualified to give; say you framed options

## Markets and commercial awareness

### 21. What factors have influenced markets recently and how might they affect our clients?
- **Tests:** The "genuine interest" criterion, with the client lens
- **Structure:** Three factors; one client consequence each; one view
- **Facts to weave in:** Hormuz oil shock; gilts at 5.36%; Bank of England 6-3 hold; Fed hike expected; AI concentration; gold
- **Trap:** Reciting headlines with no "so what"

### 22. Tell us about a recent article on financial markets.
- **Tests:** Reading habit
- **Structure:** What it said; what you think; what a wealthy client should do
- **Facts to weave in:** Eye on the Market on AI technicals; the Mid-Year Outlook
- **Trap:** Choosing a tabloid story

### 23. How do banks make money: investment banking, wealth management, lending, sales and trading?
- **Tests:** Basic business literacy
- **Structure:** One sentence each; then the Private Bank's four legs
- **Facts to weave in:** Part 2.2; fees $15.5bn, NII $6.8bn in AWM
- **Trap:** Muddling fees and spread

### 24. What are the greatest challenges facing financial services in five years?
- **Tests:** Industry view
- **Structure:** Three forces; the one that matters most for private banking; why
- **Facts to weave in:** Part 5.5 and 5.6
- **Trap:** "Fintech disruption" with no mechanism

### 25. Where would you invest your own money now? Which asset classes suit UHNW clients?
- **Tests:** Conviction and suitability instinct
- **Structure:** Your allocation; the client's is different because of horizon and liquidity
- **Facts to weave in:** LTCMA returns; 15 to 30% alternatives; gold up 130%
- **Trap:** Hot stock tips

### 26. Define HNW and UHNW.
- **Tests:** Vocabulary
- **Structure:** $1m and $30m by convention; J.P. Morgan's ladder
- **Facts to weave in:** Part 5.2
- **Trap:** Guessing

### 27. What are the risks of emerging markets?
- **Tests:** Structured thinking
- **Structure:** Currency, liquidity, governance, geopolitics; then why the house still likes them
- **Facts to weave in:** "The new strategic investment case for emerging markets"
- **Trap:** One-sided answer

### 28. Will the Bank of England hike? What does it mean for a client with a £20m mortgage?
- **Tests:** Applied macro
- **Structure:** The vote split; the pricing; the client's floating cost; the hedge
- **Facts to weave in:** 3.75%, 6-3, four hikes priced
- **Trap:** Forgetting the client

### 29. Is AI a bubble?
- **Tests:** Handling a contested question
- **Structure:** Both sides; where you land; what a client should do about concentration
- **Facts to weave in:** Cembalest's four risks; Erdoes: "AI itself is not a bubble" (reported)
- **Trap:** Certainty

### 30. Why would a wealthy client not just buy gilts at 5.3%?
- **Tests:** The most common client objection
- **Structure:** Inflation, currency, concentration, tax on coupons versus gains, opportunity cost
- **Facts to weave in:** Savings tax to 22/42/47% from 2027
- **Trap:** Agreeing with the client

## Client scenarios (see Part 11 for the method)

### 31. A client wants 100% in tech stocks.
- **Tests:** Suitability instinct
- **Structure:** Acknowledge; ask why; show the concentration risk; propose a path
- **Facts to weave in:** Cembalest on AI concentration
- **Trap:** Either refusing or complying

### 32. A client lost 20% last year and wants to leave.
- **Tests:** Composure; retention
- **Structure:** Listen; review against the plan and the benchmark; involve the Investor and team lead; no promises
- **Facts to weave in:** "Clients vote with their feet"
- **Trap:** Defensiveness

### 33. A client asks about cryptocurrency.
- **Tests:** Handling a request outside the shelf
- **Structure:** What the house offers and does not; the risks; the small-allocation frame; escalate
- **Facts to weave in:** 89% of family offices hold none
- **Trap:** Personal opinion as advice

### 34. Explain a 60/40 portfolio, or asset allocation to a nervous client.
- **Tests:** Plain English
- **Structure:** One sentence definition; one analogy; the client's own numbers
- **Facts to weave in:** LTCMA: adding 30% alternatives lifts 60/40 from 6.4% to 6.9%
- **Trap:** Jargon

### 35. Justify a 1% fee.
- **Tests:** Value articulation
- **Structure:** What the fee buys (team, balance sheet, access, performance record); what it does not
- **Facts to weave in:** Part 3.3
- **Trap:** Apologising for the fee

### 36. You discover a colleague recommending unsuitable products.
- **Tests:** Conduct rules
- **Structure:** Rules first; escalate; document; do not confront alone
- **Facts to weave in:** SM&CR conduct rules; Consumer Duty
- **Trap:** Doing nothing "to be a team player"

### 37. Markets crash tomorrow: your communication plan.
- **Tests:** Proactive service
- **Structure:** Who first (biggest exposure, most anxious); what message (the plan, not the prediction); who does what
- **Facts to weave in:** Connect Coach flags; "prepare for the worst"
- **Trap:** Waiting for calls

### 38. Advise a founder one week after a $100m exit: first, second, third.
- **Tests:** Sequencing
- **Structure:** Safety of cash and KYC; the plan and the four lenses; phased deployment; then structuring you should have started a year earlier
- **Facts to weave in:** Pre-liquidity planning
- **Trap:** Starting with products

### 39. Earn trust with a sceptical prospect.
- **Tests:** Sales craft
- **Structure:** Ask, listen, bring one useful thing, follow up, never oversell
- **Facts to weave in:** Referrals convert best; centres of influence
- **Trap:** Pitching

### 40. A client mentions their listed company is "about to be bought".
- **Tests:** Market abuse
- **Structure:** Stop; do not act; tell compliance; document
- **Facts to weave in:** Part 11 decision tree two
- **Trap:** Telling the Investor

### 41. A managing director asks you to open an account before the documents arrive.
- **Tests:** Controls under pressure
- **Structure:** Rule; fastest compliant path; escalate if pushed
- **Facts to weave in:** Part 11 decision tree one
- **Trap:** "Just this once"

### 42. A client wants to move to Dubai because of the tax changes. What do you say?
- **Tests:** Live UK context
- **Structure:** Not tax advice; the residence facts; how the bank serves them in both places; involve the Wealth Advisor
- **Facts to weave in:** Part 6.4; "amazed at how many... have decided to stay"
- **Trap:** Giving tax advice

## 9.3 Ten questions only a researched candidate could ask

1. "The UK banker postings are cut by market team: UK Team, Entrepreneurs and Executives, Financial Institutions under Paul Ferry, Institutional Wealth, and a London-based Middle East desk. Which team would an intern most likely sit with, and does the client work differ between the domestic and the international teams?"
2. "Every banker posting says 'partner with other Client Advisors in the team to maximise team strength', and the Middle East posting lists the specialist bench: Investor, Capital Advisor, Trust and Estate Advisor, Wealth Advisor. How does an intern get exposure across those seats rather than only shadowing bankers?"
3. "The Lending Advisor posting calls the role 'internal sales leader for the credit product'. How much of a UK team's growth today comes from lending versus discretionary mandates?"
4. "Adam Tejpaul told Euromoney the biggest share opportunity is 'within the European franchise', with EMEA adviser headcount up 9% and assets up 25%. Where does the UK sit in that mix against Continental Europe and the Middle East?"
5. "James Chilvers' team specialises in legacy non-domiciled individuals. After April 2025, how has the split between Maya Prabhu's UK domestic book and the internationally mobile clients shifted, and what did the 'amazed at how many decided to stay' comment mean in practice?"
6. "The FCA's CP25/36 would let clients with over £10m opt up to professional status and require every existing elective professional to be re-reviewed within a year. If finalised, would the UK Private Bank move most of its book, or keep retail protections as a differentiator?"
7. "Erdoes has said advisor hiring will 'normalize later this decade' and that home-grown advisors are half of new hires. What does that mean for the 2029 analyst class, and how many of the current UK bankers came through the programme?"
8. "Connect Coach is on 12,000 desks. What has it changed in a London banker's morning, and what has it not?"
9. "The Investment Solutions internship was posted the same day as this one and describes itself as 'the development pipeline to subject matter experts'. How do the two intern cohorts interact on a client proposal?"
10. "What is the single deliverable a London Private Bank intern is most often judged on by week nine, and who reviews it?"
