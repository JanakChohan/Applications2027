4.2 The design spectrum

{{FIG:spectrum}}

Four design axes on which J.P. Morgan sits at or near one end. Uniform global model versus local heritage: "we operate the only private bank in the industry with a uniform business model globally" [S316]; UBS is the only peer with comparable global uniformity; Coutts, Barclays and HSBC UK are local. Balance-sheet-heavy versus asset-light: J.P. Morgan and UBS lend at scale; Goldman's private wealth and the Swiss partnerships lend less; independent advisers not at all. Owned-by-the-firm versus owned-by-the-adviser: J.P. Morgan seeds books and pays salary plus bonus; Morgan Stanley and Merrill pay a grid and advisers move with books. Open shelf versus house product: J.P. Morgan runs 850+ external alternatives managers alongside its own funds; some Swiss houses and Goldman lean more proprietary; independent advisers are fully open. Positions are the author's judgement from the firms' own descriptions [Inferred].

4.4 Where the firm does not lead, stated plainly

  • Not the largest wealth manager. Morgan Stanley has $9.3tn to $10tn of client assets and UBS $4.8tn of invested assets in wealth alone against AWM's $7.1tn across asset and wealth management [S154][S155][S316].
  • Not the winner of Euromoney's global title in 2025 or 2026. Goldman won in 2025; DBS in 2026 [S177][S242].
  • Not the biggest in Europe. International Private Bank client assets of roughly $750bn against UBS's $4.8tn; market share "below 1%" in several markets [S320][S316]. UBS's cost problem and Swiss capital fight are an opening, not a lead [S253].
  • Not the UK domestic bank. Coutts (now with Evelyn, £127bn), Barclays and HSBC own sterling current accounts and the "family bank" role; J.P. Morgan's UK retail arm (Chase UK, 2.8m customers) is not joined up with the Private Bank [S256][S163][S74].
  • Not the best absolute M&A adviser. Goldman out-earns the firm in pure advisory and had the record equities year in 2025 [S153].
  • Not free of conduct history. Part 2.9.
Don't say this
"J.P. Morgan is the biggest and the best at everything." It is neither, and Erdoes' own line is "the best in the industry for our clients, not the biggest". Overclaiming is the fastest way to lose an interviewer who works there.
