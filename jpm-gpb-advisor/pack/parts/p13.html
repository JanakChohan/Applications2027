Protocol (45 to 75 seconds)
  1. Steady: "Let me make sure I've understood." Restate.
  2. Sort: rule, client, process, speed. "First, is there anything here I can't do?"
  3. Act: verbs and people. "I'd check, then call, then bring in the Capital Advisor because..."
  4. Close: "And I'd log it and tell my team lead." Stop.
Priority order
  1. The rules and the firm's name
  2. The client's outcome; fair treatment of every colleague
  3. The process and the record
  4. Speed

"I'd rather be slow and right than fast and wrong, and I'd tell you that to your face."

The never list
  • Never confirm a client's name.
  • Never open before KYC is complete.
  • Never act on or repeat non-public information.
  • Never promise a loan or a return.
  • Never give regulated advice as an intern.
  • Never badmouth a rival, a colleague or a former employer.
  • Never say "biggest and best".
Counters
  • Interruption: let them finish; "good point, and..."
  • Contradiction: concede facts, never rules.
  • Silence: count three; offer to go deeper.
  • Escalation: acknowledge, restate the rule, offer the path.
  • Authority squeeze: "the moment we have X".
  • False choice: "I don't think those are the only two options."
  • Knowledge trap: "I don't know; my understanding is..."
  • Badmouth invite: "I'd rather say what I'd do differently."
