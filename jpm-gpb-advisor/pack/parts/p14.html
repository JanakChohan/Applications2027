Part 14

Glossary

Sixty-plus terms, each defined the way you would say it to a client.
Advisor (J.P. Morgan)
The client-facing relationship banker; elsewhere "private banker" or "relationship manager". The seat this internship leads to.
Advisory mandate
The bank recommends; the client approves each decision.
Alternatives
Investments outside listed shares, bonds and cash: private equity, private credit, hedge funds, real estate, infrastructure. Illiquid, higher fee.
AML
Anti-money-laundering: the rules and checks that stop criminal money entering the bank.
Appropriateness test
Under MiFID II, checking that a client understands a complex product bought without advice.
Asset management fees
Fees charged as a percentage of assets managed or advised; AWM's largest revenue line ($15.5bn in 2025).
AUM / client assets
Assets under management are money the firm actively invests; client assets add money it holds, advises on or custodies. Client assets is the broader, headline number.
AWM
Asset & Wealth Management, the JPMorgan Chase segment containing Asset Management and the Global Private Bank.
Basel 3.1
The final set of international bank capital rules; live in the UK from 1 January 2027.
Basis point (bp)
One hundredth of one percent. Fees, spreads and returns are quoted in it.
Brokerage / execution-only
The client instructs, the bank executes, no advice is given.
Capital Advisor
The Private Bank's lending specialist: mortgages, securities-based loans, specialty lending.
CCB
Consumer & Community Banking: Chase, the US retail bank, which also houses Chase's own wealth business and Chase UK.
CET1 ratio
Common Equity Tier 1 capital divided by risk-weighted assets; the core measure of a bank's safety. JPMorgan: 14.5% at end-2025.
CIB
Commercial & Investment Bank: the wholesale bank serving companies and institutions, including Commercial Banking since 2024.
CISI
Chartered Institute for Securities & Investment; awards the Level 4 Investment Advice Diploma and the Level 7 Chartered Wealth Manager qualification.
Client Service Associate
The team member who runs onboarding, documents, payments and meeting materials.
Connect Coach
J.P. Morgan's AI assistant for advisors: next-best-action prompts, meeting prep, talking points; 12,000 users.
Consumer Duty
FCA rules (in force 31 July 2023) requiring firms to deliver good outcomes for retail customers and avoid foreseeable harm.
Corporate trustee
A company (here a J.P. Morgan trust company) acting as trustee of a family's trust.
Discretionary mandate
The bank makes and executes investment decisions within parameters the client sets.
Drawdown fund
A private fund that calls committed capital over several years and returns it over about ten.
Elective professional client
A client who opts out of retail protections; the FCA proposes a £10m investable-assets route (CP25/36).
Evergreen fund
A private-markets fund invested up front with limited periodic redemptions; "semi-liquid".
Eye on the Market
Michael Cembalest's research note for the Private Bank, published since 2005.
Family office
A private company a very wealthy family sets up to manage its affairs; single- or multi-family.
FCA / PRA
The UK conduct regulator and the prudential regulator (part of the Bank of England), created 1 April 2013.
Fiduciary
A duty to act in the client's interest ahead of your own; AWM's "north star".
FIG regime
The four-year foreign income and gains exemption that replaced the non-dom regime from 6 April 2025.
Fortress balance sheet
Holding more capital and liquidity than required; Business Principle 5.
FSCS
The UK deposit guarantee scheme; J.P. Morgan SE London Branch participates.
Global Private Bank (GPB)
J.P. Morgan's business serving wealthy families worldwide; half of AWM's revenue.
HNW / UHNW
High-net-worth (over $1m investable by convention) and ultra-high-net-worth (over $30m).
HireVue
The recorded video interview platform; three to five questions, two-minute answers, one retry.
Information barrier
The controls that stop non-public information crossing between the investment bank and the private bank.
Inheritance tax (IHT)
UK tax on estates; now residence-based for worldwide assets after ten of twenty years' residence.
International Private Bank (IPB)
The non-US Private Bank, run from London; about 1,000 relationship managers.
Investor (J.P. Morgan)
The investment specialist who builds and runs client portfolios and leads the market conversation.
KYC
Know Your Customer: verifying identity and source of wealth before and during a relationship.
Liquidity event
The moment paper wealth becomes cash: a sale, an IPO, an inheritance.
Lombard loan
A loan secured on a portfolio of securities; also "securities-based lending".
Long-Term Capital Market Assumptions (LTCMA)
J.P. Morgan Asset Management's annual 10-to-15-year return forecasts used for strategic allocation.
Loan-to-value (LTV)
The loan as a share of the collateral's value; the lower, the safer.
Managed revenue
JPMorgan's non-GAAP revenue basis that grosses up tax-exempt income; used for all segment figures.
Margin call
A demand for more collateral or repayment when the value of pledged securities falls.
Market abuse
Insider dealing, unlawful disclosure or manipulation; a civil and criminal offence.
Market Manager
The commercial lead of a Private Bank market team; the Lending Advisor's counterpart.
MiFID II
EU and UK investment-services rules in force 3 January 2018: suitability, costs disclosure, product governance, call recording.
Net interest income (NII)
Interest earned on loans and securities minus interest paid on deposits; 28% of AWM revenue in 2025.
Net new assets / flows
Money in minus money out, excluding market moves; AWM's headline growth measure ($553bn in 2025).
Non-dom
The former UK status for residents domiciled abroad; abolished from 6 April 2025.
Open architecture
Offering external managers' products, not only the firm's own.
PEP
Politically exposed person; triggers enhanced due diligence.
Pretax margin
Pretax income divided by revenue; AWM 36% and the Private Bank 37% in 2025.
Private credit
Loans made by funds rather than banks; a $1.8tn market under stress in 2026.
RDR
The Retail Distribution Review (31 December 2012): commission ban, adviser charging, Level 4 qualification.
ROTCE / ROE
Return on tangible common equity (the firm's headline measure, 20% in 2025) and return on equity (AWM 40%).
Share of wallet
The fraction of a family's total wealth held with the firm; the aim is to be the primary bank.
SM&CR
The Senior Managers and Certification Regime: personal accountability, certified persons, Conduct Rules.
Source of wealth / source of funds
How the total fortune was made / where this particular money came from.
SpectrumIQ
AWM's AI platform that automated about 75% of equity trading and now runs proxy voting.
Strategic / tactical asset allocation
The long-run mix set from goals and the LTCMA / short-term tilts around it.
Structured note
A bank-issued note packaging derivatives to give income or capital protection.
Suitability
The MiFID II requirement that a recommendation fits the client's knowledge, finances and objectives, with the reasoning recorded.
Targeted support
The FCA's new regime (6 April 2026) for ready-made suggestions to groups of consumers without a personal recommendation.
Temporary Repatriation Facility
A 12% then 15% charge for bringing pre-2025 offshore income into the UK.
10-K
The annual report a US-listed company files with the SEC; the primary source for all segment numbers here.
23 Wall
The Private Bank's unit for the largest families and family offices; named after the old headquarters.
Wealth Advisor
The trusts, estates, tax-aware structuring and philanthropy specialist, often a former lawyer.
Wealth Management Solutions
The AWM unit that builds the advice and product platform shared by the Private Bank and Chase.
