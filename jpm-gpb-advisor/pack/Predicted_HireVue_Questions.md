## The prediction, ranked

The pattern across every reported J.P. Morgan AWM and Private Bank HireVue since 2024 is one motivation question, one or two behavioural questions, and one commercial-awareness question. The Private Bank version has no technical question; "technical" here means markets with a client lens.

### Tier 1: near-certain (prepare word-perfect shape, not word-perfect script)

1. **"What attracts you to J.P. Morgan, and specifically, an opportunity in Global Private Bank?"**
   Status: CONFIRMED as question 1 of the 2026 GPB HireVue [S269]; the AWM variant ("...an opportunity in Asset or Wealth Management?") is confirmed for 2025 [S278]. Bucket: soft-technical, where the offer is won. Tests: whether you understand the mechanism (referral engine, seeded books, balance sheet, the London international bank) or only the brand. Trap: "global leader", "prestige", "learning".

2. **"Describe what factors have influenced financial markets in recent months and how they might affect our clients."**
   Status: CONFIRMED wording for AWM 2025 [S278]; the London GPB report describes "commercial knowledge questions" in the same set [S272]. Bucket: soft-technical. Tests: the posting's "genuine interest in financial markets and macro-level economic trends", plus the client lens the Private Bank adds. Likely variants: "Tell us about a recent news article you read about financial markets" or "What is going on in the markets that would affect our clients?"

3. **One behavioural prompt from the teamwork family.**
   Most likely wording: "Please share an example of a time when you had to work as part of a team to accomplish an important goal, describing the situation, your actions, and the outcome." Status: REPORTED verbatim from a GPB London HireVue [S272]. A 2027-cycle variant reported in July 2026: a teamwork disagreement "where both positions were reasonable" [S286]. Bucket: behavioural, pass/fail. Tests: the posting's "enjoys working in teams" and the firm's "no side accounting groups" culture.

### Tier 2: likely (one of these will probably fill the second behavioural slot, if there is one)

4. **"Describe a time when you made a mistake at work or school, the situation, what actions you took, and what you learned."** REPORTED verbatim from a GPB HireVue [S272]. Tests Business Principle 12 ("we face facts") and the posting's "good judgment".

5. **"Tell me about a time when you were challenged to be persistent to reach an important goal."** REPORTED for J.P. Morgan generally [S279]. Tests the sales-resilience criterion ("desire to ultimately work with external clients in a relationship building and sales capacity").

6. **"Tell me about a time you handled a difficult client, customer or stakeholder."** REPORTED for J.P. Morgan and AWM [S279][S277]. Tests composure and empathy for a client-facing seat.

7. **"What do you think are the greatest challenges the financial services industry will face in the next five years?"** CONFIRMED for AWM 2025 [S278]. Bucket: soft-technical, industry-sentiment. Tests whether you have a long-horizon view (wealth transfer, alternatives, AI, fee compression, tax migration).

### Tier 3: possible (new 2027-cycle themes and JD-derived inferences)

8. **"Tell us about a time you evaluated an AI-generated answer you did not trust, and what you did."** REPORTED as a July 2026 theme in the 2027 cycle [S286]. Fits the firm's Connect Coach and "really smart AI and really smart advisors" line.

9. **"Describe a time you built trust with someone new, or persuaded someone to work with you."** INFERRED from "relationship building and sales capacity" and from the reported AWM list [S277]. The single most on-brand prompt for this seat if it appears.

10. **"How do you keep up to date with financial news, and what trends are you seeing?"** REPORTED in Private Bank superday accounts [S275]; possible in the HireVue as the commercial slot's lighter form.

11. **"Tell us about a time you handled sensitive or confidential information with discretion."** INFERRED from "good judgment and discretion when working with confidential information"; reported in the experienced-hire guide [S288].

12. **"How do you manage multiple deadlines or priorities under pressure?"** INFERRED from "excellent organizational skills and ability to multitask" and "thrive in a fast-paced environment"; reported in the AWM list [S277].

13. **"Tell us about a time you convinced someone using data."** REPORTED in the AWM list [S277]; tests "logical thinking and problem-solving".

14. **"What motivates you?" / "Tell us something not on your CV."** Generic J.P. Morgan bank; low probability for the Private Bank set but cheap to prepare.

### Unlikely for this programme

- A stock pitch or "where would you invest £1m" (reported at superday, not HireVue).
- Any modelling or accounting question.
- Brainteasers (the only one on any J.P. Morgan list is generic and no Private Bank candidate reports it).

## The most probable three-question set

| Slot | Prompt | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why J.P. Morgan and why Global Private Bank | Confirmed question 1 in 2026 |
| 2 | Teamwork to accomplish an important goal (or mistake and lesson) | Reported verbatim in a GPB London set |
| 3 | Factors influencing markets recently and how they affect our clients | Confirmed AWM wording; London set had a commercial question |

If there are five, expect the mistake question and the industry-challenges question to fill slots 4 and 5.

## Prep priorities, in order

1. Script the three near-certain answers to shape, with a 15-second content section each: the mechanism (Q1), your three factors with one client consequence each (Q2), the situation-action-result with your own "I" (Q3).
2. Have a bank of four behavioural stories that can be re-cut for teamwork, mistake, persistence, difficult person, trust and discretion: two stories can usually cover five prompts.
3. Refresh the commercial answer the week you record: gilt yield, Bank of England decision (17 September), Fed decision (16 September), oil, gold, AI concentration, and one J.P. Morgan house view (Mid-Year Outlook or Eye on the Market) so the answer sounds like the firm's own client conversation.
4. Use the retake as designed: first attempt on any unseen question is a throwaway.
5. Record each of the three scripted answers 15 to 20 times on camera before the real one.

## The question the whole set is really asking

Every prompt maps to one line in the posting: "desire to ultimately work with external clients in a relationship building and sales capacity", "genuine interest in financial markets and macro-level economic trends", and "highly driven person who enjoys working in teams". If each answer shows one of those three with a specific example and a number, the set is passed.
