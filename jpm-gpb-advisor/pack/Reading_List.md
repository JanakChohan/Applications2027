# Reading list: recurring sources to follow

Ordered by value for the J.P. Morgan Global Private Bank Advisor internship application (London, closes 1 November 2026).

## The firm's own voice
1. **Top Market Takeaways (weekly)** — https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/eur/en/insights/markets-and-investing/tmt — the language advisors use with clients this week.
2. **Eye on the Market (Michael Cembalest)** — https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/nam/en/insights/latest-and-featured/eotm — data-heavy and contrarian; the 2026 pieces on AI concentration and energy.
3. **Outlook and Mid-Year Outlook** — https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/eur/en/insights/latest-and-featured/outlook — the three themes (fragmentation, inflation, AI) you will be asked about.
4. **Global Family Office Report 2026** — https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/eur/en/insights/reports/2026-family-office-report — what the top of the client book holds and fears.
5. **Quarterly earnings** — https://www.jpmorganchase.com/ir/quarterly-earnings — Q3 2026 results are due in mid-October, before the deadline; read the AWM paragraph.
6. **Annual report 2025 (Dimon and Erdoes letters)** — https://www.jpmorganchase.com/ir/annual-report — strategy and values in the firm's own words.
7. **Investor Day 2025 AWM transcript** — https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/events/2025/jpmc-2025-investor-day/awm.pdf — the "path to $10 trillion" and the advisor economics.
8. **J.P. Morgan Private Bank EMEA: people and locations pages** — https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/eur/en/about-us/our-leadership — names and titles, kept current.

## Press
9. **Reuters, finance** — https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/ — fast and unpaywalled on JPM, UBS, the FCA and the Bank of England.
10. **Citywire Wealth Manager** — https://citywire.com/wealth-manager — UK hires and team moves, including J.P. Morgan UK (partly paywalled).
11. **WealthBriefing / Family Wealth Report** — https://www.wealthbriefing.com — free summaries of every private bank's results.
12. **Private Banker International** — https://www.privatebankerinternational.com — sector news incl. Julius Baer, HSBC, Coutts.
13. **Euromoney Private Banking Awards** — https://www.euromoney.com/awards/private-banking-awards — the citations describe each bank's strategy in its own words.
14. **FT Wealth and Alphaville** — https://www.ft.com/wealth — UK wealth-tax and non-dom reporting; private credit (paywalled).
15. **Bloomberg Odd Lots (podcast)** — https://www.bloomberg.com/oddlots — the plumbing of private credit, explained.
16. **eFinancialCareers** — https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news — hiring trends and programme intelligence.
17. **Spear's** — https://spearswms.com — UK HNW adviser rankings and the non-dom debate.

## Regulators and official data
18. **FCA news and publications** — https://www.fca.org.uk/news — targeted support, CP25/36 final rules, Consumer Duty findings, fines.
19. **HM Treasury and HMRC** — https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-treasury — the primary text of tax changes; watch for the Autumn Budget 2026 date.
20. **Bank of England Monetary Policy Summary** — https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/monetary-policy-summary-and-minutes — next decision 17 September 2026; the vote split matters.

## How to use this list in the next seven weeks
- Weekly: Top Market Takeaways, the Bank of England if it meets, Reuters headlines on JPM and UBS.
- Once: the Erdoes letter, the Investor Day AWM transcript, the Mid-Year Outlook, the Family Office Report.
- Before the interview: the latest Eye on the Market, the Q3 results (mid-October), the FCA page for CP25/36 final rules.
