What this event is, and what it is not

You were selected from a CV screen for a recruiting event run by the International Private Bank's early-careers team. The public listing promises four things: meeting "Bankers, Investors and Senior Leaders from the London office", a session on "the 2027 Private Banking opportunities and the recruiting process", networking, and "an interactive client scenario designed to give you insight into the work of the Private Bank" [S700]. Eligibility is tied to the 2027 summer internship, whose London applications close on 1 November 2026 [S746][S748].

Treat it as the first round of the internship process in everything but name. There is no public account of a previous "Inside Private Banking" day, in London or any other city, so the exact agenda and speakers are not known [S700]. The closest documented comparators are the US "Morning@Morgan" office visits (panels with senior and junior bankers, networking, a recruiting overview) and the Asia "Private Bank Challenge", where teams of four work a hypothetical client case and present to judges [S743][S744][S821]. Expect the London scenario to be a team exercise: profile a family or founder, map the balance sheet, decide which specialists to bring in, present.

The one rule for the day
Every host will meet twenty students who say they are "passionate about markets". The ones they remember ask a question that could only come from someone who has read what the bank itself has published. This document gives you that reading. Part 4 turns it into questions. Use three of them well rather than ten badly.

The recruiting process they will describe

The London Advisor summer internship: nine weeks, June to August, penultimate-year students, application closes 1 November 2026 on a rolling basis; CV and questions, then a HireVue video interview, then a hiring-manager review and, for most, a final-round interview day [S746][S743]. The US campus page, the most detailed public description, gives the summer shape as a first week of welcome with the whole AWM cohort, weeks two to five of training with an assessment workstream, and weeks six to nine of a senior speaker series and a capstone presentation; then a roadmap from Summer Analyst to Incoming Analyst (licensing), Analyst years one and two (portfolio reviews, business development, client presentations), Associate (a business plan, mentoring) and Senior Associate (an independent portfolio) [S743]. The London full-time programme opens with a "five-week immersive global training program" with incoming analysts from around the world [S748]. London runs a separate Investment Solutions internship and analyst programme for the "Investor" track, described as "our main source of homegrown talent" [S747][S749].

If they ask whether you have applied
"Yes, for the Advisor internship, and I have the HireVue recorded." If you have not yet, apply tonight. "I am applying this week" is an acceptable answer on Monday only if it is true by Tuesday.
