# Mayer Brown Masterclass 2026: question bank

Scaffolds, not scripts. Built 8 October 2026 from public sources; [Sxx] markers resolve to Part 15 of the main pack.

## Motivation

### 1. Why commercial law?
- **Really testing:** Whether you understand what commercial lawyers actually do, and why you'd choose it over banking or consulting.
- **Structure:** One experience; what it showed you about the work (precision, advising on real money, being trusted with detail); why advising rather than deciding.
- **Facts to weave in:** Lawyers sell advice and execution (Part 5).
- **Trap:** "I like problem solving" or "I like reading".

### 2. Why not the Bar?
- **Really testing:** Whether you understand the solicitor/barrister split.
- **Structure:** Teamwork on a matter from start to finish; long client relationships; transactional work has no Bar equivalent.
- **Facts to weave in:** Barristers are mostly advocates instructed by solicitors (Part 5).
- **Trap:** Running down the Bar.

### 3. Why Mayer Brown?
- **Really testing:** Mechanism, not adjectives.
- **Structure:** Use Part 12: one structural fact, one product, one personal fit.
- **Facts to weave in:** Top tier in derivatives, ABL, trade finance [S139]; ~15 trainees [S16]; Ford buy-in [S310].
- **Trap:** "Global firm with a diverse client base" (the event page's own sentence).

### 4. Why a US firm rather than the Magic Circle?
- **Really testing:** Whether you understand firm types.
- **Structure:** Same pay, smaller cohort, transatlantic platform; but honest that MC firms lead in some areas.
- **Facts to weave in:** £150k NQ matches MC [S234]; cohort 15 vs 70-100 [S120].
- **Trap:** Claiming US pay.

### 5. Why this masterclass?
- **Really testing:** Whether you've read the agenda and the firm.
- **Structure:** Name the agenda item you most want (practice deep dive), the question you'd bring, and the next step (vac scheme by 4 Dec).
- **Facts to weave in:** Vac scheme deadline 4 Dec 2026 [S1].
- **Trap:** Treating it as a free day out.

### 6. Which practice area interests you, and why?
- **Really testing:** Whether your interest is specific and genuine.
- **Structure:** Pick one where the firm is strong; explain the client's problem it solves; show you'd stay open across four seats.
- **Facts to weave in:** Derivatives T1, ABL T1, PRT deals [S139][S310].
- **Trap:** Picking M&A (tier 6) as your reason for applying here.

### 7. What would you do if you didn't get a training contract?
- **Really testing:** Resilience and seriousness.
- **Structure:** Reapply with what you learned; build relevant experience; stay in the profession.
- **Facts to weave in:** SQE and QWE routes (Part 6).
- **Trap:** "I'd do banking instead."

## Commercial awareness

### 15. Tell us about a commercial story you've followed.
- **Really testing:** Can you turn news into legal work?
- **Structure:** Headline; what changes for clients; what legal work it creates; your view; one risk.
- **Facts to weave in:** Part 7 stories 10-12.
- **Trap:** Summary without opinion.

### 16. What does Basel 3.1 mean for banks and their lawyers?
- **Really testing:** Technical link from rule to work.
- **Structure:** Capital up per loan; banks transfer risk; SRT and synthetic securitisation; Mayer Brown SRT award.
- **Facts to weave in:** PS1/26: 1 Jan 2027 [S190]; [S148]
- **Trap:** Getting the date wrong.

### 17. Why are pension schemes buying insurance now?
- **Really testing:** Market mechanism.
- **Structure:** Higher gilt yields improved funding; insurers compete; record 2025; PSA 2026 changes.
- **Facts to weave in:** 367 buy-ins, £38.2bn in 2025 [S201]; PSA 2026 [S200].
- **Trap:** Confusing buy-in and buy-out.

### 18. Is private credit a risk?
- **Really testing:** Balanced view.
- **Structure:** Growth ($3tn to $11tn); BoE stress exercise; benefits to borrowers; opacity and leverage risks.
- **Facts to weave in:** [S208][S243]
- **Trap:** One-sided answer.

### 19. Should UK securitisation rules be loosened?
- **Really testing:** Opinion with both sides.
- **Structure:** For: compliance cost, competitiveness, US CLO access. Against: 2008 memory, private credit warnings. Your view.
- **Facts to weave in:** CP26/6 and CP2/26, 17 Feb 2026 [S194].
- **Trap:** Not knowing what risk retention is.

### 20. London is the financial hub of the world. Discuss.
- **Really testing:** Reported Mayer Brown interview question.
- **Structure:** English law, time zone, talent; vs New York, listings drain, post-Brexit. Your view.
- **Facts to weave in:** IPO rebound from low base [S244]; UK legal market £52.3bn [S164].
- **Trap:** No view.

### 21. What are the threats to the UK economy?
- **Really testing:** Reported question.
- **Structure:** Two or three: energy and inflation, debt, growth; link one to legal work.
- **Facts to weave in:** Bank Rate 3.75%, CPI forecast ~4% [S236].
- **Trap:** A list with no ranking.

### 22. What law would you change?
- **Really testing:** Reported question.
- **Structure:** One specific law; the problem; your change; the trade-off.
- **Facts to weave in:** Could use failure to prevent fraud [S203] or SQE rules.
- **Trap:** Something vast and vague.

### 23. Why are law firms merging?
- **Really testing:** Industry structure.
- **Structure:** Scale, US access for UK firms, cross-selling; costs of integration; MB chose not to.
- **Facts to weave in:** A&OS 2024; HSF Kramer 2025; HLC 2026 [S174][S175][S240].
- **Trap:** Saying all firms must merge.

### 24. How will AI change a trainee's job?
- **Really testing:** Nuance.
- **Structure:** Less first-draft volume; more checking and judgement; clients expect it; training must change.
- **Facts to weave in:** Susskind vs TR views [S183][S167]; MB human review [S230].
- **Trap:** "Trainees won't be needed."

## Competency

### 25. Tell me about a time you showed attention to detail.
- **Really testing:** The core trainee skill.
- **Structure:** STAR, with a number; what would have gone wrong; the habit you built.
- **Facts to weave in:** GM filing error as why it matters [S102].
- **Trap:** A trivial example.

### 26. Tell me about working under pressure.
- **Really testing:** Composure.
- **Structure:** Situation, competing demands, how you prioritised, outcome, what you'd change.
- **Facts to weave in:** Hours 9:26am-8:20pm average [S15].
- **Trap:** Saying you never feel pressure.

### 27. Tell me about a mistake.
- **Really testing:** Honesty and learning.
- **Structure:** Real mistake; your share; how you told someone; the fix; the habit.
- **Facts to weave in:** SRA Code 7.11 honesty when things go wrong [S342].
- **Trap:** A disguised strength.

### 28. Tell me about a time you showed initiative.
- **Really testing:** Reported question.
- **Structure:** Something you started without being asked, within limits, and how you checked.
- **Trap:** Initiative that ignored authority.

### 29. Tell me about a time you disagreed with someone.
- **Really testing:** Handling conflict.
- **Structure:** Facts, how you raised it, outcome, relationship after.
- **Trap:** Badmouthing.

### 30. Tell me about a team you led or were part of.
- **Really testing:** Teamwork.
- **Structure:** Your specific role; one hard moment; result.
- **Trap:** "We" throughout.

### 31. What makes an effective or ineffective leader?
- **Really testing:** Reported question.
- **Structure:** Two traits each, with an example you observed.
- **Trap:** Generic list.

### 32. How would you stand out as a trainee?
- **Really testing:** Reported question.
- **Structure:** Concrete behaviours: reliable checklists, early flags, asking good questions, pro bono.
- **Facts to weave in:** Got20 [S16].
- **Trap:** "By working harder than everyone."

### 33. A task you disliked: how did you handle it?
- **Really testing:** Reported question.
- **Structure:** Do it well anyway; find the learning; never let quality drop.
- **Trap:** Complaining.

- **Facts to weave in:** Fact-find described in 2020 accounts [S21].

## Curveball

### 41. What's something you've changed your mind about?
- **Really testing:** Intellectual honesty.
- **Structure:** Old view, new fact, new view.
- **Trap:** No real change.

### 42. What would you do in your first week?
- **Really testing:** Realism.
- **Structure:** Learn systems, meet the team, ask how they like work delivered, keep a checklist.
- **Trap:** Grand plans.

### 43. Isn't [rival] better than us?
- **Really testing:** Frame-holding under an invitation to badmouth.
- **Structure:** Praise rival precisely; state your fit here.
- **Facts to weave in:** Part 4 lines.
- **Trap:** Agreeing, or attacking.

## Ten questions to ask

1. **Trainee panel:** Chambers Student says you rank up to five seats and must do one transactional seat. How did you decide what to put first, and how much did business need change it? [S16]
2. **Trainee panel:** In your finance seat, how much of the work was on repeat programmes for the same banks, and did that change what you were trusted with? [S16][S220]
3. **Trainee panel:** The firm made human review of every AI output mandatory. In practice, what do you check, and how did you learn to check it? [S230]
4. **Practice deep dive:** Derivatives and structured products reached the Legal 500 top tier this year. How much of that work is transatlantic, and where do trainees fit in? [S139]
5. **Practice deep dive:** Basel 3.1 starts on 1 January 2027. Are you already seeing more significant risk transfer work from UK banks? [S190][S148]
6. **Practice deep dive:** With deal counts down but values up in buy-ins, is insurer documentation becoming standardised, and where does that leave the lawyer? [S237]
7. **Partner / leadership:** The chair talks about going deeper with fewer clients. What does that mean for the London finance group's client mix? [S220]
8. **Partner / leadership:** Several rivals chose transatlantic mergers, including Cadwalader. What does a firm of Mayer Brown's size gain by staying independent? [S240]
9. **Partner / leadership:** London has hired leveraged finance teams from Dechert, Kirkland and Baker McKenzie. How do they work with the bank-side finance teams? [S280][S327][S226]
10. **Graduate recruitment:** What separates vacation schemers who get offers from those who don't, beyond what's on the website? [S17]
