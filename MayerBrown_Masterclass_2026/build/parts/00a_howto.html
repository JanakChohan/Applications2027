The 10-minute emergency version

1 · What the event isA four-hour SEO London masterclass at Mayer Brown, Monday 2 November 2026, for 60 candidates: firm overview, trainee panel, practice deep dive, applications overview, networking. Apply to SEO by 26 October [S340]. It sits a month before Mayer Brown's vacation scheme deadline of 4 December 2026, and vacation schemes are "the main pipeline" for training contracts [S1][S17].
2 · The firm in four linesFounded Chicago 1881; London is the old Rowe & Maw practice (1895), merged in 2002 [S70]. 2025: revenue $2.17bn, profit per equity partner $3.196m; London revenue a record $268.2m, up 20.5% [S220][S302]. Strategy: "go deeper with fewer clients", grow by hiring teams, not by merging [S220]. New London Managing Partner Chris Harvey from 1 October 2026 [S222].
3 · What it is best at in LondonLegal 500 top tier: asset-based lending, derivatives and structured products (new this year), trade finance, commercial property for occupiers, construction disputes [S139]. Securitisation and pensions are tier 2 [S141][S145].
4 · The trainee dealAbout 15 a year; four six-month seats (at least one transactional, at most two contentious); £56k then £61k; NQ £150k; SQE at BPP with a £20k grant; 80% retention [S2][S16].
5 · Your 30-second "why""The work: top tier in London for derivatives and asset-based lending. The shape: about fifteen trainees, so real work. The moment: London grew 20% by going deeper with fewer clients, and Basel 3.1 in January will drive risk transfer work the firm is known for." (Part 12)
