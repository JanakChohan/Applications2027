Where Mayer Brown does not lead: never overclaim

Don't say this
  • "Mayer Brown leads London securitisation." It is tier 2; Clifford Chance, Latham, Linklaters, Sidley and A&O Shearman are tier 1 [S141]. The US ABS awards are a US claim.
  • "It's a top pensions firm." London pensions is tier 2; HLC, A&O Shearman, Linklaters, Sackers and others are tier 1 [S145].
  • "It's number one US securitisation counsel." In 2025 Hunton ranked first as issuer counsel by deal count (159 deals); Mayer Brown was second (146 deals) but led on dollar volume [S124][S147].
  • "It pays like a US firm." No: £150k NQ against £175k at Sidley, Kirkland and White & Case [S120].
  • "It's an M&A powerhouse." London premium M&A is tier 6 [S146].
  • "It has the highest PEP" or "the best retention". Neither [S121][S129]-[S137].
