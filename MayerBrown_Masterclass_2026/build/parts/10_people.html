Reading the interviewer live

Practitioner (partner, associate)Wants to see how you think about a client's problem. Go one level deeper on content; use the deal or rule they mentioned; accept correction gracefully; ask "what is the lawyer actually checking there?"
Recruiter (graduate recruitment)Wants evidence of fit and process readiness. Be concise, organised and specific about your timeline; show you've read the site; follow up in writing.
TraineeRemembers good questions and often feeds back. Be curious and kind; never compete with other candidates in front of them.
Leader (managing partner)Thinks in strategy. One sharp question about direction, then listen.

Signals to adapt to: if they lean in and ask a follow-up, go deeper; if they glance at the room, wrap up in one sentence; if they correct you, thank them and use the correction in your next sentence.
