Goldman Sachs Asset Management, Client Solutions Group: study platform

A research and learning project for the 2027 | EMEA | London | Asset Management, Client Solutions Group | Summer Analyst programme. It is built to help you understand the business, test your own motivation and practise explaining both. It does not predict interview questions and it cannot guarantee an outcome.

Three layers are kept apart everywhere: Layer A · verified fact what credible sources establish; Layer B · interpretation what those facts suggest, with reasoning and limits; Layer C · personal fit what might appeal to you, which only your own evidence can settle.

What this platform cannot tell you

  • Goldman Sachs's internal structure, fees, headcount, scoring criteria or future interview questions. Where the only source is the former intern's call, the claim is marked Unverified account.
  • Whether you would enjoy the work. The why-trees and the personal ledger are designed to help you find out, not to answer for you.
  • Anything after [[research_date]]. Titles, policies and figures change; check the source and its date before quoting anything.

Teams and coverage

The group can be cut by client geography, by client segment, by product line and by job function, and those cuts overlap. The default view is a matrix because a single hierarchy would claim more than the evidence supports. Team names that come only from the former intern's call are marked as an unverified account.

    Mandates and commercial events

    Publicly documented wins, losses, appointments, partnerships and launches. A fund launch, a fundraising target, a commitment, an awarded mandate and funded fee-paying assets are different things, and the "kind" field keeps them apart. Public announcements rarely say which team or person originated a deal, so attribution is left as "not publicly established" unless a source states it.

      Fee calculator

      Illustrative only

      This shows how management-fee arithmetic works. Every rate is an input you choose. Nothing here is a Goldman Sachs fee, and the result is gross management fee before costs, rebates, distribution payments, performance fees and every other adjustment. It is not profit and it is not revenue attributable to any one team.

      How the model works, and what it leaves out
      1. Flows land at the start of a month. A withdrawal cannot take the balance below zero.
      2. The month's fee is the annual fee on that month's fee base, divided by twelve. With tiers, each slice of the base is charged at its own rate.
      3. The market return is applied at the end of the month, compounded monthly.
      4. Fees are assumed to be invoiced separately rather than deducted from the assets.

      Left out on purpose: performance fees and carried interest, rebates and distribution payments, the manager's costs, daily accrual conventions, minimum fees, most-favoured-nation clauses and tax. For private-markets funds, read the fund terms to find the real fee base before using either option.
