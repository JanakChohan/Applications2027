How the model works
  1. Flows land at the start of a month. A withdrawal cannot take the balance below zero.
  2. The month's fee is the yearly fee on that month's fee base, divided by twelve.
  3. The market return is applied at the end of the month.
  4. Fees are assumed to be billed separately, not taken from the assets.

Left out on purpose: performance fees, rebates, costs, minimum fees and tax.
