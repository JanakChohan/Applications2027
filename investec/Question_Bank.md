# Investec Summer Internship 2027: Question Bank

Prepared 5 October 2026. Scaffolds, not scripts. [Sxx] markers resolve in Part 15 of the complete pack. HireVue and team-round questions marked "Reported" come from Glassdoor/WSO anecdotes [S40, S41].

## Motivation and fit

### 1. Why Investec?
- **Tests:** Have you understood what is distinctive about this firm, as a mechanism?
- **Structure:** 30-second version from Part 12: one bank for the founder and the company; mid-market; a business being built.
- **Weave in:** "Only integrated and diversified mid-market focused specialist bank" [S121]; new Corporate Bank 2026 [S268]; private-client plan [S89].
- **Trap:** Adjectives ("prestigious", "entrepreneurial") with no fact behind them.

### 2. Why banking, and why now?
- **Tests:** Motivation and self-knowledge.
- **Structure:** One moment that drew you in, what you tested since, why an internship is the right next test.
- **Weave in:** FT session in the JD [S1].
- **Trap:** "Because it pays well."

### 3. Why would you give up your summer for an internship?
- **Tests:** Reported HireVue question [S41]: energy and honesty.
- **Structure:** What you want to learn, why eight weeks inside a team beats reading about it.
- **Weave in:** "Real responsibilities" [S1].
- **Trap:** Sounding reluctant.

### 4. Which three teams would you like to join, and why?
- **Tests:** Reported HR-round question [S41]. Research depth.
- **Structure:** Rank three; for each, what the team does and why it fits you.
- **Weave in:** Fund Solutions, Corporate Banking, Private Bank, a lending niche (Part 1 table).
- **Trap:** Naming Wealth & Investment (now Rathbones) [S78].

### 5. Why not a bulge-bracket bank?
- **Tests:** Did you choose Investec?
- **Structure:** Placement in one team, small enough to see seniors, mid-market clients.
- **Weave in:** Bank of about 2,400 [S278].
- **Trap:** Badmouthing big banks.

### 6. Why not Close Brothers or Shawbrook?
- **Tests:** Competitive knowledge.
- **Structure:** They lend; Investec lends, advises, brokers and banks the founder. Admit their efficiency.
- **Weave in:** S140, S142, S121.
- **Trap:** Claiming Investec has higher returns. It does not.

### 7. What does "Out of the Ordinary" mean to you?
- **Tests:** Have you looked past the slogan?
- **Structure:** Link to the consensus-veto story and to "material ownership" culture.
- **Weave in:** S83, S278.
- **Trap:** Reciting the tagline.

### 8. Tell us about yourself.
- **Tests:** Structure and story.
- **Structure:** Present, past, why here, in 60 to 90 seconds.
- **Weave in:** One Investec-specific line at the end.
- **Trap:** A chronological CV recital.

### 20. How does Investec make money?
- **Tests:** Basic bank economics.
- **Structure:** NII 58.6%, non-interest revenue 41.4%; costs; credit losses; profit £951.0m.
- **Weave in:** S70.
- **Trap:** Guessing numbers.

### 21. How do interest rates affect Investec?
- **Tests:** Two-sided thinking.
- **Structure:** Margin up when rates rise; credit stress and deposit cost too. NII fell 1.6% in FY26.
- **Weave in:** S70, S230.
- **Trap:** "Higher rates are good for banks", full stop.

### 22. What is the biggest risk to Investec's UK business?
