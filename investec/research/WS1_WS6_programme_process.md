# Investec (UK) Summer Internship 2027 (req. 14049): Programme, Process and People
Workstreams WS1, WS1B, WS6, WS7. Researched 2026-10-05. Source IDs S1-S39 (WS1/WS1B), S40-S69 (WS6/WS7).

Tags: **[Confirmed]** = read on a primary page I fetched (mostly Investec's own careers site). **[Reported]** = stated by a third party, or only seen in a search-result snippet of a page I could not open. **[Inferred]** = my own reasoning from the evidence.

**Access notes (read first).** investec.com is behind a Cloudflare bot challenge, so every investec.com/en_gb careers page returned HTTP 403. The Wayback Machine was blocked by egress policy. Glassdoor, WallStreetOasis, Prosple, Indeed and LinkedIn also returned 403 or 999. Anything from those sites comes from search snippets and is marked SNIPPET. careers.investec.co.uk (the eArcu ATS) could be fetched. Its job-search results page is behind an AWS WAF challenge, but individual vacancy pages load. I found that a vacancy's URL ID equals its requisition number plus 18 (14049 maps to /14067/), then scanned vacancy IDs 13900 to 14200 to list current postings (S21).

## 1. Findings

### WS1: What the programme is

1. **The live posting exists and matches the brief** [Confirmed, S1]. The title is "Summer Internship 2027 (14049)". Location: London, 30 Gresham Street EC2V 7QN. Department: People & Organisation. Division: IBP Business Enablement. Full time. Salary: "Competitive". Banner: "Applications will open at 09:00am on 5th October for 24 hours (subject to volume of applications)".
2. **Structure** [Confirmed, S1]: 8 weeks. Open to penultimate and final-year university students on any degree. Interns are placed in "a team within our Specialist Bank". The best-aligned team is agreed "during your HR interview from the range of business areas hosting an Internship position". Interns are embedded in one team, but Investec aims to "provide you with a clear overview of our entire business".
3. **Learning content** [Confirmed, S1]: presentations and talks from experienced staff across the business, networking, an FT session on absorbing complex information, a session on financial analysis, and Excel courses.
4. **Start month and dates.** The internship starts in July [Reported, S7/S10/S11 snippets: "Summer internships begin in July and run for eight weeks"]. Exact 2027 dates are not published in S1. A third-party page lists 6 July to 28 August 2026 at £650/week, but I traced that listing to a different (wealth-management) employer and excluded it (S30/S31).
5. **Pay.** S1 says only "Competitive". Glassdoor's salary estimates are very noisy (section 2) [Reported, S42]. There is no reliable public figure.
6. **A second, parallel 2027 posting** [Confirmed, S2]: "Summer Internship 2027 – 10000 Black Interns Programme (14116)". It has the same department, division and body text, plus "in conjunction with the 10000 Interns Foundation". The 24-hour banner is not shown on that page. For the 2025 and 2026 cycles, snippets show the "in conjunction with the 10000 Interns Foundation" wording inside the main posting [Reported, S6/S13]. For 2027 it has been split into its own requisition [Confirmed for 2027].
7. **"IBP" = Investec Bank plc** [Reported, S23: "Investec Bank plc (IBP) is the principal banking subsidiary of Investec plc"]. The ATS uses "IBP Business Enablement" as a division label for central functions too. For example, "UK Equities Client Asset Operations Junior Analyst (13997)" is Department "Business Enablement", Division "IBP Business Enablement" [Confirmed, S19]. So the internship requisition is held administratively by HR (People & Organisation) inside the IBP central-functions division. It does not mean interns work in HR [Inferred].
8. **How the programme changed by year:**
   - 2024 cycle: "CVs can be submitted from October 16th 2023"; 8 weeks; "curious, energetic, and enterprising" [Confirmed, S14, Investec LinkedIn post].
   - 2025 cycle: opened Monday 7 October 2024 and closed Sunday 13 October 2024, "subject to the volume of applications" (about one week) [Reported, S12 fetched / S13 snippet]. The body text (Specialist Bank, HR interview, FT/Excel, entrepreneurial/self-starter/be you) is essentially the same as 2027 [Confirmed by comparing S12 with S1].
   - 2026 cycle: req. 12452, run with the 10000 Interns Foundation; applications closed around 14 October 2025, subject to volume; July 2026 start [Reported, S6 + Glassdoor job-listing snippet]. The ATS page for 12452 now shows "Vacancy Closed" [Confirmed, S5].
   - 2027 cycle: the window is now **24 hours** from 09:00 on 5 October 2026 [Confirmed, S1]. The window has shrunk from about a week to one day, which points to rising application volumes [Inferred]. Trackr predicted 12 October 2026, based on the prior cycle; Investec opened a week earlier [Confirmed S15 vs S1].
9. **Conversion** [Reported, S7/S9/S24 snippets]: "Many of our interns have been allowed to rejoin the business in an early career role." Named example: Lucas Hunt, a Mechanical Engineering graduate, was a 2022 summer intern and returned in 2023 as an analyst in Fund Solutions. **The rotational graduate programme is paused**: "we have decided to pause our rotational graduate programmes for now while we redefine our early career offerings in the UK"; summer internships "remain as usual" and other roles are "advertised on an ad-hoc basis" [Reported, S8/S9/S11 snippets]. So there is no standard intern-to-grad-scheme pipeline. Return offers would be into specific entry-level team roles [Inferred]. One Glassdoor intern lists as a con the "Lack of graduate programme restricts junior development" [Reported, S43].
10. **Other UK early-careers products** [Confirmed/Reported]:
    - Navigate, "a three-day experience of the world of work" for final-year students, listed with the internship under UK "Exposure Programmes" [Reported, S10 snippet; the UK status for 2026/27 is unknown].
    - A Springpod virtual work experience for ages 16-21, running 23 June 2026 to 23 June 2027, with modules on the Specialist Banking Business, Technology in Banking, Sustainability in Banking, Employee Experience and Employability [Confirmed, S16].
    - A historic Springpod "Investec Insight Programme" (2021) [Confirmed, S17].

    - The careers events page currently lists no events [Confirmed, S18].
    - **No UK spring week, industrial placement, or separate CIB/Private Bank/Treasury/Technology internship was found for 2027.** The vacancy-ID scan (S21) found only the two summer-internship requisitions as current early-careers postings. The scan returned blanks for many IDs, which could be rate-limiting, so this is [Confirmed for IDs that loaded; incomplete coverage].
    - The careers home page shows an "Early Careers" category with "1 vacancy" [Confirmed, S3]. This is presumably one of 14049/14116. The second may be filed under another category [Inferred].
    - Outside the UK: New York ran a separate 10-week "Summer Internship 2025 – Securities Desk" [Reported, search snippet of a careers.investec.co.uk page]. Dublin has hired a "Graduate Trainee Dealer" (12078) [Reported snippet]. Neither is relevant to req. 14049.

### WS6: Interview process

**Official process** [Reported: investec.com S7/S8/S11 snippets, consistent across several snippets]:
1. "You submit your CV on the application portal when vacancies open in October." Applications go through the ATS (eArcu) "Apply Now" [Confirmed, S1].
2. "If shortlisted, you will receive a link to complete a video application."
3. "Selected candidates are then invited to an interview with the Early Careers Lead",
4. "followed by a panel interview with the respective team you have chosen to join."

S1 calls stage 3 the "HR interview", where the team is chosen [Confirmed].

**What candidates report** [Reported, forum anecdote; Glassdoor/WSO snippets S40, S41, S44]:
- **Video stage = HireVue.** "3 questions with one minute to prepare and one minute to answer for each question, with one practice run in the beginning." Questions are "mostly strengths based with no technical questions".
- Reported HireVue questions: "If you had to have dinner with one person, who would it be?"; "If you would name one person who inspires you, who would it be and why?"; "Why would you want to take time off your summer holiday to do an internship?"; "Tell us one thing about yourself that we wouldn't think to ask"; "Why Investec?"
- **HR interview**: "HR screening call with two people – 30 minutes". It covers "general motivational questions and personality questions". You are asked to name your "top 3 teams you'd like to intern in and why".
- **Team interview**: "hiring manager interview – 30 minutes"; "interviewed by two people on the chosen team"; one reviewer mentions "an analyst"; "nothing technical, just situational questions such as giving a time you had to juggle multiple deadlines, and why you want to work in Investec, what you could add to the company". Reviewers describe it as "more of a conversation than an interview". They say Investec doesn't "focus on what you have on paper but how you will fit into the Investec team".
- **Metrics**: difficulty 2.1/5; 75% positive experience (company average 66.2%); an average of 52 days to hire; one candidate's process took 3 weeks.
- **Negatives**: "HR was very communicative and prompt", but "some interviewers were not interested in interviewing".
- **Online tests or assessment centre?** None in Investec's own process description [Reported S7/S8]. One aggregator (S26) claims online tests and an assessment centre, but that page is generic, SA-focused and AI-style filler. **Treat it as unreliable** [Inferred]. No UK candidate snippet mentions numerical/verbal tests, Pymetrics or an AC.
- **Interviewer mix** [Inferred from the above]: round 1 is HR/early-careers (the Early Careers Lead, possibly with a Chapter 2 talent partner, which would explain "two people"). Round 2 is business-side: 1-2 people from the allocated team, possibly an analyst or associate, possibly the hiring manager.

**Timeline for this cycle** [Inferred]: the window opens 09:00 on 5 October and may close early. HireVue invitations would follow in October. On the reported 3-week to 52-day range, HR and team interviews would fall around late October to December 2026.

## 6. Interview angles

1. **Pick your top 3 teams before the HR interview.** Candidates report being asked for three teams and why. Give a reason tied to each team's real business. Examples: Fund Solutions (NAV and subscription lines to PE funds); Growth & Acquisition Finance or Direct Lending (mid-market sponsor deals); Aviation, Real Estate or Energy & Infrastructure (asset-backed specialist sectors); Private Bank (HNW/"high income" clients); Treasury dealing. Show that you understand "Specialist Bank" means a mid-market, specialist lender and adviser, not a bulge bracket.
2. **Use the JD's three "About you" bullets as your STAR answers.** "Take a project and make it your own" = a self-starter story. "Fresh ideas" = something you changed or started. "Passions and interests" = a genuine non-CV interest. Reviewers say Investec cares about "how you will fit" more than "what you have on paper".
3. **Prepare for HireVue-style personality prompts** in 60 seconds. Prepare short answers on who inspires you, a dinner guest, something we wouldn't think to ask you, and why you would give up your summer for an internship.
4. **"Why Investec?" angle.** Use the founder/entrepreneur culture ("founded by entrepreneurs… energy and optimism"), the flat structure, "Out of the Ordinary", the Gresham Street hub, and the chance to own real work in a smaller cohort. Avoid pitching it as an investment-banking stepping stone.
5. **Show you read the learning plan.** Mention the FT session (bring an FT story you have actually followed, such as UK mid-market lending or private credit growth), financial analysis, and Excel.
6. **Ask informed questions.** Ask how return offers work now that the rotational graduate programme is paused (ad-hoc entry-level roles). Ask what an intern in your chosen team typically owns by week 8.
7. **Expect two interviewers** in each live round. The team round may include an analyst. The tone is conversational and situational (for example, juggling deadlines), not technical. Keep one or two light technicals ready anyway (reading a P&L, what a covenant is) for business-side curiosity.
8. **Don't confuse the businesses.** Investec Wealth & Investment UK is no longer part of the Specialist Bank (my background knowledge, not sourced here), so don't name it as a target team.
