# WS3 + WS8 (parts 1-3): Competitive landscape, why Investec is built the way it is, and what clients buy

Prepared for: Investec UK Summer Internship 2027 (London, Specialist Bank: Private Banking and Corporate & Investment Banking)
Research date: 2026-10-05. Source IDs: S120-S149, S300-S316.
Tags: **[Confirmed]** means read in a primary or full-text source fetched today. **[Reported]** means from a search snippet or secondary outlet that was not read in full. **[Inferred]** means my own analysis built on the cited facts.
Currency and dates are given on every figure. Investec's financial year ends on 31 March, so "FY2026" means the year to 31-Mar-2026. Most peers report to 31 December, Close Brothers to 31 July, and C. Hoare to 31 March.

Access note: the investec.com pages (Who we work with, business owners, corporate broking, careers), Prosple and lse.co.uk all returned HTTP 403 to automated fetching. Anything taken from those sites is therefore SNIPPET-grade. The verbatim Investec wording below comes from full-text RNS announcements on Investegate, which I downloaded and searched.

## 1. Findings

### 1.1 Investec today (context for the comparison)
- **Group, FY2026 (to 31-Mar-2026):** adjusted operating profit £951.0m (+3.4%). ROE 13.6% (FY2025: 13.9%). ROTE 15.7%. Net core loans £35.5bn. Customer deposits £44.7bn. Total assets £63.8bn. "8,000+ employees". Established 1974. [Confirmed, S120]
- **Investec plc (UK & Other), FY2026:** adjusted operating profit £462.7m. ROTE 13.7% (FY2025: 14.5%). ROE 10.8% (FY2025: 11.2%). Deposits £22.5bn. Cost/income 52.5%. CET1 13.0%. Total assets £31.97bn. [Confirmed, S120]
- **UK & Other Specialist Banking, FY2026:** adjusted operating profit £401.6m (FY2025: £410.4m). Net core loans £17.8bn (+5.9%). Residential mortgage book +10.3%. Credit loss ratio 57bps. Cost/income 54.0%. [Confirmed, S120]
- **Outlook:** FY2027 is described as the "peak investment year". Guidance has UK & Other ROTE at 12.5-13.5%, "near the lower end of the target range". The group targets about 16% ROE and about 18% ROTE by FY2030. [Confirmed, S120] The September 2026 pre-close statement guides UK Specialist Bank H1 FY2027 operating profit "between 3% and 7% behind the prior period" and UK ROTE at 12.3-12.7%. [Confirmed, S122]
- **Structure:** Dual Listed Companies (DLC) structure. Investec plc has its primary listing on the LSE and Investec Limited on the JSE, with "no cross-guarantees between the companies". [Confirmed, S120, S121] It was the first SA company to dual-list in London and Johannesburg, in July 2002. Asset management was demerged as Ninety One in 2020. UK Wealth & Investment combined with Rathbones in 2023, and Investec holds about 44.5m Rathbones shares. [Confirmed, S127, S120]

### 1.3 Where Investec sits at one end of a spectrum (Part 2)
1. **Private bank and corporate/IB under one specialist-bank roof (One Investec).**
   - Investec's half-year 2025 wording is "the only integrated and diversified mid-market focused specialist bank, providing the capabilities of global investment banks to the corporate mid-market" [Confirmed, S121].
   - Its plan is to "bring the private client banking experience to mid-sized corporates" [Confirmed, S121].
   - In 2022, Ruth Leas (CEO, Investec Bank plc) described Investec as "the only bank providing a combined offering across the personal and business journeys of our target clients, including private banking, wealth management, corporate banking and investment banking" [Confirmed, S125].
   - The spectrum runs from pure lenders (Close Brothers, Shawbrook, OakNorth) and pure private banks (C. Hoare, Coutts) or pure advisers (Rothschild, the brokers), through to universal banks that offer everything but at mass scale (HSBC, Barclays, Santander) [Inferred].
   - **Counter-case:** HSBC UK also reports collaboration, with referrals from CMB to its Private Bank up 8% in 2025 [Confirmed, S149]. Barclays PBWM similarly sits next to an investment bank. The claim to be unique rests on the "mid-market and specialist" qualifier, not on the integration itself [Inferred].
2. **Niche specialist lending vs broad lending.** The UK loan book is weighted to specialist corporate niches (Fund Solutions, Direct Lending, Real Estate, Aviation, Energy & Infrastructure, Fund Finance) plus HNW mortgages [Confirmed, S120]. Investec is now pushing towards "a full-service primary bank" for private clients, with current accounts, cards and rewards [Reported, S124]. That moves it away from the niche end of the spectrum.
3. **Dual-listed UK/South Africa.** No UK peer in this set has a DLC structure. The Southern African business delivers the higher ROE (FY2027 guide 18-19%), while UK & Other guides 12.5-13.5% ROTE [Confirmed, S120]. This brings diversification and an SA private-bank model (128,000 SA private-banking clients vs 8,200 in the UK [Reported, S124]), but also complexity.
4. **Mid-sized.**
   - Investec UK total assets are £32.0bn, against Santander UK at £266.8bn, HSBC UK at £355.9bn and Close Brothers at £12.1bn [Confirmed, S120/S300/S149/S140].
   - It is big enough to provide balance-sheet lending plus ECM and M&A, yet small next to the ring-fenced majors [Inferred].
   - On the ring-fencing threshold: it rose from £25bn to £35bn of core deposits on 4-Feb-2025, and banks with little investment-banking activity have a secondary exemption [Reported, S137]. I found no Investec statement confirming it is outside the ring-fencing regime, so this remains [Inferred]: Investec Bank plc's UK & Other deposits of £22.5bn are below the £35bn core-deposit threshold. Do not claim "not ring-fenced" as fact in interview without checking the annual report.
5. **Entrepreneurial culture and flat structure.** Investec's own careers wording (snippet) says it "chooses a flat structure as an organisation design" and describes an "entrepreneurial culture promoting entrepreneurial flair and freedom to operate" under the "Out of the Ordinary" brand [Reported, S132]. The group says it was "built in the spirit of entrepreneurship" [Reported, snippet].
6. **Deposit funding through a direct savings platform (Investec Save).** Investec sells savings online through Investec Save, a separate platform from the private bank, rather than through branches [Reported, S133]. UK deposits of £22.5bn exceed UK net core loans of £17.8bn (31-Mar-26) [Confirmed, S120]. Awards: Commended for Best Notice Account Provider in the 2025 Moneyfacts Awards [Confirmed, S134]; Money Comms "Personal Savings Provider of the Year" 2026 [Reported, S135].
7. **Wealth delivered through a partner, not in-house.** Since 2023, UK wealth sits with Rathbones. Investec is now "evolving our strategic partnership from a referral-based model to an integrated assets-under-advice model. Investec will now lead the client relationship and advice" [Confirmed, S120]. This is unusual, because Coutts, Barclays and Arbuthnot keep wealth in-house [Inferred].

**Evidence for One Investec and cross-selling numbers:**
- "the power of One Investec" (FY26 Private Client wording) [Confirmed, S120]
- "further entrench clients in our ecosystem, grow market share and drive cross-divisional and cross-border collaboration" (HY Sep-2025) [Confirmed, S121]
- "Rising shared clients" and "increasing client overlap with Private Banking". This refers to Wealth & Investment International (SA and Switzerland), not the UK [Confirmed, S120]
- I found **no published percentage of UK clients who use more than one business.** One search snippet claimed a target of "1,000 clients in the UK to be using at least two Investec solutions by 2030". I could not verify it: it was not in the BusinessDay article I fetched, and it may be conflated with something else. Treat it as unverified [Reported, low confidence, S315].
- An internal restructuring created "a unified Corporate mid-market division that is focused on a single integrated strategy" (HY Sep-2025) [Confirmed, S121].

### 1.4 Things that can be said about Investec but not about rivals (Part 3)
1. **It is the only firm in this peer set that combines a UK HNW private bank with mid-market corporate lending, and also has its own ECM, M&A and broking and research arm.** Investec ranked #1 overall in Extel UK small and mid-cap broker rankings in 2026, for the 4th year running [Reported, S130]. Close Brothers, Shawbrook and OakNorth lend but don't advise. Peel Hunt, Panmure Liberum and Numis advise but don't lend. Coutts and Hoare don't do IB [Inferred from S140-S145, S305-S308].
2. **It is the only one of these banks with a UK/SA dual-listed (DLC) structure,** with no cross-guarantees between the two companies [Confirmed, S121].
3. **The private-client definition is income-led and entrepreneur-led, not only asset-led.** In 2018 the threshold was about £300k a year in income or more and £3m-plus net assets. The head of the private bank said "We are not always especially useful to high net worth individuals who are simply looking to preserve their wealth" [Confirmed, S123; dated 2018]. That is a deliberate contrast with heritage wealth-preservation banks such as Hoare and Coutts [Inferred].
4. **It has outsourced UK investment management to Rathbones while taking an equity stake** (about 44.5m shares; £76.6m share of profits in FY26) and is now moving to lead the advice relationship itself [Confirmed, S120]. No peer has this arrangement.
5. **It is a top-ranked SMID broker, a specialist lender and an online savings brand in one group.** This set of businesses is unusual in the UK market [Inferred, from S130, S134, S120].
6. **Its UK private bank is small and growing from a low base:** 8,200 UK private-banking clients, with a target of about 5,000 more and £25m of extra operating profit by March 2030 [Reported, S124]. This means interns join a scaling business, not a mature one [Inferred].

**Where Investec does not lead (state these plainly):**
- **Returns.**
  - Investec plc ROE was 10.8% and ROTE 13.7% (FY26) [Confirmed, S120].
  - That is lower than Shawbrook (underlying RoTE 17.2%), OakNorth (adjusted ROE 22%), Coutts PB&WM (ROE 21.7%) and Barclays PBWM (RoTE 26.3%) [Confirmed, S142/S144/S146/S148].
  - It is better than Close Brothers (RoTE 5.5%) [Confirmed, S140].
  - Returns are guided lower in FY2027, and UK Specialist Bank profit is guided down 3-7% in H1 FY27 [Confirmed, S122].
- **Scale.**
  - The UK loan book (£17.8bn) is smaller than Shawbrook's (£19.2bn) and about the same size as Santander UK CCB alone (£18.9bn) [Confirmed].
  - HSBC UK CMB alone has £76.2bn of loans [Confirmed, S149].
  - Coutts plus Evelyn now oversee more than £127bn of AUMA [Reported, S147].
- **Efficiency.** Investec UK cost/income is 54.0%. Shawbrook targets the mid-30s, OakNorth reports an efficiency ratio of 26% and HSBC UK CMB a cost-efficiency ratio of 30.5% [Confirmed].
- **Valuation.** A snippet reported a price-to-book of about 0.96 [Reported, S139, low confidence on date]. The DLC structure and exposure to SA are commonly cited as complications [Inferred; no analyst source read].
- **Credit.** The UK credit loss ratio of 57bps is above the group through-the-cycle range of 25-45bps [Confirmed, S120].
- **Legacy issues:** a £30m motor-finance provision and the German cum-ex investigation are disclosed [Confirmed, S120].
- **Advisory league tables.** Rothschild is far larger in M&A, and Panmure Liberum claims the most listed broking clients [Reported, S302, S306].

### 1.5 What clients buy (Part 4)
- **Private clients.**
  - 2018: income of about £300k+ and net assets above £3m. Target segments were corporate executives and entrepreneurs, internationals and professionals, and financial-services professionals. About 90,000-100,000 people in the UK have the relevant needs [Confirmed, S123; dated].
  - Investec's current site has client pages for business owners, finance professionals, private-equity professionals, corporate professionals and international clients [Reported, S128 snippet].
  - 2026 strategy: "HNW and high-income clients, expanding into the affluent segment" through an omni-channel approach [Reported, S124].
  - **"Time-poor, asset-rich" was NOT found** in any Investec source. Do not use it as a quote. The nearest supported framing is "high-income professionals and entrepreneurs who need capital, not just wealth preservation" [Confirmed meaning, S123].
- **Mid-market corporates.**
  - Investec's offer is "providing the capabilities of global investment banks to the corporate mid-market" and "bring the private client banking experience to mid-sized corporates" [Confirmed, S121].
  - Leas: "the midmarket space is particularly underserviced by the bulge bracket banks", and SMEs and smaller corporates want "a highly relationship-orientated and service-focused offering, which they cannot get from the High Street banks" [Confirmed, S125].
  - What clients buy: specialist lending (fund finance, direct lending, real estate, aviation, energy & infrastructure), ECM and advisory, broking and research (about 110 listed clients), and FX and rates hedging through Treasury Risk Solutions [Confirmed S120; Reported S129].
- **Awards.**
  - Extel UK SMID #1 overall (2026, 4th year) [Reported, S130].
  - Moneyfacts 2025 Commended, Best Notice Account Provider [Confirmed, S134].
  - Money Comms Personal Savings Provider of the Year 2026 [Reported, S135].
  - WealthBriefing European Awards: UK Private Bank, Customer Facing Digital Capabilities (Investments) [Reported, S312].
  - Spear's Awards 2026 finalist, Financial Services Firm of the Year [Reported, S313].
  - Euromoney Private Banking Awards: Investec's wins found were for Africa (best for client service 2026; best for philanthropic advisory 2025) [Reported]. The **Euromoney 2025 UK "Best private bank" went to Standard Chartered, not Investec** [Confirmed, S136].
- **Counter-case.** For HNW clients, Coutts and Barclays offer bigger balance sheets, in-house wealth and (per the cited figures) higher returns. For corporates, HSBC UK was voted Euromoney Best Bank for Corporates in the UK and is rated top three in its own mid-market segment (turnover £15-350m) [Confirmed, S149]. OakNorth and Shawbrook offer faster and cheaper specialist credit. Investec's case therefore depends on depth of relationship and integration, not on price or scale [Inferred].

## 6. Interview angles
1. **"Why Investec and not Close Brothers / Shawbrook / OakNorth?"** They are lenders. Investec lends, advises, brokers and banks the founder personally. Cite the "only integrated and diversified mid-market focused specialist bank" wording (S121), and acknowledge that Shawbrook and OakNorth earn higher returns at lower cost.
2. **"Why not Coutts or Barclays Private Bank?"** Use Tholet's 2018 line that Investec is less about wealth preservation and more about providing capital to entrepreneurs (S123). Coutts is consolidating wealth by buying Evelyn (S147), while Investec has outsourced investment management to Rathbones and is focused on banking the client (S120).
3. **"What is One Investec?"** Use Investec's own words: "the power of One Investec" and "entrench clients in our ecosystem... cross-divisional and cross-border collaboration" (S120, S121). Give a concrete example: the founder of a mid-market company uses Investec for growth debt or an IPO through the broking team, then banks personally with the private bank. Be honest that HSBC also measures CMB-to-PB referrals (S149), so the edge is depth and mid-market focus, not the idea itself.
4. **Show you know the numbers and the soft spots.** Investec plc ROE is 10.8% vs Coutts at 21.7%. FY27 is the "peak investment year" and UK H1 FY27 profit is guided 3-7% lower (S120, S122). Then turn it into a question: "How does the Specialist Bank see the Private Client and mid-market investments lifting UK ROTE towards the 2030 targets?"
5. **The broking edge.** Extel #1 UK SMID broker for the 4th year (S130) shows the "capabilities of global investment banks for the mid-market" in practice. Peel Hunt (147 clients) and Panmure Liberum compete for the same mandates but cannot lend.
6. **Commercial-awareness hook.** Ring-fencing reform (threshold now £35bn) and UK mid-market consolidation (Santander/TSB, NatWest/Evelyn, Shawbrook's IPO, Close Brothers' motor-finance hit) are reshaping the field Investec competes in. Explain how a mid-sized, nimble bank benefits, and mark which parts are your own view.
