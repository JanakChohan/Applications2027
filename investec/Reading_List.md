## Recurring sources

| Source | What it's good for | Access / cost | How to use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Investec RNS** (investec.com IR, investegate.co.uk, londonstockexchange.com) | Primary results, trading updates, buybacks, Rathbones-related notices | Free | Read the first two pages of the FY/interim release and the pre-close; next is 19 Nov 2026 |
| **Investec Focus** (investec.com/en_gb/focus) and **Focus Radio UK podcast** | House views from Investec economists (e.g. Ellie Henderson, Callum Macpherson) on rates, oil, bonds; UK leadership interviews | Free | Quote the house view on BoE rates in interview; listen to 2-3 recent episodes |
| **Bank of England MPS / Monetary Policy Report** | Rate decisions, vote splits, inflation forecasts (MPRs in Feb, Apr/May, Jul/Aug, Nov) | Free | Read the one-page Summary after each meeting; next 5 Nov 2026 |
| **BoE Financial Stability Report / FPC record** | Systemic risks: private credit, leverage, bank capital | Free | Skim the overview for "risk" talking points |
| **FT Lex** | Sharp 300-word takes on bank results and deals | Paywalled (student discount / library access) | Search "Lex Investec", "Lex Close Brothers" after results days |
| **FT (banking, Moral Money, Unhedged)** | Deep UK banking and Budget coverage | Paywalled | Use for the Budget bank-tax debate |
| **Reuters** | Fast, neutral wire coverage of results and regulation; often republished free (e.g. CNBC Africa) | Free/limited | Best non-paywalled factual source |
| **Bloomberg** | Deals, capital markets, scoops (e.g. Investec FLAC issue, Budget date) | Paywalled; use headlines plus the Bloomberg podcasts | Track headlines, then confirm elsewhere |
| **City AM** | Free London financial daily: Investec strategy interviews, IPOs, City politics | Free | Daily skim; good for quotes from Investec management |
| **Financial News (FN London)** | City careers, hires, M&A league tables, investment banking moves | Partly paywalled | Team moves and deal flow |
| **The Banker** | Global banking strategy, rankings, regulatory analysis | Partly paywalled | Background on business models (specialist vs universal) |
| **MoneyWeek** | Accessible macro and markets explainers; live BoE coverage | Partly free | Plain-English macro for revision |
| **This is Money / Sharecast / Alliance News (via AJ Bell, Hargreaves Lansdown)** | Free market reports summarising RNSs | Free | Quick factual results summaries |
| **Moneyweb / BusinessLIVE / Daily Investor (SA)** | Investec Limited and South African banking news | Free/partly | Don't forget SA is ~half of group profit |
| **FCA and PRA websites** | Policy statements (motor finance PS26/3, SM&CR PS26/6, Basel 3.1 PS1/26) | Free | Read the press-release summary, not the whole PS |
| **Law-firm regulatory blogs** (Regulation Tomorrow, Mayer Brown, Slaughter and May bulletins) | Plain summaries of new rules and dates | Free | Fast way to understand regulation |
| **EY / PwC IPO Watch, LSEG data** | UK IPO and ECM activity statistics | Free | Quarterly numbers for capital-markets questions |
| **Podcasts:** FT News Briefing, FT Banking Weekly/Behind the Money, Bloomberg Surveillance/Merryn Talks Money, The Economist "Money Talks", Investec Focus Radio | Daily habit, 10-20 min | Free | Commute listening; note one story per day in a log |
