Summer Internship 2027 (14049). Investec, London, 30 Gresham Street. People & Organisation, IBP Business Enablement.
Are you looking for an internship within financial services? Are you curious about the world of financial services and what it involves? Are you ready for an out of the ordinary Summer in 2027?
We are recruiting penultimate and final year university students into our business and we are open to any degree subject. Immerse yourself in Investec over Summer as you experience our business over our eight-week programme.
If you have an entrepreneurial spirit combined with a passion and energy to succeed, we have an opportunity for you to flourish in our environment.
Successful applicants will become part of a team within our Specialist Bank. You will gain insight into our culture and the opportunity will provide you with exposure to our senior leaders. As an intern, you will be given real responsibilities and have an opportunity to contribute and deliver to whichever team you join. Although embedded into one team we aim to provide you with a clear overview of our entire business.
Satisfy your hunger to learn with presentations and talks from experienced Investec employees across our business. There will also be a number of networking opportunities. Gain insight into how you can absorb complex information easily from the Financial Times and how to immerse yourself in financial analysis. Excel courses.
Entrepreneurial thinker - we want to hear your fresh ideas. Self-starter - show us your ability to take a project and make it your own. Be you - bring your whole self to work. We want to know about your passions and interests. All interns must be in their penultimate or final year of University study.
