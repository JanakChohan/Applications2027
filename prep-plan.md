# HSBC Immersive Assessment — Two-Week Prep Plan

A realistic, day-by-day schedule for the **online immersive assessment** (Cappfinity) and the follow-on **video Job Simulation**. Assumes ~60–90 minutes on weekdays and a longer block at each weekend. Adjust to your own timeline — the *sequence* matters more than the exact days.

**The strategy this plan is built on** (see `hsbc-assessment-briefing.md` §3–§5): the test is a **hard cognitive filter** (be accurate on the data questions) plus a **strengths-based behavioural profile that cross-checks you for authenticity and consistency** (be genuinely, consistently yourself — do not perform an "ideal banker"). Everything below serves those two goals.

---

## Week 1 — Foundations, diagnosis, and the cognitive engine

**Day 1 — Map the process & the values (60 min).**
- Read the full briefing once. Note which stage(s) your specific programme includes (the invite email is authoritative — it may be one or two online stages).
- Learn HSBC's four current values cold: *We value difference · We succeed together · We take responsibility · We get it done.* Write one real example from your own life for each. Ignore the older "Open/Connected/Dependable" wording on some prep sites.

**Day 2 — Diagnostic on the vendor's own engine (75 min).**
- Do Cappfinity's official demo (`practice.cappassessments.com`) and, once you've applied, HSBC's Candidate Hub. This is the closest thing to the real interface.
- Take one timed numerical and one verbal set. Record your accuracy and seconds-per-question. This is your baseline.

**Day 3 — Numerical reasoning drilling (75 min).**
- Focus on the classic killers: percentage change, ratios, currency/unit conversion, reading multi-source tables/graphs. Aim for ~75–90 s per item **with accuracy first**.
- Practise with a **physical calculator** on the desk (it's permitted; a calculator app/tab could look like focus-loss).

**Day 4 — Verbal & inductive reasoning (60 min).**
- Verbal: answer strictly from the passage; discipline your use of "Cannot Say"; don't import outside knowledge. Inductive: pattern/sequence sets.

**Day 5 — Situational Judgement mechanics (60 min).**
- Practise the **rank-5-options (1 = most likely → 5 = least likely)** format. For each scenario, silently tag which HSBC value the "good" options express — this trains judgement without memorising answers.

**Day 6 (longer block) — Full cognitive mock + review (90–120 min).**
- Do a full-length mixed cognitive mock under time pressure. Then **review every wrong answer** and write down *why* it was wrong (misread unit? rushed? wrong operation?). Error-logging is where the gains are.

**Day 7 — Rest or light review (30 min).**
- Skim your error log. No new material.

---

## Week 2 — Behavioural authenticity, the video simulation, and logistics

**Day 8 — Strengths self-audit (60 min).**
- List 6–8 genuine strengths (things you're good at **and** that energise you) with a concrete story for each. These power the personality items, the video answers, and any later strengths interview.
- Practise the personality preference-scale format: answer honestly and **consistently** — the test cross-probes the same traits and flags contradictions.

**Day 9 — SJT deep practice with values mapping (60 min).**
- Work through SJT scenarios; after each, check that your top-ranked action reflects a real HSBC value and that you'd *actually* do it. Aim for decisive, honest rankings — not fence-sitting, and not maxing every "desirable" trait.

**Day 10 — Video answer practice (75 min).**
- Record yourself answering "introduce yourself" and "your biggest strength" in ~2 min each, from **bullet notes, not a script**. Watch it back for eye contact and natural delivery. This closes the biggest gap in most prep packs.

**Day 11 — The written email task (60 min).**
- Practise writing a clear, structured **~2,500–3,000-character** email reply to a work scenario: acknowledge → prioritise → propose action → note follow-ups. **Type it directly** (don't draft-and-paste). Keep your natural voice — over-polished/boilerplate text can read as AI-generated.

**Day 12 — Full mixed mock (90 min).**
- Simulate the immersive assessment end to end (cognitive + SJT + personality) in one focused sitting, at a steady pace. Remember: "untimed" but time is recorded — don't over-deliberate, don't rush into errors.

**Day 13 — Environment & integrity check (45 min).**
- Set up your real sitting: quiet room, good front lighting, webcam/mic tested, notifications off, **home Wi-Fi (no VPN)**, one device you'll use start to finish, physical calculator + pen & paper ready. Re-read the on-screen instructions/declaration when you open the real test — those are the binding rules.

**Day 14 — Light review & reset (30 min).**
- Re-read your strengths stories and error log. Sleep well. Sit the assessment when you're fresh, not late at night.

---

## Quick-reference: do / don't

**Do:** be accurate before fast · answer behavioural items honestly and consistently · use a physical calculator · type answers directly · speak to camera from bullet notes · complete each section in one sitting on your home connection.

**Don't:** perform an "ideal banker" · open extra tabs/apps mid-test · use a VPN or switch devices/networks · paste drafted text · read a script to camera · treat the "practice" video as unassessed · make careless numerical errors.
