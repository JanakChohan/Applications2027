### 1.2 Duty line 1: "Solve problems for our FX customers ... maintain relationships ... deal with post sale activity"

**Which business.** LSEG FX, inside Markets. Platforms: **FXall** (dealer-to-client, multi-dealer request-for-quote and workflow for buy-side and corporates) and **FX Matching** (dealer-to-dealer central limit order book), plus a US Streaming FX Spot business and post-trade/clearing links to LCH ForexClear. [Confirmed: S22 "FXall, our dealer-to-client platform, and FX Matching, our dealer-to-dealer platform"; S24 "our US-based Streaming FX Spot business"; S45 "LSEG Workspace, FXall, FX Matching and LCH ForexClear"]

**Numbers.** FX revenue £272m in 2025 (+7.5%); £145m in H1 2026 (+7.6%); average daily total volume $561bn in H1 2026 (+5.8%). [Confirmed: S22, S24] LSEG won Euromoney's "World's Best FX Venue" and "World's Best Venue for FX Spot" 2026. [Reported: S45, LSEG's own page]

**Recent product news to know.** Deposits trading on FXall; FXall integration with LoopFX (block liquidity); FXall integrated into LSEG Workspace; Forward First Fixing passed $330bn in Q2 2026; Streaming FX Spot first trades on AWS Outposts with "an estimated 70% latency improvement". [Confirmed: S45, S24, S23]

**Day to day (what the intern would do).** [Inferred from S17, S11 and S2 unless marked]
- Handle client queries from the FX account management or customer success queue: users who cannot see a liquidity provider's prices, set-up of new trading entities, allocation problems, confirmations not flowing to the client's order management system.
- Prepare client usage reviews: volume by currency pair and product, hit ratios, the number of liquidity providers used, and adoption of newer features (Deposits, Forward First Fixing). The Singapore FX Sales role describes "regular client engagement activities, including business reviews" and "accelerating solution adoption". [Reported: S17]
- Keep clients informed about platform changes, release notes and outages. This is "keep them informed" in the JD. The London Technical Account Manager role describes the same pattern: "Produce and maintain customer-facing communications ... service announcements, technical guidance, readiness materials". [Reported: S11]
- Post-sale work: onboarding and connectivity (FIX, API), entitlement set-up, renewals support, tracking contract or billing issues. Manila has an "FX Venue Onboarding" team (R0122593), so some onboarding is done offshore and London coordinates. [Confirmed for the Manila role's existence: S4]

**Counterparties.** Banks as liquidity providers and dealers; buy-side asset managers; corporate treasurers; hedge funds; for FX Matching, interbank dealers. S17 lists "banks, buy-side institutions, and other market participants" and contacts across "trading, treasury, eFX, sales, operations, and technology functions". [Reported: S17]

**What good looks like.** Client retention and renewals, revenue growth on assigned accounts, faster issue resolution, adoption of new functionality. S17: "Achieve individual revenue, growth, retention and targets across assigned accounts". [Reported: S17] For an intern: a client review pack the account manager actually uses, or a fixed recurring issue.

**Tools.** LSEG Workspace (FXall is integrated into it, S23), Excel, CRM (LSEG uses Salesforce in other Markets client teams, S21), Power BI or Tableau for usage MI (named in the sibling BA internship, S5). [Confirmed that these tools are used at LSEG; Inferred that the FX team uses each one]

**Structural fact it reveals.** FX is an over-the-counter market with no single exchange. LSEG earns from venue access, transaction fees and workflow, and volumes rise with volatility: "greater volumes driven by higher volatility in the market" (S23). The account manager's job is to keep LSEG embedded in client workflows so that volume stays when volatility falls. [Confirmed for the volatility link: S23; Inferred for the implication]

**Control angle.** LSEG is hiring an "FX Market Surveillance Product Director" in London (Paternoster Square) to own surveillance "across Spot, Forward, Swap, NDF, Options" and to "detect potential market abuse, misconduct, and anomalies" on its FX trading platforms. [Confirmed: S14] Client data is confidential. You never tell one bank what another bank's flow looks like.

The division's International Securities Market and Sustainable Bond Market have been key engines of growth" (S15, NY DCM business development JD). [Reported: single primary job posting; treat the numbers as LSEG's own claim]
- Themes the debt team covers: "sustainable finance, Islamic finance, local currency finance, project and infrastructure bonds, structured finance, emerging tech and digital bonds" (S15). [Reported]
- The DCM team works with "FIG, SSA, EM issuers, bankers, law firms and advisors" and must "collaborate with the broader capital markets team including market research and intelligence, assist with internal reporting for LSEG management and work with colleagues in structured finance in LSEG FX and the Data & Analytics division" (S15). - Equity Primary Markets Americas "has attracted close to 120 Americas companies to list in London in last 10 years" and says "The London Stock Exchange has the highest proportion of international companies among its issuers of any major exchange" (S16). [Reported: LSEG's own claim]
- The Private Securities Market (PSM) under the FCA's PISCES regime: LSE was "the first operator to be granted approval" (S22) and it "successfully hosted its first transactions" in H1 2026 (S24). [Confirmed]
- LSE 24, "a new 24/5 trading venue ... which will launch in H1 2027", announced July 2026 (S24). Market Supervision analyst roles for LSE 24 are open in Toronto and New York (S4). [Confirmed]
- Digital Markets Infrastructure (DMI) launched September 2025 with Microsoft; six fund providers onboarded by H1 2026. A planned digital gilt (DIGIT) link with HSBC Orion by Q1 2027. [Confirmed: S22, S24]

**Day to day.** [Inferred, built from S15 and S16]
- **Debt listings analysis:** build tables of bond admissions by issuer type (sovereign, supranational and agency; financials; corporates; emerging-market issuers), currency, use of proceeds (green, social, sukuk) and geography. Compare London venues (Main Market vs ISM) with competing listing venues for international bonds. Feed origination teams with pipeline targets, for example issuers who have issued in Europe but never listed in London.
- **Valuations:** for equity primary markets, comparable-company valuation work, for example how a sector trades in London against New York, to support the pitch that London is not structurally cheaper. Expect to discuss the "London valuation discount" debate carefully. Present it as an analytical question, not as a fixed view.
- **Shareholder analysis:** analyse shareholder registers and investor bases of listed or prospective issuers. Questions include: who owns London-listed peers, how much is international, how did the investor base change after a listing.
- **Geographic analysis:** market sizing and targeting by region, for example the Americas, Gulf or India pipeline for debt and equity, as in S15 and S16.

**Counterparties.** Issuers (treasurers, heads of funding, CFOs), investment banks (DCM, ECM syndicate), law firms, nominated advisers (for AIM), investors, and government bodies. S15: "treasurers, heads of funding, origination and syndication professionals, capital markets lawyers and investors". [Reported]

**What good looks like.** Accurate, sourced data; a clear "so what" for the origination banker; pipeline conversions and admissions. The business is judged on admissions, capital raised and listing revenue (Equities revenue was £412m in 2025, S22). [Confirmed for revenue; Inferred for the KPI set]

**Tools.** LSEG Workspace and LSEG data (the Group's own product), Excel, PowerPoint, possibly Python or SQL. The BA internship sibling asks for "Python, SQL ... Tableu, Power BI" (S5, LSEG's spelling). [Confirmed that these are named; Inferred that the research team uses them]

**Structural fact it reveals.** London's debt market is mainly an international listing venue, not a domestic one. Growth comes from foreign issuers choosing London (ISM and Sustainable Bond Market as "key engines of growth", S15). The equity business is fighting a structural war for listings against New York, and is using new products (PSM, DMI, LSE 24) to widen the "funding continuum" (S23). [Reported/Inferred]

**Control angle (critical).** London Stock Exchange plc is a **Recognised Investment Exchange**. Its Primary Market and AIM Regulation team (PMAR) "is responsible for first line primary regulatory oversight of all of LSE plc's markets operated in its capacity as a Recognised Investment Exchange", covering AIM, ISM and PSM (S13). [Confirmed: S13, LSEG's own posting] Because the team sees pipeline information about issuers before it is public (planned IPOs, bond admissions), this is the area where **inside information and issuer confidentiality** matter most. You must never discuss a named pipeline company outside the team. Separation between commercial origination staff and the regulation team (PMAR) is part of how an RIE manages conflicts. [Inferred: I did not find a public LSEG document describing internal information barriers; treat as reasoning]

   - LSEG owns 94.4% of LCH Limited (S23 subsidiaries list). [Confirmed: S23] Do not use Wikipedia's older 82.61% figure (S44), which is out of date.

3. **Market integrity risk (surveillance).** Market Supervision monitors "all secondary market trading on London Stock Exchange ('LSE') markets, Turquoise Global Holdings Limited and Turquoise Global Holdings Europe ... to identify activity which may indicate market abuse as outlined in the UK Financial Conduct Authority's Code of Market Conduct and MAR" and submits STORs (S10). [Confirmed: S10]

**Day to day for an intern.** [Inferred] Pull and clean exposure or margin data; reconcile risk reports; write commentary on why a metric moved; help prepare a regulatory or committee report; test controls and gather evidence; analyse incident logs. **Internal clients / external counterparties.** Clearing members (large banks), their clients (buy-side), regulators, internal Risk committees, 2nd line Risk (CRO function under Balbir Bakhshi, S23), Internal Audit. [Confirmed for names; Inferred for interactions]

## 6) Interview angles (smart things to say and ask)

**Things to say (each is backed by a source)**
1. "I understand the internship sits in the Markets division, which since January 2025 combines what used to be Capital Markets and Post Trade, so a client can raise capital, trade and clear inside one LSEG division. RepoAgent, built by Post Trade Solutions and Tradeweb together, is a good example of why that combination matters." (S22, Q1, Q5)
2. On FX: "FX revenue grew 7.5% in 2025 and FXall is now inside Workspace. I'd be interested in how account management drives adoption of things like Forward First Fixing and Deposits trading, because that keeps volume when volatility drops." (S22, S23, S24, S45)
3. On debt listings: "London's debt markets look international by design: about 1,300 borrowers from over 60 countries, with ISM and the Sustainable Bond Market as the growth engines. Analysis of where the next issuers come from by geography and issuer type seems to be exactly what the research bullet in the JD is about." (S15, flag that the figure is LSEG's own)
4. On risk: "SwapClear cleared a record $1,941 trillion of notional in 2025 and NTI rose in H1 2026 because volatility increased cash collateral. Risk and revenue at a CCP move together, which is why risk reporting matters so much." (S22, S24)
5. On control: "At a Recognised Investment Exchange, primary markets work sees issuer pipeline information before it is public, so confidentiality is part of the job from day one." (S13; the confidentiality point is my inference)
6. On operations: "I noticed a large share of Markets operations roles are in Manila and Colombo, so I expect operational improvement work in London to involve working closely with those teams." (S4)

**Questions to ask (strengths interview and assessment centre)**
1. "The graduate JD mentions a rotational programme but also says you are aligned to one team from day one. How do rotations work for Markets graduates today?" (S6, S51 conflict)
2. "How do you decide which of the five businesses an intern joins, and can interns see more than one, for example LSEG FX and LCH ForexClear?"
3. "Since Capital Markets and Post Trade became one Markets division, what has changed for client teams day to day? Do clients now have one coverage contact across trading and clearing?" (S22)
4. "LSE 24 launches in H1 2027, during the internship. Will interns have any exposure to launch readiness?" (S24)
5. "The Business Analyst internship mentions World-Check. Do Markets interns ever work with Risk Intelligence?" (S5, unresolved gap 9)
6. "What does a great intern deliverable look like in this team: a recurring MI pack, a one-off strategic analysis, or client-facing material?"
7. "How do teams in Primary Markets handle confidential pipeline data with interns?"

**Traps to avoid**
- Do not call LCH "the LSEG clearing division" without naming LCH Ltd and LCH SA as separate legal entities in different countries (S22, S28).
- Do not say "Capital Markets division" as if it were the current name. Say "Markets, which merged Capital Markets and Post Trade in 2025".
- Do not quote "25,000 employees" as the latest figure. The Annual Report says 28,516 at end-2025 (S23).
- Do not take a fixed public view on whether London listings are "undervalued". Frame it as an analytical question.
- Do not name specific LSEG pipeline issuers or claim inside knowledge.
