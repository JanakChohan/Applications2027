### Story 3. Global bond sell-off squeezes the UK Budget (25 Sep 2026; Budget 28 Oct 2026)
- Headline / outlet / date / URL: "Bond rout leaves Healey facing tax rises or spending cuts", London Business News (Londonlovesbusiness.com), 25 Sep 2026, https://londonlovesbusiness.com/bond-rout-leaves-healey-facing-tax-rises-or-spending-cuts/ [S243].

- Why it matters to LSEG: Volatile rates markets increase trading and hedging. Tradeweb (rates trading) and LCH SwapClear (interest rate swap clearing) benefit from high volumes [Inferred, consistent with H1 data in S221]. Higher gilt volatility also raises margin requirements at clearing houses, a risk management issue for LCH [Inferred].
- Why it matters to the intern role: Risk teams monitor exactly these moves; FX customer teams see sterling volatility around Budgets [Inferred].
- Interview question: "If gilt volatility stays high into the Budget, does that show up first in SwapClear sterling volumes or in Tradeweb gilt trading, and how would the Risk team think about margin spikes for clients?"

## 6) Interview angles (how to use this in the strengths-based video interview and assessment centre)

1. Lead with one Markets story, not a group-level data story. Best options for this role: FXall Forward First Fixing (FX customers), Airtel Money or PISCES (primary markets / shareholder analysis), SwapClear records and EMIR 3 (risk), LSE 24 and tokenisation (strategy).
2. Show you understand cyclical vs structural growth. "Markets grew 11.9% in H1, helped by Iran-war volatility. The structural pieces I find interesting are FFF, Post Trade Solutions and PSM, because they grow even when volatility falls."
3. Show you understand the customer. For FX: the pain point FFF solves is manual processing of fixing-related forwards for asset managers; LSEG cites a 90%+ reduction for its largest client.
4. Show you can connect the dots (JD asks for this): UK gilt volatility leads to more swap hedging, which shows up in SwapClear volumes and Tradeweb rates trading, but also higher margin calls, which is a risk team issue.
5. Show balanced judgement on London listings: real momentum (H1 proceeds +215%, Airtel Money) but from a low base, with big names delaying (Visma, AS Watson, Loveholidays) and PE using continuation funds.
6. Have a view on activism: "Elliott's pressure produced record buybacks. I think the strategic case for keeping Tradeweb is the Workspace integration and data synergies; the counter-argument is the conglomerate discount."
7. Know the dates: Q3 trading statement 22 Oct 2026; Budget 28 Oct 2026; Airtel Money listing 14 Oct 2026; US 23x5 6 Dec 2026; DIGIT by Q1 2027; LSE 24 ETPs H1 2027; cash ISA cap Apr 2027; UK T+1 11 Oct 2027.
