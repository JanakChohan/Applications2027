The protocol, one line each

Steady: breathe out, restate the ask, no verdict. Sort: name the two things at stake and which tier wins. Act: two or three verbs, named owners, a time, one LSEG fact. Close: outcome, owner, record; then stop.

The never list

  • Never guess a number. "I don't know, and I won't guess. What I do know is..."
  • Never share or hint at another client's trading, or an issuer's pipeline.
  • Never promise what you don't own: fix times, discounts, admission dates.
  • Never skip, backdate or fake a control or a record. Escalate instead.
  • Never give a market view or advice to a client.
  • Never badmouth a past team, a rival, or an interviewer's challenge.
  • Never say LSEG bundles SwapClear with Tradeweb or ForexClear with FXall: open access forbids favouring its own venues [S305].
  • Never call Rachel Reeves Chancellor: John Healey is, since July 2026 [S243, S244].
  • Never say LSEG owns 82.6% of LCH: about 94% [S300, S121].

Eight frame-breakers: the counter in one line

  1. Interruption: "Good point. Let me take that, then finish the step I was on."
  2. Contradiction: "Which part would you change?" If they're right: "I'd fix that; the rest holds because..."
  3. Silence: count three. "In short, X because Y. Shall I go deeper on any part?"
  4. Escalation: "That raises the stakes, not the order. I'd still do X, and tell my manager sooner."
  5. Authority squeeze: "I'd help them get it done: approved route, or escalate. Not skip."
  6. False choice: "I don't think those are the only two options. Keep the rule, then work hard for the client."
  7. Knowledge trap: "I don't know that and won't guess. What I do know is... I'd check with..."
  8. Badmouth invitation: "I'd rather talk about what I could control. What I learned was..."

Change your mind for a new fact, never for new pressure. Say which it is.

Five composure trees, one line each

Two seniors, one resource: find each deadline, try a split, else your manager decides. Skip a control: which control, approved exception? If none, escalate fast; never skip. Overheard inside information: precise, non-public, price-moving, or not sure? Stop, don't share or trade, tell Compliance today. Your error sent to a client: tell your manager within the hour, draft the correction, fix the cause. Incomplete data at deadline: reversible? Send best estimate, assumptions in bold. Not reversible? Escalate for time or a senior decision.
