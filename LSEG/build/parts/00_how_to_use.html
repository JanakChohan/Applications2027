What this pack is

This is a research pack for one application: the LSEG Business Management Summer Internship 2027, requisition R0123386, based at 5 Canada Square in London [S1]. It covers the role, the team, the firm, its rivals, the industry, the rules that shape it, the news, the recruitment process, and how to hold your ground in a scenario question. Everything comes from public sources read on or before 5 October 2026. Every non-obvious fact carries a marker such as [S72], which you can look up in the source table in Part 15.

The pack separates three kinds of claim. You should keep them separate when you speak, too.

Confirmed
A primary source (LSEG's own reports, a regulator) or two credible outlets. Say it as fact.
Reported
One credible source, such as trade press. Say "it was reported that".
Inferred
The author's reasoning from the evidence. Say "my reading is", and be ready to be told you are wrong.
Inputs that were not supplied

The brief left the candidate fields blank: no degree, year, CV, prior knowledge score, interview date or interviewer names. The pack therefore assumes a penultimate-year undergraduate with little finance background (about 3 out of 10). It treats the next step as the application (closing 30 October 2026, reviewed on a rolling basis [S1]), and it builds the CV grill in Part 11 as a method with placeholder examples. Once you have an interview date or a name, add them and re-read Parts 9 to 11.

3. Why LSEG, in 30 seconds

Say this in the interview

"What draws me to LSEG is how it is built. It owns venues like the London Stock Exchange, Tradeweb and FXall. But its clearing house, LCH, has open access written into its rules: it must clear on fair terms even for trades from rival venues, and banks still sit on its risk committee. That is why the market trusts it with over 90% of cleared interest rate swaps. Business management works where those businesses join, and that is where I want to learn." [S303, S305]

4. The one regulatory point per placement

  • FX customers: the FX Global Code, updated December 2024, sets the conduct standard. Never share one client's flow with another [S343].
  • Debt listings research: the new prospectus regime (POATRs) has been in force since 19 January 2026. Issuer pipelines are confidential and can be inside information under UK MAR [S151, S348].
  • BI and strategy: T+1 settlement in the UK and EU from 11 October 2027 changes post-trade workloads. LSE 24 launches in H1 2027 [S158, S24].
  • Risk: LCH sets initial margin at 99.7% confidence. Every member default it has handled stayed within the defaulter's margin [S93, S351, S354].

5. The priority order when good things collide

Rules and market integrity first, then fair treatment of every customer, then the process and the record, then speed. The sentence that holds every frame: "I'd want to get this right for the client without cutting a corner the firm would have to explain later." Part 11 explains why this order fits LSEG in particular.

6. The honest weak spots, so nothing surprises you

The share price fell about 41% from February 2025 to February 2026. Much of that came on fears that AI tools would erode LSEG's data business, and the activist fund Elliott built a stake [S90, S88]. London's listings market is still small next to New York's [S192]. Open access let Euronext move its clearing away [S301]. You can be clear-eyed about all three and still want to join. Part 8 shows how.
