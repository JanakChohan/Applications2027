Part 6

The role's own industry and its regulation

The four markets an intern could land in, how they got here, the rules being rewritten around them right now, and the one sentence to say about each rule.

Part 5 explained the industry in general. This Part zooms into the specific businesses named in the JD: London Stock Exchange, LSEG FX, Tradeweb, Turquoise and LCH. The UK is in the middle of the biggest rewrite of its market rules since 1986. Almost every rule below has a date between mid-2024 and late 2027, which is exactly when you would intern (June 2027) and graduate (summer 2028). Inferred

Don't say this

"The first Eurobond was listed in London in 1963." It was arranged in London by S G Warburg, but listed on the Luxembourg Stock Exchange [S174, S175]. Say: "The Eurobond market was born in London in 1963 with the Autostrade deal, led by Warburg, even though the bond itself was listed in Luxembourg." Getting this detail right is exactly the kind of care a debt listings team notices.

Key term

Eurobond: a bond issued in a currency other than that of the country where it is issued, sold to international investors. ICMA: "It is generally accepted that the Eurobond market began with the Autostrade issue for the Italian motorway network in July 1963" [S174].

Key term

MiFID / MiFID II / MiFIR: EU rules on investment services and trading venues. After Brexit the UK kept its own amended version, "UK MiFIR", which it is now changing piece by piece. FSMA 2023 (the Financial Services and Markets Act 2023) is the law that lets it do so.

Primary markets and debt listings

This is the London Stock Exchange's home turf: helping companies and governments raise money. The equity side has had a hard decade; the debt side is quieter but steady. Since 2024 the government and FCA have changed nearly every rule an issuer touches. Inferred

Key term

IPO (initial public offering): a company's first sale of shares to the public, with admission to trading. Prospectus: the legal document describing the security and the issuer, so investors can judge it.

Key term

Listing vs admission to trading: "listing" means joining an official list with its rules; "admission to trading" means the venue lets the security trade. On a regulated market you usually need both; on an MTF like AIM or ISM the exchange alone admits. Inferred

UK Listing Rules (UKLR)Listings
When FCA PS24/6 published 11 Jul 2024; rules in force 29 Jul 2024 [S150]
What Scraps the old "premium" and "standard" listing segments "in favour of a new commercial companies category for equity shares" [S150], commonly called ESCC (equity shares in commercial companies) Reported. Fewer hurdles: for example, no mandatory shareholder votes on most large deals and easier dual-class share structures [S202] Reported. The FCA called it "the most significant changes to the UK's listing regime in over 3 decades" [S150].
Why LSEG Lowers the cost and friction of listing in London, so the LSE can win IPOs back from New York and Amsterdam. EY says "recent reforms to the UK's listing regime are beginning to have a tangible impact" [S142].
Say "Since July 2024 London has one main listing category instead of two, which removes a big reason founders chose New York."
POATRs: the new prospectus regimeListings
When Public Offers and Admissions to Trading Regulations 2024; FCA rules PS25/9 (and PS25/10 for public offer platforms), both 15 Jul 2025; in force 19 Jan 2026 [S151]
What Replaces the UK Prospectus Regulation inherited from the EU. PS25/9 creates a new FCA sourcebook, the Prospectus Rules: Admissions to Trading on Regulated Markets (PRM) [S151].
Why LSEG Changes the cost of raising money on the Main Market, for both shares and bonds, because the prospectus is one of the most expensive parts of a listing. Inferred The JD's "debt listings" research sits right on top of this.
Say "Since 19 January 2026 prospectuses for London's regulated markets sit under the FCA's new PRM rules, so the question for the debt listings team is whether that makes the Main Market more competitive with MTF routes like ISM."
Source uncertain

Detailed POATRs thresholds (for example a 75% limit for further share issues without a prospectus, or protected forward-looking statements) and the SI number were not on the FCA page read for this pack. Do not quote them. If asked, say you know the regime went live on 19 January 2026 under PS25/9 and that you would want to check the thresholds.

UK Listing Relief (stamp duty)Listings
When Autumn Budget 26 Nov 2025; applies to shares newly listed on a UK regulated market on or after 27 Nov 2025 [S182]
What A three-year relief from the 0.5% stamp duty reserve tax (SDRT) on buying shares in newly listed companies [S182] Reported. EY also refers to "the newly announced UK Listing Relief" [S192].
Why LSEG Makes newly listed UK shares cheaper to buy, which helps IPO demand. Only applies to regulated markets, so it favours the Main Market over AIM. Inferred

Debt listings: Main Market, ISM and the Sustainable Bond Market

The JD singles out "debt listings". This is less glamorous than IPOs but London has deep roots in it, going back to the 1963 Eurobond. LSE offers two main routes and one label on top. Inferred

  • Main Market: the regulated market, used for debt listings including gilts and sovereign bonds Inferred. Prospectuses now fall under the FCA's PRM rules [S151].
  • International Securities Market (ISM): an MTF for debt sold to professional investors; launched 8 May 2017 with first admissions on 15 May 2017; admission is approved by LSE, not via the FCA's Official List [S176] Reported.
  • Sustainable Bond Market: launched 11 Oct 2019, building on the Green Bond Segment from 2015. LSE says it "was the first major exchange to launch a dedicated Green Bond Segment" [S177] Confirmed.
Key term

Professional investors: banks, fund managers, insurers and other institutions treated as able to look after themselves, so the rules for selling to them are lighter than for retail savers. ISM is built for them, which is why it can be quicker and simpler than a regulated market listing. Inferred

Source uncertain

Our research does not contain ISM or Main Market fees, timetables, minimum denominations, the number of bonds listed, or LSEG's debt listings revenue. Figure 25a therefore keeps those steps generic. If the debt listings team comes up, ask about these rather than guess: "How do issuers choose between ISM and the Main Market today?" is a strong question.

Equities trading, transparency and market data

Share trading in London is split across the LSE order book, MTFs like Turquoise and Cboe Europe, dark books and bank systematic internalisers. The rules here decide where trades can happen and who owns the data they produce. Inferred

Key term

Systematic internaliser (SI): a bank or firm that trades against its own book with clients on an organised, frequent basis. It competes with venues for the same share trades. Pre-trade transparency means publishing bids and offers before trading; post-trade transparency means publishing the price and size after. A deferral lets a dealer delay publishing a large trade so it can hedge.

Share trading obligation (STO) revokedTrading and data
When 29 Aug 2023, by commencement of FSMA 2023 [S167]
What "On 29 August 2023, the Share Trading Obligation (STO) in Article 23 of UK MiFIR was revoked" [S167]. The STO forced certain share trades onto venues; firms can now trade those shares more freely, including on non-UK venues. Inferred
Why LSEG Removes a Brexit-era friction for UK firms trading EU shares, but also removes a rule that pushed flow onto venues like LSE and Turquoise. Mixed for LSEG. Inferred
Say "The UK dropped the share trading obligation in August 2023, which made London more open, but it means LSE and Turquoise have to win flow on quality, not by rule."
Bond and derivatives transparencyTrading and data
When FCA PS24/14, published 5 Nov 2024; rules in force 1 Dec 2025 [S156]
What "A simpler and more timely post-trade transparency regime based on fewer deferrals" for bonds and derivatives [S156].
Why LSEG More bond trade data published faster: good for price discovery, and the foundation for the bond consolidated tape. Tradeweb is a bond venue that publishes data under these rules. Inferred
Say "PS24/14 is the bond transparency rulebook; it is not the tape itself, it is the data the tape is built from."

When FCA CP26/31 (policy statement plus consultation), first published 19 Nov 2025, updated 31 Jul 2026; procurement early 2027; operating "in 2027 or early 2028" [S157]
What The equity tape will include post-trade data plus "the first level of pre-trade data (the attributed best bid and offer)". The tape provider (CTP) must share income with data contributors [S157].
Why LSEG LSE and Turquoise will be contributors and may get an income share, but the tape also offers a cheaper alternative to buying each venue's data. Inferred Including pre-trade best bid and offer makes this more powerful than a post-trade-only tape. Inferred

UK MAR and market cleanlinessTrading and data
When UK Market Abuse Regulation; FCA market cleanliness statistic 2025: 41.1% [S166]
What UK MAR bans insider dealing, unlawful disclosure of inside information and market manipulation; issuers must disclose inside information promptly and keep insider lists Inferred. The FCA's market cleanliness statistic (share of takeovers with abnormal share price moves before the announcement) was 41.1% in 2025; the 5-year average is 33.74% [S166]. The FCA says the revised method gives "systematically higher" figures [S166].
Why LSEG Venue operators must monitor for abuse. A rising cleanliness number is a reputational issue for London as a market. Inferred It also connects directly to LSEG's value of Integrity.
Say "The 2025 market cleanliness figure was 41.1%, but the FCA changed its method, so I would not read it as a sudden jump without checking the footnote."
Key term

Inside information: precise, non-public information that would move a security's price if known. Insider list: the record an issuer keeps of everyone with access to it.

LSEG's own move: LSE 24. Announced 21 July 2026, LSE 24 is a separate venue trading 24 hours a day, five days a week. It "will be available for client testing by the end of 2026, with Exchange Traded Products (ETPs) launching as the first asset class in H1 2027, subject to regulatory approval" [S196]. Confirmed It will mix order book and RFQ functionality [S196]. This is not a regulation, but it lands in the middle of the regulatory timeline and answers the retail demand that the ISA changes are trying to create. Inferred

FX electronic trading

FX is the largest market in the world, and London is its centre. LSEG FX runs FXall, where banks trade with clients (dealer-to-client, D2C), and FX Matching, where banks trade with each other (dealer-to-dealer, D2D), with LCH ForexClear clearing some FX derivatives [S120].

Other facts worth holding: spot is 31% of turnover and FX swaps 42%; the top four centres (UK, US, Singapore, Hong Kong) account for 75% [S163]. The UK is also 49.6% of global OTC interest rate derivatives turnover [S162]. Confirmed The Bank of England: "The UK remains the single largest centre of foreign exchange activity with share of 37.8% of global turnover" [S162].

Key term

Spot FX: exchanging currencies for near-immediate delivery (usually two days). FX swap: a spot trade plus a reverse forward trade, used by banks and corporates to manage funding. Swaps are the biggest single product at 42% of turnover [S163].

Key term

D2C and D2D: dealer-to-client platforms (FXall) let corporates and asset managers request prices from several banks. Dealer-to-dealer platforms (FX Matching) let banks trade with each other, usually on an order book. LSEG runs both.

FX Global Code: 2024 reviewFX
When GFXC three-year review completed 10 Dec 2024; updated Code published Jan 2025 [S164]
What A voluntary set of 55 principles of good practice for the FX market, written by the Global Foreign Exchange Committee (GFXC) and first published in 2017 [S204] Reported. The 2024 review aimed "to strengthen the Code's guidance on FX settlement risk and to increase transparency around certain types of FX transactions and the use of client-generated data" [S164].
Why LSEG FXall's clients (banks, corporates, asset managers) sign statements of commitment to the Code. Questions about how platforms use client data and how settlement risk is reduced are live client topics an FX intern would hear. Inferred
Say "The FX Global Code was updated in December 2024 to tighten guidance on settlement risk and on how client data is used, which is exactly what a platform like FXall has to answer to."
FX settlement risk: the BIS measurementFX
When BIS Quarterly Review, 15 Jun 2026, based on the April 2025 Triennial Survey [S205]
What "Just over one third of the average daily settlement volume in April 2025, or $5.2 trillion, was settled via PvP, which eliminates settlement risk" [S205]. About 10%, or $1.4tn a day, settled with no protection at all [S205]. CLS has offered PvP since September 2002 [S204] Reported.
Why LSEG Settlement risk is the biggest unsolved problem in FX. Any LSEG FX workflow that helps clients route more trades into PvP or clearing (ForexClear) answers a regulator-backed need. Inferred
Say "BIS found about $1.4 trillion of FX a day still settles with no protection, so post-trade is where FX innovation will be judged."

EMIR 3 and the active account requirementClearing
When EU; in force 24 Dec 2024; active account requirement applies from 25 Jun 2025 [S161]
What Some EU firms must hold an "active account" at an EU CCP and clear part of their euro and Polish zloty interest rate derivatives and euro short-term interest rate derivatives through it: at least five trades per sub-category per reference period [S161] Reported.
Why LSEG A direct attempt to move euro swap clearing from LCH to EU CCPs such as Eurex. Eurex's share of EUR swaps rose from 8.4% to 13.5% in a year to Q3 2025 [S139] Reported. LCH's defence is netting: splitting a portfolio across two CCPs raises margin for the user. Inferred
Say "EMIR 3's active accounts went live in June 2025; the test is whether euro swap share keeps moving to Eurex or whether the cost of splitting netting sets keeps users at LCH."
EU equivalence for UK CCPsClearing
When European Commission decision 31 Jan 2025; three-year extension to 30 Jun 2028, applying from 1 Jul 2025 [S160]
What Lets EU firms keep using UK CCPs like LCH. Commissioner Albuquerque: "With this extension of equivalence for UK CCPs we are safeguarding EU financial stability and avoiding short-term risks", and the extension "will allow the recently agreed EMIR 3 measures to start taking effect, increasing clearing in the EU and reducing our exposure to UK CCPs" [S160].
Why LSEG The single most important date for LCH. Without renewal, EU members could face limits on using LCH after 30 Jun 2028. Inferred
Say "EU equivalence for LCH runs to 30 June 2028; that is the cliff edge, and the EU said openly it is buying time for EMIR 3 to pull clearing home."
UK CCP rules move to the Bank of EnglandClearing
When FSMA 2023 rule-making powers; BoE consultation Jul 2025 on an FMI Rulebook; final rules no earlier than mid-2026 [S197]
What The Bank of England gains powers to write its own rules for CCPs and CSDs, replacing retained EU law (UK EMIR) [S197] Reported. Separately, it is "consulting ... on ... permitting tokenised assets as eligible collateral for CCPs" [S184].
Why LSEG LCH Ltd's UK rulebook will be rewritten by its home regulator, and tokenised collateral could change what members post as margin. Inferred

T+1 settlementClearing
When UK draft SI, "The Central Securities Depositories (Amendment) (Intended Settlement Date) Regulations 2026", published 20 Nov 2025, comments to 27 Feb 2026; T+1 "from 11 October 2027" [S158]. EU Regulation (EU) 2025/2075, OJ 14 Oct 2025, applies from 11 Oct 2027 [S159]
What Trades in shares and bonds must settle one business day after the trade, not two. UK and EU move on the same day, so cross-border trades stay aligned [S158, S159].
Why LSEG Less time to fix errors means more automation and same-day trade checks; less counterparty risk; LCH and venue operations must adapt. Risk teams will watch settlement fails. Inferred It lands four months after a June 2027 internship starts.
Say "T+1 arrives on 11 October 2027 in both the UK and EU, which halves the time to fix a broken trade, so operations and risk teams will be judged on automation."
Source uncertain: T+1 legal status

Only the draft UK statutory instrument (November 2025) is confirmed [S158]. Whether the final SI has been made in 2026 is not verified. Say "the UK has legislated in draft for T+1 on 11 October 2027, the same day as the EU", not "the law is in force". The EU regulation is published [S159] but was read via a secondary source.

The commercial backdrop. In 2025 LSEG paid £1.2bn to cut the founding banks' share of SwapClear's revenue surplus from c.30% to 15% for 2025 and 10% from 2026 to 2045 [S120]. Key term

Vertical silo vs open access: a vertical silo owns trading and clearing for its products and keeps them together (Deutsche Börse describes its "vertically integrated business model" [S127]). Open access means a CCP clears trades from many venues. LCH is the open-access model, partly owned with banks; Euronext pulling its clearing in-house is the silo model winning one round [S120].
