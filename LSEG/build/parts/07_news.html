1. Record half, guidance raised, but the shares fell

LSEG H1 2026 Interim Results and Interim Report, 30 Jul 2026 [S220, S221]; Reuters via Global Banking & Finance Review, 30 Jul 2026 [S223] Confirmed

What happened. LSEG's income for January to June 2026 was £4,799m, up 8.4% on an organic basis (stripping out currency moves and acquisitions). Why it matters to LSEG. 3. LCH: record clearing, with EMIR 3 in the background

LSEG Interim Report H1 2026 [S221] Confirmed; EMIR 3 commentary from Eurex, J.P. Morgan and others [S257] Reported

What happened. A clearing house (CCP) stands in the middle of a trade so that if one side goes bust, the other still gets paid. LCH's SwapClear cleared US$1,165 trillion of interest rate swap notional in H1, up 29.4%. Client trades rose 23.8% to 3.283m. ForexClear (FX clearing) cleared US$33.3 trillion, up 45.2%, with 41 members. OTC Derivatives revenue rose 13.6%. LSEG added more than 80 Post Trade Solutions customers and launched TradeAgent with 11 customers live [S221].

The policy threat. Under the EU's EMIR 3 rules, since 25 June 2025 large EU firms must hold an "active account" at an EU clearing house (in practice Eurex) for euro and zloty interest rate derivatives and clear a representative number of trades there. The first reports were due by 31 July 2026. Roughly 80% of euro interest rate derivatives are cleared outside the EU, mainly at LCH; Eurex has about 20% [S257].

Source uncertain

The 80% / 20% split comes from search snippets and partly from Eurex, LCH's competitor, which has an interest in the number [S257]. Use it as "roughly four-fifths" and attribute it.

Why it matters to LSEG. SwapClear is a crown jewel. Any EU rule that forces business to Frankfurt is a strategic risk. So far, volumes are still at records [S221] Inferred.

Why it matters to your seat. Risk BI and strategy Risk teams watch margin and member exposures; strategy teams watch whether euro volume is leaking to Eurex.

Your question or opinion

Ask: "With the first active-account reports in, has the requirement moved meaningful euro swap volume to Eurex, or is it mostly a box-ticking exercise?" Suggested position: H1 records suggest the threat is slow-moving; the bigger date is the end of EU equivalence for UK CCPs on 30 June 2028 [S160].

4. LSEG FX: Forward First Fixing records and Euromoney wins

LSEG FX update, 19 May 2026 [S238]; LSEG FX updates page [S239]; Interim Report [S221] Confirmed; FX Markets snippet [S240] Reported

What happened. A "fixing" is a benchmark exchange rate set at a fixed time, such as the WM/Reuters 4pm London fix, which LSEG owns [S221]. Asset managers often must trade at the fix. Forward First Fixing (FFF) on FXall lets them pre-arrange the forward part of those trades electronically, hours ahead. FFF passed US$1.5tn of cumulative volume, with a US$82bn daily peak on 31 March 2026, 30+ asset manager and corporate clients and 18 partner banks. LSEG is waiving FFF brokerage fees for the rest of 2026, and cites a 90%+ cut in manual processing for its largest client [S238]. Q2 2026 was another record quarter at over US$330bn [S221]. Across LSEG FX, average daily volume was US$561bn in H1 (+5.8%) and FX revenue £145m (+7.6%). US Streaming FX Spot ran its first trades on AWS Outposts with about 70% lower latency [S221]. LSEG won Euromoney 2026 World's Best FX Venue and Best Venue for FX Spot, did the first Deposits trade on FXall and integrated LoopFX [S239]. A "Split Risk" credit-intermediated forwards service is in preparation [S240].

Why it matters to LSEG. FX revenue grew slower than the rest of Markets. Products that remove a manual job for a client are how FX wins share without relying on volatility Inferred.

Why it matters to your seat. FX customers The JD's first bullet is "Solve problems for our FX customers". FFF is the best example in this pack of exactly that: a client pain point (manual processing), a product, and a measured result.

Your question or opinion

Ask: "At what point does adoption justify switching FFF fees on, and how would you avoid losing clients when you do?" Both sides: waiving fees speeds adoption and builds a habit; but every month free makes it harder to charge later. Suggested position: switch on fees in tiers once the largest clients depend on the workflow, and lead with the operational savings rather than the price.

LSEG press release, 21 Jul 2026 [S227]; Interim Report [S221] Confirmed

Suggested position: participants and spreads first, because volume follows only if trading costs overnight are acceptable.

Digital and tokenisation

10. Mansion House and the digital gilt: HSBC and LSEG link up

Mondo Visione (speech text), 14 and 15 Jul 2026 [S245]; Ledger Insights, Jul 2026 [S246]; Interim Report [S221] Confirmed

What happened. Rachel Reeves' last Mansion House speech covered ISA and advice reform, pensions investing an extra £10bn in private markets, stablecoins, tokenised deposits, and the UK becoming the first G7 country to issue a digital sovereign bond "by early next year" [S245]. A depository is where ownership of securities is recorded; a digital one records it on a distributed ledger, allowing near-instant settlement. On 13 July HSBC Orion became the first firm approved to go live as a Digital Securities Depository in the Bank of England's Digital Securities Sandbox. HSBC and LSEG signed an MoU (memorandum of understanding) for a link: HSBC Orion as the issuer-side depository, LSEG's depository as the investor-side one. The digital gilt, called DIGIT, is due by Q1 2027 [S246, S221].

Why it matters to LSEG. It puts LSEG in the plumbing of tokenised government debt, alongside its Digital Markets Infrastructure (DMI) and the new Digital Settlement House (DiSH) [S221].

Why it matters to your seat. Markets research BI and strategy Debt listings and digital markets research meet here.

Your question or opinion

Ask: "Why do you think LSEG chose to be the investor-side depository rather than compete to be the issuer-side one for DIGIT?" Suggested position (Inferred): the investor side is where LSEG's existing client relationships are, and it keeps LSEG in the flow of every future digital issue, not just one.

Three reading-list warnings

Treat share prices from aggregator sites as low confidence and check the live price on the day. Many free sites republish Reuters copy with their own "takeaways" added; quote only the Reuters text. Reuters supplies news into LSEG Workspace, so mention that relationship if you quote it in an LSEG interview [S223]. For a paywalled article, quote only the headline and date unless you have read it.

The 15-minute daily routine

Minutes 0 to 5: LSEG itself

Scan LSEG press releases and RNS. On the first working day of each month, add Tradeweb's monthly volumes. Write one line: "What did LSEG announce, and which division?"

Minutes 5 to 10: the market

One Reuters LSEG or exchanges story, then City AM's markets page. Note one number: the gilt yield, sterling, or an IPO price. Ask: "Would this raise or lower volumes at LCH, Tradeweb or FXall?"

Minutes 10 to 15: one specialist

Rotate: Monday The TRADE, Tuesday FX Markets headlines, Wednesday Global Custodian, Thursday Ledger Insights, Friday Bank of England or FCA news. Finish by writing a one-sentence opinion.

Adapted from the draft reading list, which suggests about 20 minutes a day (10 daily, plus specialist sources twice a week and regulators weekly). Event days override the routine: on 22 October (Q3 statement) and 28 October (Budget), spend the 15 minutes on that event alone [S252, S243].

The habit that pays off

Keep a running note of the "one-sentence opinions" from the routine. By the video interview you will have twenty or more, and the strengths-based format rewards candidates who sound like they enjoy following markets, not ones who crammed the night before.
