# Reading list for LSEG Markets interview prep

Prepared 2026-10-05. Purpose: a short routine to stay current between now and the interview stages. Access status is my best understanding as of today; items marked [Inferred] were not checked in this sweep. Every [Sxx] resolves in Part 15 (source table) of the interview pack. Part 7 of the pack has the news these sources produced up to 5 October 2026.

## Suggested routine (about 20 minutes a day)
- Daily (10 min): LSEG press releases and RNS, City AM markets page, one Reuters LSEG story.
- Twice a week (10 min): The TRADE or Markets Media (trading and market structure), FX Markets headlines, Global Custodian (post-trade).
- Weekly: Bank of England and FCA news pages; FT Alphaville if you have access; LSEG Insights blog.
- Event-driven: LSEG Q3 Trading Statement 22 Oct 2026 [S252]; UK Budget 28 Oct 2026 [S243]; Tradeweb monthly volumes (first week of each month); BoE MPC decisions.

## Warnings
- Treat share price snippets from aggregator sites as low confidence; check the live price on the day.
- Many free sites republish Reuters copy with their own "takeaways" added; quote only the Reuters text.
- For paywalled articles, quote only the headline and date unless you have read the article.
