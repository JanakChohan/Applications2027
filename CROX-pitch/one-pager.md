## Why the market is wrong (variant perception)

1. **Street EPS ignores buybacks.** Guidance excludes them and consensus follows. Crocs retired about 10% of its shares in each of 2025 and 2026 and has $1.7bn authorised (23% of market cap).
2. **North America is distorted, not collapsing.** Promotions were cut on purpose in May 2025; an accounting shift hides DTC growth until Q3 2027. Underlying DTC grew 2-5% in each quarter of 2026. (Lowest-confidence leg of the thesis.)
3. **This is a majority-international brand.** 51% of Crocs Brand revenue, growing 9-12%, with share one-third of mature levels in China, India, Japan, Germany and France.

## Proprietary analysis

Scoring nine revenue lines for fad exposure puts **16.4% of revenue ($674m) at risk**. Run through the cost structure that cuts EPS to $7.39 (-49%), worth about $52 at 7x. The March 2026 low of $76 priced nearly all of that. Today's price embeds about a 40% chance of my milder bear case.

## Catalysts

FY2027 guide about 11-Feb-2027 (my ex-buyback EPS $16.61 vs $16.01 consensus); Q3 2027 lapping of the North America accounting shift; continuous buybacks; tariff litigation.

## Top risks

1. Clog fad unwinds in North America (still -2% in Q3-26; teen rank No. 10).
2. Earnings quality: operating income 14% below FY23; EPS growth is share count; non-GAAP tax rate 18% vs 23% GAAP.
3. CEO sold $14.1m in six months, no 10b5-1 plan disclosed; three CFOs in 16 months.

**Kill criteria:** North America DTC ex-shift negative two quarters running; international growth below 5% two quarters running; FY27 margin guide below 21.5%; acquisition above $500m; leverage above 1.5x.

## What I would not say

The stock is not cheap against Deckers (10.1x vs 10.9x forward P/E), it is in the top quartile of its own five-year P/E range, and it has no patent moat. The long is about carry and compounding. After +34% in a month, start at half size and add below $140.
