Item 5.07. Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.
On June 10, 2025, Crocs, Inc. (the “Company”) held its 2025 annual meeting of stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”) as a virtual meeting online live via audio webcast. The results of the matters submitted to a vote of the stockholders at the Annual Meeting were as follows:
(a) The nominees for election as Class II directors to serve until the 2028 annual meeting of stockholders or until their successors are duly elected and qualified, were elected based on the following votes:
