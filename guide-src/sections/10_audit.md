<p class="sectionmeta">Your actual document (Janak_Chohan_CV_4.docx), taken apart and rebuilt. What breaks, what's strong, every bullet judged — then a complete rewrite you can copy.</p>

The good news first: **this is already an above-average CV.** Your Pictet and Box bullets are specific, mechanism-rich and quantified — genuinely strong. The work here is not repair; it is (a) fixing a structural parse risk, (b) **rotating the CV onto its target axis** (Section 0), and (c) tightening three off-target sections.

## 9.1 ATS / parse audit — what survives, what breaks

<div class="box warning" markdown="1">
<span class="lbl">The one structural problem: every role header is a table</span>

Your CV places each employer/role/location/date block inside a **1×2 two-column table** (seven of them, plus the Interests line). Single-column body text, standard headings and no graphics are all good — but those **tables are your main parse risk.** Older parsers (Schroders runs **Taleo**; early-careers systems vary) can read a two-column table out of order, producing scrambled output like "*London, UK Global Client Group… Pictet Asset Management Jun 2026.*" Modern parsers (Workday, SuccessFactors) usually cope, but you're formatting for the **weakest** system you'll hit.

**Fix:** delete every table. Put company + role on one line, location + dates right-aligned via a **tab stop** (not a table cell). Same visual result, zero parse risk. This is the single highest-priority change.
</div>

## 9.2 Structural changes, with rationale

1. **Lead with the First.** Fold "Average Grade: 81% (First Class)" into the UEA line so it's read in the 7-second scan. It's your strongest filter-passer (Section 0) — don't let it sit on a separate line below.
2. **Re-point the whole document buy-side / client-facing.** Your Pictet Global Client Group work is dead-on-target and stays at the top. The **Goldman trading content** and the **Oakwood valuation content** currently argue *against* your target — trim and re-frame both (below).
3. **Kill the anti-client line.** "*Selected the hedge fund track over market making, running directional risk rather than client flow*" must go — it explicitly advertises the opposite instinct to the one client-servicing/coverage roles hire for.
4. **Add a Skills line.** Salesforce, Excel, IMC (when registered), languages — genuine tools only, findable keywords (Section 4.4).
5. **Compress the 2019 accounting placement to one line.** It's seven years old; keep it only for the attention-to-detail signal, tightly.
6. **Add 2–3 relevant modules** under Education to surface "investments/regulation" vocabulary naturally.
7. **Decide on the missing roles** (coffee shop, real estate, UBS/Barclays/JPM) — see 9.5.
