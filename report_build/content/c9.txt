#|12|Glossary|Part 6 — Every technical term used in this report, alphabetised
GL|**Active management** — Employing people to choose investments with the aim of beating an index. Costs more than passive; must justify the fee through outperformance.
GL|**Alternatives** — Investments outside conventional traded stocks and bonds: private credit, distressed debt, real estate, hedge fund strategies. Usually illiquid, usually higher-fee.
GL|**Attribution (performance attribution)** — The analysis that decomposes a portfolio's return relative to its benchmark into causes: duration positioning, sector allocation, individual security selection. What client-facing analysts spend a great deal of time producing.
GL|**AUM (assets under management)** — The total market value of money a firm manages. The base on which fees are charged.
GL|**Basis point (bp, "beep")** — One hundredth of one percent, 0.01%. 100 basis points = 1%.
GL|**Benchmark** — The index a portfolio is measured against. Beating it is the definition of success for an active manager.
GL|**Bond** — A tradable loan. The issuer pays interest (the coupon) and repays the principal at maturity.
GL|**Carried interest ("carry")** — A share of a private fund's profits paid to the manager, typically 15–20% above a hurdle rate. Distinct from, and additional to, the management fee.
GL|**Closed-end fund** — A fund with a fixed share count that trades on an exchange. Can hold illiquid assets and use leverage because it never faces redemptions.
GL|**Cost-income ratio (CIR)** — Total costs divided by total revenue. The standard efficiency measure for asset managers. Lower is better; Allianz's Asset Management segment ran at 60.2% in Q2 2026.
GL|**Coupon** — The interest a bond pays, as a percentage of face value.
GL|**Credit rating** — An agency opinion on default likelihood. Investment grade is BBB− and above; high yield (junk) is BB+ and below.
GL|**Credit spread** — The extra yield a riskier borrower must pay above a comparable government bond. Widens when investors are nervous.
GL|**Default** — Failure to make a promised payment.
GL|**Duration** — Sensitivity of a bond's price to interest rate changes, quoted in years. Price change ≈ −duration × change in yield.
GL|**ETF (exchange-traded fund)** — A fund whose shares trade on an exchange throughout the day. Usually passive; PIMCO's are mostly active.
GL|**Fixed income** — The bond market, broadly. Called "fixed" because payments are usually predetermined.
GL|**Flows (net flows)** — New client money in, minus client money out. The truest measure of whether clients are choosing a manager.
GL|**Form ADV** — A disclosure document US investment advisers must file with the SEC. Part 2A is the client-facing brochure and contains PIMCO's published fee schedules.
GL|**High yield** — Bonds from riskier issuers rated BB+ or below. Higher coupons, real default risk.
GL|**Investment grade** — Bonds rated BBB− or above, considered relatively safe.
GL|**LDI (liability-driven investing)** — Constructing a portfolio whose value moves in step with a pension scheme's future obligations, insulating its funding position from rate moves.
GL|**Leverage** — Using borrowed money to increase exposure. Amplifies gains and losses.
GL|**Management fee** — An annual charge as a percentage of assets, payable regardless of performance. The core of asset management revenue.
GL|**Mandate** — A client's instruction to manage a specific pot of money to specific rules. Also used to mean the account itself.
GL|**Maturity** — The date a bond's principal is repaid.
GL|**MFN (most-favoured-nation) clause** — A contractual right for a client to be offered any better fee terms given to a comparable client. A structural constraint on pricing.
GL|**Mutual fund / open-ended fund** — A pooled vehicle that creates and cancels units as investors buy and sell.
GL|**Operating leverage** — The property that, when costs are largely fixed, profit moves proportionally more than revenue in both directions.
GL|**Over the counter (OTC)** — Traded directly between two parties rather than on an exchange. Most bonds trade this way, which is why dealer relationships and scale matter.
GL|**Passive management** — Tracking an index mechanically rather than choosing investments. Very cheap.
GL|**Performance fee / incentive fee** — A fee charged only on outperformance above a benchmark.
GL|**Rack rate** — A published list price before negotiation. PIMCO's Form ADV fee schedules are rack rates.
GL|**RFP (request for proposal)** — The formal document a prospective institutional client issues to solicit bids from managers. The start of an institutional sales cycle.
GL|**Securitised** — Many individual loans (typically mortgages) pooled and sliced into tranches with different risk levels. PIMCO's area of deepest expertise.
GL|**Separate account / segregated mandate** — A portfolio managed for a single client, who owns the securities directly rather than owning fund units.
GL|**SMA (separately managed account)** — A smaller separate account, usually delivered through an adviser platform.
GL|**Structural alpha** — Return earned by exploiting the fact that many bond-market participants (central banks, insurers, regulated institutions) are not trying to maximise profit. PIMCO's central intellectual claim for active bond management.
GL|**Total expense ratio (TER) / expense ratio** — The all-in annual cost of holding a fund, as a percentage of assets.
GL|**UCITS** — A European regulatory standard allowing a fund to be sold across borders. PIMCO's UCITS range (PIMCO GIS) is domiciled in Dublin.
GL|**Uncalled capital** — Money investors have committed to a private fund but not yet transferred. Counted in platform AUM though not yet invested — a reason alternatives AUM is not directly comparable to fund AUM.
GL|**Wholesale / intermediary distribution** — Selling through private banks, wealth managers, platforms and advisers rather than direct to institutions or retail investors.
GL|**Wrap programme** — An arrangement in which an adviser bundles management, custody and trading into a single fee.
GL|**Yield** — The actual return earned buying at today's price and holding to maturity. Moves inversely to price.
