# Goldman Sachs Summer Analyst — The Real Process
### London / EMEA · targeting the 2027 Summer Analyst Programme · the truth, not the brochure

> **⏰ Timing that matters right now:** the **2027 EMEA Summer Analyst application opens 15 August 2026** — i.e. **tomorrow**, as this is written on 14 Aug 2026. Review is **rolling with no fixed deadline**, and early application is a real, repeatedly-cited edge. If you're eligible, getting in during the **first 1–2 weeks** is one of the highest-leverage things you can do.

---

## 1. Eligibility — which year do you actually need to be in?

**Official rule:** Summer Analyst is for students *currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's* in their **penultimate or final year of study.**

**The graduation-year maths for a summer-2027 London intake:**

| Your degree | Graduating | In 2026–27 you are | Your primary target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3-year UK degree | **2028** | Year 2 (penultimate) | **2027 SA — this cycle (opens 15 Aug 2026)** ✅ |
| 4-year (placement/abroad/Scottish MA) | **2028** | Year 3 (penultimate) | 2027 SA — this cycle ✅ |
| 3-year UK degree | 2027 | Final year | Eligible, but converts to full-time not a return offer — you're the secondary cohort |
| 4-year / integrated Master's | **2029** | Year 1–2 | **2028 SA** is your natural target; 2026–27 = **spring-week** territory, not SA |

- **If you graduate in 2028, the cycle opening tomorrow is squarely yours.**
- **If you graduate in 2029, verify carefully** — you're most likely a *2028* Summer Analyst candidate and a *spring-week* candidate now. Applying to SA a year early is generally a wasted application.
- **Discipline:** officially *school-agnostic, major-agnostic.* Any degree is accepted for front-office; Engineering expects coding ability (not necessarily a CS degree).
- **Grades:** Goldman publishes **no formal UCAS tariff or 2:1 cut-off** on its UK pages. The *effective* bar is very high (see §6). *(Confidence: medium — absence of a stated minimum is confirmed; the real bar is inferred from forums.)*
- **Visa/right to work:** the London office historically sponsors international summer analysts; the 2025 intern class spanned 90+ nationalities. **Confirm sponsorship wording on the live role listing.** *(Confidence: medium.)*

**Source:** [Goldman EMEA Summer Analyst page](https://www.goldmansachs.com/careers/students/programs-and-internships/emea/summer-analyst-programme) · [Prepare page](https://www.goldmansachs.com/careers/students/prepare)

## 3. The full process, stage by stage (London SA)

### Stage 0 — Where you apply (the platform)
- Public entry: **goldmansachs.com/careers/students**. Individual roles live on Goldman's **own applicant system, `higher.gs.com`** (e.g. `higher.gs.com/roles/...`) — **not** a third-party like Workday.
- Your CV is **parsed by an ATS.** The application captures personal/education details, **division + location choices**, languages, CV upload, and **word-limited motivation/competency questions** (historically ~300 words) — see §7 on what this means for your "cover letter."

### Stage 1 — Online aptitude tests — **⚠️ genuinely uncertain, read carefully**
This is the most contradicted point across sources:
- **Test-prep vendors** claim a **numerical test (~20Q/20min)** + **verbal test (~10Q)**, and for **Engineering a HackerRank coding test** (2–4 problems, 120–180 min).
- **But Goldman's own "Prepare" page** describes the flow as **Application → HireVue video → Superday**, with **no standalone aptitude test** for standard/front-office roles (only Engineering's HackerRank). Recent EMEA forum accounts (2024–26) mostly report going **straight from application to HireVue.**
- **Most likely current reality:** for **front-office / most business divisions in EMEA, there is no separate timed numerical/verbal test in recent cycles** — the HireVue is the first automated hurdle. Vendor prep pages are partly legacy / partly conflated with other banks. **Engineering does get HackerRank.**
- Goldman does **not** use pymetrics/game-based tests (that's other banks).
- *(Confidence: medium — commercially-motivated vendors disagree with Goldman's official page + recent forums. **Verify on your specific role.**)*

### Stage 2 — The HireVue video interview (you specifically asked how this is accessed)
**How you get it / who gets it — the honest version:**
- Official wording: *"If your application is selected, you will be asked to participate in a video interview."* The invite arrives as an **emailed link to HireVue (hirevue.com)**; you complete it **in your browser, asynchronously**, within a deadline.
- **On "the HireVue is selective, how do they access it":** officially there's a CV screen first ("if selected"). **In EMEA practice in recent cycles the CV screen before HireVue has been *light* — a large share of complete, eligible applicants receive the HireVue link within days to ~1–3 weeks.** So the **HireVue itself is the first *heavy* screen**, not the CV step before it.
- **There is no hidden portal or insider unlock.** The link is triggered simply by submitting a **complete application to an eligible role.** The only real levers are: apply **early**, submit a **complete, tailored** application, and **network via My GS Events / a referral** to make sure your CV gets a proper look.
- Once invited, you typically get a **short window (~24–48h up to a week)** to finish it.
- *(Confidence: medium — the "most eligible applicants get the HireVue" pattern is well-attested on WSO/Reddit for EMEA but is not stated officially and can tighten in any given year.)*

**Format:**
- **Asynchronous, pre-recorded, one-way.** Roughly **3–6 questions** (some report up to 7), **~30 min total.** ~**30 seconds to prepare**, up to ~**2 minutes to answer** each.
- **Content varies by division:** front-office = behavioural/competency + light commercial (e.g. *"explain what an investment bank does in your own words,"* motivation, teamwork/leadership stories, markets awareness); **Engineering = HackerRank coding** instead of/alongside the video.
- **Treat it as a real interview, not a formality** — because so many people receive it, it's where a large fraction are cut.

### Stage 3 — Superday / final round
- Official: *"If selected following your HireVue, you'll be invited to a series of final round interviews,"* and Goldman runs **"between two and five interviews for campus hires, depending on the division."**
- Typical London shape: **multiple back-to-back ~30–45 min interviews** with **Analysts, Associates and VPs** (often ~3 interviewers). **Behavioural + technical** (IB: accounting/valuation/markets; Markets: mental maths, brainteasers, commercial awareness; internal: competency + problem-solving). Can include **case studies, group exercises or focus groups.**
- One secondary source cites **~25% of Superday attendees getting offers** — *indicative only.* *(Confidence: low.)*

### Stage 4 — Timeline (2027 cycle)
- **Opens: 15 Aug 2026.** **Rolling review, no fixed deadline** — Goldman says *"apply as soon as you are ready,"* and roles are typically cut off in a **Sept–Nov window** as they fill.
- **Early submission is a genuine, repeated advantage — target the first 1–2 weeks.**
- Approximate stage timing (secondary-source model, *not* official; individuals move faster if they apply early): **HireVue ~Oct–Nov 2026 → interviews ~Nov–Dec 2026 → Superday ~Jan–Feb 2027 → offers ~Feb–Mar 2027.** *(Confidence: medium.)*

## 4. Divisions & how many you can apply to

**London SA divisions** (the official page lists ~18 teams; the main ones):
- **Global Banking & Markets** — the merged division covering **Investment Banking (Advisory/Coverage)** *and* **FICC & Equities (Sales & Trading)**
- **Asset & Wealth Management** · **Engineering** (HackerRank) · **Global Investment Research** · **Risk** · **Operations** · **Controllers/Finance** · **Compliance, Legal, Internal Audit, Human Capital Management, Corporate Treasury, Tax**, etc.

**How many applications per cycle — sources conflict:**
- Some guides say **up to 2 divisions / 3 locations**; others say **up to ~4 business+location combinations per recruiting year**, with a **5th auto-withdrawing an earlier one.**
- **Working assumption: ~3–4 role/location combinations max — don't spam.** **Check the exact count the portal shows you when you apply.** *(Confidence: medium.)*

**Is it one reused cover letter?** **No — and this matters for your plan (see §7).** You pick division(s)+location(s) and answer **division-specific motivation/competency questions.** Screeners look for *"specific division work, named deals or themes, a clear 'why this team'"* — **reusing identical generic essays across four applications is a named top rejection cause.**

## 6. Recent changes & the forum "truths" the brochure won't tell you

**What's changed (2024–26):**
- **Volume up, odds down:** 360k+ applications (2025), **0.7%** acceptance, third straight sub-1% year. The screen is tighter than ever → early + tailored matters more.
- **AI warning:** Goldman EMEA campus recruiting has **warned students against using ChatGPT/AI to answer HireVue and interview questions** — they watch for canned AI answers. **Write in your own voice.** ([Fortune, Jun 2025](https://www.fortune.com/2025/06/11/goldman-sachs-students-ai-chatgpt-interviews-amazon-anthropic))
- **Reorg:** Investment Banking + Global Markets now sit under unified **Global Banking & Markets.**
- **DEI:** Goldman has **softened formal DEI metrics/targets (2025)**; diversity insight programmes still exist as pipelines but the framing is lighter.

**Forum truths (well-attested, *not* official — treat with judgement):**
- **Target-school reality (London):** officially "school-agnostic." On WSO, London front-office is described as **elitist — dominated by LSE/Oxbridge/Warwick/HEC and top European schools**, with non-target CVs often triaged into a separate stream. **A referral and a spring week materially help**, and non-target success stories do exist. *(Confidence: medium — strong anecdotal consensus, no official confirmation.)*
- **Spring weeks / insight programmes = the fast-track:** Goldman EMEA runs early pipelines — the **Possibilities Series**, a **Virtual Insight Series**, and a **Future Possibilities Insight Event** (Year 12–13). **Strong spring-week/insight performers are widely reported to get fast-tracked toward Summer Analyst** — one of the most reliable routes in, especially for non-targets. *(If you're a 2029 grad, spring 2026/27 is high-value — don't skip it.)*
- **ATS keyword screening:** CVs are parsed — mirror the role's language, name specific deals/teams/products, avoid generic motivation text.
- **The rolling deadline is real and punishing:** waiting for a "deadline" that doesn't formally exist is a common self-inflicted rejection. **Apply in the first 1–2 weeks.**

## 7. What this means for your "cover letter" (important)

You planned **one cover letter reused across roles.** The research adds an important wrinkle:

- Goldman's application is built around **division-specific, word-limited motivation questions (historically ~300 words)** rather than a single free-form letter, and you can hold **~3–4 division/location applications** at once.
- **Reusing one generic block across all of them is a flagged rejection cause.**
- **So the right model is:** build **one master narrative** (your story + the insight-day hook + why Goldman — the Structure-B letter from Deliverable 1) as the **spine**, then **lightly re-point the final "why this team" beat** for each division you apply to. The hook, the story, and the culture-mirroring stay constant; only the last division-specific sentence(s) flex.
- This keeps you inside Sam's philosophy (the firm-level story is the reusable engine) **and** satisfies Goldman's "why this specific team" screen.
