# Canonical JD duty lines (use these exact labels D1-D10 in Parts 1 and 11)
Source: THE JD, Technology Strategy Consulting - TST graduate, Belfast (25060) [S2], plus sister posting [S3] for D9. See findings/WS1_role_team.md section B for the decode.
D1  Senior client engagement and rapport ("work with their most senior leaders and top-level executives"; "build rapport with clients and ensure they have confidence in your capability")
D2  IT operating model ("move away from IT, the department of computers, to technology as a business partner and enabler")
D3  The digital core and the technology landscape ("create a digital core"; "Discovering how technologies support everything an organisation does, and which technologies work best")
D4  Business case and value ("improving financial performance, accelerating speed to market"; "Help assess technology investments, identify opportunities to improve value, agility, efficiency, and user experience")
D5  Net zero and inclusive ways of working
D6  Large-scale transformation, design through implementation (PMO, RAID, status reporting)
D7  Requirements: "Working with individuals from our clients' business and IT teams to understand what the problems and needs are, and transforming those into technical requirements"
D8  Documents and client materials ("Creating documents crucial to the delivery and execution of the programme, alongside materials presented back to clients")
D9  "Using firm-approved AI tools responsibly to support client delivery" [S3]
D10 Teaming across the wider Technology & Transformation practice (the matrix, alliances, delivery centres)
