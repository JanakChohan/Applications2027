Part 16

Gaps and open questions

What this pack could not establish, why, and how you can find out yourself; where sources disagree and what the pack did about it; and an honest check against the brief.

How to use this part

No outside researcher can see inside a private partnership. Some things Deloitte simply does not publish (day rates, utilisation targets, scoring rubrics). Some pages were paywalled or blocked. And several research workstreams hit a shared limit on web searches, so they switched to reading primary pages directly. This part lists every gap the research files recorded, grouped by topic. Use it three ways:

  • Do not bluff on these points. If an interviewer raises one, say what you know and what you could not confirm. That is a strength, not a weakness.
  • Turn some into questions. Gaps marked "ask" make good, genuine questions at an event or at the end of an interview.
  • Check the dated ones. Some will close on a known date (for example, when London roles go live on 5 October 2026 [S14]).
