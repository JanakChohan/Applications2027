How to pitch to each type

Recruiter or assessor

Their question: does this candidate meet the criteria and show the capabilities?

Give them: clear STAR stories that are easy to score, the Shared Values in action, a calm manner.

Avoid: vague answers that leave them nothing to write down.

Manager or senior manager

Their question: can I give this person work on Monday?

Give them: reliability, learning speed, Excel and PowerPoint basics, how you handle a deadline and feedback.

Avoid: grand strategy talk with no sign you can do the work.

Director or partner

Their question: would I put this person in front of a client, and will they stay?

Give them: one sharp, defended view on the client market, real motivation, integrity.

Avoid: long answers. Lead with the point.

The pitches above are [Inferred] from the stage evidence in [S260], [S264] and [S286]. They are general patterns, not Deloitte policy.

Reading the interviewer, and adjusting honestly

Adjusting to your interviewer is about relevance: giving the level of detail they need. It is not about pretending to be someone else or flattering them. Use these signals [Inferred]:

SignalWhat it probably meansAdjust by
Detailed follow-ups on how you did somethingThey are testing whether you can do the work.Give steps, tools and numbers. Keep stories concrete.
Questions about clients, markets or “so what”They think commercially, often a senior person.Lead with your conclusion. Link to client value.
They interrupt or look at the clockYour answer is too long.Finish the sentence, give the result, stop.
They push back on your viewA test of judgement, not a sign you are failing.Restate their point fairly, then hold or update with a reason.
They share their own experienceRapport is building.Ask one genuine follow-up question, then return to your answer.
DON'T DO THIS

Do not research interviewers' private lives or social media, or mention personal details about them. Do not mirror opinions you do not hold. If you learn your interviewer's name, it is fine to read their public Deloitte profile for their team and role, and nothing more.
