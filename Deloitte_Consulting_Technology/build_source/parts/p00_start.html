Who this is for, and where you are now

This pack is for a UK undergraduate applying for 2027 entry to Deloitte UK's consulting or technology business: a graduate role, a Summer Vacation Scheme (Deloitte's internship) or an Industrial Placement. It assumes you know little about consulting today (about 2 out of 10), that no interview is booked, and that pictures help you learn. It is built only from public sources, each tagged with an ID such as [S14] that you can look up in Part 15.

You are at the application stage, and timing is the most urgent fact in the pack. Deloitte's 2026/27 timeline says Consulting, Advisory, Enabling Functions and Real Estate roles open on 5 October 2026, and Technology roles open partly on 28 September and partly on 5 October [S14] [Confirmed]. On 4 October only a first wave of Consulting roles was live, in Belfast and Edinburgh [S1] [Confirmed]. Deloitte runs "a strict one application per year policy" across its early-careers programmes [S14] [Confirmed], and it closes roles early when volumes are high [S13, S261] [Confirmed]. So you get one shot, and an early one is better than a polished late one.

The 10-minute emergency version

If you read nothing else, read this. Each fact is one you can say out loud, with its source.

£5.81bnDeloitte UK revenue FY26, +2%, UK and Switzerland consolidated [S407]
£1.75bnUK Technology & Transformation revenue FY26, +4% [S407]
US$74.5bnDeloitte global revenue FY26, +3.8% in local currency [S70]
784UK equity partners, a record, up 6.5% [S407]
  1. The year. Deloitte UK's financial year ends on 31 May. In FY26 revenue rose 2% to £5.81bn and distributable profit (the pool shared by the owning partners) rose 14% to £899m [S407] [Confirmed].
  2. The precision point. £5.81bn is "UK and Switzerland consolidated" [S407]. Deloitte's metrics split it: UK £5,014m, Switzerland £795m [S88] [Confirmed]. Say "the UK and Swiss firm" when you quote £5.81bn.
  3. Four businesses. Since 2024 Deloitte runs four businesses. UK FY26: Technology & Transformation £1.75bn (+4%), Tax & Legal £1.36bn (+2%), Audit & Assurance £998m (+3%), Strategy, Risk & Transactions Advisory £913m (+1%). They sum to about £5.01bn, the UK figure [S407, S88] [Confirmed].
  4. T&T is your business, and it has had a bumpy ride. It is the largest UK business, about 35% of UK revenue [S88] [Inferred]. It fell 10% in FY25, then grew 4% in FY26 on ERP, cyber and human capital work [S89, S407] [Confirmed].
  5. The global contrast. Globally Deloitte made US$74.5bn, but T&T was the slowest-growing business at 2.5% [S70] [Confirmed]. In the UK it was the fastest. That contrast is a ready-made question.
  6. The leaders. Darren Graves became UK CEO on 1 June 2026; Jane Whitlock chairs the UK Board; Hayley McKelvey joined the UK Executive as the first Chief AI Officer [S407] [Confirmed]. Hammad Rafique leads UK T&T [S274] [Confirmed].
  7. The EMEA firm. On 1 June 2026 the UK firm joined Deloitte EMEA, led by Richard Houston: 16 firms, about 132,000 people, about €20bn of revenue and over €1.5bn of planned investment over four years [S277, S407] [Confirmed].
  8. The independence point. Deloitte also audits. UK rules let an auditor sell only a short "whitelist" of other services to the large listed companies, banks and insurers it audits (public interest entities), with non-audit fees capped at 70% of the audit fee [S39, S364] [Confirmed]. So consulting is built mostly on clients Deloitte does not audit: £3,819m of non-audit revenue came from non-audit clients in FY25, against £155m from audit clients [S35] [Confirmed].
  9. AI inside the firm. Deloitte's own generative AI tool, PairD, reached 75,000 people in Europe and the Middle East by January 2024 [S328], and UK staff sent it about a million prompts a month in FY25 [S85] [Confirmed]. In October 2025 Deloitte rolled out Anthropic's Claude to more than 470,000 people, with 15,000 to be certified [S116] [Confirmed].
  10. AI in your job. The JD's sister posting lists "Using firm-approved AI tools responsibly to support client delivery" as a duty [S3] [Confirmed]. "Firm-approved" means client data stays inside approved tools.
  11. The Australia lesson. In 2025 Deloitte Australia agreed to repay part of a A$440,000 government contract after its report was found to contain fabricated references and a misquoted court judgment; the revised report disclosed AI use [S388] [Confirmed]. The lesson: you own every line you send.
  12. The recruitment stages. Application, Immersive Online Assessment, Job Simulation (recorded), Final Stage (a 30 to 40 minute topic discussion and a 50 minute skills and motivation interview), outcome. Each step comes "within four weeks" of the last [S260] [Confirmed].
  13. The values. Use the five Shared Values: Lead the way; Serve with integrity; Take care of each other; Foster inclusion; Collaborate for measurable impact [S266] [Confirmed]. Prep sites still quote an old list of four; do not use it [S264].
  14. Partner numbers, if asked. 784 equity partners is the year-end headcount from the results release; the accounts give 799 on a full-time-equivalent average basis [S407, S88] [Confirmed]. Quote 784.
  15. The market. UK consulting was worth about £21.8bn in 2025 after 3% growth, with 6% forecast for 2026 [S150] [Reported].
SAY THIS IN THE INTERVIEW (THE 30-SECOND WHY DELOITTE)

"I want to start where the shape of the firm helps me learn. Deloitte keeps audit, tax, advisory and technology in one partnership, and last year showed why that matters: in FY25 T&T revenue fell 10%, but the firm fell only 1% [S89, S305]. That stability pays for £180 million of technology investment and a new Chief AI Officer [S407]. And in FY26 T&T was the fastest-growing UK business, on ERP, cyber and human capital work [S407]. That is the work I want to learn."

ONE QUESTION TO ASK THEM

"Globally T&T grew slowest at 2.5%, but in the UK it grew fastest at 4%. What is the UK doing differently?" [S70, S407]

DON'T SAY THIS

"Deloitte UK made £5.81bn." That figure includes Switzerland [S407]. Also avoid "Deloitte is the biggest consulting firm on every measure": Accenture is about the same size overall [S129].

The master diagram: the firm, the industry and the role on one page

Read Figure 33 from top to bottom. Clients have problems and pay fees (layer 1). Industry forces decide what can be sold, to whom and at what cost (layer 2). Deloitte's model turns that into four businesses, a network, alliances and delivery centres, with an independence wall between audit and the advisory businesses for audit clients (layer 3). How to read this pack: tags, sources and boxes

Every non-obvious fact carries a source ID in square brackets, such as [S407]. In the PDF it is a link to the source table in Part 15, which gives the outlet, title, date, web address and whether the page was read in full. A confidence tag follows the claim:

TagWhat it meansExample
[Confirmed]A primary source (Deloitte, a regulator, a statute) or two credible outlets agree.UK T&T revenue £1.75bn in FY26 [S407] [Confirmed].
[Reported]One credible source, or a primary page that may be out of date. Fine to use; say where it came from.The UK consulting market grew 3% in 2025 [S150] [Reported].
[Inferred]This pack's own reasoning from sourced facts. Present it as your view.T&T is about 35% of UK revenue [S88] [Inferred].
[Estimate]A calculated figure with stated assumptions. Labelled on charts too.UK T&T fee-earning headcount of roughly 5,000 to 7,500 [S23, S24] [Estimate].
[Anecdote]A candidate's or employee's own account. Useful colour, never proof.An experienced-hire candidate reported a director round [S286] [Anecdote].
