# Reading list: staying current for a Deloitte UK Consulting / Technology interview

Prepared 4 October 2026. Built from the pack's research (news workstream WS5 and industry/regulation workstream WS4). Source IDs in brackets match Part 15 of the PDF pack. Where no stable URL was captured in the research, the entry says so rather than guessing one.

## How to use it

- **Weekly, about 20 minutes:** one trade news site (Consultancy.uk or City AM), plus Deloitte's Monday Briefing.
- **Quarterly:** the Deloitte UK CFO Survey and Accenture's results.
- **The week before an interview:** re-read the Deloitte UK and Deloitte Global results releases and check the Deloitte UK press room for anything new.
- Keep a one-page log: date, headline, one sentence on why it matters to Deloitte, one question you could ask.
