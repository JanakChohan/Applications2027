# Lead verification notes (read by all writers; these override conflicting statements in other findings files)

## 1. Findings
- RESOLVED: the £5.81bn FY26 revenue figure. Deloitte's own footnote 1 says: "All revenue numbers presented herein, unless otherwise noted, represent UK and Switzerland consolidated" [S407] [Confirmed]. The four business lines named in the release (T&T £1.75bn, SR&T Advisory £913m, Tax & Legal £1.36bn, Audit & Assurance £998m) are each introduced as "our UK ... business" and sum to about £5.02bn. UPDATE: Deloitte's FY26 metrics document confirms the split: UK £5,014m, Switzerland £795m, total £5,809m [S88] [Confirmed]. In the pack: quote £5.81bn as "UK and Switzerland consolidated", the four business lines as UK figures summing to £5.01bn, and Switzerland £795m as its own bar labelled "Switzerland".
- Partner counts: the press release says equity partners rose 6.5% to 784 (headcount); the metrics/accounts give 799 equity partners on an FTE-average basis, 1,453 partners in all, and 23,970 FTE employees, which the release rounds to "25,000 people" [S88, S407]. Quote 784 as the year-end headline and note the FTE basis if using 799.
- Footnote 3: "Deloitte's advisory businesses include the Technology & Transformation (T&T) business as well as the Strategy, Risk & Transactions Advisory (SRTA) business" [S407] [Confirmed].
- Distributable profit £899m is also UK and Switzerland [S407]. Average profit per equity partner £1,125,000 (FY25 £1,051,000) [S407].
- Total UK tax contribution £1.86bn: £1.165bn collected for HMRC plus £696m borne [S407].
- Investment in technology £180m in FY26 vs £158m in FY25; 3,500 new hires including more than 1,950 graduates, apprentices and interns; over 6,000 of 25,000 people promoted; 48 promoted to partner; 68 salaried partners converted to equity; equity partners up 6.5% to 784; bonus pool up 14%; average in-grade salaries up over 4% [S407] [Confirmed].
- Voluntary redundancy on enhanced terms offered to "less than 3% of our A&A business" due to low attrition [S407].
- New UK technology delivery centres "fully operational in FY26" [S407].
- Leadership: Darren Graves UK CEO from 1 June 2026; Jane Whitlock Chair of UK Board; Hayley McKelvey joined UK Executive as Chief AI Officer "responsible for driving the firm's AI strategy across client services and internal operations"; Richard Houston CEO of the new EMEA firm from 1 June 2026 [S407] [Confirmed].
- Legal structure, verbatim from release: "Deloitte LLP is a limited liability partnership registered in England and Wales with registered number OC303675 and its registered office at 1 New Street Square, London EC4A 3HQ ... Deloitte LLP's affiliate (Deloitte Management Services Limited) is a shareholder in Deloitte EMEA BV (Deloitte EMEA). Deloitte EMEA is a member firm of Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu Limited (DTTL). DTTL and Deloitte EMEA do not provide services to clients." [S407] [Confirmed]
- Midlands office relocating to One Centenary Way, Birmingham in October 2026; new offices in Aberdeen, Belfast, Bristol and Manchester over the last two years [S407].
- Graves quote: "As AI makes intelligence increasingly abundant, the role our people play will matter more, not less. AI can't replace lived experience or replicate the judgement and unwritten know-how built..." [S407]
