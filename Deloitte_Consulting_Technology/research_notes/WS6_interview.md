# WS6 + WS7: Interview intelligence and the people you will meet (Deloitte UK, Consulting / Technology, 2027 entry)

Prepared 2026-10-04. Source range S260-S299. Tags: [Confirmed] primary source or two credible outlets; [Reported] single credible source or candidate report; [Inferred] my reasoning.

Important framing. Deloitte UK's official early-careers process (S260) is NOT the same as the US Deloitte Consulting process that dominates English-language prep sites (group case, multiple case interviews). Many third-party guides blend the two. Where they conflict, trust S260 and S261 (deloitte.com/uk) first.

## 1. Findings

### A. The recruitment process, stage by stage

#### A0. Overview and which programmes use it

- Deloitte UK runs one common process for Summer Vacation Schemes, Industrial Placements, Graduate and BrightStart programmes. Entry Level Apprenticeships merge stages 2 and 3 into a single "OneStop" assessment [S260] [Confirmed].
- Five stages: (1) online application, (2) Immersive Online Assessment, (3) Job Simulation, (4) Final Stage Assessment, (5) outcome [S260] [Confirmed, also matches S263 and S264].
- Some programmes stop early. Career Shapers and Aspire end after stage 2. Spring Into Deloitte ends after stage 3. The Summer Vacation Scheme final stage is the interview only, delivered virtually [S260] [Confirmed].
- One application per programme, per academic year (September to August). If you apply to more than one, only the first counts, and you cannot transfer an application between roles [S261] [S262] [S263] [Confirmed]. Practical meaning: pick your one Consulting or Technology & Transformation role carefully.
- Rolling closure. Deloitte says it "close[s] applications early when we get a very high volume of submissions" [S261] [Confirmed]. The 2027 Consulting Graduate Programme advert says applications "will close on a rolling basis when we have enough applications" [S272] [Reported]. So this is effectively rolling recruitment. Apply early.
- Opening dates. The official timelines page I could read still showed the 2024 cycle: Consulting and Advisory opened on 5 October 2024; Audit, Tax and some Technology on 28 September 2024; Scotland programmes on 21 September 2024 [S262] [Confirmed for 2024 only]. The page had not been updated for the 2027-entry cycle when fetched. [Inferred] the 2027-entry cycle opens on a similar late September / early October pattern; the Higherin "register your interest" listing for the Consulting Graduate Programme 2027 already showed "deadline has expired" on 2026-10-04, which suggests the register-interest phase is over and live adverts are now on Deloitte's own site [S272].

#### A1. Stage 1: Online application form (confidence: HIGH)

- About 15 minutes, one sitting, progress is not saved [S260] [Confirmed].
- Eligibility screening against academic criteria. Graduate and work-experience roles typically require "a 2:1 or higher in your undergraduate degree"; BrightStart needs about 104 UCAS points from top three A-levels [S260] [Confirmed]. The 2027 advert: "a minimum 2:1 in any degree discipline and have graduated by September 2027 for the Autumn 2027 intake" [S272] [Reported].
- Have GCSE and A-level details to hand [S260] [Confirmed].
- Extenuating circumstances can be explained in the form for individual consideration [S260] [S261] [Confirmed].
- If you meet the programme requirements, you move automatically to stage 2 [S260] [Confirmed]. TargetJobs says results within two weeks [S263] [Reported]; WikiJob says immediate [S264] [Reported]. Treat the official "automatic" wording as correct.
- No cover letter or long-form motivational questions are described by Deloitte for this stage [S260] [Inferred from absence; the official page lists none].

#### A2. Stage 2: Immersive Online Assessment (confidence: HIGH on structure, MEDIUM on timings)

- Two parts assessed together: a behavioural assessment (rank responses to realistic workplace scenarios, by your own preferred approach) and a cognitive assessment (numerical and verbal reasoning) [S260] [Confirmed].
- No overall time limit, but some questions are timed. Do it in one sitting [S260] [Confirmed]. TargetJobs puts it at about 30 minutes [S263]; WikiJob about 40 minutes [S264] [Reported].
- You receive a strengths report, which is NOT the outcome, within four weeks [S260] [Confirmed].
- Official advice from the recruitment team: "go with your gut instinct, don't try to second guess what it is we might be looking for" [S260] [Confirmed].
- Practice tests are on Deloitte's "Candidate Zone" [S260] [Confirmed]. Deloitte warns that paid third-party sites claiming insight "lack Deloitte endorsement" [S260] [Confirmed].
- Third-party sites describe it as "game-based" with about 35 questions across five sections [search snippets from prep vendors] [Reported, low reliability]. Deloitte does not confirm a question count.

#### A3. Stage 3: Job Simulation, the video stage (confidence: HIGH on components, MEDIUM on timings and platform)

- Deloitte's own description: ranking response options, multiple choice, written responses, and video responses. It includes "scenarios referencing AI", with no prior AI experience needed because the information is given inside the questions [S260] [Confirmed].
- It is a one-way recorded assessment, not a live interview [S260] [Confirmed]. Deloitte UK does not name the platform on its page [S260]. Prep sites call it HireVue [S287] [Reported]. Do not state the vendor in interview.
- Durations reported: about 40 minutes [S263], about 45 minutes [S264], 40 to 60 minutes with 2 to 4 video questions, 30 to 60 seconds thinking time and 2 to 3 minutes per answer [S288] [Reported, single vendor].
- TargetJobs describes video answers of 30 seconds to 2 minutes, report-summary written responses and an email task [S263] [Reported].
- Reported tasks: summarising data about AI with a recommendation; replying to a co-worker's email about workload problems [S288] [Reported].
- Outcome: Deloitte says stage 3 is completed within four weeks of stage 2, and a strengths report follows [S260] [Confirmed].
- Official advice: "Find a comfortable and quiet place to complete the assessment" [S260] [Confirmed].
- Integrity and AI. Candidates must read Deloitte's "Integrity & AI guidance" before the assessments. Non-compliance is treated as a breach of process [S260] [S261] [Confirmed]. Implication: do not use generative AI to write your answers.

#### A4. Stage 4: Final Stage Assessment (confidence: HIGH for current structure; LOW for group exercise or partner interview at graduate level)

- Format: Graduate, Industrial Placement, BrightStart and Entry Level Apprentice candidates attend in person at their linked Deloitte office. Summer Vacation Scheme is virtual [S260] [Confirmed].
- Mandatory preparation: you are given a topic to research and analyse beforehand [S260] [Confirmed]. TargetJobs says candidates choose from four pre-selected topics [S263] [Reported].
- Exercise 1, Topic Discussion: 30 to 40 minutes, a "two-way discussion" with an assessor who asks questions throughout [S260] [Confirmed]. Older reports describe this as a 5-minute presentation followed by Q&A, with broad topics such as "globalisation" [S264] [Reported, older format]. [Inferred] Prepare a 5-minute structured opening anyway; it works for both formats.
- Exercise 2, Skills and Motivation Interview: about 50 minutes, "questions to learn more about your alignment with the capabilities we look for at Deloitte" [S260] [Confirmed]. TargetJobs lists question types: competency, motivational, scenario-based, personal (working style and values), and topical (industry or societal issues) [S263] [Reported].
- Summer Vacation Scheme: interview only, no topic discussion [S260] [Confirmed].
- Group exercise and partner interview. Some Glassdoor UK reports (dates not visible to me) mention group exercises, case studies and a partner interview for the final offer [S289] [Reported, SNIPPET]. US-centred guides say Deloitte "consistently uses a group case interview in its final round" [S292]. The current official UK page lists only the two exercises above [S260]. [Inferred] For 2027 UK graduate entry, expect topic discussion plus interview, not a group case. Treat a group exercise as possible only if your invitation says so.
- Official advice: "Our assessors are here to get the best out of you, they are not trying to trip you up or trick you" [S260] [Confirmed]. Dress: "when in doubt, overdress" [S263] [Reported, attributed to Deloitte].
- Outcome: within four weeks, with feedback for development [S260] [Confirmed]. WikiJob reports 1 to 3 weeks [S264] [Reported].

#### A5. Stage 5: Outcome

- Success: offer, then onboarding via Candidate Zone [S260] [Confirmed].
- Unsuccessful: you may reapply in the next academic year [S260] [S263] [Confirmed].

#### A7. Contextual and "blind" recruitment

- In September 2015 Deloitte UK announced that recruiters would no longer know which school or university candidates attended, and that a contextual algorithm would weigh grades against school and personal circumstances. The example: "3Bs at A Level in a school where the average student achieves 3Ds, is identified as exceptional performance." Then-CEO David Sproul announced it. About 1,500 hires a year were covered [S267] [Confirmed with S268]. The partner was Rare Recruitment, whose data covered exam results from over 4,000 schools and 2.5 million postcodes [S268] [Reported, SNIPPET].
- Correction to the brief: the date is 2015 (announced 29 September 2015), not 2016 [S267] [Confirmed].
- Current status: the 2026 official pages talk about extenuating circumstances and inclusive assessment design, and Disability Confident accreditation [S260]. They do not name Rare or describe school/university-blind interviewing [S260] [S261]. [Gap] I could not confirm whether the blind policy and Rare contextual data are still in use for the 2027 cycle.

#### A8. What Deloitte says it assesses

- Official framing: "alignment with the capabilities we look for at Deloitte" [S260] [Confirmed]. The capability list itself is not published on the page I read [S260].
- Business-area pages say what they look for. Consulting: curious, open-minded people who enjoy collaboration, networking and relationship-building, with a strong learning orientation [S269] [Confirmed]. Technology: "passion and curiosity", "resilient and innovative", enjoy "solving problems" and "learning" [S270] [Confirmed].
- Deloitte's five Shared Values (global, current): Lead the way; Serve with integrity; Take care of each other; Foster inclusion; Collaborate for measurable impact [S266] [Confirmed]. New joiners' induction covers the firm's purpose and shared values [S294] [Reported, SNIPPET].
- Warning: WikiJob still describes interviews as structured around "four core values (Integrity, Outstanding value, Commitment, Diversity)" [S264]. That is an older Deloitte values set. Use the five Shared Values above [S266] [Inferred that S264 is outdated].
- The brief mentioned "Deloitte Talent Standards". I found no UK early-careers page using that phrase [Gap]. Do not cite it.

5. How would you approach being successful in a new team? REP [S288] (paraphrase). Tests: adaptability, learning.
6. A senior colleague asks for help late in the day. What do you do? REP [S288] (paraphrase). Tests: prioritisation, Take care of each other, boundaries.
7. A colleague emails about a discrepancy in your work. How do you respond? REP [S288] (paraphrase). Tests: ownership, integrity, written tone.
8. Describe a time you were successful in a team. REP [S288] (paraphrase). Tests: teamwork with measurable result.
9. Written task: summarise data about AI and make a recommendation. REP [S288]. Tests: synthesis, structured writing, so-what thinking.
10. Email task: reply to a co-worker raising workload problems. REP [S288]. Tests: empathy, escalation judgement.
11. "Introduce yourself to the team, give your view on a workplace-values topic, and respond to a business scenario for the case client." Practice question labelled UK Job Simulation by the vendor, not candidate-reported [S287]. Tests: same as above.
12. AI-referencing scenarios where all needed information is given in the question. OFF [S260]. Tests: responsible AI judgement, not AI knowledge.

### C. What follows the offer

- Personal Independence. "You will be required to clear Personal Independence checks before you join us." These restrict holding certain financial interests in audit clients, and secondary employment with SEC audit clients [S261] [Confirmed]. A search snippet adds that you must be "cleared by the Personal Independence Team before you can join the firm" and may need to evidence compliance [S293] [Reported, SNIPPET]. Plain English: Deloitte audits many listed companies, so staff must not own shares in some of them. Expect to declare investments, including ISAs or pension self-selected holdings.
- Pre-employment screening. A search snippet from Deloitte UK careers content says new joiners are contacted by Security Watchdog, complete an online questionnaire, upload right-to-work and proof of residency, and sign a consent declaration [S293] [Reported, SNIPPET, exact page not confirmed]. An older Indeed answer names Verifile and says checks include references, qualifications and credit history, taking 2 to 4 weeks [S291] [Reported, low reliability, undated].
- BPSS (the UK government's Baseline Personnel Security Standard). [Gap] No Deloitte UK source says BPSS is standard for all graduates. [Inferred] It is likely required only for people staffed on UK government work, and higher clearance (SC) for some public sector and defence projects. Do not assert this as fact.
- Start dates. "Our graduate programmes start in September. To join us, you must have received your undergraduate degree result and be available to start on our specified dates" [S261] [Confirmed]. Autumn 2027 intake for 2027 graduates [S272] [Reported]. In 2024, 200 further joiners started in December [S293] [Reported, SNIPPET]. In September 2026, 1,353 people started in the first week; 44% (590) were based outside London across 21 UK locations [S271] [Confirmed].
- Support before joining: a "Deloitte buddy" [S261] [Confirmed].
- Training. Graduate programmes include professional qualifications [S269] [S272] [Confirmed]. Technology roles may include CISA and technology certifications [S270] [Confirmed]. A snippet mentions a "Graduate Learning Academy" with workshops, leadership sessions, coaching and networking [S294] [Reported, SNIPPET, source page unclear]. Separately, Deloitte runs 12-week paid technical academies in software development, cloud engineering, cybersecurity and data analytics; 130 people progressed from these into technology roles in 2026 [S271] [Confirmed]. Note: those are technical academies, not the same thing as the Consulting graduate programme.
- Deloitte University EMEA. Correction to the brief: it was near Brussels (La Hulpe, Belgium) from 2013, but moved in 2024 to a purpose-built campus near Paris, France. The UK site says it can host up to 500 delegates a day with 22,000 square metres of learning space and 260 bedrooms [S283] [Confirmed]. Location is Bailly-Romainvilliers, east of Paris; inaugurated June 2024 [S284] [Reported, SNIPPET]. [Gap] Whether UK graduates attend in year one is not stated [S283].

### D. WS7: The people you will meet

#### D(i). Who interviews at each stage, and how to pitch

| Stage | Who | Evidence | Pitch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Application, Online Assessment | Nobody live. Automated eligibility and scoring | [S260] Confirmed | Accuracy. Gut-instinct honesty on ranking tasks |
| Job Simulation | Recorded; reviewed by recruitment team or assessors. Platform not named by Deloitte UK | [S260] Confirmed; vendor [S287] Reported | Imagine a busy manager. Answer in the first 15 seconds, then evidence |
| Topic Discussion | An "assessor" | [S260] Confirmed | Two-way. Show structure, then go deep on follow-ups. Defend a view, accept good challenge |
| Skills and Motivation interview | "Senior person from your chosen service line" | [S264] Reported | Mix competency stories with a clear "why this team" |
| Possible extra / older format | Senior managers, directors or partners | [S286] [S289] Reported | Shorter, commercial, judgement-focused |

[Inferred] The early-careers recruitment team (named staff like "Blair", "Becky", "Lax", "Amelia" give advice on the official page [S260]) runs logistics. Final stage assessors are usually practitioners: managers or senior managers, sometimes directors. Pitch shifts with seniority:
- Manager / senior manager: "Can I give this person work on Monday?" Show reliability, Excel and PowerPoint basics, learning speed, team behaviour.
- Director / partner: "Would I put this person in front of a client, and will they stay?" Show a sharp, defended opinion about the client market, real motivation, and values (integrity, inclusion).

## 6. Interview angles

- Say: "I know the final stage is a two-way topic discussion, so I prepared a view, not just a summary, and I'm happy to be challenged on it." Shows you read the official format [S260].
- Say: "Technology & Transformation grew 4% to £1.75bn in FY26 while the firm grew 2%. That tells me where clients are spending, and it's where I want to build skills." [S279]
- Say: "Darren Graves joined as a tax graduate in 2001 and is now CEO. That kind of path is part of why I value a firm that develops people from graduate level." [S273]
- Say: "The new Chief AI Officer role covers internal operations as well as client work. I'd like to be the graduate who uses those tools well and checks the output." Links AI to integrity [S274] [S260].
- Say: "Of the Shared Values, 'collaborate for measurable impact' is the one I can evidence best," then give a STAR story with a number [S266].
- Avoid saying the old values "Integrity, Outstanding value, Commitment, Diversity". They are outdated [S264] vs [S266].
- Avoid naming HireVue or citing paid prep sites as insight; Deloitte explicitly distances itself from them [S260].
- Ask about Deloitte EMEA (launched 1 June 2026, over €1.5bn planned investment) and what it means for cross-border graduate work [S277].
- Ask how staffing works for regional starters, citing 44% of September 2026 starters outside London [S271].
- Ask about personal independence rules in practice, which shows you understand Deloitte is also an auditor [S261].
- Say, if asked about fairness in hiring: "I read that Deloitte introduced school- and university-blind interviews and contextual recruitment back in 2015." Then ask whether it still applies [S267].
