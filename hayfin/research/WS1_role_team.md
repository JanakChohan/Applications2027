### 1.5 Decoding each JD duty line

**JD line 1: "Supporting marketing and fundraising activities, incl. pitch materials, DDQ responses, investor presentations."**

Pitch materials. A pitch book for a private credit fund normally covers: firm overview (AUM, offices, team), strategy and market opportunity, origination edge, investment process and committee structure, track record (gross and net IRR, MOIC, loss rate by vintage), portfolio construction, ESG integration, risk management, terms summary, and case studies. [Inferred from S25, S30, S31] Hayfin's own messaging gives the themes: sole-lender and club deals (40% of the DL book sole lender), lower leverage and tighter documentation in Europe, under 5% tech exposure in private credit, and "minimal exposure to software credits deemed most susceptible to AI disruption". [Confirmed] [S31][S7][S29] An intern updates numbers, refreshes charts, checks consistency with the latest quarter, and keeps a version log. [Inferred]

DDQ responses. A due diligence questionnaire is a standard list of questions an LP sends before investing. The main templates:
- ILPA DDQ 2.0, released November 2021. ILPA says the goal is "to reduce the administrative burden imposed on LPs, GPs, placement agents and other industry bodies by standardizing the most frequent and important diligence questions". It is not mandatory. It was built with private equity as the reference point, and ILPA says it "was not designed with" private credit in mind. [Confirmed] [S46] Its ESG section is based on the PRI LP responsible investment DDQ. It added topics such as succession planning, co-investments, GP-led secondaries, credit facilities, and cybersecurity. It comes with a separate Diversity Metrics Template. [Reported] [S47]
- AIMA DDQ. Modular. The 2017 edition first covered private credit and private equity. There is an Illiquid Credit module (2019) and a closed-end private credit module (ACC 2023), with a 2025 edition. [Reported] [S48]
- ESG questionnaires. PRI-based questions, SFDR questions, and climate data requests. Hayfin is a PRI signatory since 2018 and scored 4/5 on Policy Governance and Strategy, 4/5 on Direct Fixed Income Private Debt, 5/5 on Confidence Building Measures. Six Hayfin funds disclose under SFDR Article 8. Hayfin shares its PRI report "with investors upon request". [Confirmed] [S25]
- Bespoke DDQs from large pensions, SWFs and consultants. These often reuse ILPA or AIMA structure but add their own questions. [Inferred]
What an intern does: pull the latest approved answer from the DDQ library, update stale numbers (AUM, headcount, team bios, fund stats), flag anything that changed since last quarter, route new questions to legal, compliance, risk, ESG or the deal team, and proofread. The Glassdoor listing describes "drafting RFP/DDQ responses for senior review" and keeping "the firm's FAQ current". [Reported] [S20]

Investor presentations. Quarterly update calls, annual meetings, Advisory Board meetings (twice a year per fund), and investor days. Hayfin's New York Investor Day in April 2026 had "over 90 investors, partners, and colleagues", with an LP dinner the night before. [Confirmed] [S31][S25] Intern work: prepare the deck, briefing notes on attendees, logistics, and take notes. The Chicago analyst posting lists "logistics, follow-up tracking, and briefing notes". [Reported] [S19]

Typical examples: an LP asks for exposure to a sector (for example software or AI-sensitive names), a look-through of loans to a country, a PIK breakdown, a cash flow forecast for capital calls, a currency breakdown, a tax form, or a consultant's quarterly data template. [Inferred] Hayfin says that in the DLF V raise "LP scrutiny of downside protection" was "even deeper than in previous cycles, particularly around use of PIK, technology exposure and loss rates". [Confirmed] [S30] These are exactly the ad hoc questions an intern will see. [Inferred]

**JD line 3: "Developing case studies on investments for marketing and investor comms."**

A case study is one or two slides on a single deal: borrower, sponsor, sector, why Hayfin won (sole lender, speed, structure), terms (senior secured, leverage, covenants), ESG angle, and outcome. Hayfin already publishes ESG case studies in its Quarterly Investor Letter. [Confirmed] [S25] Public deal news gives raw material: for example Condis (EUR305m, September 2026), Juvisé Pharmaceuticals (EUR550m refinancing, February 2026), Hyve (June 2026). [Confirmed] [S37]

The compliance bright lines for case studies:
- Fair, clear and not misleading. COBS 4.2.1R(1): "A firm must ensure that a communication or a financial promotion is fair, clear and not misleading." [Confirmed] [S49]
- Past performance. COBS 4.6.2R requires that past performance is not the most prominent feature, covers five years (or since inception) in complete 12-month periods, states the period and source, carries a warning that past performance "is not a reliable indicator of future results", flags currency risk, and, if shown gross, discloses the effect of fees. [Confirmed] [S50] Note: Hayfin's investors are professional clients. The COBS 4.6 past-performance detail is written mainly for retail communications, so for Hayfin it is best practice rather than always a hard rule. COBS 4.2.1R applies regardless. [Inferred; S51 did not settle the client-category mapping]
- Cherry-picking. Picking only winning deals for case studies creates a misleading picture. In the US, the SEC Marketing Rule (Rule 206(4)-1) bans showing gross performance without net performance of equal prominence. SEC staff FAQs in 2023 required net figures for extracted single-deal performance. A 19 March 2025 FAQ allows gross-only extracts if they are "clearly identified as being calculated on a gross basis", and the total portfolio's gross and net performance is shown "with at least equal prominence" over "a period that includes the entire period" of the extract. [Confirmed] [S53] Hayfin has US offices and a US entity (Hayfin Capital Management LLC appears in its legal disclaimers), so US marketing rules are relevant for US-facing material. [Reported for entity name] [S27] [Inferred for applicability]
- Selection policy. Good practice is a written rule for how case studies are chosen (for example all deals above a size, or a mix of good and bad outcomes) and compliance sign-off on every external slide. [Inferred]
- Greenwashing. The FCA anti-greenwashing rule, ESG 4.3.1R, requires any reference to sustainability characteristics to be "consistent with the sustainability characteristics of the product or service" and "fair, clear and not misleading". [Confirmed] [S52] Hayfin trained its "Investor Relations and Partner Solutions teams" on this rule in 2024. [Confirmed] [S25] ESG case studies need extra care. [Inferred]
- Hypothetical performance. Hayfin's legal page carries a long disclaimer for hypothetical or model performance. [Confirmed] [S27] Any modelled returns in a pitch need that disclaimer. [Inferred]
- AIFMD marketing. In the EU, pre-marketing must be notified to the regulator within two weeks, cannot include subscription documents "whether in a draft or a final form", and any subscription within 18 months of pre-marketing counts as marketing. [Reported] [S54] UK managers marketing into the EU rely on each country's national private placement regime. [Reported] [S54] An intern should never send a draft LPA or subscription form to a prospect without clearance. [Inferred]

**JD line 4: "Assisting with quarterly LP reporting, incl. updating performance data and proofreading reports."**

What Hayfin sends each quarter, per its Stewardship report [Confirmed] [S25]:
- Summary of Investments and Portfolio Update: "A line-by-line track record is provided for both realised and unrealised investments", with amount invested, called, carrying value, realised proceeds, expected gross IRR at investment and fund level, deployment and repayment profile, and portfolio characteristics.
- Portfolio Breakdown: per-investment detail including LTM EBITDA, enterprise value and leverage.
- CEO Letter: released through the administrator portal.
- Audited financials: annual, at 31 December, within 90 days of year end.
- Plus a Quarterly Investor Letter with ESG case studies. [Confirmed] [S25]

Key metrics an intern must understand:
- IRR: the discount rate that sets the NPV of all cash flows to zero. Gross IRR is before fees and carry. Net IRR is after. [Inferred, standard definition]
- MOIC (multiple on invested capital): total value divided by invested capital, usually gross. [Inferred]
- DPI (distributions to paid-in): cash returned divided by cash called. [Inferred]
- RVPI (residual value to paid-in): NAV divided by paid-in. TVPI = DPI + RVPI, usually net. [Inferred]
- Subscription line effect: borrowing at fund level before calling capital raises IRR without changing the multiple. [Inferred] The new ILPA Performance Template shows net IRR and TVPI with and without subscription line effects. [Reported] [S45 and search summary]
- Capital account statement: each LP's opening balance, contributions, distributions, fees, expenses, carry and gains, ending balance. [Inferred] The ILPA Reporting Template has "two main components: a capital account statement, and a schedule of fees and reimbursements". [Reported] [S45]

ILPA templates (new in 2025):
- ILPA released an updated Reporting Template (v2.0) and a new Performance Template on 22 January 2025. [Confirmed] [S43][S45]
- The Reporting Template replaces the 2016 version "for Funds still in their investment period during Q1 2026" or funds starting on or after 1 January 2026. [Confirmed] [S43]
- The Performance Template applies to new funds commencing on or after 1 January 2026. It has a Granular method and a Gross Up method. Travers Smith says first delivery is due after the quarter ended 31 March 2027. It can be used by private credit funds, though it is "quite US-focused". [Reported] [S45]
- Adoption is voluntary, but "investors may put pressure on sponsors to adopt". "Modifications to the template are, generally speaking, no longer permitted." [Reported] [S45]
- Relevance to Hayfin: DLF V was still investing in 2026 (over 50% deployed at close). [Confirmed] [S29] So DLF V reporting is likely in scope if Hayfin adopts the template. [Inferred] A summer 2027 intern could be working on the first Performance Template deliveries. [Inferred]

Proofreading in practice: check every number ties to the source (administrator report, portfolio monitoring system), check dates and currency, check totals sum, check that commentary matches the numbers, check names of borrowers are spelled correctly and that confidential names are not disclosed where they should not be. [Inferred] Dartmouth Partners' IR analyst profile: "Materials must be prepared in a timely manner, but also with little to no errors." [Reported] [S59]
