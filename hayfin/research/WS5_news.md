#### Story 1. Hayfin closes Direct Lending Fund V above EUR15bn

  - Target was EUR10bn per a 9fin headline of 5 March 2026 [S238]. Article body not read. [Reported, SNIPPET]

  - Rated note feeder for insurers contributed about USD600m of investable capital; Proskauer advised [S221] [S237]. Hayfin says it was "one of the first priced by a European direct lending manager" [S222]. [Confirmed for size; "one of the first" is Hayfin's claim]

#### Story 6. The US semi-liquid fund redemption wave (the defining story of 2026)
- Headline (example): "Blackstone's BCRED Caps Redemptions After Investors Seek 10%"
- Outlet and date: Bloomberg (via Bloomberg Law), 4 June 2026 [S243] (SNIPPET; Bloomberg not read in full). Follow-up: "Goldman Sachs private credit BDC redemptions come in below 5% again in Q3", PitchBook News via Yahoo Finance, 30 September 2026 [S244] (FETCHED).
- Facts:
  - BCRED (about USD79bn) received Q2 requests of about 10% of shares and paid 5%, after lifting its limit in Q1 [S243]. [Reported: Bloomberg headline plus several republishers; not read in full]
  - Goldman Sachs Private Credit Corp. Q3 requests were 2% of shares, after 4.99% in Q1 and 3.24% in Q2. PitchBook says the 5% limit "was exceeded at most peer funds in the industry this year" and that requests "have begun to fall" in Q3 but remain above 5% at many [S244]. [Reported]
  - With Intelligence: "Private wealth products, most notably non-traded BDCs, have come under huge redemption pressure in 2026" [S241]. [Reported]
  - Secondary trackers report Blue Owl's OCIC at about 16.8% Q3 requests and BCRED at about 10% for Q3. I could not read the filings, so these are not used as facts here. [Unverified]
  - Hayfin's framing: "a surge in redemptions and gating in some US semi-liquid private credit vehicles has forced price discovery" [S227]. "Recent strains within semi-liquid US private credit funds for retail investors have accelerated LP demand for conservative fund structures" [S221].
- Beginner explanation: A non-traded BDC is a US fund that lends to companies and is sold to wealthy individuals. Investors can ask for money back each quarter, but the fund normally only pays out up to 5% of the fund per quarter, because the loans can't be sold quickly. In 2026 far more than 5% asked to leave, so funds "gated" (paid only part).
- Why it matters to Hayfin: Hayfin says its private credit funds use "fully locked-up institutional drawdown funds, with no retail capital" [S227]. It turned the stress into a selling point and says it helped DLF V [S221]. It also suggests US lenders may pull back from Europe [S227]. [Reported: Hayfin view]
- Why it matters to PSG: This is the number one LP question of 2026: "What is your liquidity mismatch?" PSG must explain fund terms (drawdown, lock-up, evergreen redemption mechanics) clearly. Hayfin also offers "institutional evergreen vehicles" [S221], so the candidate should be ready to distinguish institutional evergreen from retail semi-liquid. [Inferred]
- Opinion the candidate could offer: "The gating shows the product worked as designed, but the marketing did not. The lesson for IR is that liquidity terms must be explained so clearly that no investor is surprised. That is a PSG job, not just a legal one."

#### Story 8. Record US private credit default rates, driven by PIK and deferrals
- Headline: "Private Credit Defaults Climb To 6.3% In August, Fitch Says"
- Outlet and date: Benzinga via Webull, 14 September 2026, citing Fitch Ratings [S245] (FETCHED). Earlier: "US Private Credit Defaults Hit New Record of 6% in April: Fitch", Bloomberg via Bloomberg Law, May 2026 [S234] (SNIPPET).
- Facts: Fitch US private credit trailing 12-month default rate 6.3% in August 2026, up from 6.1% in July, above 6% since April. 89 issuers, 109 default events. Interest deferrals and PIK substitutions were 47% of events; maturity extensions under stress 41%; bankruptcies and restructurings only 4%. Borrowers with under USD25m EBITDA defaulted at 12.0%. Healthcare providers 9.9%; technology software 0.6% [S245]. [Reported: single fetched source citing Fitch; April record also in Bloomberg headline S234]
- Caution: Fitch's private credit default definition is broad (it counts PIK switches and extensions), so it is not comparable to a bond default rate. [Inferred]

- Why it matters to Hayfin: Hayfin published "A Tale of Three PIKs" on 28 July 2026, arguing for "good PIK", "bad PIK" and "fake good PIK" (PIK agreed at entry but never truly covered by value) [S229]. LPs asked about PIK in DLF V diligence [S222]. [Reported]
- Why it matters to PSG: Expect LP questions on PIK share of income, non-accruals and amendments. Quarterly LP reports increasingly break these out. [Inferred]
- Question: "Fitch says almost half of US private credit defaults are PIK or deferrals. How does Hayfin report PIK income to LPs, and does it distinguish structured PIK from amendment PIK in quarterly reports, as the 'Three PIKs' article suggests it should?"

### 1C. Regulation of the role

#### AIFMD II (EU)
- Transposition deadline 16 April 2026. Linklaters, 16 April 2026: "EU AIFMs are now subject to stricter rules for loan origination, delegation and liquidity management" [S251]. [Confirmed: Linklaters + Skadden]
- Loan-originating AIF rules: leverage caps 300% (closed-ended) and 175% (open-ended); retain at least 5% of loans originated and sold; ban on "originate to distribute"; loan funds closed-ended by default unless liquidity can be shown [S251] [S252]. Grandfathering for funds set up before 15 April 2024 [S251].
- Liquidity: open-ended AIFs must pick at least two liquidity management tools from an EU list and disclose them; one-year grace for existing funds [S251].
- Delegation: at least two full-time EU-resident persons in senior management [S251] [S252].
- Annex IV enhanced reporting applies from 16 April 2027 [S251].
- Level 2 delay: on 6 October 2025 the Commission said it will not adopt some non-essential Level 2 measures before 1 October 2027, including liquidity standards relevant to open-ended loan funds [S252]. CONTEXT (older), still live.
- Member state status: Neither Linklaters nor Skadden listed who transposed on time [S251] [S252]. A search summary (CMS guide, not fetched) said Spain expects adoption in Q4 2026. [Unverified]
- Hayfin relevance: Hayfin has a Luxembourg office and EU fund vehicles (inferred from JD office list).
