The DDQ: twenty topics, and the hard ones for Hayfin

The industry template is ILPA DDQ 2.0 (November 2021) with 20 topics: firm, fund, succession and key persons, strategy, co-investments, GP-led secondaries, credit facilities, investment process, team, alignment of interests, market environment, fund terms, governance, risk and compliance, track record, accounting and valuation, reporting, legal, data security and technology, ESG, and diversity [S299]. The DDQ workflow skillA vendor estimates 40-60 hours per DDQ across IR, finance, compliance and legal [S190]. The real skill is an answer library: each answer tagged with owner, date and source, so stale answers flag themselves. That is where AI tools help safely: first drafts from approved text only, every fact human-checked, nothing confidential pasted into public tools. Hayfin's Chicago IR posting asks for AI "used with careful attention to accuracy" [S19].

RFPs and consultant databases

An RFP (request for proposal) is a formal search by an investor or consultant with fixed questions, word limits and deadlines. Consultants also screen managers through databases such as eVestment, which covered 16,231 GP profiles in Q1 2026 (vendor figure) [S55]. Hayfin's Chicago posting mentions tracking "consultant scoring activity" [S19]. Because DLF V had more consultant involvement than any earlier Hayfin fund [S222], database accuracy is now a fundraising tool, not admin.

The case study, built properly

One-page case study template (illustrative): 1. Situation borrower, sector, country, sponsor, why it needed capital 2. Why Hayfin sole lender? speed? sector team? incumbency? 3. Structure senior / unitranche, size, leverage, covenants (only cleared terms) 4. Protections equity cushion, security, documentation 5. Outcome realised or current status; if shown, gross AND fund-level net 6. Disclosures selection basis; "past performance is not a reliable indicator..."; hypothetical-performance wording where relevant; Compliance sign-off

The rules: fair, clear and not misleading (COBS 4.2.1R) [S49]; past-performance standards (COBS 4.6.2R) [S50]; for US material, single-deal gross figures only with whole-portfolio gross and net at equal prominence (SEC FAQ, 19 March 2025) [S53]; sustainability claims must match the product (anti-greenwashing rule, ESG 4.3.1R) [S52]. Hayfin's own PE Solutions update shows the house style: gross and net TVPI defined and hypothetical performance labelled under Rule 206(4)-1 [S330].
