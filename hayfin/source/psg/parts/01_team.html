What PSG actually is: the machine

Four engines, the data that flows through them, and where an intern plugs in

The job description lists four duties: marketing and fundraising materials, ad hoc requests, case studies and quarterly LP reporting [JD]. Those are outputs. Underneath sit four engines that produce them. Hayfin's public bios and postings show each one [S1][S19][S20][S305].

The data spine: where every number in a PSG document comes from

The single most important technical idea in investor relations is provenance: every figure must trace back to one approved source, and the same figure must match everywhere. A DDQ, a pitch book and a quarterly report that disagree by one decimal place will be noticed by a consultant.
