Where Hayfin does not lead (so you never overclaim)

  • Not the largest European DL fund. Ares ACE VI (€17.1bn) is bigger and ICG SDP 5 (~€15.2bn) is comparable [S135][S132].
  • Not the largest manager. Ares $671bn, ICG $126bn, Tikehau €53.5bn, against Hayfin's ~€40bn [S136][S133][S141][S224].
  • Not in the PEI Private Credit 200 top 10 (capital raised 2021-25): Ares is first, ICG sixth [S144]. DLF V falls in the next ranking window [Inferred].
  • Not top of the European deal league tables in H1 2026: Ares (31 deals), Arcmont (23), Apollo (20) led Debtwire's ranking [S186].
  • No PDI Lender of the Year Europe award found for 2024 or 2025; Pemberton won for 2025 [S139]. Hayfin did win Mergermarket's Private Credit Lender of the Year in September 2026 [S224].
  • No wealth or retail product, the fastest-growing capital pool; Ares raised $1.0bn into its European evergreen fund in a single quarter [S136]. A deliberate choice, but a gap.
  • Mid-sized CLO platform, similar to Permira's [S143][S225], and the US CLO arm is under review [S225].
  • "Conservative closed-end" is not unique. ICG's CEO: "we have no open-ended or so-called semi-liquid structures in direct lending" [S134].
Don't say this"Hayfin is the biggest" or "Hayfin has the best returns". Neither is supported. Say "one of the three largest European direct lending raises on record" and "the largest step-up between vintages among its peers".
