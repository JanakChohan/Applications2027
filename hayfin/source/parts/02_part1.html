The role, decoded line by line

What a Partner Solutions intern actually does, who for, how it is judged, and who sits around you
Key terms for this partInvestor relations (IR): the team that raises money from investors and keeps them informed afterwards. Fundraising: persuading investors to commit to a new fund. DDQ (due diligence questionnaire): a long standard list of questions an investor sends before investing. RFP (request for proposal): a formal search where an investor or consultant invites managers to answer a fixed questionnaire for a mandate. Consultant: a firm such as Mercer or Cambridge Associates that advises pension funds on which managers to hire. SMA (separately managed account): a single-investor vehicle that follows a strategy with custom terms. Pitch book: the main presentation used to market a fund.

What the team is called, and why the name matters

Hayfin calls its investors and borrowers "partners". Its Approach page says partners "consist of both our borrowers and investors, which include public and private pension funds, sovereign wealth funds, insurance companies, endowments, financial institutions and family offices" [S28]. The investor-facing team was called "Business Development" in 2020 and 2021 and became "Partner Solutions" by March 2023 [S12][S13][S11]. [Confirmed]

The new name is a description of the job [Inferred]. The team does not only sell a fund. It builds solutions: the same lending strategy delivered through an unlevered fund, a levered fund, an SMA, an evergreen vehicle or a rated note feeder for insurers, depending on what each partner needs [S222]. The PSG co-heads put it this way: managers must "remain disciplined in how they invest, while becoming increasingly flexible in how investors access those investments" [S94].

A small error in the JDThe JD says "Special Memorandum Accounts (SMAs)". The industry term, and Hayfin's own term, is Separately Managed Account [S25]. Use the correct phrase in interview. If it comes up, you can say lightly that you assumed SMA meant separately managed account. That shows attention to detail without scoring a point.

Typical requests [Inferred from S30, S19]: exposure to a sector (software, AI-sensitive names); a look-through by country; the share of income paid in kind (PIK); a cash flow forecast for future capital calls; a currency breakdown; a tax form; a consultant's quarterly data template. What good looks like. Acknowledge the request the same day, confirm what exactly is wanted, pull data only from the approved source, have a senior person check before anything leaves, and log it so the next request is faster. What it reveals. Hayfin's investors are large institutions with their own reporting systems. Half of AUM is pension money and almost a fifth is insurance [S305]. Duty 3. "Developing case studies on investments for use in marketing and investor communications."

A case study is one or two slides on a single deal: borrower, sponsor, sector, why Hayfin won (sole lender, speed, structure), terms (senior secured, leverage, covenants), ESG angle and outcome. Public raw material in 2026 includes Condis (€305m unitranche, Spain) [S231], Hyve (fully underwritten for Hellman & Friedman) [S233], CloserStill (repeat borrower since 2018) [S220] and Juvisé (€550m refinancing) [S70].

This is the duty with the sharpest compliance edges. Know them unprompted:

  • Fair, clear and not misleading. FCA COBS 4.2.1R: "A firm must ensure that a communication or a financial promotion is fair, clear and not misleading" [S49]. [Confirmed]
  • Past performance. COBS 4.6.2R requires that past performance is not the most prominent feature, covers complete 12-month periods, states the source, warns that it "is not a reliable indicator of future results", and discloses the effect of fees if shown gross [S50]. These rules are written mainly for retail communications; Hayfin's investors are professional, so treat them as best practice and check with Compliance [Inferred].
  • No cherry-picking. In the US, the SEC Marketing Rule (Rule 206(4)-1) bans gross performance without net performance shown with equal prominence. A 19 March 2025 SEC staff FAQ allows gross-only figures for a single extracted deal only if the whole portfolio's gross and net performance is shown with at least equal prominence [S53][S173]. Hayfin's own published strategy updates carry this language [S330]. [Confirmed]
  • Greenwashing. The FCA anti-greenwashing rule (ESG 4.3.1R) requires sustainability claims to be "consistent with the sustainability characteristics of the product or service" and "fair, clear and not misleading" [S52]. Hayfin trained its "Investor Relations and Partner Solutions teams" on this rule in 2024 [S25]. [Confirmed]
  • Confidentiality. Borrower names and numbers in a case study must be public or cleared. Loan terms are usually confidential under the facility agreement [Inferred].

Duty 4. "Assisting with quarterly LP reporting, including updating performance data and proofreading reports."

Hayfin's Stewardship report lists exactly what goes to each fund and SMA every quarter [S305]: a Summary of Investments and Portfolio Update ("A line-by-line track record is provided for both realised and unrealised investments", with amount invested and called, carrying value, realised proceeds, expected gross IRR at deal and fund level, deployment and repayment profile); a Portfolio Breakdown with each company's LTM EBITDA, enterprise value and leverage; a CEO Letter sent through the administrator's portal; and audited financials within 90 days of 31 December. ESG case studies are "drafted by the ESG function and overseen by the Investor Relations team" [S305]. [Confirmed]

Key term · the return metrics you will touchIRR (internal rate of return): the annual return that makes all cash in and out net to zero. Gross = before fees and carry; net = after. MOIC: total value divided by money invested. DPI (distributions to paid-in): cash returned divided by cash called. RVPI: remaining value divided by cash called. TVPI = DPI + RVPI. Subscription line: a short-term bank loan to the fund that delays capital calls; it lifts IRR without changing the multiple. LTM EBITDA: a company's last twelve months of earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation. Leverage: debt divided by EBITDA.

The 2026 change you should know. ILPA released an updated Reporting Template and a new Performance Template on 22 January 2025. The Reporting Template applies to funds "still in their investment period during Q1 2026" or starting on or after 1 January 2026 [S43]. The Performance Template's first delivery is due after the quarter ending 31 March 2027, according to Travers Smith [S45]. DLF V was still investing in 2026, so it is likely in scope if Hayfin adopts the templates [Inferred]. A summer 2027 intern may work on the first deliveries. Hayfin's adoption is not public [Gap].

What good proofreading looks like. Every number ties to its source (administrator report, monitoring system); dates and currencies are right; totals add; commentary matches the numbers; borrower names are spelled correctly and none are disclosed that should not be. A recruiter's description of IR work: "Materials must be prepared in a timely manner, but also with little to no errors" [S59].

The role's value chain and its two channels

PSG's senior hires since 2023 include a head of consultant relations from BlackRock (Bringardner), an ex-Mercer consultant (Rob Kneip), an ex-JANA CIO (Carew) and an ex-Meketa principal (Atkinson) [S1][S4][S272]. That is a deliberate strategy to win the gatekeepers [Inferred]. Consultants pick managers for dozens of pension funds at once, so one consultant "buy" rating can unlock many commitments.

A realistic career path from this seat

  • The ladder: Analyst, Associate, VP or Principal, Director, MD. Hayfin uses Principal, Director and MD titles in PSG [S9][S1].
  • Two tracks: product and reporting (content, DDQs, structuring) or coverage (relationships, a region) [Inferred].
  • How Hayfin's PSG seniors got there: other managers' IR teams, investment consultants (Mercer, JANA, Meketa), allocators (Texas Permanent School Fund), and internal moves (Supranowicz from High Yield) [S1][S289].
  • A role model: Camilla Coriani started with an Intesa Sanpaolo investment banking internship, moved to IR at FiveSixty, then sales and structuring at 1OAK, joined Hayfin in 2015 and is now Head of MENA and Southern Europe [S288].
  • The internship programme: Hayfin says it has run a 10-week programme since 2021, with 64 interns from 28 universities, and calls SEO London's Alternative Investments Programme "the main talent recruiter for the previous three years" [S16].
