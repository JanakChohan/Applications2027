# SHL and the Barclays Online Assessment

### The firm, the full product catalogue, how every test is scored, what the integrity systems detect — and exactly what it takes to pass at Barclays

**Version 1.0** · Generated **1 August 2026**

A companion volume to *The Definitive Guide to UK Finance Early-Careers Online Assessments* (which covers thirteen providers) and *The Aon (cut-e) Assessment Deep-Dive*. This report goes deep on one publisher — **SHL**, the oldest and most-deployed name in occupational assessment — and then applies all of it to one employer: **Barclays**.

## Why this report exists

Assessment publishers operate on **information asymmetry**. The employer knows which instruments it bought, which norm group it selected, and where it set the bar. The vendor knows the scoring algorithm, the item bank and the integrity telemetry. **The candidate knows none of it** — and is told, in effect, to turn up and be measured. That asymmetry is not accidental: some of it protects test security (a defensible interest), but much of it simply survives because nobody assembles the public record.

This report assembles the public record for SHL, and then does the harder thing: applies it to a named employer. It answers the questions candidates actually ask and vendors rarely address —

- **What is SHL, and why would a bank like Barclays buy it** rather than a rival?
- **What is every product in the catalogue**, and how do they link into one hiring system?
- **What are the actual questions**, format by format?
- **How is each instrument scored** — raw to scaled to normed, and who decides the threshold?
- **How well do I have to do to pass — and does it depend on the role I applied for?**
- **What do the anti-cheating systems detect**, how are flags actioned, and how do honest candidates avoid tripping them?
- **What, concretely, do I do to pass the Barclays online assessment?**

## How to read the evidence tags

Every substantive claim carries a label saying how much to trust it. This is the discipline that separates this report from the confidently-wrong content that dominates search results for these terms.

- **`[EMPLOYER]`** — Barclays' own official statement (careers pages, published process descriptions). The gold standard for what Barclays actually does.
- **`[VENDOR]`** — SHL's own claim, not independently verified. Authoritative about *what they sell*; still marketing.
- **`[VENDOR-manual]`** — extracted from SHL's own technical documentation. The strongest vendor evidence: psychometrics as they describe them to professionals, not to buyers.
- **`[INDEPENDENT]`** — peer-reviewed research, regulators, or independent journalism.
- **`[CANDIDATE, n=X]`** — candidate testimony, with a count of independent agreeing reports. Real-world but anecdotal; `n=1` is a single account.
- **`[PREP-VENDOR]`** — a commercial practice-test seller. Often accurate on *format*, but structurally motivated to make tests sound harder, and routinely states confidential cut-scores as fact. Corroborated before use.
- **`[INFERRED]`** — my reasoning from psychometric first principles, with the reasoning shown so you can check it.
- **`[UNKNOWN]`** — not publicly available. The report says *why* and gives the best proxy rather than inventing a figure.

**The standing promise: no number in this report is invented.** Cut-scores, pass rates and norm data are almost universally confidential. Where a figure would be a guess, you will see `[UNKNOWN]` and a reasoned range — and every one of them is collected in the Confidence Register (Chapter 12), so you know exactly which parts to trust least.

## A note on volatility, and on the Barclays vendor question

Bank recruitment processes **change every cycle**, and vendor contracts churn. This report states the position as of **1 August 2026** and dates its claims accordingly. One specific caution is flagged up front because it shapes Part IV: the public record on **which vendor supplies Barclays' online assessment is contested** — different sources, and different years, point to different providers. Chapter 9 resolves what can be resolved from dated evidence and is explicit about what remains uncertain, rather than picking the tidier answer.

**Verify against your own invitation email.** It names the tool, the deadline and the rules — it is the single most authoritative document about *your* assessment, and it outranks every guide, including this one.

## Ethical boundary

This report is about **understanding and legitimate preparation**. It does not reproduce live test items, answer keys or leaked question banks — those are copyrighted, they rotate, and relying on them is the fastest route to being flagged. The integrity chapters are written for **false-positive avoidance and informed compliance**, not evasion. Nothing here helps you cheat; a great deal of it helps you stop losing marks you should be winning.
