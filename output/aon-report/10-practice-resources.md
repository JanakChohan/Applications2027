# Chapter 10 — Practice Resources, Compared Honestly

One principle governs everything in this chapter: because Aon's tests are **generated per candidate and often adaptive, memorising questions is structurally worthless.** What practice buys — and it buys a lot — is **mechanic familiarity** (no test-day seconds lost decoding an unfamiliar interface), **automated operations** (percentage change, operator-reading, T/F/Cannot-Say discipline as reflexes), and **pace calibration** under the real clocks. Buy or use practice for those three things only, and judge every resource below against them. All entries are `[PREP-VENDOR]` unless noted; every commercial provider has an interest in making the tests sound harder than they are.

## Paid — worth it only for specific gaps

| Resource | Covers | Honest assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **JobTestPrep cut-e pack** | Simulations of numerical/verbal/cls/clx/ix/lst/eql/mtu | The broadest replica set; buy if you need timed, format-faithful volume beyond the free tier |
| **JobTestPrep smartPredict pack** | Interactive replicas of all four games with **unlimited generated items** | The only meaningful way to drill the games at volume; the generated-item design mirrors the real thing's logic |
| **JobTestPrep chatAssess pack** | A 90-minute extended simulation + 15 SJT practice sets | Useful for SJT reasoning practice — but remember the real test is ~20 minutes and **keyed to your specific employer's values**, which no generic pack can teach |
| **Fintest paid tier** | Finance-flavoured numerical/verbal | Good fit for bank batteries specifically |
| **12minprep switchChallenge course** | Switch-specific drilling | Niche; the free operator-reading habit matters more than the paid volume |
| **Pilot-prep providers** (pilotaptitudetest.com, cadetprep.academy) | scales mt (avi), nav, cmo aviation batteries | Only relevant for aviation; noted for completeness |

**What not to pay for:** anything promising "real cut-e questions," "leaked banks," or "the answers" — structurally impossible (Chapter 9: "sample solutions do NOT exist"), ethically off-limits for this report, and practically a scam by definition. Also skip duplicate packs: one replica source per format family is enough.

## How to practise (the method, not just the materials)

1. **Diagnose free and official first**: Aon demos + one free timed set per format → find your two weakest formats.
2. **Drill the operations, not the items**: percentage/ratio extraction for numerical; statement-first + Cannot-Say discipline for verbal; dimension-checklists for the logic family; operator-reading for switchChallenge; units-digit tricks for eql/digitChallenge.
3. **Always timed, always the real clocks** (12 min/37 items feels nothing like untimed practice), and always applying the **marking rules from Chapter 8** — practise *leaving blanks* on unknowns and eliminate-then-commit guessing; the habit must be automatic before test day because it is the opposite of SHL-trained instinct.
4. **Two or three familiarisation runs per game** — then stop; the games measure traits, and grinding them past familiarity yields nothing further.
5. **Keep an error log by cause** (misread / wrong tab / arithmetic slip / rule missed / time-out) and drill the cause, not the score. The full six-week programme in the companion guide (Chapter 6.5) slots these materials straight in.
6. **Don't practise the questionnaires** (shapes, views, squares, ADEPT-15) beyond one look at the format — preparation there is reading the firm's competency language and deciding your authentic presentation, per Chapters 5–6.
