## Closing note — the asymmetry, measured

Set out this way, the asymmetry this report was commissioned to attack turns out to have a specific shape. What Aon keeps genuinely secret is **parameters**: penalty magnitudes, algorithms, thresholds, telemetry specs. What is knowable — and now, in this report, known — is **structure**: the formats, the marking logic, the strategic rules robust to every hidden parameter, the anti-cheat architecture and its remedies. Structure is what preparation runs on. The remaining parameter gaps close one of three ways: Aon publishes (rarely), regulators force disclosure (the ACLU docket may), or **you extract your own numbers after sitting, via a subject-access request** — the one instrument of transparency that is yours by right, template in the companion guide, Chapter 6.7.
